## 2. The reusable gate: consequence → motive → journey → encounter
Use the existing Memo World Encounter Binding, World Memory Engine and civilization Memo/Novel handoffs—not a new operating system.

For every proposed spread, authors supply:
1. **Verified input:** Week, fixture, final result if known, evidence reference and actual last-seen location.
2. **Fiction authority:** Exact approved scene receipt, or mark the premise “review draft / proposed.” Separate fact, character belief, rumor and reader-only knowledge.
3. **Consequence:** What is different emotionally, socially or physically? Name its owner and duration. If nothing physical changed, say so; reputation and unresolved desire are sufficient.
4. **Away desire:** What would the visitor still want if there were no scheduled game? Why pursue it this week? For environmental Niño, substitute a world-process explanation; do not invent human intentions.
5. **Journey:** Last established location → approved route edges → encounter venue. Include returns home, access/mode restrictions and travel budget. No automatic weekly teleport/reset.
6. **Home agenda:** What is the host already doing before the visitor arrives? The host is not merely waiting to be challenged.
7. **Collision:** Why this visitor interrupts this agenda at this location now. What neither can obtain without confronting the other.
8. **Choice and cost:** At least two meaningful choices, with costs other than “lose the game.” Permit humor, embarrassment, loyalty, hospitality, ingenuity or restraint—not six revenge duels.
9. **Conditional endings:** Home win / away win / unresolved real-world status. No future score, winner or irreversible canon change before evidence.
10. **World delta:** Existing entity affected, old value, proposed new value, persistence class, source and approval owner. Rumor stays rumor; temporary physical changes require cleanup.
11. **Production proof:** Geography, silhouette/anatomy, prop ownership, route entry, continuity, weather and readable action.
12. **Handoff:** Preserve proposal status in existing packets. Author decision first; production feasibility review next; governance resolves actual canon/release gates.

Required existing binding fields remain: `encounter_id, page_id, home_team, away_team, venue_id, venue_type, division_context, terrain, climate, world_state_in, persistent_landmarks, route_from_away_origin, travel_validated, new_or_provisional_location, prior_appearances, world_state_out, canonization_notes`.

**Gate result vocabulary:** PASS / PROVISIONAL WITH NAMED GAP / HOLD. “Travel connected” does not equal “all access validated.” “Creative draft ready” does not equal “publication eligible.”

## 3. Six causal briefs

### A. Duckhook away at TDS — hospitality versus control
**Verified Week 6:** Duckhook beat Niño 132.40–119.86 at Jackson; TDS beat LLC 138.06–137.69 at the temple. Both are 3–3.

**Carryover boundary:** Jackson has an older recorded chili-contamination cleanup question; no inspected record resolves it. The Falls Protocol draft provides negotiation residue, not a new bridge or contract.

**Proposed engine:** Duckhook wants to prove that managing a disorderly course means something beyond winning there: he seeks agreement on a modest hospitality exchange with the temple. TDS is already arranging how guests may approach and use an existing temple space, reluctant to let another visitor turn hospitality into outside control. Neither motive is established history.

**Why here/now:** The scheduled visit creates the first practical occasion to test cooperation. The task could be a shared meal, a constrained game or a guest-access ritual; it need not be another golf scene. The interesting collision is an outsider optimizing what the host considers a relationship.

**Journey:** Jackson → Mire Hill → Mud’s Swamp on Delta Causeway → temple on Falls Path. Approved graph lower-bound sum: 18 hours, not an asserted itinerary or exact elapsed time. No assumed golf-cart access along the whole route. Wetland footing and waterfall sound affect staging; no snowfall or transplanted northern climate.

**Choice/stakes:** Duckhook can abandon the efficient plan to respect a guest custom; TDS can make room without surrendering control. Humor comes from incompatible definitions of a successful visit, not three interchangeable heads.

**Conditional endings:** Duckhook win can reward adaptation rather than conquest. TDS win can preserve the boundary while allowing a limited invitation. Either may leave a proposed recurring courtesy; no access treaty enters canon automatically. If unresolved, stop before the deciding action.

**Production/gaps:** TDS is one body with exactly three serpent heads. Recover approved Falls Protocol ending and Jackson cleanup status before naming actual repairs. Pittsy closure can remain background uncertainty, not the encounter’s invented cause.

### B. Niño away at DK — preparation versus exposure
**Verified Week 6:** DK beat Red 118.96–108.08 away; Niño lost to Duckhook 119.86–132.40. DK is 4–2; Niño 2–4.

**Carryover boundary:** The ruins encounter does not prove DK conquered land. Niño is environmental, so “away character wants revenge and walks uphill” is invalid.

**Proposed engine:** DK wants to turn an away victory into a credible demonstration of readiness at his existing stronghold. The counterforce is an environmental event that tests what has actually been prepared versus what merely looks impressive. This offers character choice without humanizing the weather.

**Why here/now:** The Week 7 pairing supplies the narrative frame; a grounded atmospheric propagation explanation must supply the physical cause. A pressure/weather progression is a candidate, not a verified storm track. Do not equate every chilly gust with Niño.

**Journey:** DK’s return is Red’s Ruins → Lake Bridge → Slob’s camp → Arsenal → DK stronghold, graph lower bound 19 hours. Niño’s Week 6 Jackson appearance cannot be connected to Upper Valleys by a human road route. **Environmental propagation is a named HOLD**, requiring existing world/climate ownership to establish a plausible transition.

**Choice/stakes:** DK can protect prestige or interrupt the demonstration to safeguard what matters. The cost can be admitting a preparation gap—not simply rebuilding a bigger fortress.

**Conditional endings:** DK win suggests preparation and adjustment held; Niño win reveals the gap and creates a bounded proposed cleanup consequence. No destroyed stronghold, territorial loss or new storm powers without approval. Unresolved status leaves the system under test.

**Production/gaps:** DK rendering remains HOLD. Keep this text-only. Northern gusts may shape atmosphere, but no snow or invented meteorology. Resolve propagation and character visual authority before visual production.

### C. Chins away at Obi — welcome versus proof
**Verified Week 6:** Chins beat Chili 131.78–125.24 away; Obi lost to Slob 123.38–143.70 away. Chins is 3–3; Obi 4–2.

**Carryover boundary:** The Rodeo creative amendment selected a visitor/host dynamic; exact final prose still needs confirmation. Foothill Fix draft allegations do not establish that the league result was illegitimate.

**Proposed engine:** Chins wants his road hospitality to earn genuine recognition rather than be dismissed as a traveling joke. Obi has returned to a mountain base and is already designing a fair, legible test after a frustrating away encounter. Chins brings a social offer; Obi initially tries to convert it into a controlled evaluation.

**Why here/now:** The mountain visit makes Chins’s usual easy welcome hard to offer. Their encounter becomes whether generosity can count as competence, and whether Obi can accept an exchange he cannot fully score. A duel is only one possible structure.

**Journey:** Obi returns Slob camp → Lake Bridge → mountain base, lower bound 9 hours. For Chins, account for last week’s Chili visit, not just a home reset. A connected alternative avoiding disputed Pittsy placement is Chili Stable → Chins Commons → Jackson → Mire Hill → Chamber → Lake Bridge → Obi base. Lower-bound sum 43 hours from Chili, or 31 from Commons if a return there is established. No invented shortcut. Caravan access through the final ascent remains unvalidated; a small foot-carried offering is easier to support than a wagon atop the ridge.

**Choice/stakes:** Chins can perform for approval or offer something without applause. Obi can enforce an arbitrary test or acknowledge a different kind of value.

**Conditional endings:** Chins win may puncture Obi’s overcontrol; Obi win may recognize Chins without conceding the test. Either can change mutual regard as proposed memory, not rewrite the Week 6 result.

**Production/gaps:** Cold gusts, leaves and clothing/prop behavior sell altitude without snow. Verify mount/cart clearance if retaining a caravan; do not promote the proposed test or new mountain landmark.

### D. Chili away at Mud — a fair exchange after a cruel margin
**Verified Week 6:** Chili lost to Chins 125.24–131.78 at home; Mud lost to HMB 136.80–136.92 at home. Both have reason to reassess, but their motives need not match.

**Carryover boundary:** Blackwater’s deed/chest business does not make HMB owner of Mud’s land. An extraordinary narrow loss supports emotional interpretation, not invented legal consequences.

**Proposed engine:** Chili wants to show that something from his stable/food culture has value outside his own venue. Mud is already deciding whom to trust on his difficult home ground after a painful near-win. The encounter can be an exchange that requires cooperation before competition, making both resist dependence.

**Why here/now:** Chili brings an offer to the scheduled away visit; Mud can provide access or local knowledge that Chili cannot substitute with bravado. A swamp-side preparation task, hospitality trial or awkward barter can support comedy. A hunting motif remains optional; no canonical 14-point quarry is conjured.

**Journey:** Chili Stable → shared march → TDS temple → Mud’s Swamp, graph lower bound 18 hours. Shared March connectivity is not proof that Dark Horse or a loaded wagon can traverse Falls Path. Either stage a plausible foot-arrival/transfer using existing access, or hold animal/vehicle staging for validation. Do not invent a ferry or stable.

**Choice/stakes:** Chili can listen instead of displaying mastery; Mud can accept help without treating it as surrender. Each risks embarrassment or obligation, not ownership of an entire region.

**Conditional endings:** Chili win can produce earned mutual respect; Mud win can recover confidence without cruelty. Any borrowed object must be returned or explicitly recorded. No deed transfer, dead animal, new infrastructure or permanent scar by default.

**Production/gaps:** Warm wetland autumn, muddy access and restrained seasonal signs; pumpkins only if story-useful and locally plausible. Confirm approved Blackwater aftermath and Dark Horse route access if retained.

### E. HMB away at Red, GOTW — witness versus possession

**Proposed engine:** HMB wants a meaningful encounter that cannot be reduced to possession of the belt; Red is already reclaiming authorship of what his home/place means after a loss. Their conflict asks whether being recognized requires defeating, hosting or being witnessed by a champion. This can preserve the appeal of a private, intimate contest without locking basketball or a first-to-eleven rule.

**Venue gate:** The sports fixture names Red home, but existing encounter policy defaults GOTW to neutral unless approved override. **Do not silently locate this GOTW at Red’s court/ruins.** Option 1: approved home exception, then ruins story. Option 2: neutral venue, with Red’s home agenda explaining what he leaves behind.

**Journeys:** If home exception: HMB can continue from Week 6 Mud → Mire Hill → Chamber → Lake Bridge → Red ruins, lower bound 25 hours; an established return to HMB keep instead requires the separate Keep → Obi base → Lake Bridge → Red route, 30 hours, plus earlier return. If Compact Neutral Grounds: Mud → Mire Hill → Chamber → Grounds is 8 hours; Red → Lake Bridge → Chamber → Grounds is 19. All are graph lower bounds, not actual travel claims.

**Choice/stakes:** Red can ask for acknowledgment instead of seizing a trophy; HMB can participate without letting status dictate the encounter.

**Conditional endings:** Red win grants this week’s win, not the belt. HMB win need not erase Red’s dignity. Any public legend or private understanding remains proposed memory until accepted.

**Production/gaps:** Resolve GOTW venue before scene layout. No default crowd, private court or belt wager. Coastal autumn can be milder; do not force a mountain Halloween environment.

### F. LLC away at Slob — rules versus belonging
**Verified Week 6:** LLC lost to TDS by 0.37, 137.69–138.06, away; Slob beat Obi 143.70–123.38 at home. LLC is 2–4; Slob 3–3.

**Carryover boundary:** Falls Protocol draft negotiations do not grant LLC construction rights; Foothill Fix draft behavior does not authorize a new history of sanctioned cheating.

**Proposed engine:** LLC wants an agreement that produces actual trust rather than another elaborate document. Slob is already preparing a communal gathering or contest on existing camp grounds, where social acceptance matters more than paperwork. LLC’s precision meets an unwritten norm whose purpose may be generosity rather than exclusion.

**Why here/now:** The away visit gives LLC a chance to participate rather than transact. Slob cannot simply dismiss structure if an unfair ambiguity harms a guest. A meal, cooperative game or disputed contribution can be more revealing than another money/poker negotiation.

**Journey:** Continue from TDS temple → Mud → Mire Hill → Chamber → Lake Bridge → Slob camp, lower bound 22 hours. If a return to LLC boardroom is independently established, Boardroom → Storm City → Chamber → Lake Bridge → Slob is 16 hours, excluding that earlier return. No helicopter, new landing pad or implied instantaneous commute.

**Choice/stakes:** LLC can surrender a contractual advantage; Slob can make an implicit rule explicit without losing warmth. The encounter tests reciprocal trust, not whether either is a one-note fool.

**Conditional endings:** LLC win can reward participation; Slob win can preserve hospitality without exploiting ambiguity. A proposed recurring courtesy may persist; a signed constitutional agreement or new camp building may not be invented.

**Production/gaps:** Slob camp remains a provisional location. Use existing footprint; no new man cave. Keep travel fatigue and upper-valley gusts consistent. Verify final Week 6 scene residue before recalling specific documents or dialogue.

## 4. Author-room review and production challenge
The registered Core Seven lenses guide the simulated author room: world depth; ensemble consequences; setup/payoff; propulsion; culture/POV; limits/costs; close character voice. These are original task-specific editorial checks, **not claims that Tolkien, Martin, Rowling, Grisham, Le Guin, Sanderson or Abercrombie participated or endorsed any pitch**. No imitation mandate.

For each fixture, authors should compare three structures: intimate social friction, physical collaborative problem, and public/private status conflict. They may reject every proposed engine above. Demand a distinct away desire and home agenda before polishing dialogue. Preserve all twelve league members as agents of their own stories.

The verified twelve production-advisory domains then challenge the author's chosen structure:
- Production design: existing usable space, not a newly invented landmark.
- Environment: route/terrain/season make the collision plausible.
- VFX: ordinary weather versus Niño remains distinguishable.
- Costume: autumn behavior, not compulsory Halloween costume.
- Performance: independent desires and readable choices.
- Stunts/action: traversable movement and meaningful physical cost.
- Silhouette/animation: canonical bodies; Wilson integrated four-legged gorilla-centaur; TDS one body/three heads.
- Stills/composition: encounter relationship clear without explanatory caption.
- Pipeline: proposal versus locked asset/state stays explicit.
- Color: regional autumn, not one orange filter across all regions.
- Editing: prior-week cause → arrival → collision → choice survives compression.
- Sound: waterfall, wind and gathering cues create place without substituting for motivation.

Registry reference bench names are reference identities, not external reviewers. The simulation supplies domain questions only; it does not fabricate their opinions.

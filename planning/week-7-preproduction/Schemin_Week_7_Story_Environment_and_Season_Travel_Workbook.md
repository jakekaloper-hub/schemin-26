## 1. What this is—and what stays open

This replaces the need to juggle the **Week 7 Open Development Packet**, **Season Travel Continuity Audit** and **Remaining-Season Travel Planner** while developing scenes. It combines their creative alternatives, continuity rules and full Week 7–14 itinerary. It is not a manuscript, approved location register or production release.

**All six Week 7 story choices remain OPEN.** Jake’s rough cinematic ideas stay alongside the twelve alternatives below; he described two as especially unsettled without identifying them. No shortlist is silently selected. Authors lead creative judgment; production challenges whether the scene can actually be staged. The author/production review here is an **AI role simulation**, not a live meeting, named professionals’ statements or authenticated Bullpen runtime invocation.

**Evidence precedence:** the 25 October schedule retrieval supplies all remaining regular-season fixtures and provider H/A fields, superseding the older audit’s “future schedule unavailable” limitation. It does not verify future scene venues or outcomes. The Atlas/registered-location snapshot and checked graph supply geography and lower bounds. The inspected world-state ledger still ends at Week 3; Week 6 sports results do not supply approved closing whereabouts or automatically promote fictional consequences. Week 7 results are not asserted here.

This workbook keeps the requested Sunday observation → Monday-night outcome reconciliation → Tuesday production workflow as **gates**, not proof those steps have already happened or a promise of a background watcher. If used on Tuesday, enter at evidence reconciliation—not at automatic story or release approval.

### The causal encounter test

For both principals: **last approved appearance → consequence/carryover → independent reason to travel → accessible shared site → action that changes something → onward obligation**. Home designation, residence, host domain, physical zone, last whereabouts and precise venue are separate. No weekly reset home; no geographic rewrite to rescue a plot. A score is not a real owner’s motive or personality.

Use existing landmarks first, then propose a modest within-domain subsite if it improves the encounter. A main base is not compulsory. A neutral crossroads may work only through the recorded venue override; a long trip alone is not permission. The physical-environment cells below distinguish **existing features**, **seasonal dressing proposals** and **NEW proposed subsite names**. None creates a building, travel edge or canon record.

### Simulated author / production review synthesis

- **Author lenses:** world depth, ensemble consequences, setup/payoff, propulsion, culture/viewpoint, limits/costs and distinct voice. Write two openings before choosing an ending; don’t convert all six games into arrival–duel–humiliation.
- **Twelve production-domain checks:** production design (functional props); environment (terrain/access); character VFX (canonical body); costume (regional season); performance (independent wants); stunt blocking (safe coherent motion); pose/silhouette (readability); unit stills (no premature victory); VFX pipeline (necessary effects only); color (regional distinction); editing (six-story variety); sound/motion (sensory premise readable in a still).
- **Resulting editorial rule:** use the world as a cause of action, not painted background. Existing features and proposed small sites generate questions; authors still select, rewrite or reject the story. No external human participation is claimed.

**Existing feature → proposed seasonal dressing → NEW proposed subsite.** The first phrase comes from the registered parent location; bracketed provisional/interpretive status is retained. The planning anchor is **not automatically the actual venue or main base**. Try the existing ridge, yard, channel, court or pavilion first; use the proposed subsite only if it improves both characters’ motives. New sites must pass the existing candidate/venue process; a neutral move still needs a recorded override. None is promoted by this document.

### 1. Duckhook away at TDS

**Continuity constraint:** TDS’s waterfall, temple stone and vegetation support several exterior choices; the compact golf course is still a candidate. Confirm last-scene whereabouts, transport and access. Pittsy’s closure cause remains unknown.

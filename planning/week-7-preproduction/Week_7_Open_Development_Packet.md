## The room’s method

Seven editorial checks remain active: world depth; ensemble consequences; setup/payoff; propulsion; culture and viewpoint; limits and costs; distinct voice and human-scale action. Rather than vote for a winner now, the room tests whether each pair offers a genuinely different kind of story.

For each fixture, write the first 120–180 words of A and B without an ending. Then ask: can the reader identify two independent agendas, why this meeting happens now, and something that can change? If not, replace the engine instead of decorating it. Do not force every scene into arrival → challenge → humiliation → victorious pose.

## 1. Duckhook away at TDS

**Carryover and route:** Both won Week 6: Duckhook over Niño, TDS over LLC by 0.37. That supports a contrast between two successful approaches, not an obligatory revenge story. Jackson → Mire Hill → Mud’s Swamp → temple is a connected source route candidate, not a required endpoint for every story. Its mode, actual last-seen position and elapsed time remain to be checked; no all-route golf-cart assumption. Pittsy’s closure is unresolved, not an explanation to invent. A supported site elsewhere in TDS’s domain could preserve the visit without requiring the temple interior.

### A — The Guest Takes Over
**Engine: competence becomes an intrusion.** Duckhook arrives intending to offer a practical hospitality exchange after his own home encounter. TDS is already preparing a small guest activity in an existing temple space. Duckhook starts improving its arrangement; the host’s apparently inefficient order has a purpose the visitor has not asked about.

**Opening test:** The visitor moves one ordinary item. TDS puts it back without explanation. The reader initially understands both actions.

**Turn and payoff question:** An improvement solves one problem but creates another. Can Duckhook ask before acting, and can TDS explain without converting hospitality into obedience? The eventual result may color who yields first, but neither outcome licenses a permanent access treaty.

**Observe Sunday:** Relative steadiness versus dependence on one major contribution, with timestamped evidence. Does the comparison actually support two different methods, or is that just the writer’s assumption? **Reject or revise** if the scene requires an unsupported temple custom or a caricature of either principal.

### B — The Last Hole Is Not the Point
**Engine: concealment through play.** The optional golf hook becomes a negotiation over when to leave, not another test of managerial control. The visitor has a reason to continue; the host has a practical task waiting. The exact reason must be supplied as proposed connective fiction or drawn from approved character material, never attributed to a real owner as fact.

**Opening test:** One puts equipment away while the other has already prepared another attempt. Fading light and wet footing constrain how long the game can continue.

**Turn and payoff question:** The apparent excuse to stay is exposed as an excuse, but not necessarily a shameful one. Can the encounter end honestly rather than competitively? **Observe Sunday:** Whether the matchup remains open late and whether the apparent decisive moment genuinely changes. Late uncertainty may support the duration; it does not prove companionship. **Reject** if the approved venue cannot support the game or the emotional premise is unearned.

## 2. Niño away at DK

**Carryover and route:** DK won away at Red; Niño lost at Jackson. A connected route toward DK’s stronghold runs through Lake Bridge, Slob’s camp and Arsenal; an actual return home is not established by that connectivity. Either engine can be tested at an authorized domain site encountered along a credible itinerary, rather than forcing a ceremonial homecoming. Niño is an environmental phenomenon, not a traveler with revenge, dialogue or an interior monologue. Its physical propagation from the preceding appearance remains a named evidence gap. Do not use a road graph to solve that gap.

### A — The Thing That Must Stay Dry
**Engine: practical triage.** DK has prepared an impressive presentation at the existing stronghold. Changing weather makes protecting one ordinary, canon-compatible item more urgent than maintaining the appearance of control. The item is deliberately not specified as a sacred artifact, person or major new asset without source support.

**Opening test:** He finishes aligning the display, notices water reaching something outside the composition, and must choose which action to interrupt.

**Turn and payoff question:** Saving the useful thing makes the presentation look worse. Can an unattractive adjustment be the successful action? **Observe Sunday:** Actual changes in the competitive situation and responses visible in verified lineup/event evidence, if available; never invent managerial decisions. **Reject** a generic heroic rescue or unsupported damage. DK’s visual conflict hold still prevents final rendering.

### B — The Wrong Explanation
**Engine: a small sensory mystery.** DK notices that a familiar physical signal has changed. He first blames the wrong ordinary cause. Evidence forces him to look elsewhere. The plot is about attention and revised understanding, not protecting a display.

**Opening test:** Something that normally moves or sounds does neither; nearby material still responds to the gusts. Select the signal only from an established setting or label it a proposed temporary detail. Birds remain present; silence does not establish migration.

**Turn and payoff question:** His first explanation is plausible but incomplete. Can he change his mind before the consequence becomes obvious? **Observe Sunday:** An early apparent advantage that later proves misleading, if that actually occurs. **Reject** if the payoff depends on a new Niño power, inexplicable weather or an unreadable absence in a still image. Both A and B remain on environmental-propagation HOLD until grounded.

## 3. Chins away at ObiWan

**Carryover and route:** Chins won at Chili; Obi lost at Slob. Neither result proves a personality trait. Chins’s onward journey must account for the preceding away venue, not teleport him home. The prior long route through Commons and Jackson is only one connected candidate, not a shortest-path finding. Audit the shorter candidates and their access assumptions. Wagon access on the final ascent is unvalidated; a suitable within-domain meeting lower on the approach may support either engine better, subject to the actual location/venue rules.

### A — Two Ways to Carry It
**Engine: incompatible methods during shared work.** Chins brings a small, plausible contribution to the visit. Obi is already completing a seasonal task within the existing base. Each insists that the other’s way of handling an ordinary load is needlessly difficult. Cooperation becomes necessary without a declared duel.

**Opening test:** Both reach for the same end, leaving the other unsupported. The mismatch reveals their immediate intentions before any explanation.

**Turn and payoff question:** The efficient technique is not necessarily the socially generous one. Can each accept a contribution that cannot be measured the same way? **Observe Sunday:** Whether verified contributions are broadly distributed or concentrated; use that only as a structural analogy. **Reject** if the task exists solely to stage a joke, or requires a new bridge, terrace or dangerous ledge.

### B — A Challenge With an Exit
**Engine: consent and the meaning of acknowledgment.** Retain the optional duel, but begin with the visitor offering an honorable way not to fight. Obi must decide whether declining would feel like hospitality or evasion. Chins must decide whether he wants a contest or acknowledgment.

**Opening test:** A clear invitation is followed by an equally clear chance to refuse. Neither is treated as cowardly for considering it.

**Turn and payoff question:** The contest may reveal that they were seeking different forms of respect. **Observe Sunday:** The actual shape of the competition, including whether a comeback remains feasible; do not equate a fantasy loss with moral failure. **Reject** borrowed film quotations, arbitrary new combat law or a predestined lesson for Obi. Unlike A, this is a deliberate encounter rather than an accidental collaboration.

## 4. Chili away at Mud Dogs

**Carryover and route:** Both lost Week 6; Mud’s margin was 0.12. Blackwater draft material does not prove a deed transfer. A candidate route runs Stable → shared march → temple → swamp. Dark Horse, wagon and final access restrictions require validation. The encounter can occupy an authorized place within Mud’s domain rather than a residence; neither engine needs a newly invented cornfield. Southern wetlands remain warmer and damp.

### A — The Unclaimed Sighting
**Engine: desire versus restraint.** Use their shared hunting interest without presuming a kill, photograph or canonical 14-point animal. A fleeting sighting could become an occasion for boasting, but either person moving to claim it may spoil it for both.

**Opening test:** One sees something the other has not yet noticed. The first meaningful action is whether to point, speak or remain still.

**Turn and payoff question:** The apparent competition becomes a shared pleasure neither can fully possess. Does either need a witness badly enough to destroy the moment? **Observe Sunday:** Verified narrow margins and late dependencies, if present, may shape suspense rather than dictate a hunting result. **Reject** if the image requires an unverified species, exact antler count, forbidden hunting action or a sentimental ending unsupported by the scene.

### B — Let the Visitor Lead
**Engine: trust on another person’s ground.** Mud initially follows Chili’s confident reading of a small route choice within existing accessible terrain. When Chili notices his uncertainty, he must choose between admitting it and continuing a performance. Mud knows more than he says but has a reason not to turn every step into a correction.

**Opening test:** The visitor chooses a direction before the host has finished explaining. A short silence follows, not a laugh track.

**Turn and payoff question:** Chili may discover a useful observation Mud overlooked, while still needing local knowledge. Can expertise be reciprocal? **Observe Sunday:** Evidence of reversal or recovered footing, not imaginary route incidents. **Reject** a new dangerous swamp crossing, humiliation of the visitor or a host who maliciously endangers him. This is a relationship negotiation through navigation, not another hunt or scarecrow gag.

## 5. HMB away at Red Leopards — Game of the Week

**Carryover and venue:** HMB won by 0.12; Red lost at home. No weekly result transfers the championship belt. The encounter policy defaults GOTW to neutral; a Red-home court or ruins scene needs the existing exception process. Both alternatives remain viable only in a venue branch actually approved for staging. The private basketball/first-to-eleven idea is a personal creative input, not proof of a built court or new league rule.

### A — Before Anyone Keeps Score
**Engine: cooperation becomes competition.** At an approved existing play space, both have to make the modest setup usable before their private game can begin. HMB is trying to finish without fuss; Red keeps changing one detail that determines how he will be seen in his own chosen contest.

**Opening test:** They agree on the task but repeatedly undo each other’s work. The belt is present only if its placement is canon-safe, not a trophy wager.

**Turn and payoff question:** One generous adjustment becomes a competitive disadvantage later. Does the beneficiary acknowledge it? **Observe Sunday:** Actual changes of advantage and whether an apparently small early contribution matters late. **Reject** invented sabotage, unsafe wet/cliff basketball or a crowd inserted to explain status.

### B — The Witness Who Won’t Pose
**Engine: disagreement about recognition.** Red wants one particular moment acknowledged; HMB does not want the encounter converted into a staged image of what the belt means. Neither has to be vain or cruel. One wants a precise gesture; the other offers a different, less performative form of respect.

**Opening test:** One arranges where the other should stand. The other quietly moves to a position that changes the meaning of the moment. No camera or modern device is required unless approved.

**Turn and payoff question:** Is acknowledgment still meaningful if it does not look the way its recipient imagined? **Observe Sunday:** What is objectively notable in the final contest, including a possible close finish; no score is sufficient proof that either wants public recognition. **Reject** a coerced confession, belt transfer or fabricated historical grievance. Unlike A, the primary action is negotiation over meaning, not cooperative physical setup.

## 6. LLC away at Slob

**Carryover and route:** LLC lost at TDS by 0.37; Slob beat Obi. The Falls Protocol does not prove construction rights, and the Foothill draft does not establish sanctioned cheating. Continue LLC from the temple through Mud, Mire Hill, Chamber and Lake Bridge unless an intervening return is verified. Slob’s camp remains provisional; an already-supported domain site may serve the social task better than treating the camp as his residence. No unsupported helicopter, landing pad, mansion or new man cave.

### A — The Guest Runs the Room
**Engine: an accidental reversal of social roles.** Slob is preparing an ordinary gathering or card game in an existing usable space. A small interruption leaves LLC briefly doing the host’s task. LLC can execute the rules perfectly yet miss what makes a guest feel welcome; Slob may discover that clarity is a form of care.

**Opening test:** The person who came to be received is suddenly explaining where something belongs. His first instruction is correct and awkward.

**Turn and payoff question:** Each must decide whether to rescue the other quietly or expose the mistake. **Observe Sunday:** Verified broad contributions versus isolated moments can inform ensemble pacing, not assign real motives. **Reject** a swarm of invented NPCs, a permanent new institution or the assumption that LLC cannot be warm. Poker is optional, not the entire personality of the scene.

### B — The Scare They Have to Finish Together
**Engine: a failed performance becomes shared work.** The visitor discovers an unfinished, modest Halloween preparation too early. Rather than cashing this out as an automatic poker tell, the pair negotiate whether to abandon it, expose the trick or finish it for a later occasion.

**Opening test:** The attempted concealment takes more effort than the proposed scare. Both know, and neither admits knowing immediately.

**Turn and payoff question:** Accepting help gives up control; refusing it preserves an absurd inconvenience. Can a small collaboration coexist with competition? **Observe Sunday:** Real interruptions and reversals may guide rhythm only if verified; do not invent an injury, outage or bench decision as a gag. **Reject** an elaborate haunted house, a locked Week 8 sequel or a compulsory humiliation. This option uses awkward intimacy; A uses a temporary role exchange.

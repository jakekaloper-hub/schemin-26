## Historian rule

History is preserved when it explains:
- provenance;
- a major design/canon change;
- manuscript evolution;
- verified league history;
- why current architecture exists.

History is not preserved in the active plane merely because deletion feels uncomfortable.

## In-place vs archive decision

Use **archive relocation** when:
- the old path itself falsely implies authority;
- no active dependency requires the path;
- semantic retrieval risk is high.

Use **in-place historical labeling** when:
- current manifests explicitly reference the path;
- path stability has production value;
- the artifact's historical status can be made unambiguous.

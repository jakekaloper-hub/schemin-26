Mitigation: known-pass and known-fail calibration set, false-positive/false-negative review, Umpire inspection, versioned thresholds.

## Risk 8 — native ChatGPT cannot expose enough binding evidence
Mitigation: define the minimum safe observable receipt in Phase 3. Never invent hidden provider details. If evidence is insufficient, native rendering remains HOLD.

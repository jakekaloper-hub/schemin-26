## North Star

Develop one physically coherent, original, Earth-inspired secondary world that grows from authoritative Schemin' '26 geography. Its twelve owner characters inhabit distinct homes/domains embedded in seven existing regions and functioning civilizations. Locations, travel, environment, architecture and history must be internally consistent and reutilizable across Memo OS, Living Novel OS, Atlas/World Engine, cinematic environments and an eventual navigable 3D representation.

**First demonstration:** From the Atlas, select the Trade Jedi Mountain Base, follow an approved route to another existing location, inspect spatially consistent buildings/property, and produce an approved-reference-grounded illustration. This is a future acceptance target; no current claim of such a working product.

## Invariants carried from prior systems

1. **One World Model; one Atlas spatial control plane; many derived views; no forked geography.**
2. World Engine V1.1 physical sources (`world/data/`) remain authoritative for existing locations, regions, routes, domains, and states.
3. Season-scoped food-division overlays are nonexclusive and must not shift home geography.
4. Owner-specific character canon is owned by Character Canon; exact asset hash, mount, per-subject binding and independent raster QA stay mandatory for character-bearing artwork.
5. Only 12 principal/selectable Novel POVs; other residents are ambient unless separately authorized as non-principal supporting world inhabitants.
6. Evolution has explicit event provenance, effective period and rollback/version history. Historical prints remain historical.
7. The Commissioner has approved a **methodology and ambition**, not parcel acreage, exact travel times, a new continent boundary, additional sovereign states, a new settlement name, or new 3D assets.

## Decision rights

- **Commissioner:** ratifies major world-defining new details, visual identity changes, property site choices and physical-canon promotion.
- **Closer/Groundskeeper:** organize review and execution with authenticated Director runtime where available.
- **Atlas/World:** spatial/geographical source ownership, route/hydrology integrity.
- **Librarian/Character Canon:** precedence and character/asset identity.
- **Visual Lead/Architect:** environment/camera/geometry/renderer interface.
- **Scout/Beat Writer/Novel authors:** verified league events, narrative use and chronology.
- **Independent Umpire:** evidence-backed production/release checks; simulated review is non-authoritative.

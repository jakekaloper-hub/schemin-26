# Mission #175 — Phase 1 Civilization Foundation (Design Candidate)

**Status:** DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE / NOT CANON / NOT RELEASED.
**Existing owner:** Universe OS V1.2 civilization context, under World Engine V1.1 physical truth and Atlas V2 spatial projections.

## North Star
The world must feel inhabited on an ordinary Tuesday. Build richer **unnamed, confidence-tagged environment contexts** rather than named NPCs, competing locations or a new world store.

## Proposed context fields (additive, interpretive, not yet machine authority)
- `region_id`: existing `REG-*` from `world/data/physical_zones.json`.
- `anchor_location_ids`: only already active `LOC-*` identifiers, if a context is location-bound.
- `settlement_pattern`: derived from the seven-zone geography bible; no invented named town.
- `building_materials`, `ambient_industries`, `street_activity`, `seasonal_pressures`: bounded background detail.
- `transport_dependencies`: describe routes and waterways without inventing `ROUTE-*` edges.
- `institutional_context`: reference already approved League institutions only.
- `evidence_sources` and `confidence`: preserve source status; descriptive inference is not canon.
- `consumer_permissions`: Memo, Novel, Atlas, environment-only Art; never authority to mutate location, character or world state.

## Seven-zone baseline (existing canon-derived, not new settlements)
| Zone | Existing environmental emphasis | Ambient context examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Northern Spine | Headwaters, constrained passes, mining and mountain pasture | Pack carriers, quarry crews, pass inns |
| Upper Valleys & Foothills | Orchards, pasture, academy/market towns | Market carts, dairy workers, roadside smiths |
| Central River Basin | Floodplain, bridges, courts, guilds, milling and finance | River quays, grain porters, civic crowds |
| Western Tablelands | Ranches, frontier caravan roads, spice and livestock trade | Stable hands, caravans, livestock markets |
| Eastern Forest & Storm Coast | Rainforest, rocky harbors, navigation and ship repair | Harbor crews, storm shutters, timber yards |
| Southern Wetlands & Delta | Swamps, slow rivers, raised towns, levees and boats | Ferries, fish markets, levee maintenance |
| Inner Sea Littoral | Maritime ports, finance, shipping and entertainment | Dock brokers, warehouses, busy piers |

## Central Basin pilot — design only
Use existing League Chamber, League Archive, Pittsy's Book and other verified Basin anchors as **context references**. Explore a continuous ordinary-day scene: food and grain arriving at quays; cart traffic moving toward markets; archive clerks and civic activity; spectators discussing a verified League event. This is not a new location, map coordinate, road or story character. Before scene promotion, resolve actual LOC IDs and route evidence.

## Phase 1 QA gates
1. Seven zone IDs resolve; zero new canonical REG/LOC/ROUTE IDs.
2. All contexts cite existing physical-zone and atlas doctrine.
3. No named NPC, POV, biography or new singular-being population.
4. Season divisions remain overlays, not settlement ownership.
5. El Niño meteorology is not inferred from ordinary storms.
6. No character-bearing image output without approved reference/hash/mount/binding.
7. Same environment facts can be consumed by Memo, Novel and Art without becoming independent authority.
8. Independent Umpire and authenticated ConsultEngine required for eventual production promotion.

# Digital Universe Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Content spine
Finalize Weekly Story Package schema, identity mapping, provenance classes and Week 2 source inventory.

## Phase 1 — Week 2 vertical slice
Build Home shell, Week 2 Hub, GOTW, ObiWan/Mud Dogs chapter, character dossier, standings/power rankings and Original Edition. Use still art + restrained motion placeholders before expensive media generation.

## Phase 2 — Media pipeline
Implement adapter interfaces, candidate asset registry, approvals, responsive encodes and narration/sound workflow. Produce one 30–90 second Week 2 film.

## Phase 3 — Full Week 2
Transform all six matchups; add waiver/Pittsy/State of Play modules and character/timeline updates.

## Phase 4 — Season ingestion
Normalize prior/future weeks into WSP packages. Automate factual ingestion and draft story scaffolds while retaining editorial approval.

## Phase 5 — Living season
Rivalry pages, character arcs, division races, search, timeline, weekly social cuts and evolving homepage.

## Phase 6 — Postseason
Playoff presentation, elimination/championship states, season-film index and championship documentary assembly.

## Not yet
3D world/metaverse; autonomous public publishing; full synthetic voice cast; real-time game animation; vendor-locked media database; expensive long-form generation every week.

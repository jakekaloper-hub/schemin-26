The official Week 2 stills are the visual anchors. Prefer depth, environmental animation, camera movement and compositing over regenerating principal characters.

## Safety rails
- Principal character identity drift = reject.
- Generated score/text = reject; composite verified typography after generation.
- Extra limbs/props that alter canon = reject.
- Motion that contradicts the still's spatial logic = reject.
- Every accepted shot links to story beat ID + source page + generation provenance.

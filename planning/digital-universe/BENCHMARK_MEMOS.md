# Official Benchmark Memos

## Status
Jake supplied the official Week 1 and Week 2 published editions on 2026-09-25. These are the current quality floor. Future editions may improve naturally; Digital Universe work must not regress beneath their editorial and visual standard.

## Week 1 — editorial grammar
Fourteen pages establish: cinematic cover; Opening Drive; verified scoreboard; six matchup features; Week That Was; Evidence Room; Pittsy's Book; next-week division/GOTW setup; Awards/Final Receipts.

The key storytelling doctrine is cumulative evidence: Week 1 creates receipts and questions that later weeks answer.

## Week 2 — visual grammar

## Web translation rule
Do not extract the artwork and place it into generic cards. Translate the publication's physical/editorial world into motion and interaction: depth, environmental animation, evidence props, marginalia, score overlays, soundscape, narration and continuity callbacks.

## Motion rule
Prefer animating implied layers in approved art over regenerating principal characters. This protects character fidelity and makes the published still the visual anchor.

## History rule
Week 1 and Week 2 are Chapters 1 and 2 of one season. Each new WSP appends character, rivalry, team and league-state events.

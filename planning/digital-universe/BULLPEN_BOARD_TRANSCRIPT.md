# Bullpen Board — Pro Schemin’ Digital Universe

Status: R&D convergence session
Authority: Jake / Schemin ’26
Prototype: published Week 2

## Opening
The Closer reframed the assignment: do not build a PDF viewer and do not build ESPN with custom colors. Build a persistent fantasy-football storyworld whose factual timeline is generated by the real league.

## Roundtable
**Architect:** The atomic unit cannot be a web page. It must be a structured Weekly Story Package. PDF, website, video and social are renderers.

**Scout:** Every factual beat needs evidence. Score, roster, standings, transaction and schedule data must retain source/freshness metadata. Narrative may interpret events; it cannot alter them.

**Librarian:** Historical editions are immutable. Current team names and historical names need identity mapping so renames never rewrite old weeks. Character identity persists across names.

**Beat Writer:** A week needs narrative hierarchy: cold open, headline/GOTW, six matchup chapters, consequences, character-arc updates, league-state changes, next-week hooks.

**Creative Director:** The visual language should feel like a connected cinematic universe. Each character needs motifs, locations, props, silhouette rules and continuity references, not merely a portrait.

**Motion Director:** Do not animate everything. Select 3–5 hero moments per week. Use layered stills, camera moves, atmospheric effects and short generated shots. Preserve recognizable characters.

**Audio Director:** Audio should begin with narrator + sound design. Character voices are optional and require explicit voice/identity policy. A strong announcer/narrator can carry the first prototype.

**UX:** Mobile is primary. A Week Hub should scroll like an interactive magazine/documentary chapter. Users should reach scores immediately but choose to descend into story.

**Engineer:** Media vendors must be adapters. Store prompts, source assets, output metadata and approvals. Never make the archive dependent on one generation vendor.

**Editor:** The published PDF remains an original edition. The web edition can recompose it, but cannot silently revise the historical artifact.

**Umpire:** Separate FACT, EDITORIAL, FICTIONALIZED PRESENTATION, and GENERATED MEDIA in the content model. Accessibility and provenance are release requirements.

**Producer:** Weekly production cannot become a 40-hour film shoot. Define tiers: Core web issue every week; Hero Motion for GOTW; Social Cut; optional Premium Film for playoffs/championship.

**Historian:** Every approved week should append events to character/team/rivalry timelines. By playoffs, the season documentary is assembled from accumulated canonical beats rather than reconstructed from memory.

**Performance:** Full-bleed video is expensive on mobile. Poster frames, responsive assets, lazy loading and reduced-motion behavior are mandatory.

**Experimental:** The homepage can evolve with league state: streaks, eliminations, rivalries, division races and championship lineage.

**Skeptic:** Avoid synthetic complexity. No 3D metaverse, live multiplayer world, bespoke voice cast, or dozens of vendor integrations for V1.

**Closer:** Convergence: build the content spine first, then prove one Week 2 cinematic vertical slice.

## Board resolution
1. Weekly Story Package becomes the canonical publishing contract.
2. Week 2 is the controlled benchmark.
3. V1 experience = Home + Week Hub + GOTW cinematic chapter + one matchup chapter + character arc + standings + Original Edition.
4. Motion is selective, not universal.
5. Narration + sound design precede character voices.
6. Tool vendors remain replaceable.
7. Every generated asset records provenance and approval.
8. No public release until Jake approves the vertical slice.

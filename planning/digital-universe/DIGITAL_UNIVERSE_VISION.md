# Pro Schemin’ Digital Universe — Vision

## North star
Turn a real fantasy-football league into a cumulative digital sports mythology without sacrificing factual integrity.

The league creates events. Schemin Data Gateway establishes truth. Canon establishes identity. Memo OS creates editorial meaning. The media layer turns approved story beats into art, motion, audio, video, web and social expressions.

## Product model
```
REAL LEAGUE
  → VERIFIED EVENTS
  → WEEKLY STORY PACKAGE
  → STORY / CHARACTER CONTINUITY
  → MEDIA ASSETS
  → WEB + PDF + VIDEO + SOCIAL
  → PERMANENT SEASON HISTORY
```

## Experience
Opening Schemin should feel less like opening fantasy software and more like entering this week's episode of a season-long sports series.

A visitor can consume at three depths:
- **Score:** what happened?
- **Story:** why did this week matter?
- **Universe:** how did it change characters, rivalries and the season?

## Long-term outcome
At championship week, the archive already contains the verified events, approved narratives, character changes, art and motion from every week. The championship experience and season film therefore emerge from accumulated history.

## Principles
- Facts are immutable evidence.
- Stories interpret facts; they do not replace them.
- Characters persist across team renames.
- Published editions remain immutable.
- Mobile first.
- Spectacle is concentrated on meaningful moments.
- AI assists production; humans retain editorial authority.
- No vendor owns the archive.

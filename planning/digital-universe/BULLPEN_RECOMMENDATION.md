# Bullpen Recommendation

## Decision
Build the **content spine and Week 2 vertical slice before a full website or full video pipeline**.

The decisive architectural move is the Weekly Story Package. Once Week 2 exists as structured verified story data, the same package can drive web, PDF, motion, video, social and historical timelines.

## First build
1. Inventory the official Week 2 assets/data/copy.
2. Encode Week 2 into WSP v0.1.
3. Create the mobile Week Hub shell.
4. Implement GOTW and ObiWan vs Mud Dogs as the first cinematic chapters.
5. Add one character dossier and factual provenance drawer.
6. Add original Week 2 memo.
7. Only then generate a small number of motion/audio candidates.
8. Test with the league-member experience: fast facts first, deeper story optional.

## Production philosophy
Use AI as a film crew, not as the historian. The historian is the canonical Schemin system.

## Closer call
Do not spend the next cycle shopping for tools. Prove the production grammar using existing approved Week 2 material. Once the grammar is stable, benchmark current vendors behind replaceable adapters.

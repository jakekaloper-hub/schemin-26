# Week 2 Immersive Proof of Concept

## Purpose
Demonstrate what the already-published Week 2 becomes when its canonical material is freed from the PDF format. The PDF remains unchanged.

## Opening
Black/ambient loading field → Schemin ’26 mark → “WEEK TWO.” Sound rises. A cinematic crop of the official cover appears with restrained depth/motion. Headline: Red Leopards' GOTW win over Chili Cheesers. Immediate controls: ENTER THE WEEK / SCORES / ORIGINAL EDITION.

## Chapter 1 — GOTW
Red Leopards vs Chili Cheesers.
- full-screen hero art
- score/result visible in accessible HTML from verified Week 2 package
- scroll-driven reveal of the Sonic chili-dog storyline
- 8–12 second hero motion shot derived from approved character references
- narrator bridge
- “What changed” card: standings/character consequence

## Chapter 2 — Matchup vertical slice
ObiWan Jacoby vs Mud Dogs.
Sequence:
1. swamp establishing frame
2. Mud Dogs bicycle motif enters
3. Trade Jedi reaction/counter-frame
4. verified score/result overlay
5. short editorial beat about ObiWan's loss
6. character timeline appends Week 2 consequence

This is a cinematic interpretation of a verified matchup, not a replacement for its factual result.

## Character arc
Selecting The Trade Jedi opens a dossier drawer: identity, team, record entering/leaving Week 2, Week 1 event, Week 2 event, approved visual canon and appearances. No championship belt.

## Data layer
Standings and power rankings remain crisp HTML/cards beneath the cinematic surface. Freshness/provenance drawer exposes data timestamp/source.

## Audio
V1 uses one narrator plus environmental sound design. Captions on by default for narrated sequences; mute is persistent. Character voices deferred.

## 30–90 second Week 2 film
0–05: Schemin title / Week 2
05–20: GOTW setup
20–35: Red Leopards/Chili climax
35–55: rapid montage of other matchups
55–70: league-state consequences
70–85: Week 3 hooks
85–90: Schemin mark / archive Week 2

## Mobile
Vertical-first composition. No autoplay audio. Motion respects reduced-motion preference. Heavy media lazy loads after core scores/headlines.

## Original edition
A permanent “Original Week 2 Memo” artifact sits beside the immersive edition with publication date and immutable status.

## Acceptance
The prototype succeeds if a league member can identify what happened, experience one storyline cinematically, inspect factual provenance, recognize characters without drift, and reach the original memo from a phone.

The Last Field / Champions' Walk

The Belt and the Michael Farrell Trophy

The Belt recognizes the reigning competitive champion. The trophy carries the league's memorial tribute to former member Michael Farrell. The remembrance is dignified and separate from the rivalries and celebrations nearby.

The source archive does not establish the precise date the trophy acquired its memorial name, nor authorize invented details of Michael Farrell's life or death.

Within this proposed fictional world, every completed championship ends at the same Last Field. The original gates and stone orientation persist beneath the later broadcasting galleries.

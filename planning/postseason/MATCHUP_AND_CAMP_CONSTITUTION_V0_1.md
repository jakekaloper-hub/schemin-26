## A. Decision: physical challenge vs duel?
RECOMMENDATION: a postseason encounter is a PUBLIC, SPECTATED, RULE-BOUND HEAD-TO-HEAD CHALLENGE. A duel is one allowed subclass, not the island's compulsory visual grammar. Otherwise fifty-plus historic winner-bracket matchups become repetitive and contradict the Novel's encounter law. Every challenge must be contestable by two distinct opposed parties, legible in images, constrained by a terrain-specific venue and win condition, and certified.
Five format families to rotate:
1. Combat / challenge of arms: bounded duel, nonlethal arena control, shield-breaking or disarm; always preserve twelve-principal survival law.
2. Course / traversal: chase, ascent, crossing, tidal causeway, target retrieval, marked race; clear first/last or control condition.
3. Tactical / siege: hold the standards, capture a gate, breach an obstacle, transport objective; crew/retinue may act without replacing two principals.
4. Skill / sport: precision throw, golf-like route, equestrian challenge, obstacle trials, measured targets; uses established physical/technology constraints.
5. Judgment / enterprise: negotiated logistics, arbitration trial, trade convoy contest, construction/strategy under officials; inherently physical/observable, not two characters just arguing at a desk.
No contest can be rigged fictionally via last-minute invented magic, hidden assassins, or changes to real head-to-head result. The aim is Hunger Games-style jeopardy of elimination and Goblet of Fire-style variety, but as independent original Schemin institution rather than borrowed properties.

## B. The encounter contract: eight playable beats
1. GOVERNING DOCUMENT: name participants by time-correct team ID and aliases, round, true seed, venue, mission/objective, equipment and arbiter.
2. TWO APPROACHES: physically different travel/camp/psychological posture, suitable to approved POV lens; no invented real-person quote.
3. PUBLIC INTRODUCTION: procession, commentator introductions, historic stakes and island-wide bracket status before kickoff.
4. OPENING PLAY: one party takes an actual tactically intelligible initiative, which the other answers.
5. COST AND REVERSAL: terrain, retinue, tactics and time affect both; no denial of opposing character agency.
6. VISIBLE DECISIVE ACTION: winner completes objective, losing contender visibly fails/cannot recover. Narrow score produces narrow finish but no invented exact mechanical conversion of fantasy points to seconds.
7. OFFICIAL CONFIRMATION: arbiter certifies winner based on sourced fantasy matchup; herald/production desk issues island-wide bracket update and semifinal berth.
8. AFTERMATH: victor returns to Contenders' Quarter for preparation; loser proceeds to Shore Quarter; public and competitors reinterpret the next matchup without changing its result.

Every single historical winners-bracket game can reuse the 8-beat skeleton while changing venue, visual goal, and character motivations.

## C. Equal legitimacy / the bye
Byes honor top seeds in the years when actual rule settings supply them. The bye entrant resides in the island's contender quarter during the opening round: trains, studies, observes, fields media, waits, and cannot claim a fictional preliminary opponent or foreknown semifinal foe. The bracket, not authorial preference, sets their path.
Matchups are head-to-head; the same-round games collectively determine who survives. Individual contests should be given distinct physical venues and separate coverage teams, then all results cohere on one central event board. Exact cross-game influence is about bracket advancement, opponent identification, lodging, spectator attention, support, trade and reputation, not score adjustment.

## D. Campus living: what happens between matchups
ARRIVAL: all playoff entrants arrive by verified/built routes, pass neutral inspection, receive suite/house assignment, encounter former champions, inspect Champions' Walk and receive a written challenge assignment. Five nonqualifiers are welcome in the beach quarter when postseason commences.
PRE-EVENT: practice footage/notes, kit inspection, press appearance, public parade, tailgates, opening brief, competing supporters at cafés, and tension around familiar opponents.
GAME WEEK: each venue receives officials, heralds, field marshals, timekeepers and a distinct commentator crew; the centralized desk carries other results; spectators can attend physically or receive authenticated delayed public news through the communication network.
WINNER RECOVERY: repairs, training-yard reservation, medical/rest period, new match-specific preparation, anonymous public scouting gossip (not fabricated insider sourcing), media obligation and changing entourage morale.
LOSER TRANSITION: official removal of contention standard, packing quarters, escorted public walk to beach (may be somber, comedic or defiant), reception by earlier losers, watching next round, no forced humiliating hazing.
BYE LIVED EXPERIENCE: isolation, risk of overconfidence, training, tactical scouting and cultural pressure to prove the bye was deserved, while maintaining the actual bracket advantage.
CHAMPIONSHIP WEEK: two finalist households relocate to protected Last Field approaches, elite suite hospitality, formal memorial acknowledgement, the only full arena ceremony, official result, trophy presentation, Belt ceremony and new inscription AFTER verification.
AFTER SEASON: event grounds close, seasonal commercial visitors depart, staff conserve records, repair wear and update dated venue state; only safety/maintenance caretakers remain year-round.

## E. Camps and age of participation
Camp presence is temporary and governed by institutional leasing/access, NOT permanent sovereignty or owner-controlled island property. Founding 2015 appearance, repeat visitor tenure, finals, runner-up losses, championship victories and nonparticipation are all separately archived. A champion may be commemorated but may not claim immunity, privileged officials, a rigged ground or exclusive property rights. Accommodations differ in ornament and material culture, not competitive fairness.
VETERAN: multiple historically proven qualifications can earn a familiar tent court, archived flags, re-used family table, staff friendships, carved doorway marks, recognizable equipment stores.
OCCASIONAL: issued a still high-quality standard residence with fewer historic marks; returning visitors might be confronted by architectural changes since their last trip.
FIRST RECORDED APPEARANCE: public introductory herald, first berth token, assigned lodge, awkwardness navigating ceremonial geography and the crowd. Label first-ever only after complete league history and owner-slot identity confirmation; an old slot under new name is NOT a first arrival automatically.
REIGNING CHAMPION: carries 2025 ceremonial standing into 2026, but still must qualify to defend title under actual rules; never position its 2026 bracket placement as predetermined.
Historical slot appearance counts 2015–2025 live only in the historical dossier, with owner identity confidence marked unknown. No direct visual cloning of modern characters into unresolved old team slots.

## F. The visitor & entertainment ecosystem
Professional coverage system: fixed venue booths for play-by-play + color analyst + impartial field reporter; one roving historic archivist; one independent result certifier; one central desk and logistical producer. Previews and summaries must include: two competitors/seed, opponent history bounded by verified source, objective briefing, location landmarks, history of site, live or certified conclusion, immediate bracket implications, official winner and Losers' Beach arrival.
Modern production aesthetic lives in external Memo OS PNGs and Commonwealth website; only authenticated lore-approved signaling/radio/visual technologies can exist literally in the fictional world. In-world word can move by herald, courier, public placard, signal tower, boat and printed result. Avoid instant all-region social media by narration.
Sports pilgrimage culture: harbor arrival parties, organized tailgates, seasonal sponsor/vendor stalls, roast and cookouts, period-appropriate wagering separate from official prediction, mock rival banners, guest suites, private former-champions lounge, public grandstands and beachfront watch parties. Social scenes may be bawdy, loud and comic; Michael Farrell memorial and trophy presentation are treated with restraint.
Real spectators need practical circulation, lavatories, stalls, accessible sight lines, refuge from rain, food safety, rescue and safe evacuation. Record annual site state rather than render each arena immaculate forever.

## H. Inklings/Bullpen challenge panel — simulated methodology, not authenticated participants
- **Tolkien/Le Guin world-depth counterweight:** Does each venue feel built out of island material and labor? Where do officials, food, repairs and ordinary caretakers come from? Does architecture show dated layers?
- **Martin/Abercrombie ensemble counterweight:** Does losing close doors and open human drama? Are both competitors agents, and are veterans ever embarrassingly humbled rather than granted plot armor?
- **Sanderson rules counterweight:** Can the viewer infer the objective and an ending? Can score provenance be audited, and are byes meaningful without being imaginary games?
- **Grisham/Rowling serialized-pacing counterweight:** Are round transitions propulsive, with repeatable institutions and long-running payoffs, without dwelling on exposition?
- **Lewis moral-imagination counterweight:** Is a triumph meaningful without putting supernatural destiny, false virtue, or immortality into a score?
- **Scout / Analyst:** Correct period, seed, team identity, source score, playoff status and bracket.
- **Architect / Groundskeeper:** Different grounds with common island topology; season-specific maintenance and room allocations; no dead-end routes.
- **Librarian:** Historical provenance and protection of Michael Farrell's memorial; no implicit owner continuity through aliases.
- **Umpire:** Independent challenge to false venue canon, unsupported season simulation and all 2026 predetermination.
- **Closer:** No promotion without separate fact, physical, story, visual and commissioner acceptance.
Hypothetical adjudication: choose challenge-first over duel-only; favor durable arena library over yearly improvised sets; ensure nonqualifiers remain socially present; protect immutable championship site; do NOT assert independent director votes or tests.

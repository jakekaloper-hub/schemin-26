## The island's historical logic
The Last Field is conceptually the same final site for every year; physical foundations and entry arch remain recognizable. Other sites are chosen from a changing inventory, with old arenas, upgrades and retired grounds. A round's victories update the central notice board, the remaining bracket, the sealed or opened contenders' suites and Losers' Beach arrivals. Champions' Walk gets a new season inscription AFTER settlement. Nonqualifiers and the eliminated may continue social lives on the shore, without being treated as dead or compelled to attend.
Seasonal participants are verified by ESPN team-slot ID; NOT automatically the same actual owner/person or current fictional 2026 character through historic renames.

## Evidence and canon hazards
- Playoff formats change: 2015–16 two two-week series; 2017–20 six qualifiers, 2017–20 W14–16; 2021–25 seven qualifiers, W15–17. Do not render twelve years as seven-qualifier championship series.
- Some ESPN winner-bracket rows for byes appear with opponent null and winner UNDECIDED; they are byes, not invented head-to-head scores.
- Team-slot and rename continuity does not establish the current 2026 owner is the historic contestant. Historical fame is dated evidence, not future character rights.
- Site names, trial forms, camp development dates, heralds, crowds, suites and the island itself are creative proposals; no claim they are actual ESPN locations.
- Michael Farrell memorial canon is protected. Do not invent a death date or purpose-built memorial construction date. The exact historical trophy-naming history needs Founder-supplied verification.
- 2026: entrants, seed, finals, individual encounter, champion and island-wide new damage are deliberately UNWRITTEN.

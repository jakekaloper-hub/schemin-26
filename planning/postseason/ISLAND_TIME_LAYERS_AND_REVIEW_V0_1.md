Camp tenure comes from distinct qualifying seasons by slot; a new team name or owner without verified human continuity is not proof of a new person's first trip. Returning flags/ceremonial lodging may preserve social memory; actual grounds, scoring rules and arbiters remain equally neutral to new and old competitors.
Separate counters: qualification visits; winning-bracket victories; finals appearances; championship titles; season-by-season display names; owner identity confidence; lodging residency periods. Do not give slot 12 a hereditary kingdom merely for its 10 slot-level visits.

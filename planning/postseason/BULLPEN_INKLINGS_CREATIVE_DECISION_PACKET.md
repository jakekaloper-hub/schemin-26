## Executive creative ruling (RECOMMENDED, NOT PROMOTED)

**Do not choose "all duels" versus "all challenges."** Every playoff pairing is a *head-to-head Contest Encounter with an observable, adjudicated physical conflict*. The test, terrain, and tactics vary; the outcome is never arbitrary. Make the spectacle feel like a sovereign professional sports championship **without conferring actual political sovereignty upon the victor**. The island is operationally closed outside the annual postseason. Losers' Beach is a coherent refuge, never an equally prestigious alternative to advancing. The Last Field must anchor every championship in the invented historical setting without falsifying documented fantasy results or bypassing the current championship-only venue reservation.

## Genuine creative expansion beyond the initial Founder prompt

1. **The Gates-of-Seven ritual (2021+):** seven qualified standards are raised at the harbor on postseason opening. Top seed's standard is carried directly to the Reserve House; six proceed toward the three first-round arena pairings. This marks a bye as honorable exemption, not a fictional victory.
2. **The Falling Standard:** after official result lock, one of two paired flags is lowered at the defeated arena and the central bracket court raises the survivor's. Courier vessels and heralds then carry the same authenticated outcome elsewhere; no instantaneous future-predicting scoreboard.
3. **The Shoreward Descent:** a paved public path joins the contender gates to Losers' Beach. The same physical descent appears in multiple annual episodes — a harsh recurring image for elite semifinalists and former champions.
4. **The Empty Finalists' Gates:** although two teams qualify for the final, the Last Field's opposite entrances remain closed during earlier rounds. The grounds can be glimpsed from the hill but no normal playoff matchup uses them. This reinforces venue rarity.
5. **The Anniversary Stones:** every completed season leaves a physically plausible dated ledger-stone or archival inscription; a prior winner has a record in stone, not a magic ghost or permanent unrestricted statue.
6. **The Sleeping Arenas:** each season activates only a subset of historically proven sites; retired/older sites have visible dates, wood rot, safety fences, restoration records and distinctive plant succession. Reopening one is an event that demands logistical justification.
7. **The Broadcast Ladder:** local announcers at every arena → centralized verified bracket desk → League-wide maritime/road herald network → real-world Memo OS screens/pages. This permits very professional sports coverage without inventing video technology in the fictional world.
8. **Earned Residence vs Social Camp:** the playoff qualifier gets temporary access to a contender house; repeated visits earn social prestige, not exclusive in-world sovereignty. The veteran beach resident can have a superb annual cabana but still be unmistakably bitter about another wasted season.
9. **Historical Contradiction as drama:** 2022 13–1 Red Leopards exited the semifinal; 2023 top seed El Niño also exited; 2024 ObiWan won; 2025 ObiWan lost to The Immortal. No fictional "best regular season always crowned" inevitability.
10. **The Island's Last Night:** after final award, participants and island staff depart, and custodians cover the remaining inscription stone. The site falls quiet until the next spring of repairs and annual postseason opening.

## Screen story beats by event class

| Event class | On-ground experience | Broadcast package | Island-wide consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| First arrival | Ferries, secured team lodgings, public procession, herald check-in, first look up at Last Field | Intro/seed/debut/history/venue | Sets brackets, admissions and temporary camp locations |
| Bye | Reserve House; preparation, study, optional safe public appearance | Analysis of bracket paths and potential opponents, not fake game highlights | Gives rest, not the title |
| Quarterfinal | Standalone arena, two sides, differentiated trial, live crowds and overseers | Dual contender POVs, trial objective, counterplays, verified final sequence | Winner advances; loser departs beachward; central desk updates |
| Semifinal | Higher-capacity neutral arena and advanced broadcast/spectator arrangements | Stakes, prior round memory, opponent adaptations, decisive exchange | One finalist emerges from each side; two more beach arrivals |
| Championship | Fixed Last Field; two old gates, premium suites, public terraces and public tailgates, independent trophy dais and protected memorial observance | Full historic succession package; championship result certified before final art/copy | Champion receives Belt and Farrell Trophy; new archived inscription |
| Post-final | All 12 eligible for ceremony and departure; losing finalist descends after award | One-year retrospective and future uncertainty, without pre-writing next season | Seasonal island closes; heritage state preserved |

## Precinct operational and broadcast realism

- Crew: event steward, contest arbiter, route marshal, veteran groundskeeper, score registrar, commentator, analyst, field reporter, record historian, hospitality steward, security captain, medical staff and cleaning crews. Personnel are **ambient supporting inhabitants**, not a thirteenth principal POV.
- Every quarterfinal site needs its own fenced/marked stage, warmup and recovery zone, public and contender circulation, elevated commentary platform, emergency exit and wayfinding back to the central desk.
- The central desk may synchronize *confirmed* results; in-fiction narrative might present concurrent trials as overlapped, while real NFL scoring periods remain the governing chronology.
- Owners' suite and supporter tailgates must not privilege one finalist. The final stadium has public access, private hospitality and quiet remembrance spaces without treating the Trophy as luxury decor.
- Crucial user goal: postseason is the end-of-year event the whole Commonwealth anticipates. Fans care deeply, money and reputation move, but their material world does not literally exist because games occur.

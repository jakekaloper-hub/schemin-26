# THE LAST HAND BEFORE THE LAST FIELD
## Inklings Historical Semifinal Screenplay — 2020
**Fixture:** HIST-POST-2020-SF-1 · **Venue:** Crown Stair · **Draw:** Last Hand Poker  
**Verified result:** The Grinch defeats Red Leopards **166.32–143.72** in the 2020 semifinal. The Grinch subsequently reached the fixed Last Field final.  
**Status:** Original noncanon secondary-world screenwriting, not evidence of an actual card contest or of historical owners' personal appearance. Owner-character personas personally play and decide throughout.

### EXT. CROWN STAIR — LATE EVENING

At the foot of the enormous steps, merchants are closing their stalls.

The season has moved past the first round. The island's six qualified contenders have been reduced to four, then this evening to two finalists. In the upper fortress, the protected residence assigned to the first seed is empty. Its occupant has reached the semifinal court.

Two standards hang above the ceremonial gate: THE GRINCH and RED LEOPARDS.

The stairs lead toward an arena whose architecture predates the current public banners. At the eastern arch, a few old board-game scratches are visible beneath a polished inspection plate. They were preserved rather than leveled flat when the larger event desk was added.

Farther beyond the ridge sits the Last Field.

The semifinal does not take place there. No one who has not survived this round may walk through its contestant gate.

An EVENT HERALD lifts a sealed ceramic draw tablet.

**HERALD**  
Last Hand Poker.

Some spectators groan. They were hoping for swords.

A woman sitting below the upper benches leans forward.

**SPECTATOR**  
Do they have to fight?

**OLDER SPECTATOR**  
Watch what they stake. It will hurt enough.

The announcer's raised platform is high enough to see the single competition table at the center of the protected stone court. Two sets of chairs face one another. The dealer's place is neutral and physically outside both contestants' private working space.

At this era's broadcast desk, the programme director records the ceremonial introduction for the annual archive. The corresponding contemporary film may use close-ups and score graphics, but no global real-time video relay is asserted as established 2020 World OS technology.

### EXT. CROWN STAIR — THE CARD COURT

THE GRINCH approaches the table, glancing toward the short stack of neutral challenge markers set beside each seat.

RED LEOPARDS studies the same arrangement from opposite the dealer.

The two contenders are the only authorized decision-makers. A dealer may shuffle and distribute the approved cards; the dealer cannot choose whether either competitor bets, folds, holds or calls.

An ARBITER reads the rules.

**ARBITER**  
Three rounds of hands. Each contestant makes the decisions for himself. The cards are identical in law, not in luck. Markers track only this fictional challenge. They do not replace the certified League record.

Red Leopards takes the chair.

**RED LEOPARDS**  
They expected us to break ourselves on those stairs.

**THE GRINCH**  
Perhaps they decided watching us sit still would be worse.

The dealer turns over the first card.

### PHASE ONE — AN OPENING BET

The first round allows only a limited opening stake. Both contestants know the permitted range before a card is dealt.

The Grinch receives the first hand, sets the cards low against the table edge and watches Red Leopards choose a starting marker.

Red Leopards places two on the line.

The Grinch places one.

A faint disapproval runs through the crowd, which cannot see the hidden cards. To them the first move looks timid.

**HERALD**  
The Grinch opens with a single marker. Red Leopards has raised.

**ANALYST**  
Now the Grinch must decide whether to let the other player control the pace.

The Grinch studies the public cards and calls with a second marker. Not a flourish. A considered correction.

Red Leopards does not move. That stillness is a tactic in its own right.

The dealer reveals the remaining public card. The Grinch slides one card facedown and lays the rest beside it. Red Leopards opens the hand only after the authorized signal.

The official scores the stage markers on a separate board. Neither is announced as the semifinal survivor.

The Grinch looks toward the eastern arch where a row of old scoring-plate marks can be seen.

**THE GRINCH**  
This place keeps every bad decision.

**RED LEOPARDS**  
Only the ones somebody bothers to record.

### PHASE TWO — THE COUNTER

The dealer resets the approved deck under the eyes of both contenders. A more demanding table rule permits one public challenge token to be moved from a secure position into the contested center.

The Grinch chooses not to move the token immediately.

Red Leopards acts first, pushing one toward the center and keeping the remaining markers arranged in a neat column.

The Grinch studies that arrangement rather than the rival's face.

The cards come down.

This time the Grinch begins with a stronger opening stake. Red Leopards pauses before answering, letting the spectators hear the wooden chair creak beneath the shifting weight.

**HERALD**  
A larger wager from The Grinch.

**ANALYST**  
He remembers the first hand. The question is whether Red Leopards believes he can afford the correction.

Red Leopards calls, then pushes another marker forward. It is a challenge to finish the exchange sooner.

The Grinch turns the cards over slowly.

A visible pair. Nothing more.

The spectators cannot see whether an additional hidden card will complete the hand. The Grinch has left the decision to continue resting entirely on the rival's willingness to pay for more information.

Red Leopards shifts a marker to the contested center, watching The Grinch's hands.

The Grinch considers folding. A single finger rests at the edge of the cards.

Then The Grinch calls.

The dealer reveals the final common card, and the two players complete the stage. One of the public marker plates catches on the edge of the board as the dealer resets the table for the final phase. The ARBITER records the shallow abrasion and orders a protective cover over the plate; the permanent stone of the eastern arch is unharmed.

Red Leopards looks down at the mark.

**RED LEOPARDS**  
There. Now it has something to remember.

**THE GRINCH**  
Not yet what it means.

### PHASE THREE — THE LAST HAND

For the final stage the table is placed beneath the eastern ceremonial arch.

The two contestants have no assistants. An impartial dealer provides the final hand, then withdraws behind the boundary line. All choices—from the first stake to the closing reveal—belong personally to The Grinch and Red Leopards.

No hand has to resemble a real-world scoring period. The contest is fiction, while the official semifinal outcome is external and already archived.

Red Leopards opens decisively.

The Grinch studies the public cards. A second look reveals a possible straight that a less patient reader would miss.

The Grinch calls.

Red Leopards raises again.

A ripple of argument moves through the stands. The oldest spectators have seen matches at this arena end in physical strain; they are learning that fear can make hands tremble over wood as much as over a sword.

**ANALYST**  
A wrong choice can end your evening even if nobody lays a hand on you.

The Grinch lifts one private card and sets it back down.

Red Leopards does the same, watching the other player's face.

A new card arrives. Both contestants must decide whether to remain in the hand.

Red Leopards presses a marker toward the center with a controlled, deliberate movement. The Grinch answers by matching it.

The dealer looks to the arbiter.

**DEALER**  
Final reveal?

**ARBITER**  
Both have completed the legal decisions. Show the cards.

The contestants turn their own hands over. The dealer records the physical game's conclusion on the competition board.

Neither contestant is declared League semifinal winner from a poker hand, and no cards are converted into fantasy-football points. The final authority remains the sealed historic sporting ledger.

The crowd is so quiet that the old eastern arch seems to amplify the scrape of the last marker as it is returned to its tray.

### RESULT CERTIFIED

The official League record is placed on the scoring platform.

**RESULT KEEPER**  
Semifinal. Red Leopards: **143.72**. The Grinch: **166.32**.

The Grinch remains seated for an instant.

**RESULT KEEPER (CONT'D)**  
The Grinch advances to the Last Field.

The rival standards change. Red Leopards's banner is lowered. A public courier leaves for the central bracket court, while the upper stands answer with a delayed roar.

**RED LEOPARDS**  
I thought you had a hand worth folding.

**THE GRINCH**  
So did I.

The statement may be truth, misdirection or the last move of a contest already finished. The Grinch gives nothing more.

Red Leopards rises and looks beyond the arch toward the final stadium.

This is the last view of it available from the contender's path this season.

### EPILOGUE — THE TABLE BELOW THE ARCH

The crowd disperses.

At the eastern arch, the plate scratched during phase two is carefully lifted from its removable bed. The record keeper notes which hand and which year had marked it. The plate is replaced with an inspected safe fitting.

The original stained plate is sealed for the island archive.

A groundskeeper examines the old stone beneath.

**GROUNDSKEEPER**  
Not everything leaves a mark you can see from the stands.

The table is covered. The festival lights are extinguished across the semicircular court.

Red Leopards travels toward the one seasonal resort at Losers' Beach, where men have learned to watch others receive the championship. The Grinch returns to the secure finalist quarters, no longer entitled to the first seed's rest but now eligible for the final.

The Last Field lies beyond the ridge.

FADE OUT.

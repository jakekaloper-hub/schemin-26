## Precinct interpretation

The island opens to the public **only for postseason**. It is a *rugged championship survival precinct with a modern sporting operating system*, not a tropical theme park. Its man-made luxury exists *within* a harsh natural environment; the causeway, stadium and resort improvements are a visible history of attempting to master that environment.

The fixed arena constitution from 2015 onwards is: **two historic semifinal grounds**, an unchanging **Last Field championship arena**, and first-round/quarterfinal grounds that come online when the 2017 and 2021 tournament expansions require them. Old retired training grounds remain physically present but do not replace round-assigned sites without a separately governed amendment.

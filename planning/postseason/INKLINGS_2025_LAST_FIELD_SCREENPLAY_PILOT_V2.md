FADE IN:

### EXT. THE MONUMENTAL CAUSEWAY — LATE AFTERNOON

A current of spectators moves toward the island. The causeway is far older than the bright screens now hanging above its inspection gates. Salt water drives through the arches beneath the elevated span. Security stewards keep the crowd moving while a narrower, barricaded lane admits carts carrying parts of tonight's temporary challenge.

A supply cart carries a square of dark stone marked **LAST FIELD — WESTERN STAGE RIG — INSPECTED**.

On either side of the road, two banners alternate in the wind: **OBIWAN JACOBY** and **THE IMMORTAL**.

The spectators do not talk about next week's match. There is no next week's match.

A RUNNER strides into the broadcast compound carrying a sealed score and stage ledger.

### INT. ISLAND BROADCAST CONTROL — CONTINUOUS

A professional director watches a wall of divided feeds: the empty final field, the Contenders' Fortress, the crowd on the bridge, the memorial approach, and the sole beach hotel glittering far below.

The CENTRE-DESK ANCHOR addresses the cameras.

**ANCHOR**  
Last year, one of these gates received a champion. It will receive him again tonight. That does not mean the other gate must agree with the ending.

On a second display, an official past-result card shows **OBIWAN JACOBY — 2024 CHAMPION**. Another identifies The Immortal's surviving route through the 2025 quarterfinal and the narrow **0.40 semifinal**.

**ANALYST**  
Four tenths of a point put The Immortal in this stadium. The difference between *making* this field and *winning* it has ruined better predictions than ours.

The director signals for an image from the stadium's oldest stone.

### EXT. THE CHAMPIONS' WALK — SAME

The camera moves along eleven-season masonry—ten completed champion records visible before tonight's result. The original 2015 inscription is small and weather-darkened. Later plaques have more polished surrounds. The path's architecture carries the cost of expansion without pretending the entire institution was built in one season.

Beyond a guarded garden stands the Michael Farrell remembrance precinct.

The public procession passes quietly beside it. A member of the memorial staff attends to the grounds. No one invents a biography, funeral, speech or naming date.

The procession becomes loud again beyond the memorial gates.

### EXT. THE LAST FIELD — PRE-CONTEST

An enormous modern stadium has grown around the original championship oval. A ring of screened suites sits above stone terraces that predate the screens. The western and eastern contestant gates are the same width, angle and alignment as in prior years.

At the field centre, tonight's challenge apparatus has been assembled over removable, load-bearing surface plates. It resembles a quarry cut into three sectors: a safe simulated rubble approach, an elevated tactical crossing, and a final exposed standard protected behind padded barriers.

It is not the island's actual Iron Quarry. It is a temporary **QUARRY AMBUSH** rig on the **LAST FIELD**. Its simulated rock walls can be removed. Its old arena walls cannot.

A FIELD ARBITER checks both gates.

**ARBITER**  
Three passages. Each contestant must advance under his own effort. No delegate completes a station. No supporting retinue crosses the marked line.

A scorekeeper raises an empty result card.

**ARBITER (CONT'D)**  
The island records what the Contest certifies. The apparatus cannot award a championship of its own.

#### CEREMONIAL DRAW INSERT

A clear casing above the field displays the selected challenge: **QUARRY AMBUSH**. An official records its challenge ID and verification mark in the archive.

The screens do not yet display a winner.

### INT. CONTENDERS' FORTRESS — OBIWAN'S CORRIDOR

The corridor is unusually quiet. Five earlier contenders have already lost access to these secure quarters. Two finalist rooms remain open.

OBIWAN JACOBY tests his shoulder in a controlled movement. The strain is visible in his timing rather than in an invented medical diagnosis.

He looks toward a wall-mounted image of the original western gate.

**OBIWAN**  
It did not open for a king last year.

He folds the old champion's cloak over a chair.

**OBIWAN (CONT'D)**  
It opened for me.

He leaves it behind.

### INT. CONTENDERS' FORTRESS — THE IMMORTAL'S CORRIDOR

THE IMMORTAL presses a sore palm against the railing while the arena crew completes a pre-contest inspection. There are no aides who can perform the tasks instead.

He hears the crowd reading ObiWan's name outside.

**THE IMMORTAL**  
Let them remember.

He lowers his hand.

**THE IMMORTAL (CONT'D)**  
They cannot remember the part we haven't done.

### EXT. LAST FIELD — GATES OPENING

The gates rise.

The public sees both entrants in one unbroken wide shot. The camera holds long enough for the audience to recognize the identical approaches and the untouched original stone behind the newer rigging.

The announcers fall silent.

## PHASE ONE — THE MARKED RUBBLE

The challenge opens in an enclosed gravel-and-stone simulation. Both contestants must navigate separated but equivalent approaches and claim a protected marker. Dummy rock and breakaway scenery are designed to fall *away* from both competitors.

ObiWan enters quickly, taking the narrower direct path. His shoulder is stiff, but he chooses precision over speed. He places his own hand on the first standard.

The Immortal refuses the direct route. He moves through a wider sequence of barricades, reading ObiWan's path through the rig's open central space.

ObiWan hears the first thud of The Immortal's landing.

**OBIWAN**  
Too wide.

The Immortal answers without looking over.

**THE IMMORTAL**  
Then why are you watching?

A movable wall closes its staged course—not by magic, but on a visible mechanical track. Neither is harmed. Both must choose a route.

ObiWan reaches the first marker and sees The Immortal emerge almost beside him. Neither has ended the contest. Both have acted personally.

**CUT TO: BROADCAST BOOTH**

**PLAY-BY-PLAY**  
You can see the first choice already: ObiWan wants the straight line. The Immortal is making him turn his head before every step.

**ANALYST**  
And that's expensive when the field is built to shorten between passages.

The screen briefly shows the former champion's 2024 final gate—but only as archival context, never as a promise about tonight.

## PHASE TWO — THE ELEVATED CROSSING

The apparatus opens onto a sloped causeway of stone-faced blocks, joined over deep catch nets. The blocks have handholds and protected side rails. The central standard may be captured only after a contestant negotiates the crossing personally.

ObiWan accelerates, avoiding the highest section to spare his shoulder. He pivots at a narrow corner, slips, and catches himself with his free hand. The motion leaves a bruise beneath his protective garment. His hand stays on the rail.

The Immortal climbs the more difficult upper route. His palm strains. He pauses—but refuses to call for a substitute.

**FIELD REPORTER**  
Both are still in the course. ObiWan has changed his angle. The Immortal is taking the high route on one of the old field's most visible lines.

The Immortal's knee strikes a padded guard while he recovers balance. He continues, moving more carefully.

ObiWan reaches across a gap for the second marker.

The Immortal has already positioned himself at the opposing side.

For a moment, both men hold separate ends of the same protected standard. Their personal leverage, not a retinue's intervention, decides the next position.

**OBIWAN**  
You carried this all the way here.

**THE IMMORTAL**  
I carried it through the Grove.

The camera shows only the competitors, the apparatus and the objective. A brief archival insert recalls the **152.20–151.80** semifinal, then returns to the present.

The Immortal releases his grip deliberately. ObiWan falls into a safe low landing—but must spend precious effort recovering his position.

**ANALYST (V.O.)**  
The early pressure has become a problem of recovery. Neither can replay a previous round's advantage once the gate opens.

The field marshall raises a flag. The third passage is ready.

---

## PHASE THREE — THE FINAL STANDARD

A set of expendable barricades unlocks from the stage-rig floor. The last standard stands on an elevated central island. Every competing movement is visible from the stands and every safety gate remains functional.

The contestants are winded. The Last Field itself is not harmed.

ObiWan moves first. He tries a direct approach around the left rig. A controlled burst of stage smoke obscures the empty space behind him. The audience roars, but the smoke's exit route is clear and away from the protected memorial sightline.

The Immortal steps through the other passage. His sore knee makes his landing awkward. He has to choose between a quick leap and a slower, more secure step.

He chooses the step.

ObiWan reaches the outer wall. He looks to the old western arch for a fraction of a second—the same arch that framed his victory the year before.

The Immortal reaches the central approach. ObiWan lunges for the marked line. The Immortal counters. Both have their hands on the final contested object, and neither has anyone to take the task from him.

The referee signals the end of the physical demonstration. The two contenders separate.

Silence.

The stage crew does not raise a winner's flag.

## RESULT CERTIFIED — THE ELEVENTH CHAMPION

A scorekeeper receives the **certified sporting record**.

The main display remains blank until the arbiter signs it.

**ARBITER**  
The official Contest record for the final: ObiWan Jacoby — **109.56**. The Immortal — **161.07**.

A pause.

**ARBITER (CONT'D)**  
The surviving champion: **THE IMMORTAL**.

The stadium detonates in noise. The words are echoed across the broadcast galleries, tailgate grounds, and the beach hotel's outdoor terrace. A giant scoreboard turns to the verified record.

No screenplay device has changed the numbers. The in-world trial has dramatized the already verified 2025 championship.

The Immortal sinks onto one knee. He is exhausted, bruised and victorious.

ObiWan remains standing for a moment.

He looks again at the western gate.

### CUT TO: LOSERS' BEACH — NIGHT

A modest classical resort stretches along the sheltered western shoreline. The men already eliminated have stopped their private jokes to watch the last announcement. The reflected stadium lights ripple in the pool.

No one calls the beach a victory.

A distant commentator says:

**COMMENTATOR (V.O.)**  
Eleven come here wanting the same thing. Eleven finish without it.

The beach faces the stadium. It cannot alter it.

## EPILOGUE — THE TITLE AND THE MEMORY

At the Last Field the crew removes the equipment that could obscure the protected presentation axis. The new champion approaches the dais.

The Belt is presented as the sign of current competitive supremacy.

The Michael Farrell Trophy is presented separately, with the remembrance precinct acknowledged in silence. No unverified historic naming origin or personal speech is invented.

The Immortal accepts the championship.

On the Champions' Walk, a vacant 2025 stone awaits its inscription.

ObiWan walks out through the west gate. A medic meets him at the secure post-contest corridor; he accepts an examination and continues toward the seasonal resort, not toward a new fight. His prior title remains in the museum. Tonight's one has passed.

### EXT. THE LAST FIELD — SERVICE CORRIDOR — LATER

The crowds have begun leaving across the causeway. Inside the arena, workers inspect the removable stage where the controlled smoke effect had gone off.

One of the sacrificial fittings carries a darkened mark.

A WORKER begins to polish it away.

The CHIEF GROUNDSKEEPER stops him.

**CHIEF GROUNDSKEEPER**  
Not that one.

**WORKER**  
It's stained.

**CHIEF GROUNDSKEEPER**  
It's dated.

He marks the fitting for safe removal and preservation. The original stone beneath it is inspected, cleared and sealed for the next season.

**CHIEF GROUNDSKEEPER (CONT'D)**  
The field stays. The night stays with it.

The camera rises from the soot-marked fitting to the original twin gates.

For the eleventh completed year, they are still standing.

FADE OUT.

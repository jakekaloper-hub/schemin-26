## Reconcile with Interview I and previous project work

Interview I F04 sets **permanent round-specific arenas**. Interview II F12 establishes **varying drawn challenges within those fixed arenas**: both are controlling. The old option-based allocations from Interview I are superseded as draw authority, but preserved as a recoverable editorial comparison.

Interview I F05 upgrades to a modern-style sporting resort. Interview II F15 means that polished new facilities do **not** erase the site's physical history. Every competition leaves an identity-bearing trace; damaged hazards can be safely sealed, restored, enclosed, archived or mapped, never silently deleted.

Interview I F06 allows veteran-status cultural familiarity, but Interview II F18 ensures **no veteran can hire a substitute to compete** or gain a mechanical advantage. Lodgings are still temporary.

Interview I F09 makes Last Field fixed and the Michael Farrell memorial integrated. Interview II F15 affects it too: allow subtle cumulative physical history of each final (repaired scuff in gate, commemorative resurfacing line, archived cracked panel, stone inscription) **while never compromising maintained arena safety, foundation, orientation, championship-only use, or protected memorial**.

Keep Losers' Beach deliberately modest, one central resort district for reflection and relaxation. Its residents can watch the tournament, but it is not a second elaborate competition complex.

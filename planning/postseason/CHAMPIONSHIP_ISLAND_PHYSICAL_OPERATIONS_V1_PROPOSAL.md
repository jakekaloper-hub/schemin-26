## 0. Immutable boundaries

1. The real ESPN contest determines every postseason participant, seed, first-round bye, pairing, score, progression and championship outcome. Novelty and staging never determine results.
2. An *Encounter* is NOT necessarily swordplay. Each matchup must be visually and causally two-sided and witnessed, but may take the form of a duel, territorial trial, pursuit, race, sporting test, tactical campaign, contested feat, or rule-governed negotiation. Distinct challengers retain agency. Never reduce a playoff to two mascots posing.
3. Elimination means loss of title eligibility, **not death**. Principal-character permanent death is prohibited.
4. The island is used publicly and competitively **only for the annual postseason**. Between seasons it is closed to spectators and routine competitions. A small custodial/naval/maintenance presence protects structures and handles repairs, without running a normal open-to-all city.
5. The island is NOT already active Atlas geography. No new LOC-* ID or route is allowed on the canonical live map without the location-promotion gate; candidate IDs below are editorial handles.
6. The existing \`CAND-CHAMP-LAST-FIELD\` currently specifies an intended Central Basin location and championship-only unlock. Relocating this venue to the proposed island is a **material Atlas/canon change pending Founder and Umpire reconciliation**. The mainland League Chamber, Archive, Compact Neutral Grounds and Hall of Keeping are not silently moved offshore.
7. The Last Field is always the single *fictional* championship site for the 2015–2025 reconstruction; this is a **creative historical proposal**, not a claim ESPN verifies physical venues. Its 2026 reveal and present use must still honor the existing championship-only publication gate.
8. Michael Farrell's name and memorial are protected. No death circumstances, personal history, supernatural presence, invented quotations, or retroactive claims about the trophy's naming date.
9. No past team slot is equated to a real owner across seasons without independent owner-identity evidence. See appearance ledger.
10. Interpret “the Commonwealth revolves around the championship” as immense civic, reputational, economic and cultural importance, **not** the literal idea that the world ceases to exist without the League.

## 1. Physical island hypothesis

**Proposed geographic affiliation:** Inner Sea Littoral, beyond the mapped southeastern/lower coastline, reachable from existing littoral ports and the Central Basin port network. It remains inside the seven-zone causal geography, not a new eighth region. Avoid placing it by invented map coordinates, precise travel times, acreage, or sea depth before actual spatial QA.

### Physical gradients and hydrology

- **Northern/leeward harbor bay:** sheltered arrival basin, seawall, maritime customs, boatyards and loading courts; enough natural depth/harbor engineering for peak seasonal transport; weather gates and storm contingency.
- **Western sandward coast:** the warm, sheltered **Losers' Beach** with graded dunes, protective vegetation, retreat terraces, tidal limits, beach-side seasonal pavilions and emergency evacuation path. It should be desirable without equal prestige to contention.
- **Central rocky upland:** highest stable ground and permanent stonework; the *Last Field* at the island's historic core, supported by drainage, stable foundations, terraces and an independent event/service ring.
- **Eastern cliff/coastal face:** wind-exposed stone arenas, watch towers and access restrictions, reused for high-stakes differentiated contests only when weather/safety allow.
- **Inland quarry/grove:** old worked rock, sheltered forested folds and clearings explain materially distinct older arenas.
- **Fresh water/waste:** cisterns and protected reservoirs with verified source/runoff paths; marine traffic imports food, fuel, medical supplies and overflow materials; sewage and refuse cannot discharge onto the bathing strand.
- **Seasonal closure:** building custodians, keepers, repairs, winter storm preservation and restricted access. Offseason activity is maintenance/security only.

### Circulation and fairness

- Ring Road connects landing, public Market & Tailgate Commons, spectator arena gates, heritage walk, Last Field and beach.
- Three separated routes: public spectator flows; secured contender/retinue movement from Contenders' Quarter to authorized arena gates; service/medical/officiating circulation.
- Public and opponent routes may intersect at ceremonial appearances but not in preparation compounds or while a contest is active.
- Six permanent spatial QA questions for every site: who controls it, why terrain supports it, how water affects it, where access flows, what previous-season scars remain, and how it changes in different documented years.
- No arena is magically transformed between rounds; adaptations must use documented temporary structures, movable barriers and pre-approved encounter rigs.

## 3. What physically happens in a playoff encounter?

### The shared three-beat physical grammar
1. **Opening of the Gates**: verified opposing sides enter through separate gates with a referee/arbiter present, a clearly intelligible objective, hazard briefing, contested terrain and ceremonial identification. The audience understands the nature of this *particular* challenge.
2. **The Trial**: a genuine back-and-forth physical contest where both sides change strategy in response to the other. Depending on characters and venue this is a duel of mastery, endurance pursuit, artifact recovery, siege simulation, tactical capture, sporting contest, obstacle competition, a bounded race, or a rule-governed commercial contest with physical demonstration. Not every challenge uses weapons and not every high score implies supernatural strength.
3. **The Decisive Passage**: final performance beats are choreographed **after** externally verified ESPN result lock. Arbiter certifies the winner; loser is formally eliminated; herald/public-board report and the shared bracket update. No fake certainty while the NFL games are ongoing.

### Fairness and entertainment
- One head-to-head fantasy pairing => one independent tournament Encounter, even if its cinematic scene has multiple acts or stations.
- Contenders may bring their approved roster/retinue analogue under the existing translation rules, but a spectator, fan or another contestant cannot change the match result.
- Physical hazards are bounded and recoverable. Ritual humiliation or injury may be written under character canon, but no permanent death and no unapproved permanent geographic destruction.
- Do **not** use the same combat formula in all 56 historical postseason matchups. Mix agency and trial form intentionally; the decisive beat must make sense given that venue and rivals.
- Scoring can be presented to readers via Memo overlay; in-world public scores are announcements under the accepted communication technology, not magically updating digital screens.
- Coincident encounters share a bracket and world state rather than one being allowed to alter another's official score.

### Eight Encounter formats available to the author room

\`FORM-DUEL\` controlled contest of arms/technique; \`FORM-PURSUIT\` head-to-head route capture; \`FORM-ENDURANCE\` obstacle and survival trial without lethal stakes; \`FORM-TACTICAL\` arena-scale command/capture; \`FORM-SPORT\` golf, ball, race or other sporting challenge; \`FORM-WATER\` tide/dock/water obstacle under safety rules; \`FORM-ENGINEERING\` build/breach/repair trial on prearranged neutral equipment; \`FORM-NEGOTIATION\` adjudicated trade/strategic bargaining trial with visible physical commitments. Each historical outline assigns an initial FORM as **proposed fictionalization**, not a real source fact.

### The post-result ritual
At each final result, one banner advances on the central public bracket. The loser surrenders its contestant residence and is offered welcome passage to the beach quarter. Those still alive gain access to progressively smaller, quieter preparation space. They face growing pressure, not a magically changing caste or presumed political sovereignty.

## 5. Yearly lifecycle of a closed postseason island

- **Pre-Gathering, closed:** crews inspect stonework and seawalls, repair lodgings, verify water supply/sanitation, activate limited maritime logistics, prepare archival displays. No ordinary League games occur.
- **Selection Gate after regular-season lock:** actual qualifiers assigned seven contestant houses (or historically four/six), clearly separated from nonqualifier beach accommodation. The top seed receives the appropriately protected bye house, not an invented on-field victory.
- **Arrival Week:** official manifests, ferries, rival processions, crowds, market vendors, hospitality and chartered supporter transport; no every-region-is-a-mini-kingdom gimmick.
- **Quarterfinal/first elimination period:** simultaneous competition *within the same scoring period* at separate venues. Exhibition and media material may be temporally sequenced without inventing NFL game dates.
- **Semifinals:** only four remain. Lodging and public spectacle narrow; former champions and beach residents develop their own social subplots.
- **Championship:** two finalists use fixed Last Field. Dedicated upper viewing suites, tailgate terraces, public lawns, formal heraldry and arrival; memorial observance independent of spectacle. Verified winner receives Michael Farrell Trophy and Champion's Belt, commemorated without declaring sovereign rulership.
- **Post-final:** all twelve may be present, including beach community, during the ceremony and departure; final inscription after certification. Staff begin closure and island state is snapshotted for the next year.
- **Closed season:** no recreational beach resort outside postseason and no routine League encounters.

## 7. Spectator realism and the live editorial experience

- Distinct **venue headliners**: each matchup has local announcer, analytical counterpart, field interviewer, operator controlling clocks/banners/records and safety marshal.

- **Mainland dispatch network** sends printed sheets, courier reports, harbor pennants, herald proclamations and retained chronicles using permitted communications. Real-world Memo OS can imitate big-event live TV coverage without implying television exists in story canon.

## 8. Last Field and Michael Farrell succession

**The Last Field**: one ancient fixed championship ground across every *fictional* historical season, with recognizably unchanged field alignment, twin gates, record stone, final-procession axis and coastline silhouette. Stadium capacity/suites/media galleries may grow; its physical identity cannot drift or be remapped.

**Champion's Belt**: current competitive supremacy; held by reigning champion under historical succession, not a universal crown.  
**Michael Farrell Trophy**: championship legacy and remembrance. Trophy-name inception date NOT established in sources. Historical ceremonies may be shown with an award/stewardship practice, but cannot assert the physical *named* Trophy was presented in 2015 before evidence of its historical naming.  
**Memorial**: restrained, protected, separate from Losers' Beach comedy; ceremonial quiet within the thunder of sport. No invented memorial biography.

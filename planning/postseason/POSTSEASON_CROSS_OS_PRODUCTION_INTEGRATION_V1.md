## Architecture: one evidence source, one active world model, many read-only consumers

1. **Data Gateway / League Truth** determines year, rules, playoff entrants, byes, seeded bracket, fixture IDs, result status, scores, and certified champion. Historical 2015–25 results are frozen. 2026 winners remain unset.
2. **Atlas / World Engine** independently approves the actual island and monumental causeway topology, zone, tide exposure, water, circulation, LOC-* venues and routes. No consumers are allowed to manufacture these records from narrative drafts. `CAND-CHAMP-LAST-FIELD` mainland reservation requires explicit supersession if moved.
3. **Novel OS / Inklings** consumes the frozen league and time-correct Atlas/Character facts. Its work outputs individual three-stage adversarial head-to-head challenges, genuine two-sided POV actions, individual round/season literary movements, eliminations and beach consequences. Prose can propose but never commit geography.
4. **Memo OS** consumes the same round classifications and site IDs, adding its own cover, broadcast desk, headline moments, screen overlays, six-fixture week narratives and central bracket view. The format is modern sports broadcast by Founder F08, not permission to invent matchup results or league trends.
5. **Visual / Character pipelines** use currently accepted source reference bytes, subject mounting, geographic plate, art brief, camera continuity and independent raster QA. A production card is not render permission.
6. **Commonwealth site / Atlas view** consumes compiled, permitted physical-world and broadcast assets. Live tournament maps cannot show undisclosed finalists before provider results, and the 2015–25 history must be presented as history, not 2026 present.
7. **Bullpen** routes execution to existing Directors, with Beat Writer creative ownership, Scout/Librarian truth counterweights, Architect for map/runtime, Closer for cross-domain reconciliation, and independent Umpire for fact/visual/release gates. **Inklings is a full project-local production desk, not an added 19th Director.**

## The three independent timelines

- **Source-time:** Exact ESPN scoring period/round, roster names, scores, seeds, byes, results; historical year must match its actual format (four qualifiers in 2015–16, six in 2017–20, seven in 2021–25).
- **World-time:** Venue state and accommodations vary by 2015, 2017, 2021 and 2025 expansions; modern camera towers, giant screens and resort suites are later construction candidates rather than retrojected into founding seasons.
- **Publication-time:** Memo/Narrative episodes are assembled and aired only after source-correct results lock; public depictions of the 2026 Final must wait until official finalists and winner are eligible for the relevant shot.

## On-island technology: enforce the new Founder exception, not a magical continent upgrade

Founder F08 specifies real cameras, large display screens, professional commentary, video broadcast, replay, interview suites and venue control-room infrastructure. Implement at **island-scope**, with era-specific construction history, instead of rewriting the entire Commonwealth's premodern technology law.

If remote viewers cannot receive moving images in the approved world technology, present live audio/courier/print on the continent and modern graphical footage only as a Memo/site out-of-world presentation until exception is adopted. No universal electricity implied.

## The causeway is a piece of infrastructure, not a decorative line

Design from the existing Inner Sea/port adjacency without inventing coordinates or a bridge that violates chart bounds. Engineering gates require:
- measured navigable channels and weather/tide baseline;
- mainland port anchor, island landing and distinct secured/public/service lanes;
- clearance for established regional sea traffic and storm surge;
- embankment load-bearing geology, runoff and maintenance access;
- crowd-control capacity for three QFs and championship arrivals;
- redundant emergency evacuation and fallback ferry/cargo link;
- protected cable/utility distribution only if ratified.

**Status:** Existing Atlas contains Inner Sea Littoral, ports and maritime routes; the new island's exact LOC, route and monumental crossing are **not yet active**.

For every fixture, Inklings must produce an independent three-act encounter script **with** physical choreography, protagonists' counterplay, a decisive result-backed outcome, consistent backdrop and a beach/contender-quarters coda. No original writer named in the published-method advisory council is represented as personally participating.

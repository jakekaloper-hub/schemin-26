## 2015 — The First Recorded Gate

The island opened to four contenders and a small crowd. The causeway approach was rougher and narrower than it would later become. Two semifinal grounds already stood in the island's rugged uplands: Crown Stair and the Grove of Standards. At one, Discount Belichick defeated Here, Ok I took L's 268.5–199.5; at the other, Austin's Hyena's eliminated Hook, Line, and Zeeker 182.5–163. The two rival standards moved toward the central stadium while the other eight league participants watched from temporary tents near the beach.

The Last Field waited behind its permanent twin gates. Discount Belichick defeated Austin's Hyena's 211.5–187. The old stone had its first recoverable championship story. The beach had its first disappointed community. Workers closed the gates in the knowledge that a second year was now worth preparing for.

## 2016 — The Returners

## 2021 — The Seven-Contender Era

The modern three-quarterfinal system required an additional permanent first-round arena.

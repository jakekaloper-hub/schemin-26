## Corrective engineering already performed

1. **The model no longer writes the certified result.** \`run_inklings_local_scene_writer.cjs\` now presents source-neutral actors and stages to the LLM, then appends the real league winner and score **deterministically**. This prevents scores/winners from relying on model memory or inference.
2. The writer is bounded to one to three **already verified historic fixture IDs** per invocation. It is not a general future-season winner generator or an unrestricted autonomous publication tool.
3. It records only **PRELIMINARY_FORMAT_SCREEN_ONLY** instead of misleading “READY” based on prose shape alone.
4. \`audit_local_inklings_drafts.mjs\` provides a separate source/canon screen: catches false winners, invented stage-point decimals, unsupported supernatural capabilities, unprotected arena collapses, unwanted internal IDs, unexpected language scripts, unauthorized holograms/droids, spectator contradiction, invasive historic-foundation damage and short manuscripts.
5. \`test_local_model_draft_audit.mjs\` runs **eleven** synthetic positive/negative cases against that checker. The actual first bad pilots and the two corrected-prompt pilots now receive **REJECT**, with the reasons recorded.

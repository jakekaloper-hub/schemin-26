# FOUR TENTHS
## Inklings Historical Semifinal Screenplay — 2025
**Fixture:** HIST-POST-2025-SF-2 · **Venue:** Grove of Standards · **Draw:** Paintball Puzzle  
**Verified:** The Immortal defeats Dr. Duckhook, **152.20–151.80**, in the 2025 semifinal. The survivor reaches the Last Field to face ObiWan Jacoby.  
**Creative status:** Original proposed Commonwealth physical encounter, *not* actual archived paintball footage or evidence of human-owner injuries. Modern island apparatus is governed by unresolved World/Tech exception.

### EXT. THE GROVE OF STANDARDS — MORNING

The island has grown around this place without managing to conquer it.

The Grove is a proper modern semifinal stadium now, with covered commentary galleries and a high crowd ring, but the oldest trees still stand inside the protected interior perimeter. Their roots are behind low masonry, separated from the field by a broad channel of packed earth. Old stage marks have been preserved in stone; the original spectator entrance is visible beneath a newer gallery.

A stadium screen shows **SEMIFINAL — GROVE OF STANDARDS**.

A second screen displays a documentary image of the Last Field's familiar gates. It is not the same ground.

Two names appear: **DR. DUCKHOOK** and **THE IMMORTAL**.

At the central operations desk, the PLAY-BY-PLAY COMMENTATOR looks toward the empty final challenge marker.

**PLAY-BY-PLAY**  
One survivor of this Grove takes the remaining road to the championship.

**ANALYST**  
And everybody who loses today gets the same beautiful view of the Last Field from the beach. Nobody came here for that view.

An EVENT STEWARD raises a clear-walled ceremonial cylinder above the arena's entry court. Inside is a single draw tablet.

The lid opens.

*PAINTBALL PUZZLE.*

The challenge is an obstacle and deduction contest with soft dye-marking projectiles, enclosed firing booths, and a physical code mechanism. Every firing lane has a padded target wall and spectator exclusion fencing. No live shot may cross a running route. The course's mechanical panels are temporary attachments; the old trees and stadium foundations do not move.

The CHIEF OFFICIAL addresses both contestants.

**OFFICIAL**  
Three stages. Each of you walks, solves and marks for himself. No second hand on the trigger. No second mind at the code. Three doors, one certified result.

DR. DUCKHOOK examines the public puzzle board, where the first set of colored symbols is visible.

THE IMMORTAL looks at the opposite route.

**DR. DUCKHOOK**  
You can learn a great deal from where someone stands.

**THE IMMORTAL**  
Then keep your eyes open.

The gates unlock.

### PHASE ONE — THE COLORED STANDARD

An outer ring contains two equivalent shooting bays and two protected running passages. The goal is readable from any seat: mark the appropriate shield symbols, clear the firing line, then personally retrieve a physical key at the correct end marker.

The official turns the neutral board to face the crowd.

The clue reads: *The second color is never the first gate.*

Dr. Duckhook reads twice, checking the positions of three painted plates. The Immortal does the same from the opposite booth.

The first signal sounds.

Dr. Duckhook raises an approved low-energy marker, aims at the blue plate and releases a soft dye projectile. It breaks against the target backing. The booth shutter closes.

The Immortal, working independently, studies the clue and chooses the orange plate. The Immortal shoots, lowers the marker and places it safely on the secured rack.

The RANGE MARSHAL checks both bays.

**MARSHAL**  
Clear.

Only then do the running gates open.

Dr. Duckhook takes the lower route, moving carefully between padded obstacles. The Immortal chooses a longer path around the outside wall, avoiding a tight corner that would slow a turn.

For a moment Dr. Duckhook seems to be closer to the key station. Then the path bends; the earlier choice of color changes which terminal can be reached from that route.

Dr. Duckhook pauses. One step backward. Then a turn into the second corridor.

The Immortal sees the hesitation through a spectator aperture and changes pace.

Both reach their own secured station. Each contestant retrieves a different key and returns it to a protected tray. Neither key declares a winner.

**PLAY-BY-PLAY**  
They have already made two different decisions—where to shoot and where to run.

**ANALYST**  
And the puzzle is making them live with the first one.

### PHASE TWO — THE WALL THAT REMEMBERS

Groundkeepers shift a series of removable foam barriers on shielded tracks. The machinery locks before the contestants are allowed into the new stage. No ancient wall collapses. None of the old Grove trees is touched.

The next clue appears: *A mark hidden behind another mark may still count.*

Each contestant is given the same code components and the same protected bay.

Dr. Duckhook takes the first opportunity to inspect the marked plates from phase one. The dye has dried against the backing, leaving overlapping circles. Dr. Duckhook counts them, then rejects the original reading. The second symbol was never simply a color. It was a position.

The Immortal approaches the opposite set of plates. A gloved hand traces a line through a transparent shield, not touching the protected target. The Immortal realizes the outer ring was a decoy and turns toward a narrow side station.

A low whistle. A safety check.

Dr. Duckhook fires a marker at the uncovered protected target. The shot stains the lower edge. The Immortal answers with a shot at a different legal plate, then both contestants lower their markers for the range-clear signal.

The gates reopen into two isolated puzzle corridors.

At the middle checkpoint, Dr. Duckhook presses a code tile with one hand and pulls a latch with the other. The combination opens a small cabinet.

Empty.

Dr. Duckhook's face is obscured by the protected mask, but the shoulders tighten. A false answer has cost time.

The Immortal opens a separate cabinet. A short paper strip drops into the enclosed tray, showing three symbols—two familiar and one new.

**ANALYST**  
Dr. Duckhook believed the answer was behind the nearer wall. Now he has to admit the wall was part of the question.

**PLAY-BY-PLAY**  
And The Immortal has found a sequence, not yet a way out.

The Immortal attempts the sequence at a rotary lock.

It does not open.

The Immortal stops, studies the symbols again, and reverses their order.

The lock releases a guarded side gate.

Both contestants are moving. Neither is permitted to borrow a mechanic or a teammate to manipulate the puzzle.

### PHASE THREE — THE SHELTERED MARKER

The final course narrows toward the Grove's sheltered marker terrace. Its permanent stone is protected by a replacement multi-sport plate mounted on removable supports. The plate itself is part of the challenge apparatus, not the original foundation.

The arbiter posts one final instruction:

*The key is not where the color ends. It is where the course began.*

A spectator on the upper terrace shouts the answer. The stewards quiet the crowd. No outside instruction can become an extra contestant.

Dr. Duckhook retrieves the first-stage key, turning it over to examine its old notches. Dr. Duckhook remembers the blue plate and the nearer cabinet. The color was misleading, but the notch pattern is real.

The Immortal checks the key from the outer route. The Immortal has a different pattern, one shaped like the empty spaces between three marks.

A last shooting bay stands in front of the terrace. The contestants use it one at a time. Dr. Duckhook chooses a legal target; a soft dye projectile lands on the corresponding shield. The firing line closes. The Immortal then enters and marks another shield, also clearing the weapon before leaving.

The RANGE MARSHAL sounds the all-clear.

Only then may either enter the last physical puzzle.

Dr. Duckhook reaches the plate and inserts the key, turning it against moderate spring pressure. The plate resists. A second notch is missing. Dr. Duckhook searches for the alternate slot.

The Immortal arrives at the other face of the station, checks the pattern and tries the key.

The mechanism releases a first pin.

Dr. Duckhook answers by pressing a colored tile and turning the original key a quarter-turn. A second pin moves.

The audience cannot see the exact internal mechanism, but it can follow the contest: each entrant is opening one part of a shared final physical objective, and the arbiter has explained what completion means.

A small structural crack travels through the **replaceable plate**, not through the historic Grove terrace. The safety clamp captures the damaged fitting. The arbiter calls the stage complete and blocks further pressure.

Both contestants step away, exhausted.

The commentator stops mid-sentence.

**PLAY-BY-PLAY**  
No declaration yet. The final result must come from the certified record.

### RESULT CERTIFIED

The official league figures arrive at the central desk.

**OFFICIAL**  
Dr. Duckhook: one hundred fifty-one point eight zero. The Immortal: one hundred fifty-two point two zero.

The field waits.

**OFFICIAL (CONT'D)**  
By four tenths of a point, The Immortal advances.

The stadium explodes in noise. The old trees hold their silence.

The screen turns to the verified result: **THE IMMORTAL 152.20 — DR. DUCKHOOK 151.80**.

The preceding physical challenge did not produce the fantasy-football score. It gave the Commonwealth an original, publicly understandable ordeal through which to portray the verified 2025 semifinal.

Dr. Duckhook looks at the protected plate, its cracked fitting now safely locked.

**DR. DUCKHOOK**  
A quarter-turn.

**THE IMMORTAL**  
And we both saw it.

**DR. DUCKHOOK**  
No. We saw different things.

The Immortal offers no reply.

The commentator speaks more quietly.

**ANALYST**  
That is what four tenths sounds like when the island finally stops cheering.

### EXT. GROVE SERVICE WALK — EVENING

A groundskeeper inspects the damaged replacement plate. The original stone beneath it is sound. The piece has two overlapping paint marks and a narrow structural fracture.

A WORKER reaches for the bin.

**GROUNDSKEEPER**  
No. Seal it. Mark the year. Put it with the other originals.

A new plate will be installed and tested before another tournament. The old broken one will remain in the archive, a physical witness to a semifinal won by 0.40.

### EXT. LOSERS' BEACH — NIGHT

The resort is quiet in the early evening. It is one place, not a second island capital. A few eliminated contenders watch a distant screen. The Last Field is visible beyond the upper ridge.

Dr. Duckhook arrives along the shoreward path. Someone offers a chair. The water is warm and the view is fine.

None of it is the championship.

Above the beach, The Immortal's semifinal result is added to the public bracket. The last remaining contest will take place at the fixed Last Field, where the other finalist, ObiWan Jacoby, has already earned entry.

The camera holds on the original Grove entrance before turning toward the final stadium.

FADE OUT.

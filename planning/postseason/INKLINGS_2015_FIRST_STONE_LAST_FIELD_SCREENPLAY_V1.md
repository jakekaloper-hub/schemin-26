# THE FIRST STONE
## Inklings Historical Championship Screenplay — 2015

**Verified result:** Discount Belichick defeats Austin's Hyena's, **211.50–187.00** across the actual 2015 championship scoring period (Weeks 16–17).

### EXT. INNER SEA — THE CAUSEWAY — LATE AFTERNOON

The bridge looks less like an invitation than a challenge.

Wind comes in off the warm water and passes under the rough stone arches. The road above is crowded with carts carrying benches and drinking water. There are no glowing scoreboards, no camera towers, no announcers speaking through invisible devices. A messenger on foot hurries toward the island with a sealed document, dodging spectators who have stopped to watch the Last Field's original stone gates across the bay.

An OLD GATEKEEPER climbs onto a guardstone.

**GATEKEEPER**  
The field is open. Two may enter it. The record will keep one.

The words move through the crowd by repetition, becoming louder the farther they travel.

A group of eight excluded contenders watches from below the western ramp. They are not imprisoned. A few have already arranged seasonal shelters at the resort beach, but none has won the right to walk through a finalist gate.

### EXT. THE LAST FIELD — CHAMPIONSHIP OVAL — DUSK

The arena is older than the spectacle surrounding it. Its original east and west gates face one another across a level stone oval. There is no Champions' Walk yet, only an unfinished record wall with room for names that have never been inscribed.

On the outer ring stands tonight's temporary apparatus: two equivalent, individually constructed bridge lanes, each crossing a shallow training trench over a reinforced catch deck. Nets are slung under the spans and inspected by marshals before any contestant steps above them. The original foundation is neither removed nor used as an anchor for a dangerous load.

An ARBITER examines the ceramic draw tablet.

**ARBITER**  
Bridge Over Void. Three passages. Each contestant builds and tests his own crossing. Equal parts. Equal limits. No hand may work for another.

A STEWARD shows the materials: pre-cut timber, short ropes, bracing pegs, a hand-cranked tension device and a test sled weighted with sealed sand. They have been checked, not assembled.

The two contestants arrive from opposite doors.

AUSTIN'S HYENA'S studies the open lane, fingers pausing above the brace pegs.

DISCOUNT BELICHICK watches the rope sag between the first supports.

Their faces and precise bodies are not assigned here. They are season-specific Commonwealth contest personas until the actual character archive resolves visual lineage.

**AUSTIN'S HYENA'S**  
All that stone. And they ask us to build a bridge.

**DISCOUNT BELICHICK**  
It would be easier if the stone could do the walking.

No one laughs for long.

### PHASE ONE — THE FIRST SPAN

The arbiter lowers a flag. Both contestants begin.

Austin's Hyena's moves quickly, fixing the first pair of planks in a straight line. The work is economical: peg, loop, tighten, advance. A gap forms where the planks meet. Austin's Hyena's steps back and decides that speed has made the brace too narrow.

Discount Belichick works at the opposite station. Instead of spanning the trench immediately, Discount Belichick installs a low triangulated support frame. It appears slower. A spectator in the front row begins counting the pegs Belichick has used.

**SPECTATOR**  
He's building a house under it.

**SECOND SPECTATOR**  
He'd better live there, then.

Austin's Hyena's completes a first crossing and tests the structure by walking personally along the marked section. The catch deck remains beneath the bridge, but the challenge does not become less real for being protected.

Discount Belichick advances only after tensioning the rope by hand. One plank shifts. Belichick stops, kneels, returns it to its bracket and tries again.

The arbiter records the opening positions.

**FIELD HERALD**  
Both have made a passage. Neither has yet made a final bridge.

### PHASE TWO — THE WEIGHT

The first test sled is set at the edge of both lanes. No steward will push it. Each contestant must draw the weight across his own span using a dedicated handcrank.

Austin's Hyena's starts first. The sled moves fast, reaches the midpoint, then begins to pull the decking toward the outer railing. Austin's Hyena's releases tension, studies the drift, and moves a brace from the lower frame to the nearer load point.

Across the court, Discount Belichick gets the sled onto the supported structure. The load distributes more evenly, but the triangular support narrows the path. Every adjustment takes time.

The middle wind rises.

A flag strikes the east railing so hard that the spectators flinch.

**ARBITER**  
The wind is within the permitted boundary. The safety nets remain set.

Austin's Hyena's steps onto the platform to align the brace personally. The timber underfoot shudders. Belichick uses a free hand to test the rope's angle and notices a buried splinter against the cable.

A new choice: leave it and lose time later, or stop now.

Belichick stops.

**AUSTIN'S HYENA'S**  
You always wait for something to go wrong?

**DISCOUNT BELICHICK**  
Only when I've found it.

Belichick removes the splinter and retensions the line. Austin's Hyena's recovers the sled and completes the middle crossing.

The audience sees two competing explanations of how to build: speed and correction; patience and structure. Neither has become the champion yet.

### PHASE THREE — THE LAST LOAD

A final sealed sand weight is added to each sled. The contestants receive equal time for one last adjustment. The load has been tested below the protective limit; its danger is to their designs, not to spectators' lives.

Austin's Hyena's reinforces the span. The frame stands straight, and the original planks are still in place. With a decisive pull, Austin's Hyena's sends the final sled over the first section.

The second joint twists.

Austin's Hyena's takes a short step back, tightens a peg and begins again.

Discount Belichick tests the lower rope, then chooses to shorten the effective span by moving a bracing point inside the approved test bed. The approach seems less elegant than before. Belichick has sacrificed a little of the walkway to improve the load path.

Both contestants work personally, their hands reddened from the ropes.

A brace at the outer rim gives a sharp report.

A small crack opens in the **expendable test rig**. No original stadium stone fractures. The load-stop engages and the loose section settles into a catch tray.

The arbiter immediately inspects the apparatus.

**ARBITER**  
The field is secure. The damaged rig will be kept.

The remaining certified demonstration proceeds after clearance. Each contender brings his own sled to its final lawful stop.

Neither candidate result can be calculated from the demonstration alone. The island competition is a dramatic analogue of a separately recorded fantasy championship.

The arbiter looks toward the result keeper.

### RESULT CERTIFIED — THE FIRST NAME

A sealed league record has arrived from the official scoring authority.

**RESULT KEEPER**  
The 2015 championship, over its recorded final matchup period. Austin's Hyena's: one hundred eighty-seven. Discount Belichick: two hundred eleven point five zero.

A pause. A spectator in the uppermost terrace repeats the second figure to those behind.

**RESULT KEEPER (CONT'D)**  
Discount Belichick is the champion.

The field erupts.

The championship result is not a number produced by the bridge's measured load or construction time. It is the source-verified 2015 outcome, ceremonially declared after the staged encounter.

Austin's Hyena's lowers the eyes toward the old west gate.

**AUSTIN'S HYENA'S**  
There's always another bridge.

**DISCOUNT BELICHICK**  
Not this year.

Discount Belichick remains at the arena's centre. Behind him, the record wall has one blank stone waiting.

### EXT. ORIGINAL RECORD WALL — NIGHT

A mason places a chisel on the first ceremonial plaque. A worker hands him a ledger rather than a list of previous champions.

**MASON**  
Where does it go?

**GATEKEEPER**  
At the beginning.

The mason writes the champion's name beneath the date.

Down at the beach, the light in the Last Field can still be seen from the original seasonal huts. The excluded contend­ers have music, warm food and a view of the celebration. They can enjoy all three. None can enter the record.

### EXT. LAST FIELD OUTER RING — NEXT MORNING

The crowd is gone. Groundkeepers dismantle the temporary bridge apparatus.

A WORKER finds the fractured crosspiece.

**WORKER**  
This one is broken. Shall I put it with the scrap?

The old groundskeeper turns the piece over, examining the line of damage.

**GROUNDSKEEPER**  
Repair the rig. Keep that part.

**WORKER**  
Why?

**GROUNDSKEEPER**  
We'll need to remember how it failed.

The original stone field remains intact. The cracked sacrificial part is dated and stored; the material trace survives while the training ground is restored for safe reuse.

Beyond the gates, the causeway empties. The island closes to public championship use until the next postseason.

FADE OUT.

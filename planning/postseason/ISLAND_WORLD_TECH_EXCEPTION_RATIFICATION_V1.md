## Governing conflict

Current controlling Atlas Phase 1 \`world/atlas/phase-1/MODERNITY_LAYER_CANDIDATE_REGISTRY_V1.md\` preserves social function while replacing prohibited literal modernity with premodern analogues. It explicitly rejects electronic arcade, modern NASCAR stock cars, gasoline-era refueling, and firearms-style paintball absent technology advancement.

**Founder F08** intentionally authorizes a **modern championship broadcast experience**: live cameras, large electronic screens, professional commentary and modern broadcast operations on the Island. Founder F05, F14, F16 and Interview II extend this to sophisticated mixed-era trials including dirt-bike racing, marked-paint tactical contests and complex modular stage mechanisms.

Both cannot be in-world canon across all seasons without a new technology law. Inks and films can present out-of-world footage *today*, but their depiction does not itself prove a diegetic Commonwealth camera or electric generator exists.

## Architecture decision requested from World/Tech authority

**Preferred candidate (not yet ratified): Localized, League-neutral event-technology enclave.**

The championship island, closed outside postseason, may be a narrowly defined licensed exception to the general premodern technology ceiling. It has its own utility plant, safe backup power, isolated wired camera/score network, screen infrastructure and limited modern vehicle/trial permits. The permitted systems do not confer knowledge, ownership or modern technology to the rest of the Commonwealth.

The local exception may allow a 2025 broadcast feed on island and controlled nearby viewing only if there is an approved medium and power source. It does **not** imply satellite television, a global network, motorways, unrestricted modern firearms, modern ICU equipment or ordinary modern electronics in mainland households.

**Alternative candidate:** All electric broadcast layers are out-of-world Memo/website cinema visualization, while the diegetic Island itself uses professional heralds, acoustic announcers, physical boards and mechanical timing. This is technically simplest and protects the premodern law, but only partially satisfies the Founder's explicit choice of modern in-world infrastructure. Accept only if Founder and World/Tech explicitly ratify this compromise.

**Rejected:** silently backdating modern displays, gasoline vehicles or powered shooting equipment into 2015; pretending a technological exception was already approved merely because screenplays mention screens.

## Technical dependencies before promotion

The site topology's \`UTILITY_PLANT\` and \`BROADCAST_HQ\` are candidate nodes, not working power infrastructure. No real power density, diesel fuel, batteries, fiber distances or other technologies are authorized. The engineering authority must resolve:

- A source and storage method compatible with the World and seasonal island use; separate power for life safety and broadcast.
- Physical cable paths beneath the protected causeway, storm protection and 23-node service topology, never rerouting through the Michael Farrell sanctuary.
- Three simultaneously operated first-round camera systems, two dedicated semifinal crews and one Last Field championship centre, with physical staff and safe working space.
- Era-specific stadium renovations and old film/score archives, without inventing a 2015 electronic score feed.
- Legal sporting exception for modern paintball-style markers and motorized/dirt-bike contests, preserving nonlethal combat, spectators' safety and owner-characters personally performing their stages.
- A supported outbound distribution protocol if mainland fans are intended to watch live; otherwise island-only viewing and out-of-world product video.

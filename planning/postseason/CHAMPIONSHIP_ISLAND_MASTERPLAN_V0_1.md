## Foundational dramaturgy
One island, many trials, one permanent final, one champion. It is not a single melee or theme park. Every head-to-head match uses a distinct approved challenge format, physical geography, venue and spectator plan. Defeat removes championship eligibility, not life. The island operates exclusively for postseason competition, with off-season caretaking and maintenance, not routine regular-season events.
The Last Field is the only championship-final site and is physically continuous across the proposed 2015–2026 fictional island history. Losers' Beach is where the nonqualifiers and later the eliminated contenders gather; it remains connected to island broadcasts and ceremony. The Michael Farrell Trophy embodies remembrance; the Champion's Belt signifies present supremacy. No continental sovereignty follows.

## Physical matchup laws
A. Two official opponents and their chosen retinues enter one matched site through separate controlled gates. A neutral registrar issues one round-specific challenge charter: objective, arena, allowed physical means, spectatorship, time/score translation and resolution.
B. Permissible physical encounter forms: formal duel, obstacle passage, race/pursuit, capture/control, siege/tactical field, endurance, skill sport, logistical construction, ceremonial negotiation/wager or relay/team trial. No mandatory lethal combat; no spectacle detached from opponent agency.
C. Every battle/challenge follows two introductions, first move, counterplay, escalating cost, visible turning point, definitive finish, official confirmation, losers' exit. Make narrow football margins correspond to narrow fictional struggles, not fake extra points.
D. Actual provider H2H winner is inviolable; narrative form cannot award a different winner, alter standings, produce permanent principal deaths or invent unsourced real-life memories.
E. Venues must support legible line-of-sight, crowd safety, medical escape, and reasonable conditions for all canonical body types. No magic instant spectator sight without an approved setting-level technology decision.
F. Each round's games are individual but certification of any game updates the shared bracket, other contestants' next preparation, audience/press, social wagers and beach arrivals. Never arbitrarily alter another football result.

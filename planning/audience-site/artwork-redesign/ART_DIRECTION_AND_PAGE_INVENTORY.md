## Visual direction

Original painterly narrative illustration, fine engraved detail, tactile pigments, atmospheric depth, precise readable silhouettes; sophisticated illustrated publishing, no imitation of a named artist or franchise.

Deep evergreen and ink establish the shared publication frame; warm ivory carries reading text, antique gold provides restrained navigational accents. Region-specific slate, ochre, rust and marsh green differentiate environments. Large areas of image-only atmosphere are balanced with plain reading surfaces. Generated typography is prohibited; exact headings, map labels, scores and controls remain code.

## Composition and responsive rules

- Desktop entrance and location scenes: 3:2 generated master, 16:9 desktop crop, focal subject generally at 65% horizontal, 50% vertical, quiet left area for HTML copy. Character compositions use the same 3:2 source / 16:9 desktop crop, with focal subject 55% horizontal and 50% vertical, with all required anatomy and companions inside the safe field.
- Mobile: 4:5 treatment; use an independently art-directed portrait variant when cropping loses required content. A crop that hides DK’s four lower legs or Jake’s separate retriever fails. Move headings beneath art where necessary.
- Treat image, HTML heading, chapter navigation, issue controls and interactive map markers as separate layers. Do not flatten the interface into generated pixels.
- Narrative reading surfaces remain quiet and opaque enough for readable prose. No full-page background competes with body text.
- Default motion is restrained; reduced-motion retains complete information and interaction.

## Canon constraints

- `APPROVED_LINEUP_2026_10_18.json` supplies current presentation references and immutable Character IDs. Each per-character appearance record supplies exact anatomy, palette, props and negative rules.
- New website compositions do not replace historical primary assets or assert per-character primary-source supersession. Existing generation controls remain active.
- DK is one integrated gorilla-centaur with four equine lower limbs, never a gorilla riding a horse. Jake has a green sword, separate golden retriever and no championship belt. TDS has one body and three live serpent heads.
- Place art uses persistent landmarks, region terrain and current-state records. Location proper names with open questions remain unresolved. The atlas is nonmetric topology, not a survey. Its generated artwork is EMPTY central parchment only; all geometry, terrain boundaries, routes and labels are rendered in code. No map terrain is generated.
- The Country Club of Jackson cannot receive a pristine reset without cleanup evidence. Existing destruction, scars and event memories are not erased for prettiness.
- Archival novel text contains Edrin; illustration briefs intentionally depict objects and environments rather than restoring the deprecated POV character.
- Memo titles and source status remain exact. Custom site framing does not create revised official covers or promote source/review editions to released status.

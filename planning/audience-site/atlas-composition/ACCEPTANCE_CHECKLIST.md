## Whole composition

- [ ] Every anchor matches its region and landmark requirements at its projected position.
- [ ] No unapproved coastline, settlement, border, permanent landmark, distance or sovereignty claim is implied.
- [ ] Every route preserves source order, including intermediate stops.
- [ ] Current-state qualifiers survive: contested/unclaimed places are not depicted as newly owned sovereign territory.
- [ ] A named region and recognizable place label remain available without hover.
- [ ] Desktop and phone image/overlay transforms stay aligned through zoom, pan, selection, reload and return.
- [ ] Phone hit areas are usable and labels do not collide; the location list remains available as an equivalent path.
- [ ] Original output, derivatives, prompt, output hashes and source hashes are recorded.
- [ ] Independent geographic and visual review references exact image bytes and records all defects.
- [ ] Founder final visual judgment is separately recorded; no automatic promotion from a passing build.

Known regression examples: Alpine Wager must not lie over low green river valley; Country Club must not lie on an ocean inlet; the mountain-lake bridge must visibly retain its lake/bridge relationship.

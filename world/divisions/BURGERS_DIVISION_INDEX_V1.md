## Identity

Burgers is the League's **north-central hearthroad division**: high routes, upper valleys, market roads, fortified crossings and the northern edge of the central basin. Its identity is built around **hearth, stack, road, strength and public market culture**.

The burger reference is direct but cultural: layered sandwich crests, griddle/hearth guilds, roadside burger houses, public feast tables and stacked heraldic forms. Mountains are not buns; castles are not hamburgers.

## Spatial footprint

Primary physical zones:
- Northern Spine
- Upper Valleys and Foothills
- Central River Basin
- stormward eastern escarpment pockets

The division controls/uses the densest overland hinge between the northern passes and central markets. Its border with Wings is strongest along lower basin roads; its border with Pizza follows eastern ridges and trade corridors.

## Environment fingerprint

**Aerial:** dark ridges, cultivated valleys, market towns, stone bridges, long roads and smoke rising from dense settlements.

**Street:** broad market lanes, stone gutters, timber galleries, inns, smithies, smokehouses and heavy wagon traffic.

**Interior:** thick tables, iron hardware, trophy walls, stacked plates, hearths, brass and dark timber.

**Night:** amber hearthlight, forge glow and lanterned courtyards against cold ridgelines.

**Bad weather:** mud at valley roads, snow at northern passes, hard rain on slate/wood roofs.

## Architecture and materials

Core materials:
- fieldstone
- dark timber
- weathered brick
- iron
- brass
- slate in higher elevations

Settlement types:
- ridge forts
- fortified market towns
- roadside commons
- upland estates/camps
- bridge settlements

Buildings are weighty and practical. The division should feel **constructed around roads and exchange**, not like generic medieval castles scattered in mountains.

## Economy and ordinary life

Likely industries:
- grain and livestock from basin/valley country
- charcoal and ironwork
- timber
- milling and brewing
- cart/wagon repair
- toll-road commerce
- cold-weather storage

Ordinary inhabitants include millers, herders, smiths, brewers, cooks, merchants, drovers, toll clerks, road wardens and quarry workers.

## Burger culture

Canonical expression channels:
- burger/stacked-sandwich heraldry
- public griddle/hearth festivals
- roadside burger-house hospitality
- feast competitions
- guild seals built around layered circular/arched forms
- slang around stacking, pressing, turning and serving

The food itself can appear normally. The world does not treat burgers as mystical objects by default.

## Owner-domain differentiation

Martial highland presence. Current character is the **Arsenal Gorilla Warrior**. The old centaur anatomy is retired. His domain may use Arsenal-red martial banners and heavy stone/iron, but must remain recognizably Burgers territory rather than an isolated jungle kingdom.

### Kevin Zeek / Red Leopards
Stormward escarpment and rain-fed ruin country. This is the division's wetter, more overgrown edge. It prevents Burgers from becoming uniformly alpine.

### Jordan Hollingshead / Slob
Foothill camp and rough hospitality culture. Beer/tailgate/berserker identity sits naturally against roadside commons and upland camp life.

### Ben Whipple / Seven Deadly Chins
Central-basin roadside/populist territory: caravan courts, BBQ yards, heavy tools and public gathering places.

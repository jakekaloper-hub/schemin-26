## Identity

Burgers is a League-cultural jurisdiction whose 2026 home network runs through highland strategy country, D0nkey K0ng's martial uplands, TDS water corridors and Mud Dogs wetland territory.

It is **not one biome and not a sovereign nation**. The division layer follows recognized homes, roads, hospitality traditions and League institutions across the underlying physical world.

Burger identity is direct but cultural: griddle/hearth guild marks, layered sandwich crests, roadside burger houses, heavy public meals and market-feast traditions. Mountains are not buns; fortresses are not hamburgers.

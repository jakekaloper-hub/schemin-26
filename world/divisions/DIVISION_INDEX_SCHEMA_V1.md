# SCHEMIN DIVISION INDEX — SCHEMA V1

**Status:** PROPOSED / WORKING SCHEMA  
**Divisions:** Burgers, Wings, Pizza  
**Governing rule:** Divisions are persistent League identities inside a causally coherent world. They are not literal food biomes.

## 1. Required division record

Each division receives one canonical record with the following sections.

### A. Identity
- Division name
- canonical food referent
- emblem
- heraldic language
- colors/material accents
- motto/phrase if approved
- ceremonial objects
- prohibited misreadings

### B. League function
- member owners/teams by season
- division schedule role
- institutional meaning
- divisional customs
- meeting/ceremonial places
- rivalry traditions

### C. Spatial footprint
- physical zones occupied
- civil jurisdictions intersected
- core region
- border/march regions
- neutral/shared corridors
- major access routes
- waterways
- passes
- travel constraints

### D. Environmental fingerprint
- terrain distribution
- skyline profile
- vegetation
- water presence
- weather tendencies
- road types
- night appearance
- seasonal transformation

### E. Settlement / architecture fingerprint
- settlement pattern
- roofline / massing
- street or road form
- fortification style
- civic buildings
- hospitality spaces
- arena/Encounter spaces
- dominant construction materials
- wear / maintenance / age cues

### F. Economy / material life
- agriculture
- livestock/fishing/foraging
- craft specialization
- transport
- markets
- trade relationships
- recurring tools/objects
- food/hospitality traditions

### G. Food-symbol integration
The division name's food concept is expressed through culture rather than literal terrain.

Possible channels:
- crest/heraldry
- feast tradition
- market guild
- hospitality ritual
- slang
- ceremonial offering
- trophy/plateware
- tavern culture
- festival
- historic League custom

### H. Historical memory
- known verified League history
- divisional reputation
- documented recurring rivalries
- landmark events
- PROPOSED LORE kept separate from facts

### I. Owner-domain relationships
For each current member:
- residence/domain
- degree of territorial control
- home Encounter locations
- neighboring members
- recurring route
- visual continuity anchors

### J. Recurring locations
Every location:
- location ID
- status
- zone
- relationship to division
- first source
- last state
- reuse rules

### K. Visual grammar
A division brief must define:
- wide establishing shot
- medium street/road shot
- interior
- night
- bad weather
- ceremonial scene
- ordinary civilian scene
- battle/Encounter scene

Each should remain identifiable without a division label.

### L. Anti-drift
Explicit:
- forbidden generic-fantasy substitutions
- forbidden mascot literalism
- materials that do not belong
- terrain contradictions
- architecture contradictions
- wrong heraldic symbols
- repeated visual grammar borrowed from another division

## 2. Semantic locks

### Burgers
Food referent: **hamburgers / burgers**.
Do not reduce the territory to giant burger imagery.

### Wings
Food referent: **chicken wings**.
Do not silently reinterpret as generic bird wings, angel wings or dragon wings unless a specific approved heraldic abstraction still clearly derives from chicken-wing identity.

### Pizza
Food referent: **pizza**.
Do not reduce the territory to circular red/yellow motifs or giant pizza landscapes.

## 3. Differentiation test

Each division must pass all of these without labels:

1. **10-second aerial test** — recognizable from macro geography + settlement pattern.
2. **street test** — recognizable from materials + public realm.
3. **interior test** — recognizable from objects + architecture + hospitality.
4. **night test** — recognizable from lighting/infrastructure.
5. **weather test** — recognizable under rain/storm/snow without palette crutches.
6. **artifact test** — heraldry/objects communicate division identity.
7. **civilian test** — ordinary life belongs to the division even when no owner is present.
8. **owner test** — member domains feel related but are not clones.

## 4. Initial design doctrine

The three divisions should feel like different historical-cultural League jurisdictions shaped by different parts of the same world.

Their distinction should arise from a combination of:
- geography;
- transport;
- economy;
- architecture;
- settlement;
- ritual;
- history;
- hospitality;
- League culture.

Food naming is the memorable symbolic layer—not the sole cause of the civilization.

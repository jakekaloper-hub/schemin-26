# LEAGUE INSTITUTION NETWORK V1

The League is a functioning institution embedded in geography rather than a floating editorial device.

## Core nodes
- **League Chamber** — administration, arbitration, public records interface.
- **League Archive** — durable historical records, receipts, courier intake.
- **Compact Neutral Grounds** — neutral major-Encounter infrastructure.
- **Pittsy's Book** — recurring commercial wagering/settlement institution.
- **League Transaction Desk** — waiver/transaction intake within the Chamber network.

## Spatial law
Every institution resolves to a real `location_id`, physical region and approved route context. Neutrality is institutional, not placelessness.

## Information law
Information moves through couriers, notices, ledgers and official result/transaction channels. Archive knowledge is not magical omniscience.

## Future institutions
New institutions enter through World Mutation governance and must declare:
- service population;
- location;
- route access;
- information flow;
- record authority;
- neutrality / jurisdiction.

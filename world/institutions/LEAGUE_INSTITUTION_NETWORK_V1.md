# LEAGUE INSTITUTION NETWORK V1

**Status:** PHASE 7 CANDIDATE

## Institutions

### League Chamber
Location: LOC-LEAGUE-CHAMBER
Role: governance, arbitration, public League procedure.
Access: Basin Market Road + Archive-Chamber Processional + Compact Grounds road.
Neutral: yes.

### League Archive
Location: LOC-LEAGUE-ARCHIVE
Role: records, ledgers, receipts, courier intake.
Access: Archive-Chamber Processional.
Neutral: yes.

### Compact Neutral Grounds
Location: LOC-COMPACT-NEUTRAL-GROUNDS
Role: major neutral Encounters, GOTW/playoff/championship staging.
Access: Compact Grounds road from League Chamber.
Neutral: yes.

### Pittsy's Book
Location: LOC-PITTSYS-BOOK
Role: wagering/settlement/receipt institution embedded in basin market life.
Access: Basin Market Road.
Neutral: operationally shared; proprietor relationship does not make it a division state.

## Information movement
Couriers, notices, ledgers and road/river traffic move League information. The Archive is not magical omniscience.

## Spatial rule
No institution floats outside geography. Each institution must have stable location ID, physical region, access route and service population.

# LEAGUE INSTITUTION NETWORK V1

**Status:** RELEASE CANDIDATE

The League is a functioning institution embedded in the physical world.

## Core network
- League Chamber — governance / arbitration / administration.
- League Archive — records, receipts, historical custody and courier context.
- Compact Neutral Grounds — major neutral Encounter infrastructure.
- Pittsy's Book — recurring physical/commercial bookmaking institution.

## Derived functions
Transaction/waiver administration and notice/courier functions may be represented through the Chamber/Archive network without creating a new named city or institution until explicit promotion.

## Spatial law
Every institution must resolve to an active LOC-* context and active route network. No institution floats outside the Atlas.

## Governing laws

### 1. World-before-image
**The world determines the image. The image does not determine the world.**

### 2. Home-team geography
Ordinary regular-season/divisional Encounters default to the verified home team's established environment. The division label alone does not choose scenery.

### 3. Major-event neutrality
GOTWs, playoffs and championships default to approved neutral Schemin locations. Neutral means institutionally neutral, never geographically undefined.

### 4. Travel accountability
A participant must have an approved route/path to the venue. The reader need not be told every route fact. Production must know it.

### 5. Morphology-neutral personhood
Sapient nonhuman and singular inhabitants are persons under the civilization/Compact framework rather than animals or legal objects.

### 6. Population ontology
**Peopled kinds:** ObiWan, D0nkey K0ng, Red Leopards, Slob, Mud Dogs, Seven Deadly Chins.

**Singular beings:** Byars/Belt Keeper, Duckhook, El Niño, The LLC.

**Shared cross-divisional coexistence:** TDS + Chili.

### 7. El Niño manifestation
El Niño may be embodied, the explicitly resolved natural storm/disaster itself, or deliberately ambiguous. Not every storm is El Niño.

### 8. Division truth
2026:
- Burgers — ObiWan, D0nkey K0ng, TDS, Mud Dogs.
- Wings — Red Leopards, Slob, Chili Cheesers, Duckhook.
- Pizza — LLC, HMB, El Niño, Seven Deadly Chins.

Divisions are nonexclusive League-cultural/home-venue overlays, not species or residential borders.

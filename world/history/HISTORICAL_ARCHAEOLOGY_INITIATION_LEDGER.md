## Identity Resolution Rule
OWNER is persistent.
TEAM NAME is temporal.
CHARACTER is persistent unless explicit canon change exists.
Historical fiction may not project a 2026 team name backward into a season where evidence shows a different name.

## Historical Significance Filter
Candidate events for Chronicle treatment:
- championships and title-game losses;
- semifinal/playoff reversals;
- repeated owner-v-owner postseason meetings;
- record scoring performances;
- catastrophic collapses/comebacks;
- consequential trades/keeper decisions;
- sustained streaks;
- persistent matchup asymmetries;
- team rename eras;
- events that explain a current 2026 relationship, reputation, object or location.

Not every recovered game becomes lore.

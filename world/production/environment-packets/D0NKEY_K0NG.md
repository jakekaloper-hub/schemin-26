# ENVIRONMENT PACKET — D0NKEY K0NG / ARSENAL GORILLA WARRIOR

**Primary location:** D0nkey K0ng Highland Stronghold
**Secondary recurring site:** Arsenal Barbershop
**Physical zone:** Upper Valleys / Northern approach
**Division:** Burgers
**Ontology:** PEOPLED_KIND — gorilla-bodied people exist.

## Terrain / skyline
Highland shelves, strong road approaches, fortified upland settlement, distant passes.

## Architecture / materials
Heavy stone, timber, iron, Arsenal-red martial accents, training court, road-market structures.

## Roads / civic life
Highland Stronghold Road connects to Arsenal Barbershop and wider Hearthroad network. The barbershop should read as a real commercial shop, not a one-joke set.

## Inhabitants
Gorilla-bodied civilians/guards/workers may appear. They must not all wear Arsenal-red armor or carry Wilson’s signature axe/tankard.

## Forbidden drift
- NEVER centaur/equine anatomy.
- Do not turn the entire culture into a football mascot camp.

# ENVIRONMENT PACKET — OBIWAN JACOBY / TRADE JEDI

**Status:** PRODUCTION-READY ENVIRONMENT / CHARACTER REFERENCE STILL SEPARATE
**Primary location:** Trade Jedi Mountain Base
**Physical zone:** Upper Valleys and Foothills
**Division:** Burgers
**Ontology:** PEOPLED_KIND — Jedi order/community exists here; ObiWan coexists with other Jedi and non-Jedi humans.

## Terrain / skyline
High ridges, mountain basin, layered foothills, strategic overlooks, lake/bridge access, distant Northern Spine.

## Architecture / materials
Fieldstone, timber, earth-tone plaster, practical mountain compounds, map/strategy rooms, communal Jedi training/assembly space. Avoid generic temple-city repetition.

## Roads / water
Eastern Ascent → Bridge at the Mountain Lake → Upper Basin Connector.
Mountain streams/lake relationship must be visible enough to explain access.

## Ordinary inhabitants
Other Jedi plus non-Jedi human mountain/valley residents. Background Jedi should vary in age, clothing and role; they are not clones of ObiWan.

## Economy / civic life
Mountain trade, road services, orchards/pasture where terrain permits, courier traffic, strategic/League travel.

## Lighting / weather
Cool highland air, morning mist, variable mountain weather, warm interior firelight.

## Recurring objects
Maps, ledgers, road markers, training spaces. ObiWan-specific green energy sword and golden retriever remain principal-only.

## Forbidden drift
- NO championship belt.
- Do not make ObiWan the only Jedi.
- Do not turn all residents into Jedi.
- Do not copy ObiWan’s exact wardrobe/face/green sword onto civilians.
- Do not isolate the domain from roads.

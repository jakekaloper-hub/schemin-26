# ENVIRONMENT PACKET — MUD DOGS / SWAMP-BORN MENACE

**Primary location:** Mud Dogs Swamp
**Physical zone:** Southern Wetlands and Delta
**Division:** Burgers
**Ontology:** PEOPLED_KIND — sapient swamp canine/wolf people exist.

## Terrain
Mud channels, slow water, raised ground, reeds/cypress-like wetland, causeways, flood marks.

## Architecture
Raised timber structures, wetland docks, reinforced walkways, practical elevated storage.

## Roads
Falls Path / Delta Causeway network. Outsiders can reach the swamp without implying adjacency to every other domain.

## Inhabitants
Sapient swamp canine/wolf inhabitants in ordinary roles. Do not make all of them giant rabid battle beasts.

## Forbidden drift
- not a pet-dog village.
- do not make motorcycle/bike imagery permanent infrastructure.

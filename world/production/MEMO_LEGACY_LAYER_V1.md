**Purpose:** convert published Week 1–3 visual/story history into reusable world assets without literalizing every gag.

## Classification vocabulary

- **PERMANENT_WORLD_ASSET** — durable part of the world.
- **RECURRING_LOCATION** — reusable named place with continuity.
- **HISTORICAL_EVENT_SITE** — real place whose importance comes from a past matchup/event.
- **RECURRING_INSTITUTION** — League/civic function that can recur.
- **RECURRING_OBJECT** — persistent object or object class.
- **CURRENT_WORLD_STATE** — active consequence entering future scenes.
- **VISUAL_METAPHOR_ONLY** — not literal geography.
- **SUPERSEDED_VISUAL_CANON** — historically published but not current production authority.

## Cross-week world grammar

The canonical plate must visibly contain ordinary civilization between heroic domains:

- market towns;
- courier traffic;
- road houses and inns;
- food halls;
- stables;
- golf/estate infrastructure;
- quays and warehouses;
- record halls;
- public notice boards;
- bookmaking/settlement desks;
- racegrounds;
- bridges and causeways;
- service counters;
- caravan courts;
- mixed settlements.

## Anti-literalization rule

The world remembers events without becoming a permanent joke set.

Never promote these by repetition alone:
- Sonic branding;
- humiliation poses/props;
- haircut imagery as TDS culture;
- chili stains as Duckhook-domain identity;
- one destroyed office as the definition of LLC civilization;
- a raceway as the entirety of Seven Chins’ domain.

**Purpose:** mine published Week 1–3 material for already-created world language before any new atlas art invents replacements.

## Source discipline

Published memos are historical evidence. They can establish:
- recurring environments;
- recurring objects;
- institutions;
- visual/material language;
- consequences;
- story-memory attached to places.

They do **not** automatically establish:
- exact coordinates;
- sovereign borders;
- travel time;
- magical mechanics;
- governmental status;
- permanent place names unless separately promoted.

Current World Engine V1.1 remains controlling geography.

# CROSS-WEEK VISUAL GRAMMAR TO PRESERVE

## The world has ordinary life
Published memos already imply:
- bars / shops;
- stables;
- market roads;
- golf estates;
- boardrooms;
- bookmaking / settlement desks;
- record halls;
- courier/delivery culture;
- racegrounds;
- bridges;
- roadside halls;
- service counters.

Therefore the atlas cannot be only:
castle + swamp + mountain + ruin.

It needs:
**civilian infrastructure between the hero locations.**

## Recurring material contrasts
- Trade Jedi: mountain stone / timber / strategic overlook.
- D0nkey K0ng: Arsenal-red martial highland culture + civic barbershop.
- TDS: dense wet stone / waterfalls / temple.
- Mud Dogs: mud channels / raised ground / wetland wood.
- Red Leopards: storm-wet ruins / predator banners / dark vegetation.
- Slob: rough road camp / tailgate yard / training ground.
- Chili: stable / pit smoke / weathered western structures.
- Duckhook: manicured estate / golf grounds / water hazards.
- LLC: stone/mercantile city / ledgers / market / executive interiors.
- Belt Keeper: black-cliff / rune keep / championship hall.
- El Niño: exposed storm bowl / coast / weather infrastructure.
- Seven Chins: road commons / BBQ yard / caravan court.

## Institutional connective tissue
Use visual evidence of:
- public roads;
- toll/courier traffic;
- notice boards;
- ledgers;
- archives;
- market courts;
- arbiter / League sites;
- inns / food halls.

This creates one society rather than twelve mascot islands.

# WHAT MUST NOT BE COPIED LITERALLY

Do not turn one-off matchup comedy into permanent world architecture:
- giant Sonic branding;
- humiliation props as monuments;
- permanent bikes/motorcycles as defining Mud Dogs infrastructure;
- haircut imagery as TDS civilization;
- chili stains as Country Club’s primary identity;
- corporate destruction as the entire LLC city;
- a raceway as Seven Chins’ whole culture.

The world remembers the events without becoming trapped inside them.

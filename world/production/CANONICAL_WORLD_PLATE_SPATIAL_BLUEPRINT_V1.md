The illustrator may change perspective, visual scale and camera emphasis. The illustrator may not silently move a governed location to another region or invent a replacement route.

## Physical organization

- **Northern Spine** — far north / high background; source of high passes and headwaters.
- **Upper Valleys and Foothills** — north-central transition; dense owner-domain concentration and upland roads.
- **Central River Basin** — civic/trade heart; League Chamber, Archive, Neutral Grounds, Road Commons and major road convergence.
- **Western Tablelands** — dry/open west; Chili frontier and TDS–Chili Shared March.
- **Eastern Forest and Storm Coast** — wet/storm-exposed east; Red, Belt Keeper and El Niño systems.
- **Southern Wetlands and Delta** — low south; Mud Dogs, Duckhook and TDS wet-country anchors.
- **Inner Sea Littoral** — southeast/lower edge; LLC city/port and maritime trade.

## Spatial answers required before render

Every visible major location must have answers to:

1. **Why is it here?**  
   Derived from physical zone, domain function and existing Memo/World evidence.

2. **What reaches it?**  
   At least one governed road, waterway, pass, causeway or port route.

3. **What water affects it?**  
   Mountain headwater, basin river, wetland channel, storm coast, or inner-sea system.

4. **What protects or constrains it?**  
   Ridge, cliff, floodplain, swamp, open tableland, forest grade, port wall, etc.

5. **What does it connect to?**  
   Another owner domain, League institution, market corridor, neutral venue, or shared settlement.

Trade Jedi Mountain Base is not an isolated one-Jedi compound.

The environment must plausibly support:
- ObiWan;
- other Jedi;
- non-Jedi human mountain/valley residents;
- communal training / strategy space;
- roads linking the mountain society to the wider world.

Background Jedi must not clone ObiWan’s exact face, clothing, green energy sword, golden retriever relationship or personal identity.

## Executive ruling

The concept plate is **KEEP AS COMPOSITION BENCHMARK / REJECT AS CANONICAL PLATE**.

Its strongest contribution is the large-scale visual hierarchy: one world, deep atmospheric geography, a central basin with shared institutions, differentiated outer regions, visible roads/waterways, and a restrained lower registry. Its main defects are canon execution: model-generated typography, unsupported labels, incomplete route accountability, uncontrolled character reconstruction, and division/icon drift.

## Specific defects

### Typography contamination
The image model generated readable titles, labels and legend content. Even where attractive, this violates the typography firewall because misspellings, unsupported wording and renamed geography can enter the page.

**Fix:** image-generation passes reserve blank label/cartouche areas. Exact text is composited later.

### Route accountability
The concept shows many plausible roads, but their correspondence to `world/data/routes.json` is not provable.

**Fix:** the next plate must derive visible trunk roads from the Spatial Blueprint and route graph.

### Domain-to-world accountability
Several domain landmarks look good individually but read as self-contained fantasy islands.

**Fix:** every owner anchor must visibly connect to:
- a road/water route;
- a nearby settlement or civic function;
- its physical region;
- at least one wider-world relationship.

### Inhabitant ontology
The concept still reads primarily as twelve unusual principals.

**Fix:** Pass 3 must show selective ordinary populations:
- other Jedi with ObiWan;
- gorilla-bodied inhabitants around D0nkey K0ng;
- leopard-bodied inhabitants around Red Leopards;
- sapient swamp-canine inhabitants around Mud Dogs;
- ordinary humans around Slob and Seven Chins;
while keeping Belt Keeper, Duckhook, El Niño and LLC singular.

### Division presentation
The lower division registry works as orientation, but the world itself does not yet sufficiently express the divisions as nonexclusive cultural/home networks.

**Fix:** culture lives in inns, feast halls, markets, symbols, materials and League markers rather than borders.

## Closer ruling

**PHASE 1: PASS WITH REQUIRED REPLACEMENTS.**

The concept plate remains the composition benchmark. It has zero authority over canon facts, proper nouns, route topology or character appearance.

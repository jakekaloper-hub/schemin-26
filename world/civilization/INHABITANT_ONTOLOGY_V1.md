# INHABITANT ONTOLOGY V1

**Status:** COMMISSIONER-LOCKED FRAMEWORK / WORLD ENGINE V1.1  
**Scope:** What kinds of persons inhabit Schemin, how the Twelve relate to populations, and how civilization treats bodily difference.

## 1. Core rule: person before morphology

The Compact and the ordinary legal systems around it recognize **persons**, not one privileged body plan.

A recognized person may, subject to local law:
- own or inherit land;
- enter contracts and debts;
- hold institutional standing;
- travel under safe-conduct;
- bring disputes before an arbiter;
- command retainers or employ labor;
- maintain a household/domain;
- participate in sanctioned Encounters.

The Twelve are recognized persons by established standing. A nonhuman body does not make an inhabitant an animal or legal object.

## 2. Ontology classes

### A. PEOPLED_KIND
The character belongs to a broader kind/culture whose ordinary members exist in the world and may be visible in home settlements.

Locked members:
- Jake Kaloper / ObiWan Jacoby / Trade Jedi — **Jedi are an established peopled order/community in the world; ObiWan coexists with other Jedi.** His home society may include Jedi and non-Jedi humans. ObiWan remains individually distinct in face, wardrobe, green energy sword, golden-retriever relationship and personal history.
- Wilson Look / D0nkey K0ng / Arsenal Gorilla Warrior — gorilla-bodied people exist; Wilson is not a unique species.
- Kevin Zeek / Red Leopards / Predator Board — leopard-bodied people exist.
- Jordan Hollingshead / Slob on my Dobb / Frat-Bro Berserker — his home society contains people of his own broad human/berserker kind; horned gear does not by itself establish a separate biological species.
- Bobby Mitchell / Mud Dogs / Swamp-Born Menace — sapient swamp canine/wolf people exist.
- Ben Whipple / Seven Deadly Chins / People's Champ — human road/working communities exist.

A PEOPLED_KIND is not permission to duplicate the owner's costume, props, face, scars or signature objects onto civilians.

### B. SINGULAR_BEING
No population of the character's exact ontological kind is implied.

Locked members:
- Austin Byars / Belt Keeper.
- Zach Wilson / Dr. Duckhook.
- Manning Welty / El Niño / Weather System.
- David Babb / The LLC / Hostile Takeover.

These characters may be famous, politically normalized or institutionally accommodated without inventing a hidden race of Belt Keepers, duck-people, Weather Systems or LLC beings.

### C. SHARED_COHABITATION
Population origin remains intentionally unresolved, but coexistence is locked.

- Phillip Pitts / Three Dreaded Snake.
- Brandon Pryor / Chili Cheesers.

TDS and Chili share social/geographic space despite belonging to different League divisions. Their shared habitat is evidence that divisions do not segregate species, cultures or residence.

## 3. Architecture and ordinary life

### Peopled-kind domains
Home settlements should visibly support the inhabitants who live there:
- door/ceiling/road scale;
- furniture and work surfaces;
- food/storage practices;
- tools;
- sleeping arrangements;
- transport;
- public gathering space.

This should emerge naturally rather than through exposition.

### Singular-being domains
Do not design an entire civilization around the singular's anatomy.

Accommodation may be:
- personal;
- institutional;
- retrofitted;
- ceremonial;
- famous enough to be routine.

Duckhook can enter an ordinary civic building because the world has legal/social experience with nonstandard bodies and because Duckhook himself is a known singular person—not because an unseen ancient duck civilization must exist.

## 4. Social reaction rule

Uniqueness does not equal constant astonishment.

Use reaction according to:
- fame;
- local familiarity;
- political context;
- danger;
- rarity;
- prior contact.

A villager in a remote place may react to Duckhook. A League registrar who has processed Duckhook for years should not.

## 5. El Niño manifestation law

El Niño is a **SINGULAR_BEING** with two approved presentation modes:

### EMBODIED
The established humanoid storm/water/cloud/lightning character appears as a person.

### ATMOSPHERIC_MANIFESTATION
A natural storm, flood-driving weather system or disaster may itself be El Niño when the scene/world-state record explicitly marks the event as an El Niño manifestation.

Rules:
- not every storm is El Niño;
- ordinary weather still exists;
- weather follows the physical world's climate/watershed rules;
- manifestation does not permit teleporting storms or arbitrary rescue;
- origin, ultimate metaphysics and full limits remain unresolved unless later canonized.

A third story state, **AMBIGUOUS**, may be used when inhabitants dispute whether a storm was El Niño; narration must not convert ambiguity into fact without canon authority.

## 6. Division firewall

League divisions are neither species categories nor ethnic homelands.

A character may:
- live beside another division's inhabitants;
- travel through another division;
- share a settlement across division affiliation;
- host a matchup in a domain culturally attached to the host's division.

TDS + Chili is the controlling example.

## 7. Character Control Plane precedence

This ontology answers population/civilization questions. It does **not** redesign principal bodies.

Current Character Control Plane remains authoritative. Therefore Wilson Look is the current **Arsenal Gorilla Warrior**; older centaur material is historical/superseded visual canon and cannot be revived by ontology prose.

## 8. Unknowns preserved

V1 does not yet assert:
- biological origins of nonhuman kinds;
- interbreeding rules;
- creation myths as objective fact;
- whether singular beings can reproduce;
- whether El Niño is divine, magical, natural, or a category unknown to scholars;
- whether TDS has a larger same-kind population.

Those questions remain available for later worldbuilding.

# CIVILIZATION DENSITY ENGINE V1

The engine prevents the world from reading as twelve isolated set pieces.

## Causal placement law
A settlement must be justified by one or more spatial functions:
RIVER_CROSSING, PORT, MOUNTAIN_PASS, ROAD_JUNCTION, HIGH_GROUND, FERTILE_BASIN, RESOURCE_SOURCE, ESTATE_ACCESS, WETLAND_CAUSEWAY, LEAGUE_INSTITUTION, DEFENSIBLE_RIDGE, COASTAL_TRADE.

## Minimum ordinary-life packet
Every owner domain must support:
- nearby habitation;
- ordinary labor;
- food/support supply;
- transport/courier function;
- local trade;
- one or more public/shared spaces appropriate to terrain.

These support settlements are deliberately `INFERRED` until exact names, sizes or plans are separately approved.

## Principal protection
- ObiWan's society includes other Jedi and non-Jedi humans.
- Singular beings do not imply singular-being species.
- Peopled kinds may have ordinary same-kind inhabitants without cloning the principal's exact identity.
- TDS/Chili wider biology remains unresolved; shared residence is enough.

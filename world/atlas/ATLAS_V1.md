# ATLAS V1 — SCHEMIN WORLD ENGINE

**Status:** V1.1 RELEASE CANDIDATE / DATA-DRIVEN ATLAS  
**Coordinate space:** Schemin-local-v1, abstract and non-metric

## Purpose

Atlas V1 is a family of views over one world model. The map must govern story logistics without forcing the reader to absorb a gazetteer.

## Layers

1. Physical Atlas — seven causal physical zones.
2. Civil / Institutional Atlas — settlements, Archive, League Chamber, neutral grounds.
3. Division Atlas — Burgers / Wings / Pizza League overlay.
4. Owner Domain Atlas — twelve home environments.
5. Roads & Travel Atlas — approved paths and travel classes.
6. Encounter Atlas — home and neutral venues.
7. World-State Atlas — scars, floods, repairs, contamination and memory.
8. Weather Atlas — regional propagation / El Niño manifestation state.
9. Horizon/Visibility Atlas — composition continuity.

## Home-matchup geography

Ordinary matchup location starts with verified home team.

The home-team location anchors:
- terrain;
- local kind/population;
- architecture;
- climate;
- material culture;
- arrival route;
- entering state.

A designer may choose a sub-location inside that domain; they may not substitute an unrelated cinematic backdrop.

## Rendering doctrine

Rendering reads world data and cannot mutate it.

A renderer may:
- stylize;
- label;
- hide/show layers;
- alter presentation scale;
- choose camera framing.

It may not:
- move a location to another physical zone;
- rewrite a division;
- change who is home;
- erase state consequences;
- convert a singular being into a species;
- convert a peopled kind into a unique monster;
- convert generated scenery into canon.

## Coordinate doctrine

Abstract positions exist for topology and QA only.

They are not literal Earth coordinates, exact mileage or reader-facing promises.

# ROUTE, TRAVEL, HORIZON & WEATHER MODEL V1

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE CANON / WORLD ENGINE V1

## Travel classes

- **LOCAL:** within a settlement/domain; ordinary scene transition.
- **REGIONAL:** adjacent regions or same division; meaningful but routine journey.
- **CROSS_DIVISION:** crosses division/cultural boundary; should be visible in narrative or page transition when material.
- **EXPEDITION:** difficult mountain/wetland/seasonal passage; cannot be treated as instant travel.

Travel classes are narrative continuity bands, not exact mileage.

## Route law

A recurring location must either:
1. connect to an established route; or
2. be explicitly isolated and require specialized access.

No weekly page may teleport a recurring location to a different biome to improve composition.

## Horizon law

Visual briefs record camera direction and stable topography. Distant landmarks are optional unless their previously established visibility makes them compulsory.

## Weather law

Large weather events propagate through regions. El Niño character staging can intensify a scene, but the surrounding climate cannot be ignored. A major coastal storm may close routes, raise water and affect adjacent inland scenes.

## Current network

The machine-readable route set links owner domains and Week 3 recurring locations through mountain, basin, wetland, frontier and coastal corridors.

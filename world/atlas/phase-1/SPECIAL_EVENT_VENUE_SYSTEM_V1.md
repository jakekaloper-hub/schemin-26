# SPECIAL EVENT VENUE SYSTEM V1

**Status:** PHASE 1 VENUE POLICY ADDENDUM
**Purpose:** Separate ordinary home geography from rare neutral spectacle, playoffs and a championship-only reveal.

## Venue ladder

### TIER 0 — OWNER HOME / DOMAIN
Default ordinary regular-season and divisional encounter.

Authority remains the verified home team.

### TIER 1 — STORY-NEUTRAL LOCAL SITE
A neutral or shared venue used when the matchup story has a justified local reason.

Examples:
- club estate;
- market;
- fairground;
- road stop;
- wager hall.

Requires recorded venue override.

### TIER 2 — RARE NEUTRAL
Used sparingly for:
- rivalry escalation;
- special wager;
- high-comedy matchup;
- unusually public event;
- special editorial concept.

Target frequency: rare enough to retain novelty.

### TIER 3 — GOTW NEUTRAL
Major Game of the Week venue.
Must:
- be reachable;
- feel larger than a normal owner-domain scene;
- avoid favoring either participant culturally;
- support clear event identity.

### TIER 4 — PLAYOFF NEUTRAL
Postseason-capable sites.
Use neutral institutional or spectacular venues.
Playoff sites may recur across seasons but should not become ordinary-week defaults.

### TIER 5 — CHAMPIONSHIP ONLY
Reserved for the championship match and no ordinary matchup.

The site must feel like a destination the entire season has earned.

# Championship-only reservation

## CAND-CHAMP-LAST-FIELD
### Working name
**The Last Field**

### Status
FUTURE_UNLOCK_CANDIDATE

### Event class
CHAMPIONSHIP_ONLY

### Likely physical placement
Central River Basin neutral network, near major converging routes but outside any owner domain.

### Why Central Basin
- strongest all-world accessibility;
- existing League institutional gravity;
- avoids granting a home-zone advantage;
- supports large public arrival;
- can connect to Archive/Chamber/Compact without being the same site.

### Visual thesis
A monumental open field sunk into an ancient stone amphitheater at a river confluence. Twelve approach roads/standards may exist as League symbolism, but only the two finalists occupy the field.

### Ceremonial elements
- neutral arrival procession;
- empty Belt dais before the result;
- record stone / ledger alcove;
- no current champion statue;
- no prewritten victor imagery;
- weather and season state remain real.

### Unlock rule
The site may be discussed in planning but cannot become a released illustrated venue until the actual 2026 championship encounter.

### Anti-drift
Do not:
- stage GOTWs here;
- give it one finalist's architecture;
- prewrite a coronation;
- depict a winner before verified outcome.

# Neutrality test

Before using any rare/major venue, score:

1. owner control — must be absent or explicitly waived;
2. Division dominance — should not structurally favor one side;
3. route reachability;
4. active world-state hazards;
5. event-class eligibility;
6. prior historical memory;
7. visual distinction from owner homes.

Failure on 1–5 blocks use.

## Reuse budget

Rare venues should not become weekly shortcuts.

Recommended production budget:
- ordinary weeks: mostly owner domains/sub-locations;
- GOTW: neutral allowed when justified;
- rare-neutral: occasional;
- playoffs: elevated neutral frequency;
- championship: The Last Field candidate only after unlock gate.

**Venue doctrine:** rarity creates meaning.

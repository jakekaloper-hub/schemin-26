# DIVISION CULTURAL OVERLAY BIBLE V1

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE CANON ADDENDUM / PHASE 1
**Rule:** Burgers, Wings and Pizza are League-cultural overlays, not sovereign countries, species borders or single biomes.

## Shared principle

Division identity should be recognizable through hospitality, food, signage/heraldry, recurring institutions, roadside commerce, rituals, jokes, material culture and social behavior.

It should not require burger-shaped mountains, flying chicken kingdoms or pizza cities.

# BURGERS DIVISION

## Cultural thesis
**Hearty road culture, cattle/dairy economics, griddle houses, stacked meals, late-night stops and a slightly industrial appetite.**

The Division crosses mountain, valley, basin and wetland geography. Its cultural coherence comes from roads, food houses, livestock trade, public meals and League hospitality.

## Recurring cultural grammar
- griddle and hearth guilds;
- cattle markets;
- dairies/creameries;
- butcher shops;
- road diners;
- stack-house meal halls;
- fry stands;
- tailgate grills;
- cold-store/meat houses;
- roadside coach windows.

## Brand-inspired but nonliteral motifs
Real-world McDonald's, In-N-Out and similar burger chains may inspire recognizable roadside architecture, limited menus, house colors/signs, queue/window service and cult-like regional loyalty.

Do not place literal modern corporations into canon without a Commissioner technology/branding override.

## Candidate cultural sites
- **Golden Hearth Roadhouse** — fast-service burger-house analogue.
- **West Range Double-Stack** — western cult-favorite burger stop.
- **Creamery Mile** — dairy/ice-cream-style market road.
- **Black Apron Butcher Ward** — darker meat-processing/butcher district.
- **Cattle Crown Stockyards** — livestock market/fairground.
- **Midnight Griddle** — all-night diner analogue.

## Dark-side texture
Burger culture may also show slaughterhouse silhouettes, smoke, cold rooms, hooks and butcher blocks without becoming horror by default.

## Story use
Celebration, humiliation meals, road trips, wagers, greasy recovery scenes, dark industrial encounters and livestock chaos.

# WINGS DIVISION

## Cultural thesis
**Southern hospitality with heat, sauce, smoke, roadside chicken culture, festivals, clubs and unpredictable social energy.**

Wings should feel social and combustible rather than homogeneous.

## Recurring cultural grammar
- chicken-on-a-stick vendors;
- fry houses;
- sauce bars;
- smoke pits;
- poultry farms;
- fairgrounds;
- bar-and-grill roadhouses;
- club estates;
- tailgate cook sites;
- festival streets.

## Oxford / Mississippi influence
Commissioner-approved inspiration includes Oxford-style chicken-on-a-stick culture: late-night carts, college-town roads, game-week crowds, simple food becoming ritual, and rivalry sharing the same street as hospitality.

## Brand-inspired but nonliteral motifs
Popeyes-like fried-chicken energy may inform bold roadside fry houses, spice identity, queues, biscuits/sides and strong signage without importing a literal modern chain.

## Candidate cultural sites
- **Stickbird Row** — late-night chicken-on-a-stick street.
- **The Red Fryhouse** — fried-chicken outpost.
- **Sauce Exchange** — competitive sauce bazaar.
- **Rooster Fairgrounds** — livestock/festival/tailgate venue.
- **Smokehouse Turn** — roadhouse corridor.
- **Magnolia Athletic Club** — Atlanta Athletic Club-inspired elite sporting estate, secondary-world translated.

## Story use
Food dares, late-night arguments, club embarrassment, festival chaos, road rivalry and tailgate diplomacy.

# PIZZA DIVISION

## Cultural thesis
**Assembly, delivery, public exchange, urban commerce, shared ovens and the logistics of getting the right thing to the right place before it gets cold.**

Pizza spans littoral commerce, storm country and basin roads.

## Recurring cultural grammar
- communal ovens;
- pie halls;
- delivery/courier guilds;
- tile/box motifs;
- public plazas;
- warehouse districts;
- late-night takeout windows;
- market tables;
- traffic-heavy approach roads;
- shared meals.

## Brand-inspired but nonliteral motifs
Pizza Hut, Domino's and Little Caesars may inspire recognizable roof silhouettes, delivery urgency, bargain/quantity rivalry, box-stack warehouses and chain-house competition.

Do not make literal modern franchises default canon.

## Candidate cultural sites
- **Red Tile Pie Hall** — roofline/restaurant analogue.
- **Twin-Tile Courier Guild** — delivery-network analogue.
- **Three-Oven Market** — quantity/value pizza-house analogue.
- **Boxstack Wharf** — warehouse/delivery quarter.
- **Midnight Pie Window** — late-night takeaway.
- **Ovenmasters' Square** — public communal-oven plaza.

## Story use
Delivery disasters, marketplace rivalries, courier chases, late-night negotiations, public celebrations and corporate satire.

# Cross-Division rules

## Mixed districts are encouraged
Because Divisions are overlays, a city may contain Burgers, Wings and Pizza institutions beside ordinary non-League life.

## Rivalry without sovereignty
A Burgers/Wings rivalry may appear as competing food streets, club banners, supporter houses and wager customs—not a national border war unless separately justified.

## Expansion rule
A Division-flavored site receives a permanent map identity only if:
1. it has a physical-zone placement;
2. it has a non-gimmick reason to exist;
3. it offers reusable story value;
4. route access is valid;
5. it does not overwrite an owner domain;
6. provenance and status are recorded.

**Recognition target:** a scene should feel Burgers, Wings or Pizza through lived culture before a label appears.

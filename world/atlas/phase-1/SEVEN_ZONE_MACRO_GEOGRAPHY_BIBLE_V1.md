## Design law

The seven zones are causal geography. They exist because of terrain, water, climate, movement and settlement economics—not because of fantasy team identities.

Division culture and new locations must fit these zones rather than reverse-engineering terrain from a joke.

## 1. NORTHERN SPINE

### Core identity
High mountains, headwaters, narrow passes, exposed ridges and defensible valleys.

### Climate / ecology
- snow and hard winter at elevation;
- alpine meadows below ridgelines;
- coniferous and mixed mountain timber;
- sudden weather changes;
- spring runoff drives downstream water.

### Settlement pattern
- pass towns;
- quarry/mining hamlets;
- monasteries/retreats;
- fortified inns;
- isolated estates;
- summit shrines and way stations.

### Materials
Stone, dark timber, ironwork, slate, heavy wool, rope, leather.

### Ambient industries
Mining, metalworking, quarrying, timber, mountain pasture, guiding, pack transport.

### Travel feel
Vertical, expensive, exposed and memorable. Roads matter because alternatives are limited.

### Story functions
Ordeal, isolation, neutral high-stakes meetings, survival, elevation wagers and pursuit through constrained passes.

### Candidate-compatible modernity analogues
Alpine athletic club, mountain training grounds, recovery sanatorium, dye-target sporting range, winter fair, observatory/signal tower.

### Avoid
Generic snow kingdom; permanent instant travel; theme-park mountains.

## 2. UPPER VALLEYS & FOOTHILLS

### Core identity
Rolling uplands, pasture, orchards, mixed forest, estate roads and defensible ridges.

### Climate / ecology
Temperate, seasonal rain, productive pasture, creek-fed valleys.

### Settlement pattern
Market towns, estate villages, college/academy towns, crossroads, training grounds, dairy and livestock communities.

### Materials
Fieldstone, timber framing, brick, plaster, orchard wood, iron fencing.

### Ambient industries
Dairy, cattle, orchards, horse breeding, milling, crafts, education and road commerce.

### Travel feel
Connected but topographically textured; ideal transition country between mountains and basin.

### Story functions
Strategy, rivalry travel, college-town comedy, agricultural wealth, taverns and road-game arrivals.

### Candidate-compatible modernity analogues
Oxford/Ole Miss-inspired collegiate town, batting yard, coin hall, burger-roadhouse clusters, dairyland/creamery, elite athletic club, barbershop district.

### Existing anchors
Trade Jedi Mountain Base, D0nkey K0ng Highland Stronghold, Arsenal Barbershop, Bridge at the Mountain Lake, Slob Foothill Camp.

## 3. CENTRAL RIVER BASIN

### Core identity
Fertile riverlands, large roads, major bridges, river ports and the densest institutional network.

### Climate / ecology
Temperate floodplain with seasonal flooding and rich agricultural soils.

### Settlement pattern
Largest cities, civic districts, markets, courts, guild halls, fairgrounds and dense roadside commerce.

### Materials
Brick, dressed stone, timber warehouses, tile, plaster, river clay.

### Ambient industries
Grain, milling, warehousing, river transport, administration, finance, entertainment and food markets.

### Travel feel
The hinge of the world. Roads and rivers converge here.

### Story functions
Institutional politics, neutral encounters, public spectacle, commerce, gambling, tournaments, scandal and League ceremony.

### Candidate-compatible modernity analogues
Grand casino/gaming hall, fairgrounds, ninepins hall, coin arcade, batting grounds, public infirmary, courthouse/gaol, all-night road diner.

### Existing anchors
League Chamber, League Archive, Pittsy's Book, Compact Neutral Grounds, Mire Hill Battlefield, Southern Speedway, People's Champ Road Commons.

## 4. WESTERN TABLELANDS

### Core identity
Open grassland, broken stone, dry frontier, wide sightlines and long travel.

### Climate / ecology
Drier, greater temperature swing, rain shadow and seasonal grass.

### Settlement pattern
Ranches, caravan stations, frontier towns, livestock markets, smoke pits, stables, fairs and race grounds.

### Materials
Rough timber, sun-baked brick, stone, iron, hide and canvas.

### Ambient industries
Cattle, horses, hides, preserved foods, peppers/spice trade and long-haul caravans.

### Travel feel
Open, fast by mounted standards and exposed. Distance is visible.

### Story functions
Outlaw/frontier comedy, races, wagers, caravan politics, shared territory, feasts and livestock economy.

### Candidate-compatible modernity analogues
Cow farms, dark butcher district, coach-window meal houses, dye-tag fields, raceway/pit culture, truck-stop analogue, salvage yard.

### Existing anchors
Chili Stable and TDS–Chili Shared March.

## 5. EASTERN FOREST & STORM COAST

### Core identity
Wet forest, escarpments, rocky coast, storm exposure and difficult approaches.

### Climate / ecology
Heavy rain, violent coastal weather, thick forest, fisheries and storm-fed rivers.

### Settlement pattern
Cliff towns, protected harbors, forest estates, storm refuges, old cemeteries and navigation communities.

### Materials
Dark wet stone, hardwood, copper roofs, slate, rope and storm shutters.

### Ambient industries
Timber, fishing, navigation, ship repair, coastal trade, hunting and weather services.

### Travel feel
Beautiful and dangerous. Storm timing can rewrite plans.

### Story functions
Atmospheric danger, weather events, gothic history, isolation, funerary stories and elite estates.

### Candidate-compatible modernity analogues
Coastal athletic/golf club, cliff cemetery, storm sanatorium, marina, lighthouse/signal station, grand coastal gaming hall, courier harbor.

### Existing anchors
Red Leopard Ruins, Belt Keeper Rune Keep and Storm Bowl.

## 6. SOUTHERN WETLANDS & DELTA

### Core identity
Slow rivers, cypress-like wetland, mudflats, fisheries, raised roads and difficult outsider movement.

### Climate / ecology
Warm, humid, flood-prone, insect-rich and biologically dense.

### Settlement pattern
Raised towns, levee estates, boat communities, fish markets, causeway inns and reclaimed dry islands.

### Materials
Weathered timber, brick on pilings, cypress, rope, reed and stone levees.

### Ambient industries
Fishing, boatbuilding, customs traffic, reeds, salvage, river commerce and wetland agriculture.

### Travel feel
Local knowledge is power. Straight-line maps lie.

### Story functions
Humiliation, pursuit, hidden routes, flood consequences, strange roadside establishments, salvage comedy and golf-estate contrast.

### Candidate-compatible modernity analogues
Additional golf courses, bait-and-tackle market, refuse/salvage field, riverboat gaming hall, marina, levee carnival, wetland infirmary, convalescent spa.

### Existing anchors
Mud Dogs Swamp, TDS Waterfall Temple and Country Club of Jackson.

## 7. INNER SEA LITTORAL

### Core identity
Warm ports, sheltered bays, islands, cosmopolitan trade and accelerated information flow.

### Climate / ecology
Maritime, warm, storm spillover and salt-air terraces.

### Settlement pattern
Dense ports, mercantile districts, warehouse belts, pleasure quays, inns, islands and courier yards.

### Materials
Stone quays, plaster, tile, timber warehouses, brass, canvas and imported decorative materials.

### Ambient industries
Shipping, finance, warehousing, luxury goods, entertainment and smuggling/privateering.

### Travel feel
Fast by world standards through sea lanes; cosmopolitan and transactional.

### Story functions
Commerce, nightlife, gaming, corruption, corporate satire, travel-terminal scenes, maritime disaster and urban crowds.

### Candidate-compatible modernity analogues
Casino, grand coin hall, courier terminal as airport analogue, waterfront pizza/delivery district, physicians' college, marina, theatre/concert pavilion.

### Existing anchors
LLC Boardroom and LLC Storm City.

## Cross-zone design principles

### Watershed causality
Northern precipitation feeds Upper Valleys, Central Basin and Southern Wetlands.

### Economic complementarity
No zone is self-sufficient. Food, timber, livestock, metals, salt/fish, luxury goods and administrative services move between regions.

### Story mobility
New sites should preferably sit on existing travel logic, explain why people gather there, offer a reusable social function and carry consequences forward.

### Map evolution rule
A future memo may reveal subdivisions inside a zone without redrawing the seven-zone skeleton unless new evidence proves the skeleton inadequate.

**Phase 1 macro ruling:** deepen locally; preserve globally.

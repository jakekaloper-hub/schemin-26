# SCHEMIN '26 UNIVERSE ATLAS — PHASE 1 WORK PLAN

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** ACTIVE PRODUCTION GATE
**Authority:** Commissioner directive + World Engine V1.1 + Atlas Foundation Reconciliation V1

## Mission

Deepen the existing Schemin world without replacing its physical model, creating parallel canon, or turning future memo artwork into disposable sets.

Phase 1 converts the reconciled foundation into a scalable operating substrate for:
- ten remaining regular-season memo cycles;
- playoffs and championship;
- Living Novel / Novel OS;
- visual-generation packets;
- future seasons;
- retrospective history.

## Governing doctrine

1. **World before image.**
2. **Physical geography before thematic geography.**
3. **Division culture overlays the world; it does not replace the world.**
4. **A candidate location is not a canonical location.**
5. **A published event may unlock or promote a site, but artwork alone cannot silently define borders, technology or metaphysics.**
6. **Historical consequences persist.**
7. **Modern/comic references are translated through the active technology and tone rules.**
8. **Future weeks add geography; they do not reset it.**

## Modernity resolution

The Commissioner explicitly wants modern-feeling/comic-realism environments: casinos, arcades, batting cages, raceways, hospitals, college towns, golf clubs, chain-food culture, dumps, graveyards and transport hubs.

The current Novel OS technology rule remains broadly pre-modern. Therefore Phase 1 adopts a **functional-analogue law**:

> Preserve the social function, recognizable rhythm, humor and visual grammar of a modern place while translating its technology and branding into Schemin-native institutions unless the technology ceiling is explicitly changed later.

Examples:
- casino → gaming hall / wagering palace;
- airport → caravan/courier terminal unless flight technology becomes canon;
- hospital → infirmary / physicians' hall;
- NASCAR venue → speedway/fairground with period-compatible race mechanics;
- arcade → coin hall / mechanical-games gallery;
- gas station → wayhouse / oil-and-feed depot;
- chain restaurant → recurring branded-style food house without literal real-world corporate ownership.

Real-world brands may remain **creative inspiration metadata**, not diegetic canon by default.

## Site unlock rule

A future memo may:
1. reuse an existing location;
2. reveal a previously unseen sub-location inside an existing domain;
3. activate a pre-registered candidate;
4. propose a genuinely new site.

No route may jump directly from artwork to permanent canon.

## Phase 1 exit gate

Pass only if:
- all seven zones have differentiated causal profiles;
- all three Division overlays have reusable cultural grammar;
- candidate modernity is canon-safe;
- special-event venue classes are explicit;
- championship-only geography is reserved without prematurely scripting the championship;
- new locations can be promoted deterministically;
- Memo OS and Novel OS share the same world IDs;
- character canon remains untouched;
- no unsupported exact mileage or political borders are introduced.

**Phase 1 objective:** create room for surprise without creating room for drift.

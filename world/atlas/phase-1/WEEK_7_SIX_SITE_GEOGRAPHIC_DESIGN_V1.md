## Spatial method
These are **nested local sites**, not six new standalone settlements. Their parent LOC establishes physical zone, climate, owner-domain identity, approved regional route and current environmental state. Each plan below describes relative geometry and shot-compatible layout; no invented metric distances, elevations, abstract coordinates, permanent construction or approved visitor paths. Actual site dimensions remain engineering questions. All six are event-independent candidate assets, not commitments to Week 7 scripts or outcomes.

Parent LOC-RED-RUINS; REG-EASTERN-STORM-COAST; proposed GOTW exception.
- Siting: an existing stable stone shelf or cleared forecourt adjacent to the ruins; sheer escarpment and forested storm-coast cliffs remain a **distant backdrop**, not the playable edge.

- Construction gates: structural terrace stability, drain/runoff, basket mounting, sport equipment, no ruin damage, GOTW home-domain exception and commissioner-approved reason. Alternative: approved neutral venue if exception fails.

## Acceptance sequence
1. Architect verifies no duplicate active LOC or pre-existing approved equivalent, parent region, internal access and world-state ledger.
2. Scout verifies week-specific event justification if the site is selected; this site study does not select storylines.
3. Route resolver validates both participants' full regional travel chains and local entry.
4. World/Atlas reviews terrain, hydrology, built form, inhabitant ontology, cultural/technology coherence and existing visual references.
5. Umpire independently tests geometry consistency, canon safety and promotion eligibility.
6. Only after explicit approvals may a site be promoted to an active LOC with provenance, route references and derived Atlas card. Until then candidate records remain nonspatial in the active map.

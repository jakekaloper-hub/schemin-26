## Phase 1 — Three layers mandatory at each location

Each of the **155 handles** has a machine-readable dossier divided into:

1. **Layer I — Physical and arrival:** fixed ID/zone, material geography and hydrology, known route or conceptual island edge, neighboring structures, weather exposure, position uncertainty, access/engineering constraints. No fabricated metric distances, exact shorelines, tectonic/geological data, nor instant transport.
2. **Layer II — Civil, institutional and production:** labor, settlement causality, ordinary inhabitants, owner/cultural overlays, access rules, neutral institutions, trade and utility dependencies, public/private circulation, normal and exceptional seasonal use. No declaration of sovereignty from an owner-domain motif or division assignment.
3. **Layer III — Time, world-state and playoff effect:** 2015–2025 slot-level tournament records with provenance, site-specific existing event memory where sourced, scars/repair/closure, annual island venue openings and retired uses, genuine versus invented scene distinction, 2026 unknowns, and when a change may be canon-promoted.

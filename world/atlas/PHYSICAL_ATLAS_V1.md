# PHYSICAL ATLAS V1

**Status:** INTERPRETIVE CANON / WORLD ENGINE V1

The existing seven-zone world model is retained. This atlas normalizes relationships without replacing the Living Novel physical-world source.

## Macroform

The principal theater is a temperate subcontinent opening toward a warm inner sea. The north rises into the Northern Spine. Upland valleys descend into the Central River Basin. The west dries into open tablelands; the east catches moisture and storms; southern rivers slow into wetlands and delta before meeting the inner sea.

## Zone relationships

- **Northern Spine** feeds headwaters and constrains east–west travel.
- **Upper Valleys/Foothills** are the transition between high mountains and basin.
- **Central River Basin** is the transport and market hinge.
- **Western Tablelands** favor long-range mounted movement and frontier settlement.
- **Eastern Forest/Storm Coast** is wet, wooded and weather-exposed.
- **Southern Wetlands/Delta** slows heavy overland travel and rewards specialist routes.
- **Inner Sea Littoral** accelerates commerce, information and coastal movement.

## Division overlay

The divisions are League-cultural territories laid over this physical world rather than separate planets or literal food kingdoms.

- **Burgers:** north-central uplands, market roads, upper basin, stormward escarpment.
- **Wings:** western frontier through southern wetland/delta country.
- **Pizza:** eastern mountain-to-coast arc and inner-sea mercantile corridor.

Border zones are deliberately porous. League division, civil jurisdiction and owner domain are separate atlas layers.

## Coordinate doctrine

Machine files use `schemin-local-v1` abstract coordinates only for relative layout and tests. They are not miles, latitude/longitude, or proof of travel time.

## Hydrology / weather doctrine

Northern and eastern precipitation feeds the basin and southern wetlands. Major eastern storms can affect coastal/inner-sea routes and push rain inland; western country is comparatively drier. Flooding must follow watershed logic instead of appearing as isolated scene decoration.

## Travel doctrine

Travel cost is terrain-dependent:
- ridge and basin roads = normal overland;
- mountain passes = seasonal/chokepoint;
- wetland = slow without specialist route;
- river/sea = efficient bulk movement;
- major storms can block coast/roads.

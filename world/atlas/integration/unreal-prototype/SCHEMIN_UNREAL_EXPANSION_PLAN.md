## Boundary and contracts
Canonical source -> versioned, read-only validated scene manifest -> local Unreal import/staging -> rendering/exploration.
Manifest should include schema_version, source_repository, source_commit, event_id, evidence_freshness, owner_id, character_id, location_id, scene_id, reference_receipts, allowed_world_state, and explicit status (PREVIEW, BLOCKED, APPROVED).
Do not invent any missing identifiers, owner/location mappings, coordinates, or event truth.
No Unreal edits flow back to canon automatically. Proposed consequences go through World Engine approval and independent evidence.
Character-bearing production remains blocked absent exact-reference integrity, subject binding, capability validation and independent character QA.
No paid Marketplace/Fab items or services without commissioner authorization.

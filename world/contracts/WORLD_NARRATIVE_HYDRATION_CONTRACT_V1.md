# WORLD NARRATIVE HYDRATION CONTRACT V1

**Status:** RELEASE CANDIDATE  
**Authority:** World Engine V1.1 + Universe OS V1.2 + Atlas Control Plane V2  
**Consumers:** Weekly Memo OS + Living Novel OS + visual production  
**Purpose:** Make the persistent Schemin world causal narrative material without creating a second canon or duplicating Atlas data.

## 1. Governing rule

**THE WORLD IS NOT BACKDROP. IT IS CAUSAL NARRATIVE MATERIAL.**

A location-bearing story unit must inherit the same authoritative Atlas/world state for prose and visual composition. The consumer may select emphasis; it may not fork geography, routes, entering state, inhabitants, institutions, or persistent consequences.

This contract references authoritative records. It does not copy or replace them.

## 2. Required hydration before prose or composition

Resolve or explicitly mark UNKNOWN / PROVISIONAL:

1. `LOC_ID`
2. `REGION_ZONE`
3. `ARRIVAL_ROUTE`
4. `ENTERING_WORLD_STATE`
5. `PRIOR_MEMORY`
6. `ORDINARY_WORLD_LIFE` — inhabitants, institutions, rumor, prices, cleanup, ceremony, transport or public reaction where relevant
7. `MATERIAL_CONSTRAINTS` — terrain, weather, damage, route conditions or physical limits
8. `PUBLIC_KNOWLEDGE`
9. `POV_KNOWLEDGE`
10. `EVENT_DELTA`
11. `DEPARTURE_STATE`
12. `IMAGE_TELLS`
13. `PROSE_TELLS`
14. `UNKNOWN_FORBIDDEN_INVENTIONS`

Fields may be pointers to Atlas, World State Ledger, route graph, canon or evidence records. Do not duplicate canonical values merely to satisfy the packet.

## 3. Narrative use

Hydration is successful only when inherited world state materially affects at least one of:
- what a POV notices, knows, misunderstands, remembers or anticipates;
- how arrival, movement, weather, terrain or damage constrains behavior;
- how ordinary inhabitants or institutions respond;
- how prior events remain socially, materially or emotionally present;
- what the current event changes and leaves behind.

A location name alone is not hydration. Generic fantasy prose that could be moved unchanged to another Schemin location fails.

Do not solve hydration with lore dumps. Prefer behavior, sensory consequence, institutional reaction, route pressure, memory and persistent aftermath.

## 4. Visual use

Composition consumes the same entering state. Canonical terrain, landmarks, institutions, damage, routes and environmental history outrank generic fantasy scenery.

Foreground/midground/background may distribute world information when useful:
- foreground: immediate character behavior;
- midground: encounter interaction;
- background: persistent world/public life/scars/institutions/routes/environment.

This is not a mandatory three-layer quota.

## 5. Knowledge boundary

`PUBLIC_KNOWLEDGE` and `POV_KNOWLEDGE` are separate. Narration may use reader/public knowledge outside POV only when the selected narrative mode explicitly permits it. A character may not act on information unavailable to that character.

## 6. Event translation

Real league evidence triggers story pressure; it does not automatically literalize into world physics.

Trades, waivers, wagers, standings and scores require an established translation rule before becoming contracts, cargo, territorial exchange, physical player movement, weather, destruction or architecture.

Generated jokes and scenery remain non-canon unless separately approved and transacted.

## 7. Persistence

Supported event deltas may flow to Weekly World Evolution only after the event occurs and passes provenance/continuity review.

`DEPARTURE_STATE` must distinguish:
- no persistent delta;
- provisional consequence;
- approved world mutation.

Unknown geography remains UNKNOWN.

## 8. Cross-publication anti-fork

Weekly Memo and Living Novel must reference the same location/world-state identity for the same event. They may differ in POV, scene selection, prose depth, camera, composition and emphasis. They may not publish incompatible geography or persistent state.

## 9. Failure codes

- `WORLD_AS_BACKDROP`
- `WORLD_LOADED_BUT_UNUSED`
- `GENERIC_FANTASY_SCENERY`
- `LORE_DUMP_PROSE`
- `UNAUTHORIZED_GEOGRAPHY`
- `UNAUTHORIZED_NEW_LOCATION`
- `ROUTELESS_RELOCATION`
- `WORLD_STATE_RESET`
- `PUBLIC_KNOWLEDGE_LEAK`
- `POV_KNOWLEDGE_LEAK`
- `VISUAL_PROSE_REDUNDANCY`
- `EVENT_WITHOUT_WORLD_DELTA`
- `CONSEQUENCE_WITHOUT_PERSISTENCE`
- `MATCHUP_AS_ISOLATED_DIORAMA`

## 10. Gate

Before Story Lock for a location-bearing story unit, the Story Room must demonstrate a hydration reference or explicitly identify unresolved fields that remain blocking/provisional.

Before Page Lock, prose and visual packets must consume the same resolved world-state reference and preserve `IMAGE_TELLS` / `PROSE_TELLS` complementarity.

This contract adds no authority to invent a venue before live evidence and encounter-venue law resolve it.

# DERIVED WORLD DATA CONTRACT V1

Derived products may summarize, index, format, filter, render or precompute authoritative world truth.

## Approved derived classes
- Location Cards
- Homeland Cards
- sublocation handles
- world packets
- Memo hydration packets
- Novel hydration packets
- travel graph/query results
- structural environment plates
- Interactive Atlas HTML
- Canonical World Plate previs

## Prohibited behavior
A derived product may not:
- introduce authoritative world facts;
- write upstream state;
- create a canonical proper noun by repetition;
- alter route topology;
- move a location between physical regions;
- change population ontology;
- promote CAND-* geography;
- erase persistent state.

## Regeneration rule
When a relevant authority changes, stale derived products are rebuilt rather than hand-edited into disagreement.

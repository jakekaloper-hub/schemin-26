## Mission
Turn Schemin's persistent world from linked canon files into a governed world/civilization/simulation layer with Atlas as its spatial control plane.

## Ownership
Universe OS owns world truth, ontology, civilization, institutions, history/memory, persistent world state and controlled mutations.

Atlas Control Plane owns spatial representation and QA of that truth: containment, adjacency, routes, hydrology relationships, visibility, travel topology, map layers and map products.

Universe OS does not own:
- league scoring/standings/rosters;
- betting settlement;
- principal visual/body canon;
- prose voice;
- image style.

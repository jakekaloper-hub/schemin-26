# MATCHUP LOCATION GOVERNANCE V1

**Status:** COMMISSIONER-DIRECTED CANON  
**Purpose:** Make weekly Encounters obey the shared map.

## Default regular-season rule

A normal matchup is **home-anchored**.

The environment must be:
1. the home team's established home environment/domain; or
2. another plausible approved venue **inside the home team's Division Atlas footprint**.

The away side travels to it.

A visual team identity does not create the terrain. The venue inherits:
physical zone → civil geography → division overlay → home-domain culture → world state.

## Neutral-site rule

Neutral locations may be used when the story or League importance justifies them.

Typical uses:
- Game of the Week;
- playoffs;
- semifinals;
- championship;
- major ceremonial rivalry;
- League institutional event.

Neutral sites must:
- already exist or be admitted through the Location Register;
- be reachable through the travel graph;
- have a physical-zone and jurisdiction explanation;
- not be invented solely because a cinematic composition needs a backdrop.

A GOTW is **eligible** for neutral treatment, not automatically required to be neutral.

## Division rule

Division membership is a League affiliation and cultural/spatial overlay.

It does **not** imply:
- species segregation;
- exclusive national sovereignty;
- that all members live in one biome;
- that people from other divisions cannot live in the same town or march.

TDS and Chili are the explicit canon example: different division affiliations can coexist inside the same wider civil/frontier geography.

## Home-venue resolver

For each matchup packet resolve:

```
home_team_id
away_team_id
home_division_id
venue_mode: HOME_DOMAIN | HOME_DIVISION | NEUTRAL_MAJOR
location_id
physical_zone_id
civil_jurisdiction
division_overlays
route_home_to_venue
route_away_to_venue
entering_world_state
weather_state
why_this_location_exists
who_depends_on_this_place
```

Missing answers block finished environment art.

## Major-site standard

A neutral major venue must have ordinary-world utility outside the matchup:
- road junction;
- town;
- fairground;
- institutional chamber;
- old battlefield;
- raceway;
- bridge;
- market;
- fort;
- river crossing;
- pilgrimage/memorial site.

The Contest uses the world; it does not manufacture empty stadium backdrops.

## Mire Hill / Chapter III governing example

The Week 3 D0nkey K0ng–Mud Dogs Game of the Week is treated as a neutral-major Encounter at **Mire Hill**.

World explanation:
- the Hill is a low black-earth rise at the Central Basin / southern floodplain transition;
- the Delta Causeway and Basin road network pass beneath/near it;
- dark soil comes from alluvial silt, wet clay, decomposed river vegetation and recent rain;
- drainage off the rise creates churned mud on its lower approaches;
- nearby road/market settlement depends on the crossing for drovers, ferries, flood-control labor and north-south trade;
- D0nkey K0ng approaches from the northern/upper-valley road system;
- Mud Dogs approaches from the southern wetland causeway;
- the site is suitable for a major neutral Encounter precisely because both routes converge there.

The reader does not require this exposition. Production does.

## El Niño location exception

El Niño has no residence in the ordinary character sense.

When El Niño is the home side:
- resolve a Pizza-affiliated storm corridor/landfall region as the scheduled home theater;
- the storm itself may have traveled there;
- the opponent experiences the matchup as a weather disaster/event inside that location.

When El Niño is away:
- the storm physically moves into the opponent's home theater;
- weather consequences must propagate through the World State Ledger rather than appear as a humanoid visiting character.

## Dependency law
No downstream consumer may create upstream truth.

- Art may not invent locations, species, routes or canon names.
- Novel may not silently create geography/population/institutions.
- Memo may propose state change but must use mutation governance.
- Atlas represents spatial truth but does not independently invent world truth.
- Universe OS does not own principal visual/body canon or fantasy-league fact truth.

If a later gate exposes an upstream defect, repair the earliest affected node and rerun all dependent tests.

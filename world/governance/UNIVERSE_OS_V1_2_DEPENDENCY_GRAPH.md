## Dependency law
No downstream system creates upstream truth.

- Art cannot invent locations, species, routes, or names.
- Novel cannot silently fork geography.
- Memo may propose world-state deltas but cannot bypass mutation governance.
- Atlas represents spatial truth and validates it; Atlas rendering cannot mutate Universe truth.
- Character Control remains principal body/visual authority.

## Regression law
If a downstream phase reveals an upstream defect, repair the earliest affected layer and rerun all dependent tests before promotion.

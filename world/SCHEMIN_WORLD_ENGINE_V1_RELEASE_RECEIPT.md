## Release invariants

### Food divisions
- BURGERS → burgers.
- WINGS → **chicken wings**.
- PIZZA → pizza.

Food identity is expressed through culture, heraldry, hospitality, objects and ordinary food. Terrain is not novelty food sculpture.

### World hierarchy

`PHYSICAL ATLAS → CIVIL/POLITICAL LAYER → DIVISION LAYER → OWNER DOMAIN → LOCATION/ROUTE → ENCOUNTER/STATE`

The layers may overlap in a rendered map but remain distinct in the data model.

### Rendering law

**The world determines the image. The image does not determine the world.**

A renderer may alter presentation. It may not silently alter:
- physical zone;
- division membership;
- owner domain;
- route topology;
- location identity;
- persistent state;
- character canon.

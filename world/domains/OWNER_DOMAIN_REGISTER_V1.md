## Design rule

Division identity is inherited through materials, roads, economy and civic culture. Owner identity then specializes it. No owner may force the surrounding world into mascot literalism.

## Canon-change rule

Owner/character visual changes follow the Character Control Plane, not this register. The register references stable character IDs so future team renames do not move geography.

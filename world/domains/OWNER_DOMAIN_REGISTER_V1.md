## Home-Encounter rule

For ordinary regular-season and divisional matchups, the verified **home team** supplies the default environment. The encounter occurs at the home anchor above or at a newly resolved sub-location inside that home domain.

A home game does not mean “generic division scenery.” It means that owner's actual place, inhabitants, roads, climate and material culture.

## Major-Encounter rule

Game of the Week, playoffs and championships default to approved neutral sites under:

`world/encounters/ENCOUNTER_VENUE_RESOLVER_V1.md`

Neutral venues are still real locations inside the Atlas and require routes and inherited world state.

## Cross-divisional coexistence

TDS and Chili are the explicit proof that League divisions are not residential or biological segregation.

Their shared social/geographic anchor is:

`LOC-TDS-CHILI-SHARED-MARCH`

They retain separate division affiliations while sharing ordinary space.

## Ontology rule

Population status is governed by:

`world/civilization/INHABITANT_ONTOLOGY_V1.md`

A PEOPLED_KIND may populate a home settlement. A SINGULAR_BEING does not imply a hidden same-kind civilization.

## Canon-change rule

Owner/character visual changes follow the Character Control Plane, not this register. The register references stable character IDs so future team renames do not move geography or invent species.

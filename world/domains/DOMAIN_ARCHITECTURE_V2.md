# DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE V2

**Core law:** OWNER DOMAIN ≠ SOVEREIGN COUNTRY.

V2 treats each owner relationship to place as a layered domain profile: home anchor, physical region, League division overlay, population/ontology, access routes, neighboring locations, history and current state.

Fields marked `INFERRED` are deliberately not promoted to locked lore. They exist as production constraints or open causal placeholders until a governed world mutation promotes them.

## Required domain behavior
- every domain has a stable home anchor;
- every domain inherits physical-zone constraints;
- every domain can reach the wider route network;
- singular beings do not generate singular-being populations;
- division affiliation does not create a national border;
- background society can exist without cloning the principal.

## ObiWan
Trade Jedi Mountain Base is a community node, not a one-Jedi kingdom. Other Jedi and non-Jedi humans coexist there.

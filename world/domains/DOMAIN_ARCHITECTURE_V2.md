# DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE V2

**Status:** PHASE 2 CANDIDATE

Owner domain is a relationship to place, not a sovereign country.

Every domain resolves:
- principal/home anchor;
- physical region;
- division overlay;
- population/community context;
- architecture/materials;
- transport/water relationships;
- nearby institutions and ordinary life;
- historical/current state;
- neutral-network access;
- forbidden drift.

## Rules
1. Domain placement inherits physical geography and Atlas topology.
2. Division affiliation is cultural/League context, never a species or national border.
3. Singular beings do not imply same-kind populations.
4. Community-bearing principals may coexist with broader populations.
5. Trade Jedi Mountain Base includes other Jedi plus non-Jedi humans.
6. A domain must connect to the wider world through governed routes or explicit isolation.

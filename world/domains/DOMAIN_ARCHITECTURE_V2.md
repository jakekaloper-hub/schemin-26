# DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE V2

**Status:** RELEASE CANDIDATE

## Core law
Owner domain ≠ sovereign country.

A domain is a relationship among a principal, home anchor, physical region, ordinary population, routes, institutions and recurring history.

## Required domain questions
1. Where is the home anchor?
2. Which physical region constrains it?
3. Who else lives there?
4. What routes connect it to the wider world?
5. What ordinary economic/civic activity supports it?
6. Which division culture overlays it?
7. What history/state persists?
8. What visual/story substitutions are forbidden?

## Current model
All twelve profiles are derived from current owner-domain, location, route and division records. Where economy or ordinary life is not explicitly locked, the machine profile marks the detail INFERRED rather than silently promoting it.

## Special controls
- Trade Jedi Mountain Base includes other Jedi and non-Jedi humans.
- Duckhook, Belt Keeper, El Niño and LLC remain singular.
- TDS and Chili may share space across division affiliation.
- division affiliation never implies species or national border.

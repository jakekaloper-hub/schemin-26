# SCHEMIN WORLD BIBLE — ARCHIVE & RECORDS V1

**Status:** Narrow canon supplement derived from the Prologue literary system.  
**Scope:** Only institutions/objects required to make future real league events narratively reusable.

## 1. The Archive
The Archive is the Chronicle world's durable memory institution. It preserves official records, damaged records, receipts, alias changes, draft documents and other evidence. Its physical age may be shown through repairs, obsolete forms, old filing systems and unidentified handwriting. This does NOT authorize invented historical results.

## 2. Ledger vs Chronicle
**Ledger:** reduced official record: names, scores, standings, selections, transactions, dates, custody.
**Chronicle:** literary account of what those events entered, changed and left behind.
Neither may contradict verified league evidence.

## 3. Evidence classes
FACT — verified real-league state.
WORLD CANON — established Schemin-world fact.
NARRATIVE INTERPRETATION — literary meaning that does not contradict evidence.
RUMOR / TESTIMONY — intentionally uncertain in-world claim.

These are production classifications, not routine narrator vocabulary.

## 4. Names and inhabitants
Characters belong to owners, not team names. A rename changes the Archive heading/alias record, never the inhabitant's established body, identity or visual canon.

## 5. Belt custody
The Belt is a physical historical object. Current custody follows verified championship state. Its complete lineage is not invented when evidence is incomplete. Possession of the Belt proves settled prior history; it grants no advantage in an unplayed season.

## 6. Draft rights
Exact fantasy terminology remains authoritative in artifacts and data. Prose may render picks as rights of choosing, reserved names, transferred rights, occupied rounds, empty spaces and future marks when context remains intelligible.

## 7. Keeper marks
A keeper is represented as a name already reserved against a round before the live draft. The underlying real keeper round/cost remains factual authority.

## 8. Shared-world connective tissue
The twelve territories need no invented political map. They are connected by shared League procedure: common calendar, official draft sequence, Archive forms/seals, receipts, records and evidence returning to the Archive.

## 9. Receipts
“Receipt” is the Chronicle's durable motif for evidence created by consequence. It may refer literally to transactional documentation or more broadly to a settled competitive record when the context is literary. It must never falsify the underlying event.

## 10. Season opening
Before competitive results, the new ledger has twelve empty lines. Prior history may shape arrival but cannot populate the new season's win/loss record.

## 11. Intentional unknowns
Unknown history stays unknown. Do not invent old champions, scores, governments, wars, borders, owner relationships, Belt lineage or undocumented aliases merely to deepen lore.

## 12. Deep-time doctrine
Age is implied through material evidence: repaired bindings, obsolete fields, old seals, overwritten labels, storage conventions, stains, unidentified handwriting and corrections layered over corrections. The edge of an untold story is preferred to fabricated exposition.

## 13. Future weekly ingestion
Verified real event → Ledger fact → canon/alias resolution → Chronicle interpretation → provenance/contradiction audit → published story.
This permits future ESPN results, trades, waivers, renames and championships to enter the mythology without retroactive contradiction.

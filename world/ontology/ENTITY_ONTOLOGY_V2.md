# ENTITY ONTOLOGY V2

**Status:** RELEASE CANDIDATE

Universe OS separates principals from populations, communities, institutions, places, objects, forces and events.

## Principal rule
All twelve league-owner avatars are PRINCIPAL entities. Population ontology is a separate relationship, not a replacement for principal identity.

- D0nkey K0ng → peopled kind.
- Red Leopards → peopled kind.
- Slob → broader human/upper-valley community.
- Mud Dogs → peopled wetland canine community.
- Seven Chins → broader human/central-basin community.
- Belt Keeper, Duckhook, El Niño, LLC → singular.
- TDS / Chili → shared-cohabitation model; wider kind status remains unresolved.

## El Niño
The principal may relate to WEATHER_SYSTEM / ENVIRONMENTAL_FORCE manifestation, but ordinary regional weather remains independent.

## Anti-drift
Singular principals cannot be multiplied into species. Peopled principals cannot be treated as the only being of their kind. Background Jedi do not clone ObiWan.

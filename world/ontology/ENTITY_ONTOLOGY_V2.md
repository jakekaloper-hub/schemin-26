# ENTITY ONTOLOGY V2

Universe OS distinguishes a principal from a broader people/community and from the institutions or environmental forces around them.

## Types
PRINCIPAL, PEOPLED_KIND, SINGULAR_BEING, COMMUNITY, ORDER, INSTITUTION, LOCATION, SETTLEMENT, INFRASTRUCTURE, ARTIFACT, WEATHER_SYSTEM, ENVIRONMENTAL_FORCE, EVENT, HISTORICAL_SITE, JURISDICTION, RUMOR, MYTH, MEMORIAL.

## Principal model
**Community-bearing / peopled:** ObiWan, D0nkey K0ng, Red Leopards, Slob, Mud Dogs, Seven Chins.

**Singular:** Belt Keeper, Duckhook, El Niño, The LLC.

**Shared/cohabitation exception:** TDS + Chili remain explicit cross-divisional cohabitants; their wider same-kind population question stays unresolved rather than invented.

## ObiWan / Jedi lock
ObiWan is a principal Jedi who coexists with other Jedi. `ORDER-JEDI` represents the wider order/community. Non-Jedi humans may also inhabit the mountain/valley society. Background Jedi are not clones of ObiWan's face, clothing, green sword or golden-retriever relationship.

## El Niño
El Niño remains singular with approved manifestation states: EMBODIED, ATMOSPHERIC_MANIFESTATION, AMBIGUOUS. Ordinary storms remain ordinary unless an explicit world-state resolution says otherwise.

## Personhood
Sapient personhood is morphology-neutral under the current world framework.

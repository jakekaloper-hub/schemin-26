## Jedi correction
ObiWan is a principal within an established Jedi order/community. Other Jedi coexist at/around Trade Jedi Mountain Base alongside non-Jedi humans. Other Jedi do not clone ObiWan's exact face, wardrobe, sword, companion, or personal history.

## El Niño
El Niño is a principal with a weather-system/environmental-manifestation relationship. Approved states remain EMBODIED, ATMOSPHERIC_MANIFESTATION, and AMBIGUOUS. Ordinary weather remains independent.

## Spatial entities
Any placeable entity records stable location relationships and Atlas-layer participation. Atlas representation does not create ontology.

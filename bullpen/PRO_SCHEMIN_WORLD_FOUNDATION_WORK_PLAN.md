# THE WORLD OF PRO SCHEMIN' — FOUNDATION WORK PLAN

**Status:** ACTIVE / FOUNDATION PHASE 0  
**Project:** Schemin '26  
**Program:** Pro Schemin' Chronicles  
**Purpose:** Establish the permanent fictional universe and production system before adapting individual weeks into illustrated narrative chapters.

## 1. North Star

Build a persistent, living American fantasy universe in which the twelve PSFL characters are actual inhabitants—not mascots—and in which real league events continuously become history.

The weekly memos remain concise contemporary records. The larger illustrated book expands those records into scenes, chapters, journeys, relationships, consequences, artifacts, and environmental storytelling.

**The season supplies events. The universe turns those events into history.**

This foundation must support Week 1 through Week 14, playoffs, championship, offseason, future seasons, and retrospective league history without resetting the world.

## 2. Source-of-Truth Hierarchy

1. Character Canon: established character identity and visual rules.
2. Published Week 1 Memo: locked historical evidence.
3. Published Week 2 Memo: locked historical evidence.
4. Verified PSFL/ESPN league data and historical records.
5. Approved league-history records and commissioner evidence.
6. New worldbuilding approved through this plan.

Published material is not casually retconned. Team-name changes do not rewrite established character identity.

Every claim created during Foundation Phase 0 must be tagged:
- **LOCKED CANON** — published/verified and immutable absent explicit correction.
- **SOFT CANON** — established or strongly implied; expandable.
- **PROPOSED LORE** — writers' room invention awaiting adoption.
- **OPEN TERRITORY** — intentionally unresolved.

## 3. Creative Thesis

Create an original PSFL epic-fantasy world by combining two distinct influence layers:
- **Visual/artistic influence:** painterly, hand-crafted animation sensibilities associated with Hayao Miyazaki's work—expressive characters, lush living environments, atmospheric skies and weather, tactile objects and machinery, warm interiors, environmental motion, visual wonder, and meaningful quiet moments. Do not copy specific films, characters, compositions, or protected designs; translate these high-level artistic qualities into an original visual language.
- **Epic-fantasy content/scale influence:** the monumental geography, ancient history, ruins, fortresses, wilderness, journeys, material realism, danger, factions, and civilizational depth associated with LOTR-style epic fantasy.

This is **not 'Americanized Miyazaki.'** Japanese cultural coding is not the target. American/Southern/football/roadside/country-club/swamp/diner/barbershop elements arise organically from established PSFL character and league canon. They are the subject matter of this universe, while the painterly animation influence governs visual feeling and LOTR-like epic fantasy informs scale, history, geography, and adventure.

The tonal signature is **painterly wonder + epic historical scale + specific PSFL absurdity + sincere character consequence**.

A sword, golf cart, Sonic bag, motorcycle, championship belt, tavern ledger, and mountain fortress must be capable of belonging to the same civilization without feeling like disconnected jokes.

## 4. Central World Rule

**THE LEAGUE IS REAL INSIDE THE WORLD.**

The twelve characters exist. Their homes exist. Their possessions and companions exist. PSFL exists as an institution.

The foundation must answer, without cheap literalism:
- What is PSFL inside civilization?
- Why do the twelve gather?
- What does competition confer?
- Why does the Belt matter?
- What are Burgers, Wings, and Pizza?
- What authority does the Commissioner hold?
- Why are receipts preserved?
- What is Pittsy's Book?
- What do trades, waivers, FAAB, injuries, divisions, playoffs, and championships mean diegetically?
- What do these characters do when they are not in a matchup?

## 5. Bullpen Ownership

### Closer — Executive synthesis
Own final Foundation v1.0 acceptance and resolve cross-department disputes.

### Librarian — Canon & provenance
Build the canon registry, source ledger, naming/rename history, artifact registry, continuity references, and provenance rules. Reject unsupported promotion from proposal to canon.

### Beat Writer / Narrative Room — Literary architecture
Develop thematic backbone, POV rules, voice, chapter grammar, dialogue doctrine, humor/violence boundaries, ensemble structure, and the translation from factual event to dramatic scene.

### Scout / League Historian — Evidence
Extract verified league history, prior champions, divisions, records, rivalries, standings, transactions, and chronology. Distinguish historical evidence from folklore.

### Architect / Systems — World mechanics
Design the persistent state model, chronology engine, weekly ingestion contract, relationship state, location state, object state, and interfaces between ESPN/Data Gateway, Memo OS, World Bible, and Chronicles.

### Visual Development — Art constitution
Define environmental language, geography-specific architecture, material vocabulary, visual continuity, cinematography, artifact design, quiet-scene language, aging/wear, and character fidelity.

### Cartography — Atlas
Create the first coherent geography from canonical locations before adding new territory. Establish travel constraints, roads, rivers, borders, neutral ground, League HQ, settlements, wilderness, and regional identities.

### Character Room — Twelve lives
Build complete dossiers for all twelve inhabitants, separating public reputation from private character and matchup performance from personality.

### Umpire / QA — Adversarial continuity
Challenge contradictions, generic fantasy drift, unexplained teleportation, lore inflation, character drift, chronology errors, factual contamination, and unsupported canon.

## 6. Workstreams

### WS-01 — Canon Excavation
Inventory every established:
- character trait and silhouette;
- companion and possession;
- location;
- artifact;
- institution;
- phrase/custom;
- relationship/rivalry;
- historical event;
- visual clue;
- unresolved thread.

**Output:** `world/canon/CANON_EXCAVATION.md`

No new lore is allowed in this workstream.

### WS-02 — World Constitution
Resolve:
- fundamental nature of the world;
- realism/fantasy boundary;
- magic rules, if any;
- technology;
- species;
- mortality;
- communications;
- travel;
- economy;
- political/social organization;
- ordinary civilian life;
- thematic backbone.

**Output:** `world/foundation/WORLD_CONSTITUTION.md`

### WS-03 — PSFL Institution
Define the League's role in civilization and diegetic grammar for competition, Belt, divisions, Commissioner, schedules, records, receipts, trades, waivers, FAAB, Pittsy's Book, postseason, and championship.

**Output:** `world/foundation/PSFL_INSTITUTION.md`

### WS-04 — Character Bible
For each of 12 characters establish:
Name; epithet; homeland; residence; species; appearance; clothing; equipment; companion; station; strengths; flaws; desires; fears; reputation; private self; allies; rivals; history; League relationship; Belt relationship; possessions; recurring places; silhouette; material language; speech tendencies; unresolved threads.

**Output:** `world/characters/CHARACTER_BIBLE_V1.md`

### WS-05 — Atlas & Geography
Start only from evidence/implied geography, then propose connective geography.

Must include canonical/implied territories such as ObiWan's North Georgia mountain country, Mud Dogs' swamp, Red Leopard domain, Chili frontier/Dark Horse routes, Country Club of Jackson, El Niño storm territory, Arsenal Centaur stronghold/barbershop culture, Belt Keeper domain, League HQ.

**Output:** `world/atlas/ATLAS_V1.md`

### WS-06 — History & Mythology
Separate verified PSFL history from in-world interpretation. Establish eras, former champions, old contests, traditions, disputed stories, folklore, and the historical position of 2026.

**Output:** `world/history/CHRONICLE_BEFORE_2026.md`

### WS-07 — Culture & Everyday Life
Build regional food, architecture, clothing, craftsmanship, transport, music, media, taverns/diners, clubs, roadside culture, rituals, slang, gambling, leisure, ceremonies, and ordinary work.

**Output:** `world/foundation/CULTURE_AND_DAILY_LIFE.md`

### WS-08 — Literary Constitution
Define how an ESPN fact becomes story without changing the fact.

Include:
- POV system;
- narrator rules;
- prose register;
- chapter/spread grammar;
- dialogue;
- exposition limits;
- humor;
- violence;
- quiet scenes;
- travel;
- simultaneity across six weekly matchups;
- artifact/interlude pages;
- factual vs invented boundaries.

**Output:** `chronicles/LITERARY_CONSTITUTION.md`

### WS-09 — Visual Constitution
Define original visual principles and continuity controls. Every frame must answer:
1. Where are we?
2. Who lives here?
3. What happened here before today?

**Output:** `chronicles/VISUAL_CONSTITUTION.md`

### WS-10 — Living Continuity Engine
Model persistent state for:
- character condition/location/reputation;
- relationships;
- possessions;
- debts/promises;
- locations and damage;
- artifacts;
- rumors/knowledge;
- unresolved plots;
- chronology;
- source provenance.

**Output:** `world/continuity/CONTINUITY_ENGINE.md`

### WS-11 — Weekly Ingestion Contract
Design the recurring workflow:

`ESPN/Data Gateway → verified weekly facts → Memo OS → Canon delta → Story room → illustrated chronicle → continuity update`

Future weeks add history; they never reset it.

**Output:** `chronicles/WEEKLY_STORY_INGESTION.md`

## 7. Execution Gates

### Gate A — Evidence Complete
Pass when Week 1, Week 2, Character Canon, and available league-history evidence have been inventoried and provenance-tagged.

**No major world invention before Gate A.**

### Gate B — World Coherence
Pass when World Constitution + PSFL Institution + Atlas + Character Bible coexist without material contradiction.

### Gate C — Story/Visual Coherence
Pass when Literary Constitution + Visual Constitution can depict the same event consistently while preserving factual truth.

### Gate D — Future-Week Readiness
Run hypothetical unseen-week tests:
- upset;
- trade;
- injury;
- rename;
- waiver frenzy;
- rivalry rematch;
- character with no major weekly event;
- six simultaneous matchups.

The system must ingest each without retconning the world.

### Gate E — Foundation Freeze
Umpire performs adversarial audit. Closer accepts Foundation v1.0. Only then may adaptation of Week 1/Week 2 into book chapters begin.

## 8. Explicit Non-Goals During Phase 0

Do not:
- write the final 2026 novel;
- predetermine the champion or ending;
- force every matchup into literal combat;
- create lore simply to fill sections;
- redesign locked characters;
- use generic medieval-Europe filler;
- treat weekly artwork as disposable sets;
- silently canonize brainstorming;
- let a future ESPN event contradict established world physics without resolving the consequence.

## 9. Book Architecture After Foundation Freeze

Permanent hierarchy:

`THE WORLD OF PRO SCHEMIN'`
- World Bible — permanent universe
- Character Bible — permanent inhabitants
- Atlas — permanent geography
- Chronicle — historical timeline
- Continuity Ledger — persistent state
- 2026 Season Ledger — factual season record
- Weekly Memo — concise contemporary record
- Pro Schemin' Chronicles: 2026 — illustrated literary adaptation

A one-page memo matchup may expand into many book pages: setup, travel, quiet scene, confrontation, aftermath, artifact/interlude, and later echoes. The existing memo art may become a centerpiece rather than the entire scene.

## 10. Core Narrative Law

**We do not write toward a predetermined ending.**

We build characters with desires, a world with rules, and history with consequences.

**ESPN determines what happens next. The world reacts.**

## 11. Definition of Done — Foundation v1.0

Foundation Phase 0 is complete only when:
- all 12 characters have audited dossiers;
- all published Week 1–2 evidence is represented;
- world rules and PSFL rules are explicit;
- an atlas specification exists with travel logic;
- league history has provenance boundaries;
- literary and visual constitutions are enforceable;
- continuity has a persistent state model;
- weekly ingestion is documented;
- Canon Registry is complete;
- adversarial QA passes;
- unresolved questions are intentionally labeled rather than silently invented;
- the system can accept every remaining 2026 week and future seasons.

## 12. Immediate Work Order

1. Librarian + Scout: complete WS-01 Canon Excavation.
2. Character Room: build evidence-only character matrices in parallel.
3. Architect: draft schemas for continuity/provenance without deciding lore.
4. Beat Writer: extract themes already present; do not impose a final theme yet.
5. Visual Development + Cartography: extract environmental evidence only.
6. Umpire: audit the extraction for unsupported assumptions.
7. Bullpen board: review evidence and authorize proposed-lore phase.
8. Proceed through Gates B–E.
9. Only after Foundation Freeze begin adaptation exercises.

---

**Foundation doctrine:** Extract before inventing. Connect before expanding. Consequences persist. Geography matters. Characters live between Sundays. The world must survive whatever the real season does next.

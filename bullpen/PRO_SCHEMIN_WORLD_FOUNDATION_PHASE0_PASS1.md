## Creative clarification — binding

The visual direction is **not "Americanized Miyazaki."**

The target combines:
1. Painterly, hand-crafted animation sensibilities associated with Hayao Miyazaki's work: expressive character acting, lush living environments, atmospheric skies/weather, tactile objects and machinery, warm interiors, environmental motion, wonder, and quiet humanity. These are high-level principles only; do not copy specific films, characters, compositions, or protected designs.
2. LOTR-like epic-fantasy content and scale: monumental geography, ancient history, wilderness, ruins, fortresses, journeys, material realism, danger, factions, and civilizational depth.
3. PSFL canon supplies the actual cultural subject matter. Southern/American/football/roadside/swamp/country-club/diner/barbershop elements arise because they already belong to these characters and league—not because the artistic influence is being "Americanized."

Working shorthand: **painterly wonder + epic historical scale + PSFL-specific life.**

## Architect — initial state schema

The continuity system should eventually track, at minimum:

`character_state` — identity, current location, condition, reputation, possessions, active objectives  
`relationship_state` — pair, history, current tension, debts/promises, knowledge  
`location_state` — region, damage/change, inhabitants, prior events  
`artifact_state` — owner/custodian, location, condition, provenance  
`event_state` — factual PSFL source, fictional treatment, chronology, participants, consequences  
`canon_state` — locked / soft / proposed / open, source, approval date  
`knowledge_state` — who knows what, preventing omniscient-character leakage

No schema field authorizes invention by itself.

## Umpire ruling — Pass 1

**PASS WITH OPEN ITEMS.**

Character identity has a strong binding source. Week 1–2 event results and several visual environments are sufficiently established to seed the universe. The largest risk is **literalizing editorial/matchup artwork too early**. A memo illustration can establish that an image/event is canonical without automatically defining the entire metaphysics, geography, or government around it.

Therefore:
- preserve the scenes;
- preserve the objects;
- preserve the consequences;
- postpone explanations until the World Constitution board.

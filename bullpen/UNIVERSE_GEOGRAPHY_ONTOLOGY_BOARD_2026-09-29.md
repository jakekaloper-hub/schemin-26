## Commissioner clarifications

### Encounter geography
Ordinary matchups are not free-floating set pieces.

Default rule:
- the **verified home team** hosts the Encounter inside its established home environment/domain;
- that home environment belongs to the team's League-division layer even where physical geography is shared or overlapping;
- major matchups may move to an approved neutral location elsewhere in the Schemin universe;
- GOTWs, playoffs and championships use neutral sites by default, with explicit override required to use a home domain.

This means a Wings home matchup should look and behave like that specific Wings owner's environment—not a generic Wings palette.

### El Niño dual manifestation
El Niño is singular, but the character can manifest as:
1. the established humanoid Weather System; or
2. an actual natural storm/disaster when the story explicitly resolves the weather event as El Niño.

Not every storm is El Niño. The manifestation must be tagged in story/world state.

## Bullpen findings

### Cartography
The existing Novel travel graph is materially behind the released World Engine: it still says `SEED_NOT_FULL_MAP` and contains no approved edges. V1.1 must hydrate it from World Engine locations/routes and make travel assertions testable.

### Character / Civilization
Visual canon is not sufficient ontology. V1.1 needs a Kind/Personhood model that tells writers:
- whether a character is singular or belongs to a broader people;
- whether ordinary members of that kind exist;
- whether buildings/settlements are scaled for them;
- whether legal standing depends on morphology.

### Law / Institutions
Compact-recognized personhood must be morphology-neutral. The Twelve can hold property, contract, travel, participate in Encounters and appear before institutions whether human, beast-bodied, avian, elemental or singular.

### Narrative
Uniqueness is not a command to make every civilian stare. Famous singular beings are socially known. Reaction should follow context, not force an exposition scene every time Duckhook walks into a room.

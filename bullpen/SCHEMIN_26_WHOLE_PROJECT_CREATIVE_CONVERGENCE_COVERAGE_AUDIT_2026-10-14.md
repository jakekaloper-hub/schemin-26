## Architecture proposal (not authorized build)

1. **Discover and verify current contracts:** use `PROJECT_CONTROL_REGISTRY.md` + folder ownership registry. Include downstream product consumers rather than only Memo/Novel.
2. **One shared Scene Context Resolution:** freeze fact receipt and timestamp, active owner IDs, current character references, LOC-/ROUTE-/REG- world IDs, entering world state, asset hashes and time-specific interpretation. The existing Atlas Publication Convergence Contract and Visual World Packet are the starting point; do not fork.
3. **Narrative / author skill routing:** consult only relevant Author Council published-method lenses plus active Beat Writer. Authors diagnose meaning, scene transitions, tone, motifs, world texture, POV and visual beat choices. They do not overrule hard canon/data or impose a house art style.
4. **Production-specific adapters:** Memo focuses on editorial composition, ranked fields, world-opening briefing and result receipts; Novel focuses on scene/POV meaning; Atlas maps the real canonical space; Mercer consumes league data without leaking private decisions; future products inherit the same context without source copies.
5. **One canonical asset identity chain:** final subject references and local scene crops carry asset+character+temporal+location provenance. No new face or geography invented merely to populate derivative UI.
6. **World evolution fanout:** only independently accepted post-release changes enter World/Atlas mutation authority; rehydrate future Memo, Novel, Atlas, scenes and interaction surfaces.
7. **Cross-OS independent review:** unit contract assertions plus a real pilot evidence trace: `verified event → selected characters → venue/route → approved shared scene context → Memo artifact → Novel treatment → Atlas display → conditional world-delta review`. Evaluate equivalence of identities, geography and temporal facts; allow distinct render styles. No mocked Director PASS.

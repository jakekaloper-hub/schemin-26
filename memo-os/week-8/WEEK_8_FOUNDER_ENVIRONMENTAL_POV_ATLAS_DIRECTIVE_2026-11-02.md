# Week 8 — Founder Environmental POV and Atlas Expansion Directive

**Authority:** Founder decisions, 2026-11-02. **Status:** approved creative direction; not a production, Atlas-canon, Story, Fact, or page lock.

## Binding decisions
1. **Environmental POV: CINEMATIC DISCOVERY.** Introduce the living Commonwealth through geography, material history, roads, weather, habitation and camera movement. Discover the two characters within the place; do not use location-label cards to substitute for immersion. Preserve the two equal POV allocations but allow environment to connect them.
2. **Atlas: EXPAND WITH NEW SITES.** Directors may propose new named places or permanent landmarks where warranted, not merely existing-site sublocations. Proposals must go through the canonical Location Register, routes/travel graph, geography, world state, ownership and independent continuity validation. New creative names are not immediately canonical.
3. **Visual expansion: DIRECTOR DISCRETION.** The twelve-member production council determines shot count and page allocation based on narrative purpose and cinematography. Frank Masi's camera-work perspective is a creative reference, not an assertion of his actual participation or approval. Do not impose one establishing page per matchup or lock page count.
4. **No artwork or winner lock.** Storyboards and editorial/Atlas research only. ESPN results remain provisional until decided and independently verified.

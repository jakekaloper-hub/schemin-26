## Findings
- Seven front-matter pages before first matchup: compress and distribute informational context.
- Six matchup features run consecutively, typically three pages each (p8–25). The recognizable style is strong but overly repetitive.
- Utility/reporting pages are concentrated at p26–29. Move them according to causal story relevance.
- Map-heavy p5/p7 are not the desired Week 8 landscape presentation. Preserve the geography, not labels/legends/territorial colors.
- Strongest transferable qualities: premium illustrated full-page scenes, authentic character identity, strong physical settings, dramatic typography, visual causality, result receipts, in-world Pittsy's Book, and forward tease.
- Week 7 was 2:3 PDF; Week 8 target remains 9:16 and must be adapted rather than stretched.
- Week 8 editorial architecture A (Escalating Campaign) remains recommended, not Founder-locked. Maintain purposeful division pulse and GOTW foreshadowing.

## B. Character continuity and performance
**Mud Dogs:** Bobby Mitchell's massive rabid mud-covered dog/wolf beast, blue/orange markings, heavy chain, diseased teeth, violent swamp identity. Not a normal pet, not a human hunter. Hunting intelligence is conveyed through tracking behavior, scent and patient movement rather than a human rifle.
**Dr. Duckhook:** Zach Wilson's anthropomorphic white duck golfer, camouflage/floral bucket hat, golf outfit, cigar, golf-club/driver prosthetic-arm concept. Not an ordinary duck or human golfer. His absurd golf equipment is a character-specific obstacle and tool, not an excuse to change identity.
**Reference binding:** actual approved crop and manifest must be resolved from `canon/characters/VISUAL_REFERENCE_AUTHORITY_V1.json` before rendering. Existing local hash checks are not equivalent to renderer proof.
**Performance:** Mud Dogs begins predatory and assured; Duckhook initially improvises with a golfer's misplaced confidence. Both become vulnerable as visibility drops. Do not turn either into a generic action soldier.

## C. Narrative treatment: three-phase engine
**Act I — Tracks at Dusk:** The reader discovers the swamp at water level before either figure. Mud Dogs registers a disturbance; Duckhook's unusual equipment leaves identifiable traces. The hunt is physical, not a dialogue-heavy negotiation.
**Act II — The Narrowing Channel:** Mud Dogs follows scent and ground knowledge while Duckhook uses the water's reflective surface and obstructing timber. Each action closes or opens a believable route. Sunset reduces visual information and raises risk.
**Act III — The Last Light:** Both figures reach an exposed crossing. Silence, shallow water and the last orange reflection frame a decisive movement. The exact action and final image depend on verified fantasy scoring.
**Branch A — Mud Dogs home victory (conditional):** the hunter controls the final crossing; relief mixed with the toll of a difficult season. No invented scoreboard comeback.
**Branch B — Duckhook away victory (conditional):** the supposed quarry reverses the geometry of the hunt, leaving Mud Dogs alone with an emptied landscape. No fabricated physical injury or permanent swamp damage.
**Evidence insert:** player-level scoring, margin, final roster context and divisional implications must be populated from FLAIM after DECIDED; not guessed.

## E. Lighting and sound-implied visual rhythm
- Opening: late orange sidelight, visible water reflections, warm edge separation.
- Middle: orange sinks, water turns dark green/blue; fog collects in hollows but never hides both identities.
- Closing: blue-black twilight with a narrow last-light band. No arbitrary full-moon jump or impossible light-source changes.
- Sound cues for still imagery: disturbed reeds, chain tension, water rings, distant bird silhouettes. Avoid cartoon sound effects or explanatory text overlays.
- Halloween Easter egg: at most one plausible distant lantern or crow motif; no costumed figures or pumpkin centerpiece.

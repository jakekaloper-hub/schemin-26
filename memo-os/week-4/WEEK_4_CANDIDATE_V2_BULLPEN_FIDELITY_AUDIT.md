# V5.6 Week 4 Candidate V2 — Bullpen Fidelity Audit

**Artifact:** V56_WEEK4_PUBLICATION_FIDELITY_CANDIDATE_V2.png  
**Verdict:** FAIL — materially improved, but not best-attempt / benchmark-grade

## What improved

- bespoke full-page cinematic scene rather than hero + lower report panel;
- real reference-mounted ObiWan generation;
- foreground / midground / background depth;
- physical callback props;
- integrated deterministic typography and current Week 4 provider facts;
- 390px hierarchy;
- no championship belt;
- no generic dashboard or social-card structure.

## Why it still fails

1. **Scene thesis is too abstract.** “Six Roads Open” is an issue-level metaphor, not a singular sharp physical story hook comparable to Sonic delivery, barbershop humiliation, bridge jump, country-club Chili payoff, mountain wager, speedway survival, or weather invasion.
2. **Character canon drift remains.** Independent inspection found ObiWan hair reads light brown/sandy instead of canonical blond.
3. **The easiest page was chosen, not the hardest proof.** We avoided the two-character D0nkey–ObiWan matchup because current D0nkey renderer-addressable canon bytes were not available. That was safe, but it reduced the acceptance stress test.
4. **Benchmark references were analyzed but not visually mounted during generation.** The model received verbalized publication DNA, not representative Week 2/Week 3 page references as style/layout-quality anchors.
5. **Art and editorial design were still sequential.** The art was generated first, then a deterministic HTML typography system was imposed. The benchmark pages behave more like illustration and typography were planned as one editorial composition.
6. **No iterative art-direction loop before final composition.** One primary art generation was accepted into layout before a director-level compare/revise cycle against benchmark pages.
7. **Callback props are numerous but not all causal.** They enrich the scene, but several function as easter eggs instead of advancing one dominant story.
8. **No direct side-by-side benchmark review at full-size and 390px before acceptance.** Fidelity was judged by criteria, not by a controlled three-up comparison against representative Week 2 and Week 3 pages.
9. **The page still carries generic fantasy language risk.** Independent review found the scene legible as high-fantasy/Jedi imagery before it reads as uniquely Pro Schemin.
10. **Issue-level rhythm is not tested by one page.** Even a strong page cannot prove the Week 2/Week 3 publication cadence.

## Best-attempt standard

A true best attempt requires:
- representative Week 2 and Week 3 benchmark pages mounted as non-copying visual-quality references where tooling permits;
- a story hook that is specific enough to fail if team identities are swapped;
- all required active character canon bytes available for the chosen concept;
- at least one art-direct → regenerate/recompose loop;
- typography zones planned with the image composition before render;
- full-size + 390px side-by-side review against benchmark pages;
- independent fidelity approval after the final composed PNG, not just the art layer.

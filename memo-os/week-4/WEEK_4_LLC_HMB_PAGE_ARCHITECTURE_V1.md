# Week 4 LLC vs HMB — Best-Attempt Page Architecture V1

**Page archetype:** pregame confrontation / corporate throne-room collision
**Headline concept:** HOSTILE TERMS
**Subhead:** Some deals die at the signature line.

## Core story object
A thick acquisition agreement with brass seal and multiple signature lines lies between the two characters.

## Physical action
The LLC slides a fountain pen and contract across the rebuilt storm-scarred boardroom table.
The Belt Keeper refuses to sign and drives the tip of his black rune sword through the contract, pinning it to the table.

No winner implied. The conflict is a negotiation turned standoff.

## Consequence visible
- contract pierced;
- ink bottle knocked over;
- boardroom directors recoil;
- belt remains on HMB;
- LLC keeps cigar / composure rather than appearing defeated.

## Architecture

Headline zone:
upper-left / upper-center on a physical frosted-glass corporate wall or clean smoke/light zone.

Body-copy zone:
left column integrated as a cream legal memo sheet clipped to a board/portfolio, not a generic black card.

Fact/data zone:
bottom-right as a brass-edged corporate term sheet/market ticker placard, deterministically composited.

Character/action zone:
center-right, both characters at large scale across the boardroom table.

Foreground:
pierced contract, fountain pen, spilled ink, gold corporate seal, whiskey glass, broken storm-damaged folder edge.

Midground:
LLC seated/leaning forward in suit and shades; Belt Keeper standing opposite, sword pinning contract; championship belt visible.

Background:
LLC Boardroom after Week 3 Weather Event — repaired glass, storm marks/waterline residue, skyline beyond windows, board members/clerks, acquisition charts without readable generated text.

## Negative controls
- no generic fantasy castle;
- no throne room;
- no literal blood imagery;
- no winner pose;
- no generic hero poster;
- no detached lower-third;
- no generated readable text;
- no missing championship belt on HMB;
- no medieval redesign of LLC;
- no team-logo mascots.

## Text/image ratio
~82% image / 18% deterministic editorial text.

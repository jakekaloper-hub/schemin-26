## 3. Council doctrine applied

### Tolkien lens
Use inherited roads, domains, scars, landmarks and regional memory. Divisional Week should feel local because characters know the terrain and each other's worlds, not because divisions become artificial countries.

### Martin lens
Sunday is revealing ensemble power shifts. Each matchup should change the perceived balance inside its division. Avoid six isolated recap cards.

### Rowling lens
Recurring objects and institutions should carry changed meaning: bridge/lake memory, Dark Horse, Country Club, Pittsy tickets, homemade Beer Champion's Belt, weather signs. Reuse only when meaning changes.

### Grisham lens
Every scene must turn. Cut atmospheric setup once it has established pressure. Sunday now supplies enough dramatic shape that some earlier slow-burn setups should compress.

### Le Guin lens
Worldbuilding should be experienced through the character living inside it. Local culture, hospitality, roads, weather and institutions should be perceived, not explained in atlas prose.

### Sanderson lens
World rules must remain legible. Divisional overlays do not overwrite physical geography. Weather, institutions, travel and object consequences must obey established systems.

### Abercrombie lens
Sunday's best stories are human-scale reversals: silence after swagger, failed escape, reluctant ceremony, explanations collapsing, someone sitting beneath a storm. Cut spectacle that does not alter character.

## 4.1 BURGERS — D0nkey K0ng × ObiWan Jacoby

### Existing concepts
- **The Last Easy Day**
- **The Heater Becomes a Target**
- Conditional DK giant/tower dominance visual if DK won in statement fashion.

### Sunday ruling
**DK tower-dominance branch: LIKELY DEAD unless SNF/MNF produces an extraordinary reversal.**

**The Last Easy Day: STRENGTHENED, but its meaning changed.**

The strongest current story is no longer "Can Obi prepare enough?" It is:

### New primary candidate — **THE HEATER BREAKS**
Obi's ordinary confidence — gym, lake, kamado, deliberate preparation — now reads as the calm before a controlled dismantling of DK's undefeated mythology.

DK's Week 3 battle wear now has causal value: it can become the visual residue of what undefeated life costs, not merely a cool continuity overlay.

### Consultant notes
- **Abercrombie:** the strongest DK image may be absence of swagger. No roaring defeat pose; show the moment the heater stops producing heat.
- **Le Guin:** use Obi's environment to show why preparation feels normal to him rather than heroic.
- **Tolkien:** Burgers roads / Upper Valleys should make this feel like a local power changing hands, not a generic arena.
- **Martin:** if Obi finishes the win, this is a divisional power shift, not just DK's first loss.
- **Grisham:** compress DK's pregame mythology quickly; Sunday already supplied the turn.

### Strong alternatives
1. **The Heater Breaks** — myth punctured.
2. **No Easy Days Left** — Obi's leisure/preparation becomes proof of discipline.
3. **The Quiet Challenger** — victory expressed through calm, not conquest.

### World / Atlas opportunity
Use Upper Valleys familiarity. Both occupy Burgers-connected mountain geography. A route, overlook, bridge memory or familiar ridge can make the result feel local: everyone in the region knows where the unbeaten force fell.

Do not create a new battlefield.

### Character consequence if current shape holds
- **Obi:** preparation validated; confidence becomes earned rather than assumed.
- **DK:** undefeated identity ends; Week 3 damage becomes consequence, not excuse.

### Kill condition
Major late DK comeback that meaningfully overturns both score and tone.

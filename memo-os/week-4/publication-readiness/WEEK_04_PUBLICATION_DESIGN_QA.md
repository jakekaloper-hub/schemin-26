# Week 4 Publication Design QA

**Status:** PREPARED / BLOCKED_BY_RENDER  
**Authority:** Publication Design + Scout

Per-page checks:
- correct score;
- correct record;
- correct team name;
- correct owner/team association;
- spelling;
- number formatting;
- headline hierarchy;
- body-copy density;
- caption size;
- safe margins;
- crop safety;
- phone-size readability;
- art:text balance;
- no placeholder text;
- no duplicated data;
- no generative arithmetic;
- no unreadable deterministic overlay.

Issue-wide:
- page numbering;
- repeated layout grammar;
- transition rhythm;
- visual pacing;
- PDF-safe typography.

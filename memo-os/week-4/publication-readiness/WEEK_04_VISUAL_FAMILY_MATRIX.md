## Thumbnail rule

For every three-page matchup:
- setup, escalation and payoff must remain distinguishable at **120 px page height**;
- if two pages collapse to the same silhouette/composition at thumbnail scale, production stops before rendering the second page.

## Result-page family

Pages 7, 10, 13, 16, 19 and 22 share:
- same bottom result-strip geometry;
- same data hierarchy;
- same deterministic score typography;
- matchup-specific accent/portrait treatment only.

This creates issue consistency without making the storytelling repetitive.

## 1. Writing failure diagnosis

Page 2 failed because the packet contained competent thematic prose rather than a real opening. It explained divisional week, summarized the world, and repeated concepts the visuals already communicated. The same risk appeared elsewhere wherever the packet used labels such as “prose job,” “consequence,” or “pressure” without committing to actual reader-facing sentences.

The deeper defect was architectural: Week 4 had a detailed production packet but **no single authoritative reader-facing manuscript**. Production therefore had to improvise wording page by page. That invited generic connective prose and inconsistent voice.

The recovery separates:
- production instructions;
- deterministic data;
- actual reader-facing copy.

Only the final manuscript contains publishable prose.

# WEEK 4 — SCOREBOARD DERIVATIVE ASSEMBLY RULE
**Applies to:** W4-P03 / final publication page 3
**State:** REQUIRED FOR WEEK 4 REPAIR + WEEK 5 LESSON

## Core rule

The Week 4 scoreboard must be built **after the six matchup payoff/result pages are final**.

The scoreboard is an assembly product, not a fresh character-generation surface.

## Preferred technical method

1. crop the character portrait/face from the accepted payoff raster;
2. preserve the exact pixels where practical;
3. place those crops into the deterministic scoreboard row;
4. typeset team name, record, score, margin and DEF. independently;
5. never regenerate a face merely to make it fit the board.

If a payoff page lacks a usable face crop:
- use the exact canonical character reference bound to that matchup;
- match the payoff-page costume/state;
- require Character QA before placement.

## Why

This prevents:
- scoreboard-first character drift;
- early incorrect costumes/states;
- facial mismatch between scoreboard and matchup chapters;
- twelve simultaneous character generations on one page.

## Week 5 control

Move scoreboard production to the end of the matchup production cycle even though it remains near the front of the publication.

**FINAL SPINE POSITION IS NOT PRODUCTION ORDER.**

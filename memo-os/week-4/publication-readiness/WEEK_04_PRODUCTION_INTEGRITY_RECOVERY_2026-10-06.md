# WEEK 4 PRODUCTION-INTEGRITY RECOVERY RULING

**Status:** PRODUCTION HOLD / RELEASE-BLOCKING
**Issue:** #131
**Commissioner trigger:** repeated Pages 1–4 failures during manual generation
**Controlling principle:** Jake is not the first QA layer.

## Bullpen verdict

Week 4 is **not production-ready** merely because story architecture is locked.

The Sunday planning work correctly solved story/page architecture, but Tuesday live production exposed that the render-facing packet and gate did not mechanically preserve those decisions.

This is a control-plane failure.

## Evidence from Week 3

The immutable Week 3 release-forensics manifest identifies the released issue as `Pro_Schemin_Week_3_Memo_Final.pdf`, 21 pages.

Week 3's production doctrine required:

`OWNER → CANONICAL CHARACTER → CURRENT VERIFIED TEAM NAME`

and:

`FACT LOCK → OWNER RESOLUTION → CHARACTER_PACKET_LOCK → REFERENCE-ATTACHED GENERATION → CHARACTER QA → PAGE QA → PUBLICATION LOCK`

Week 3 post-production explicitly concluded:
- the issue-level publication architecture worked;
- character canon had to be enforced on the actual raster;
- critical numeric information required deterministic composition;
- QA doctrine existed but enforcement failed;
- Week 4 needed **harder evidence-bound gates**, not more prose doctrine.

The Week 3 regression suite further states:
- PASS without an evidence pointer is invalid;
- scores/records/standings/rankings/odds use deterministic typography;
- exact page masters require approval state and durable evidence;
- aesthetic approval cannot waive CHARACTER, FACT, CONTINUITY or MOBILE QA.

## Root causes

1. **Internal IDs were not firewalled from publishable copy.**
2. **Packet prose was treated as sufficient execution authority instead of explicit render fields.**
3. **Week 3 publication precedent was consulted narratively but not encoded as a hard production contract.**
4. **Critical text/data were still allowed inside generative rendering instead of deterministic composition.**
5. **Character QA was allowed to pass on semantic description without proving the current approved visual reference reached the renderer.**
6. **Candidate visibility was incorrectly treated as candidate acceptance.**
7. **Page 2 was architecturally underwritten: it described "divisional week" instead of opening this specific issue.**

## Mandatory recovery controls

### A. Publishable identity firewall
Use `WEEK_04_PUBLISHABLE_IDENTITY_REGISTRY.json`.

Internal aliases such as DK, OBI, TDS, HMB, 7DC, SLOB, LLC are production-only identifiers.

They may never appear as team names in final art unless the page explicitly calls for a non-team-label shorthand in prose.

### B. Exact division authority
- Burgers: ObiWan Jacoby; D0nkey K0ng; Three Dreaded Snake; Mud Dogs.
- Wings: Red Leopards; Slob on my Dobb; The Chili Cheesers; Dr. Duckhook.
- Pizza: The LLC; His Majesty's Blood; El Niño; Seven Deadly Chins.

Wings means **chicken wings**. Never bird/angel/dragon wings.

### C. Week 3 precedent inheritance
Week 3 Page 18, **SIX ROADS FORWARD**, proved that forward schedule/world information should be integrated into lived geography.

Week 3 Page 19 explicitly designated **D0nkey K0ng vs ObiWan Jacoby** as the Week 4 **GAME OF THE WEEK**, with:
- both principals visually prominent;
- entering records;
- dedicated environment;
- elevated treatment separate from ordinary matchups.

Therefore Week 4 Pages 4–7 inherit GAME OF THE WEEK status as a persistent chapter-level badge/treatment.

### D. Critical text and data firewall
Scores, records, team names, division names, GOTW labels, rankings, odds, ledgers, dates and issue labels must be deterministic composition.

Image generation supplies illustration/background only.

No page may be accepted because an image model "spelled it close enough."

### E. Character reference fail-closed
Current `VISUAL_REFERENCE_AUTHORITY_V1.json` controls.

A character-bearing render is not production-authorized unless the current approved reference is actually available to the renderer.

Prose descriptions and historical artwork are not substitutes.

If reference mount/binding cannot be proven in the active production path:

`CHARACTER_REFERENCE_BINDING_UNPROVEN / DO NOT GENERATE CHARACTER PAGE`

Page 2 must operate as an **issue overture** that opens the six Week 4 stories and makes the persistent world feel lived-in.

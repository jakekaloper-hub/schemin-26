# Cinematic Scene Direction — Sandbox Previs Acceptance Fixtures

**Authority:** non-canon simulation; these are synthetic geography and staging data, not approved Commonwealth locations or evidence of published results.

## Example A — Action at a threshold

Story beat: A defender prevents an intruder crossing a stone passage. No named owner, league score, or canonical character is asserted.

**Baseline:** subjects both front-facing in an unspecified wide view; armor/props/atmosphere compete equally; action direction unclear.
**Proposed:** defender holds near side at camera left; opponent enters from far right; their eyelines and weight shifts converge across the passage. No contact until the decisive instant. Camera is offset behind defender at a moderate distance; a single motivated lateral light distinguishes silhouettes. Foreground stone barrier, midground action, background constrained approach. Text reserve upper left.

**QA targets:** narrative readable without decorative props; geography/silhouette/staging coherence; no implied owner or championship iconography; no generated text.

## Example B — Quiet aftermath

Story beat: An empty council chamber after a consequential disagreement. Character-free due to outstanding source reference/generation gate.

**Baseline:** richly decorated symmetric room, equal emphasis on banners, furniture and architecture.
**Proposed:** an abandoned chair slightly off-center, a single cold daylight entry, distant empty council seats. Camera at human working height, negative space on right for editorial layout, textured historical architecture retained but subordinate to the abandoned chair.

**QA targets:** scene emotion without fictitious character; purposeful environment and light; approved Atlas source required before any canonical staging or render.

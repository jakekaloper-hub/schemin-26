## Locked core

- **Chili Cheesers hosts** at Chili's Western Tablelands / Chili Stable domain.
- Chili is the **Chili Outlaw**, with his established companion **The Dark Horse**. This is his home territory.
- **El Niño is an arriving stranger, not the sheriff, a lawman, or a bounty hunter.**
- Dark clouds and thunder accompany El Niño's arrival, disrupting the sunny, dusty frontier town and alarming the Dark Horse.
- Chili confronts El Niño over the disruption. The altercation becomes personal and escalates to an old-West **high-noon shootout**.
- The visual climax preserves the empty main street, clock approaching noon, and a conditional final image with the defeated character seen from the rear/over-right-shoulder foreground and the winner at a distance with a smoking revolver. No gore and no predetermined winner.
- The **Story Overture is written after verified Week 5 results from the eventual victor's POV**, without prematurely revealing the victory.

| **3** | **Harry Potter-Inspired Wizard Chess** | Winner delivers checkmate | Opponent reverses the board and checkmates them |
| **4** | **High-Noon Shoot-Off** | Gunslinger wins the draw | Rival wins the draw |
| **5** | **Animal Control vs. Rabid Dog** | Animal Control captures the dog | Dog escapes and defeats/humiliates Animal Control |

## Core Structure

Six distinct matchup worlds, each established before the matchup, with two possible postgame story directions determined by the winner.

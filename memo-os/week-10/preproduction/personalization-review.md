## Lewis method — consequence that belongs to this character and occasion

### L1 — ADAPT: birthday and fatherhood must drive distinct comic mechanisms

**Duck:** the humiliation should depend on this being his birthday. Let the celebration's cake, presentation space and guests become stages of the action: anticipation, failed recovery of dignity, and the party witnessing the payoff. Establish that Duck is formidable first; HMB's dominance is then an emphatic reversal of the host's expected occasion. **HOLD** a generic fight with an untouched cake in the background. No invented age, birth year, named guest or attributed real-life toast.

**TDS:** use audible timing: noise; a precarious pause; hope that the baby has settled; a louder restart. Give the three heads distinct responses—anger, pleading, listening—within one body. Chins chooses the next noisy act outside the family space. **ACCEPT** the user-directed exhausted-father performance. **HOLD** jokes that make the child the target or explain real fantasy results through neglect, fatigue or parenting. The baby's arrival is a personal milestone, not sports causation.

**Sources:** direct user personalization; Week 9 `Closed for Something Bigger`; no additional private family detail is authorized or needed.

### L2 — ADAPT: defeat needs an attempted answer, not only a larger explosion

**Obi:** give each counteroffensive an intelligible immediate objective—break an assault line, cross exposed ground, reach the commander—then show why the LLC's response defeats it. The green blade must do visible work. This makes overwhelming pressure legible and gives the ending consequence without reverting to reflective tabletop symbolism. The eventual winner branch still waits for Monday's result; an alternate outcome must resolve the same attempted actions.

**Slob:** Mud's resistance can remain fierce without ever making the match feel equal. Successive failed attempts should cost position, footing or composure. **ACCEPT** the user's overwhelming-dominance feeling. **HOLD** literal zero-probability language, a fabricated zero score, or an invented injury. The personality is in how each contestant responds to the imbalance.

**Sources:** personalization intake; current MNF fact packet; active character morphology; exact historic LLC/Mud–Slob receipts as context only.

### L3 — ACCEPT bounded embarrassment; ADAPT the meaning of the witnesses

**Chili:** his own hosted spectacle should become the instrument of his embarrassment. The crowd's presence matters because he expected to command its attention. Show a change in their response through the rope exchange, then a clear final view of the recognizable outlaw hogtied in the mud. The earlier 2025 loss makes his desire to reverse the relationship specific; it was a consolation game, not an old championship elimination.

**Red:** the cold ending should feel like recognition of an opponent who has overturned his confidence before. Keep the fear in bodily decisions and reaction, rather than announcing that his season is over. **HOLD** “eliminated,” “Niño did it again,” or a completed 2026 revenge payoff until the current result is verified. Even after a defeat, do not equate a weekly loss with a clinched postseason consequence.

**Sources:** `HIST-2025-W16-T05-T07`; `HIST-2022-W16-T02-T09`; Week 9 public humiliation/territorial continuity; current Week 10 unresolved outcome fields.

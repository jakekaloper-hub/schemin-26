## 3. The world has been lived in

For each set, build four distinguishable layers:

1. **Long-used fabric:** worn thresholds, softened rails, mineral stains, smoke above a lamp and wood polished by hands.
2. **Maintenance and adaptation:** a replacement hinge, a repaired roof edge, a reupholstered chair, reusable arena rails and cleared drains.
3. **Verified story scars:** Red’s recent goal and terrace damage, the swamp’s old racing rut, the unresolved truck situation and the retained belt.
4. **This week’s disturbance:** fresh streamers, opened wrapping, frosting, new hoof furrows, wet footprints and battlefield scorch.

The inhabitants already know their routines. Club staff close familiar shutters while dinner service continues. Event attendants tension canvas. Archivists move records under cover. A household keeps its dry threshold swept. These proposed ordinary actions make the world feel functioning and established. They do not invent historical victories, construction years or new principal characters.

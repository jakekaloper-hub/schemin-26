## Two corrections to the production record

### 1. A repeated score was wrong

Physical page 22, **One Still Standing**, visibly prints **D0nkey K0ng 172.48 / The LLC 141.86**. The Week 9 scoreboard and the freshly checked provider result agree on **D0nkey K0ng 158.70 / The LLC 129.89**. The latter is the factual result.

The Week 10 packet therefore gives every score module a selector into a single facts file. Before a rendered page is accepted, compare **every visible score occurrence** against that file, including scores embedded in illustrations. A correct main scoreboard alone is insufficient. The approved historical PDF is preserved; this review does not silently replace it.

### 2. The omitted page was a reading position, not the matching canonical ID

The omitted **planned reading-position 32** was **The Race to Seven**, canonical **W09-P40**. It was the redundant seed-versus-projected-rank department. Canonical **W09-P32**, **The Horizon Has Other Plans**, remains physical page 9. The original 35-page plan became a 34-page delivered issue by omitting Race to Seven.

Week 10 uses stable IDs independent of reading order. It does not restore the omitted department or inherit a 35-page quota.

## Visual and editorial decisions applied now

1. Retain portrait, full-bleed scenes, black/gold/burgundy editorial treatment, thin ornamental framing and autumn atmosphere where locally appropriate.
2. Stage each matchup with an establishing beat, an action that changes the situation, and a consequence. Vary wide, close, overhead and quiet reaction shots.
3. Place the score on the payoff page. The image conveys posture, spatial cause and emotional consequence; the copy supplies football context and what cannot be read from the picture.
4. Preserve the no-label world landscape. A fresh caption does not justify a new map or unapproved geography.
5. Retain Week 9 damage and character state: Red's damaged terrace, Chins's trapped truck, Mud's old ruts, HMB's retained belt, DK's four-legged body, TDS's three heads and Chili's later appearance override. The proposed partial repairs and Chins arriving on foot belong to the new Week 10 scene draft.
6. Use actual reference bytes. All 12 current crop hashes were checked; Chili's Oct 22 original screenshot was recovered and its hash matched the existing correction record.
7. Reduce density where it competes with art. The production contract specifies body-copy budgets and a 390-pixel-wide phone check after rendering.
8. New page masters must be native **1080 × 1920 PNGs**. The existing PDF's historical geometry is documented without treating it as permission to stretch a new image or reject a historical accepted page retroactively.

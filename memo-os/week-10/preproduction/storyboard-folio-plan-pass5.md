## Production contract
- One individual 1080×1920 grayscale pencil/ink storyboard per approved folio, with actual scene, blocking, camera direction, set landmarks, continuity notes, and title. No final sports scores or unsupported statistics; leave those areas physically blank.
- Render 34 individual sketches **first**; assemble six separate 3×2 contact sheets only after the six source sketches exist. Contact sheets are review artifacts, not substitutes for individual full-page artwork.
- No unrequested new imagery during planning. No generic whole-world montage in place of an assigned folio. Page-specific visual geography and characters must match exact source asset bindings.
- Inklings-style narrative gate: causal action, memory, viewpoint, humor, stakes, no invented results. Twelve-seat production gate: set, environment, identity, costume, acting, blocking, silhouette, frame, VFX, color, edit, sound. Librarian owns IDs and source provenance. Consultant review remains simulated unless actually authenticated.

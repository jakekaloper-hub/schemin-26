# Week 10 — Commissioner personalization direction

**Received:** November 16, 2026, after the first pre-production packet.  
**Source:** Jake's direct instructions in this conversation.  
**Authority:** accepted creative direction for this revision. Detailed copy and page execution remain drafts; sports finality remains a separate provider fact.

## The change in dramatic scale

The revised scenes make the scoring margins and personal context visible. Strong winners exert sustained pressure; the losing characters react in ways specific to their identity and history. The birthday, new baby and remembered playoff defeat are central story mechanisms. The scenes should read as six different episodes, with a clear setup, forceful escalation and an earned consequence.

No final art is requested in this pass. Existing page IDs and three-beat matchup slots are retained so the personal direction improves the current issue instead of creating a second competing draft.

## Six accepted directions

### ObiWan Jacoby vs The LLC — a Jedi-versus-Empire battle

Replace the quiet table contest with an unmistakably Star Wars cinematic action sequence: Jedi resistance against an Imperial assault, explosive impacts, powerful movement, an active counterattack and overwhelming pressure. ObiWan's green blade and recognizable identity remain central. The LLC commands the assault as the recognizable executive; a generic masked villain must not replace him. The two remaining Monday starters can motivate two visible counteroffensives, both actually attempted in the defeat branch.

### His Majesty's Blood vs Dr. Duckhook — the birthday is the target

Jake supplied that this matchup fell on Dr. Duckhook's birthday. Stage the confrontation at the Country Club of Jackson's birthday celebration. Both opponents should first read as formidable, followed by HMB's emphatic dominance and a spectacularly embarrassing birthday payoff. Celebration props, guests and the cake should participate in the action rather than function as generic background decoration. Do not invent an age, birth year, named guest or attributed real-life quote.

### Mud Dogs vs Slob on my Dobb — sustained domination

Show Slob's overwhelming physical superiority through successive, readable actions. Mud Dogs should not appear to be winning an even contest until a clever trick suddenly reverses it. The swamp, old ruts, exposed roots and Slob's keg remain useful identity and geography anchors. The user's “zero chance” emphasis governs the dramatic feeling, not a fabricated mathematical win probability or a literal zero score.

### Three Dreaded Snake vs Seven Deadly Chins — impossible quiet

Carry forward the new baby's arrival celebrated in Week 9. At Three Dreaded Snake's home, Chins makes an escalating, deliberately obnoxious racket that keeps restarting the exhausted father's frustration just as the baby begins to settle. Three heads create comic performance opportunities: anger, pleading and desperate listening must still belong to one body. Keep the baby and family space outside the physical contest; the joke is Chins's noise and the father's inability to restore quiet.

### Red Leopards vs El Niño — November and an old defeat return

Use Red Leopard Ruins, the verified home domain. The storm is a cold autumn catastrophe: hard wind, stripped leaves, frost, grey daylight, buckling temporary repairs and bleak visibility. Avoid repeating the earlier flood mechanism. Red's personal experience is recognition and dread as a prior lost opportunity appears to return. Research the remembered historical defeat before stating its season or consequence. His fear of a slipping season is distinct from an official playoff-elimination claim.

### The Chili Cheesers vs D0nkey K0ng — public rodeo humiliation

Keep this as the Game of the Week. Stage a large, muddy rodeo spectacle with a visibly present crowd. Let both warriors use their distinctive tools and bodies: Chili's outlaw rope skill meets the Arsenal gorilla-centaur's braced four-legged strength and controlled block. D0nkey turns the rope exchange against Chili and leaves him hogtied in the center of the mud for the entire stadium to see. The result is an athletic, comic humiliation; the hat, sunglasses, coat and separate Dark Horse remain recognizable. Temporary event stands at the neutral grounds do not silently become a new permanent city or replace the established Atlas.

## Historical research required for every pairing

Each revised story receives a source-backed prior-matchup receipt and an explanation of how that history changes the scene. Check historical owner/name continuity before assigning a renamed team to its current character. Identify consolation games accurately. Do not call a retrieved result the most recent meeting when later-season coverage is incomplete, and do not turn a bounded sample into an all-time rivalry record.

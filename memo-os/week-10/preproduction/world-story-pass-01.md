# Schemin ’26 — Week 10: Commonwealth Story & Location Pass 1

**Status: brainstorming for Jake’s review.** Prepared November 16, 2026, before Monday’s remaining game. This pass reopens story, location, department placement and copy development. Existing artist briefs are useful candidates, not approved production locks. The matchup endings below describe the current leading-result story branch; final sports results still control the published issue.

**Visual destination:** cohesive, full-page cinematic 1080 × 1920 PNG illustrations. Action, environment, text, light and factual lettering are conceived together. This file is a backstage working brief; its headings and tables are not a memo-page template.

## 1. What the Atlas and production review established

All six encounters already have a Commonwealth anchor. Five are established home domains; the Compact Neutral Grounds is a registered provisional neutral venue appropriate to the Game of the Week. The missing creative work is the precise stage inside each place: how someone arrives, where the confrontation happens, what surrounds it, how the light behaves, and what remains afterward.

We should deepen those domains through proposed local sites. Every new name below is a working label. No new active location record, permanent building, territorial claim or approved environment image is created by this brief. The checked location cards do not currently bind approved durable environment reference images; that is a reason to develop clear environments now, not a reason to stop brainstorming.

The production and Inklings reviews converge on three principles:

- **The place changes the action.** The ridge offers Obi a second attack; the veranda rail turns Duck’s retreat into a birthday catastrophe; the temple stone makes Chins’s interruption sharper; the swamp bank measures Slob’s advance.
- **Each defender protects something personal.** Home, a celebration, home advantage, family peace, hope and public reputation give six very different emotional stakes.
- **The world connects through roads, materials and news.** It does not require a single storm, a single hour, or every character visiting the same set.

The current scoring headline, **The Ceiling Breaks**, can coexist with a working story thesis: **Power gets personal.** That phrase is a discussion tool, not an approved cover line.

## 2. The six story locations

### A. ObiWan Jacoby vs The LLC — “Unlimited Firepower”

**Reminder.** An explosive Jedi-versus-Empire encounter. Obi reflects the assault back into one battery, makes a second aggressive move along the ridge, and is forced toward the inner threshold by the remaining Imperial firepower. The LLC stays recognizable in his executive suit, red tie and sunglasses. The scene is active from its opening image.

**Existing anchor:** Trade Jedi Mountain Base, in the Upper Valleys and Foothills. The registered high ridge and war-room overlook give us the vertical structure. The Eastern Ascent connects this area to the Mountain Lake Bridge.

**Proposed local site: the Ridge Gate.** An exposed road arrives below the high ridge, crosses a short defensible approach and turns into a sheltered inner arch. A ridge shelf provides the second attack route. The war-room overlook occupies the higher rock mass. The attacking command position and two initial batteries are visibly established across the approach.

**Make it lived in and scalable.** Worn stone treads, handrails, supply scuffs, a sheltered lantern and a usable doorway suggest a place people normally enter. Troop files, masonry and the retriever behind the arch establish size. At wide scale we see the ridge and tactical routes; at medium scale we understand battery, commander and gate; at close scale the same chipped coping and lantern remain recognizable. These details are proposals, not inherited architectural facts.

**Baked-in light.** Cool late-day sky gives the stone its base exposure. A warm gate lantern marks the protected interior. The green blade and red volleys create moving reflected color. Once the first emplacement burns, its orange fire becomes a fixed source that persists in later views. The remaining battery continues firing; it cannot multiply back into two active emplacements.

**Personal depth and consequence.** Obi fights to preserve agency and a threshold that matters to him. The retriever supplies a quiet personal reason underneath the explosive action. Local scorch marks and the wrecked temporary battery can carry into a selected aftermath; ownership of the mountain remains unchanged unless a later approved story establishes otherwise.

**Historical fuel:** the verified 2024 Week 12 meeting was a 0.80-point LLC win. That makes the overwhelming firepower a useful contrast with an old razor-thin result, without presenting that game as a playoff elimination.

**Issue connections.** Opening Drive introduces home under pressure. The Bridge landscape establishes the upland road network without promising a view of the hidden base. Sports Desk can lead into this chapter through the scoring behind LLC’s surge. The closing remembers surviving resistance, not a conquered Commonwealth.

**Pass 2 alternatives:** emphasize the epic elevation of the second assault, or make the arch the recurring emotional reference point. Recommended trial: combine the ridge’s tactical clarity with the arch’s personal stakes, keeping both counterattacks vigorous.

### B. Dr. Duckhook vs His Majesty’s Blood — “Many Unhappy Returns”

**Reminder.** Two powerhouses clash at Duck’s birthday celebration. Duck has one impressive club-versus-sword answer. HMB’s sustained pressure drives him backward, the established low rail catches his heel, and he falls directly into the stationary cake table. The birthday guests witness the entire humiliation.

**Existing anchor:** Country Club of Jackson, in the Southern Wetlands and Delta. Clubhouse, golf grounds and water hazards are established. The visible lowland context can include the wetland tree line and levee route.

**Proposed local site: the Clubhouse Celebration Veranda.** A covered veranda opens to a presentation terrace overlooking maintained greens and water. Guests flank the action rather than blocking its sightline. The low rail and braked cake table are present from the first view. A side service approach explains how the celebration was prepared and how it can be cleaned up.

**Make it lived in and scalable.** Worn service-door hardware, orderly chair spacing, trays and a cleared presentation area make this a functioning hospitality space. Doors, guests and cake tiers give human scale; HMB’s stance supplies the intimidating scale. The cake stays where it was placed. Its frame buckles on impact rather than rolling inexplicably into the scene.

**Baked-in light.** Warm late-afternoon light reaches the open side of the terrace; roof shade provides depth. Clubhouse lamps, celebration lanterns and candles remain motivated sources. The key proposal is to keep the cheerful light alive after the collision. The ruined celebration stays brightly visible to its witnesses.

**Personal depth and consequence.** Duck has arranged an occasion in which he should be the center of attention. HMB takes control of that occasion through force. A streamer caught on HMB’s shoulder is an optional small joke; his retained belt and core costume stay consistent. Frosting, broken furniture and embarrassed guests are local aftermath, with no need to destroy the estate.

**Historical fuel:** HMB’s verified former identity, The Immortal, beat Duck by 0.40 in the 2025 Week 16 semifinal. This carries the memory of a close high-stakes defeat into a far more conspicuous birthday embarrassment.

**Issue connections.** The Commissioner’s Note can frame competitive memory and personal occasions. The transition from wild swamp to controlled club grounds gives the Southern Wetlands more than one face. Sports Desk can follow this clash before the mountain battle, connecting the two scoring spectacles through actual player contributions.

**Pass 2 alternatives:** HMB occupies the place where Duck expected his birthday recognition, or the celebration keeps awkwardly trying to continue between exchanges. Recommended trial: preserve the strong duel and use the interrupted presentation for the final humiliation.

### C. Mud Dogs vs Slob on my Dobb — “No Close Finish”

**Reminder.** Slob dominates from first contact. The keg stops the initial charge; each attempted counter costs Mud more ground. The ending leaves the hound below the bank with the retreat visibly cut into his own terrain.

**Existing anchor:** Mud Dogs Swamp, in the Southern Wetlands and Delta. Mud channels and raised ground are established. Falls Path connects toward the temple; Delta Causeway connects toward Jackson through Mire Hill.

**Proposed local site: Rootbank Crossing.** A firm natural berm passes an exposed root beside a shallow feeder channel. An older racing rut pinches the usable approach. The same strip of ground carries all three beats, making the domination measurable.

**Make it lived in and scalable.** Local tracks, old ruts, rubbed roots, bent reeds and a familiar dry route show how a creature that knows this swamp would move. Slob’s feet stay on supported ground. The keg works as a pressure shield, with its metal rim and weight visible; it is not a floating raft. Mud remains a fierce quadrupedal hound, pushed back rather than transformed into a generic humanoid fighter.

**Baked-in light.** Low amber light enters through a specific canopy opening. A shallow channel reflects cooler sky into the lower bodies. Dark foliage creates depth behind the encounter. As Slob advances along supported ground, Mud is forced lower into the churn, but the hound’s face and contact with the terrain remain readable.

**Personal depth and consequence.** The painful part is that this should be Mud’s advantage. He knows the footing, tries the familiar route and still cannot stop the advance. Continuous paw furrows and disturbed peat record each failed answer. This mud is dark wetland peat, distinct from the rodeo’s churned basin loam.

**Historical fuel:** Slob’s verified 2025 Week 17 consolation win over Mud was only 3.15 points. The proposed current scene deliberately loses that old closeness; it does not reclassify the old consolation game as a title contest.

**Issue connections.** Opening Drive must include this matchup. A scoring-form or power-ranking discussion after this chapter could challenge the reader’s assumption that only the two largest weekly totals matter. Any such display needs its chosen metric named clearly; the current form index is not automatically the editorial power ranking.

**Pass 2 alternatives:** a shrinking strip of firm ground, or a longer retreat down the old rut. Recommended trial: the firm bank gives the clearest physical mechanism; one wider image can reveal the full retreat track.

### D. Three Dreaded Snake vs Seven Deadly Chins — “An Encore Nobody Ordered”

**Reminder.** Chins trolls a new father at home. One metallic interruption, a hopeful pause, a hand reaching for the horn, then an even louder encore. Three heads on one shared body register three different versions of frustration. The baby stays offscreen.

**Existing anchor:** Podium Shadow Waterfall Temple, in the Southern Wetlands and Delta. Waterfall, temple stone and dense vegetation are established.

**Proposed local site: the Sheltered Court.** The public approach climbs worn steps to a stone landing. A recessed, closed family door separates the guest area from the private interior. Water and foliage remain visible to one side at a believable distance. Chins’s barrel, lids and carried horn occupy the hard outer court.

**Make it lived in and scalable.** Dry swept stone near the door, a small lamp, weathered steps and a handcart place domestic life within the temple. Door scale anchors the shared serpent body. The three heads can watch the visitor, listen toward the door and plead for restraint while their one body cannot pursue all three impulses.

**Baked-in light and sound.** Cool reflected sky and waterfall spray light the outer court. A warm line of lamplight beneath the door stays stable. The waterfall provides predictable, continuous sound the family normally lives with. Chins’s sudden irregular clangs and horn blasts cut through that pattern. The pause means he briefly stops his percussion; the waterfall does not fall silent.

**Personal depth and consequence.** The emotional target is fragile household peace. A hard stone return wall can sharpen the interruption without turning the temple into a magical sound machine. Chins arrives with carried props; the Week 9 truck problem is not silently repaired. Loose lids and the eventual return of ordinary waterfall sound provide sufficient aftermath.

**Historical fuel:** TDS won both retrieved 2025 meetings, in Weeks 3 and 14. That history gives Chins an extra reason to enjoy an obnoxious answer this week without claiming a complete all-time rivalry record.

**Issue connections.** Opening Drive can hint at the family threshold. Commissioner copy can acknowledge a new life chapter without claiming private attendance or dialogue. Falls Path offers a genuine landscape transition toward Mud’s terrain. A Moves page at Chins’s home commons does not require Chins to be pictured there simultaneously.

**Pass 2 alternatives:** foreground the malicious rhythm against the waterfall, or let Chins discover how effectively a lid rattles against stone. Recommended trial: use the first as the story mechanism and the second as a small physical beat.

### E. Red Leopards vs El Niño — “That Cold Again”

**Reminder.** Red recognizes a familiar threat to a season that seemed to be building. His overcareful repairs fail under successive cold gusts. He retreats to a high perch beside his torn banner, afraid that the old collapse is happening again.

**Existing anchor:** Red Leopard Ruins, in the Eastern Forest and Storm Coast. Jungle ruins and predator banners are established. The reviewed Week 9 terrace and goal damage provide the newer scene history.

**Proposed local site: the Windward Banner Terrace.** The damaged pitch and goal sit on an exposed stepped terrace. Two repair points lie along one continuous action ground. A narrow stone exit rises to the banner ledge. Dense vegetation persists below the escarpment, giving the exposed upper ruins their lonely height.

**Make it lived in and scalable.** Patchwork braces, reused boards, old fastenings and familiar wear show Red trying to keep home functional. The first frame establishes what he has repaired and which detail he checks again. The same goal, banner pole, stepped stone and route to the perch orient every later image.

**Baked-in light.** Flat November overcast removes direct sun. Cold sky reflects from exposed metal, frost and old masonry. Red and the torn crimson banner retain the strongest color. An optional small repair lantern could be the last warm point; the existing banner alone may be cleaner and more distinctive. Niño remains embodied and recognizable, with restrained elemental light.

**Personal depth and consequence.** This is fear of repetition. The cold is dry and cutting: brittle leaves, crosswind, frost on exposed fittings, failed repairs and a stripped terrace. The forest below stays dense. The scene does not need another flood, a dead continent or an alpine replacement for Red’s established home. Red’s concern about depletion and his future belongs to his viewpoint; it is not a certified elimination or a new medical claim.

**Historical fuel:** El Niño beat 13–1, first-seeded Red in the 2022 Week 16 semifinal, after Red had won their two regular-season meetings. That specific reversal supplies the memory. The present scene can echo the feeling without inventing an unseen historical set or flashback.

**Issue connections.** Opening Drive frames recognition; the playoff-road page distinguishes fear from actual mathematical position; the closing can send Red toward his verified next opponent, TDS, carrying uncertainty rather than declaring his season over.

**Pass 2 alternatives:** track a dwindling repair light, or repeat the movement of the established predator banner. Recommended trial: the banner. It ties the emotion to a known home feature and keeps the scene visually direct.

### F. The Chili Cheesers vs D0nkey K0ng — “The Main Attraction”

**Reminder.** The Game of the Week becomes a public humiliation. Chili throws the rope; the integrated gorilla-centaur blocks with the axe haft, grounds the axe, takes the running line and pulls the still-gripping outlaw into the mud. The deliberate binding is shown clearly enough to earn the center-ring hogtie. The crowd sees the reversal and laughs.

**Existing anchor:** Compact Neutral Grounds, a registered provisional venue in the Central River Basin. Open ceremonial field, arbiter pavilion and arrival roads are established.

**Proposed local site: the Compact Grand Ring.** A large temporary rodeo occupies the ceremonial field. Tiered event stands wrap the ring; the arbiter pavilion remains a recognizable background landmark. Arrival roads feed the main gate. A separate holding rail accommodates the Dark Horse at rear-left, preserving the distinction between horse and centaur.

**Make it lived in and scalable.** Temporary truss or timber seating, gate attendants, ordinary event carts, footprints and hoof depressions make an event that was assembled, attended and will be dismantled. Close views show rope tension and mud; medium views explain the gate and action axis; wide views reveal thousands of witnesses through repeated seating tiers. Exact crowd size remains an artistic choice.

**Baked-in light.** Proposed dusk gives temporary arena lamps a clear job. Low residual sky and lamp reflections reveal damp basin loam. Pavilion light remains fixed. The climax exposes Chili’s face, binding and mud to the stands rather than hiding him in a dark corner.

**Personal depth and consequence.** Chili enters expecting an audience for his own performance. The same entrance gate becomes an emotional reference point as the audience takes ownership of his embarrassment. His rope and the centaur’s block use their distinct traits. The four-legged centaur is one body, not a gorilla riding a horse. Crowd memory, boot skids, rope marks and cleanup can remain after the contest; a permanent stadium is not required by this temporary event.

**Historical fuel:** DK’s verified former identity, Baker Moore Purdy, beat Chili in the 2025 Week 16 winners-consolation matchup. This is relevant rivalry memory, not a prior championship final.

**Issue connections.** A landscape scoreboard at the pavilion introduces the site before the encounter. The Commissioner can refer to public consequences. Next week’s verified HMB-at-DK fixture gives the newly dominant spectacle an immediate future test without scripting that result now.

**Pass 2 alternatives:** keep the pavilion/judges’ side as the continuous public witness, or repeat the entrance gate from confident arrival to the view back through the mud. Recommended trial: use the gate for the emotional arc and the pavilion for orientation; the binding remains in center ring.

## 6. Continuity decisions that matter now

- **Keep newer scene memory.** The governing world-state snapshot is explicitly dated end of Week 3. Empty derived card states do not erase the reviewed Week 9 terrace damage, racing ruts, unresolved truck situation or retained belt. Newer visual evidence and older official state must remain distinguishable.
- **Respect regional character.** One late-autumn season can contain exposed upland cold, a dry cutting storm at Red’s terrace, humid sheltered wetlands and a comparatively open central-basin arena. Red’s local weather does not automatically freeze the swamp, ruin the birthday weather or flood the rodeo.
- **Use actual connections without inventing views.** The Bridge can show the eastern ridgeline; the Jedi base is not guaranteed visible through intervening terrain. Jackson can show a wetland tree line and levee; Mud’s specific domain is not guaranteed visible. Abstract map coordinates do not establish precise miles or travel times.
- **Avoid accidental simultaneous travel.** HMB’s registered route toward Jackson passes the Jedi-base node. If his journey is shown, it must occur before the depicted assault or have another supported timing explanation. The simpler candidate is to keep that trip off-page.
- **Keep public and private knowledge distinct.** A narrated private fear is not automatically something the Commissioner witnessed. Anonymous public reactions can belong to the scene; private dialogue, named guests, a baby’s details and new medical facts require their own support.
- **Keep displays truthful.** Scoring form, editorial power rankings, official standings and conditional playoff scenarios are different claims. Their labels and data sources must remain explicit, even when integrated beautifully into one illustration.
- **Make aftermath proportional.** Mud tracks, cake wreckage, loose lids, torn repairs, a scorched approach and crowd memory can deepen continuity. They do not automatically mean permanent conquest, destroyed domains or real-life consequences.

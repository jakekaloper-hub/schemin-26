# WEEK 3 V2 CLOSER PROMOTION DECISION V1

## Board consensus
Week 3's creative direction is not the problem to solve. Preserve the ambitious illustrated-world format, personalized league intelligence, serialized matchup chapters and variable art/text ratios.

The highest-value change is to move consistency upstream.

## The Closer
**PROMOTE THE PRINCIPLES; DO NOT STACK A SECOND OS.**

Integrate the successful V2 controls into the existing V5.4/V5.3 state machine as a compact preproduction control plane.

### Promote
1. Current/temporal canon receipt.
2. Immutable identity + continuity overlay + page state.
3. Final-raster character certification.
4. Complete Issue Previs Board before finished generation.
5. Compact Page Design Packet.
6. Schemin World Atlas + World-State Ledger.
7. World Geography QA.
8. Personalized Story Intelligence Register.
9. Visual Rhythm Matrix.
10. deterministic data boundary.
11. immutable approved-asset receipt.

### Do not promote
- literal-distance cartography;
- fixed page counts;
- mandatory dialogue;
- camera quotas;
- duplicate paperwork;
- automatic canonization of image-model scenery.

## Week 4 advantage
The goal is not fewer ideas. It is fewer preventable generations. The creative team should arrive at image generation with character, world, page function, composition family, data boundary and continuity already decided.

## V2 control results
### 1. Character final-raster certification — ACCEPT
Would have PREVENTED/REJECTED the clearest Week 3 continuity defect: TDS appears with restored dreadlocks on Pages 3-4 despite the approved post-haircut continuity state. Prompt intent was insufficient; raster certification is necessary.

### 2. Immutable identity + continuity overlay + page state — ACCEPT
This is the correct character model. It separates who a character is from what Week 2 did to him and from what a Week 3 scene asks him to do.

### 3. Complete Issue Previs Board — ACCEPT
Would have DETECTED EARLIER the long run of large display headline + parchment copy + cinematic character/environment composition across the matchup section. Individual pages are strong; the repeated architecture creates issue-level visual fatigue.

### 4. Page Design Packet V2 — ACCEPT WITH COMPACTION
Would have reduced page-by-page drift by forcing explicit composition, world, continuity, data and neighboring-page relationships before generation. Keep one compact packet, not multiple redundant briefs.

### 5. World Bible + Atlas Register — ACCEPT AS CONTROLLED CANON
Week 3 successfully feels like a universe, but its spatial coherence is mostly artistic rather than formally resolved. V2 would have forced the bridge/lake, alpine route, battlefield/mire, Country Club, speedway and LLC storm territory to coexist intentionally. Do not canonize every generated background detail.

### 6. World-State Continuity Ledger — ACCEPT
Would have converted consequences into reusable world memory: Country Club aftermath, bridge as remembered decision landmark, battlefield damage, storm/flood consequences, etc.

### 7. Personalized Story Intelligence Register — ACCEPT
Week 3's strongest pages prove its value. Sonic chili dogs and the $100 Red Leopards wager create specificity that generic fantasy narration cannot. Classification prevents commissioner context from silently becoming externally verified fact.

### 8. Visual Rhythm Matrix — ACCEPT
Would detect repeated camera/headline/parchment families before expensive art generation. It should recommend variation, not enforce artificial quotas.

### 9. World Geography QA — ACCEPT
Needed for persistent-universe production. Geography must be coherent without requiring literal mile-by-mile fantasy cartography.

### 10. Anti-bureaucracy rule — ACCEPT
Controls that do not prevent defects, detect them materially earlier, or reduce rework should be retired.

## What V2 would have prevented
- TDS post-haircut continuity reset on Pages 3-4.
- generation beginning before page/world/reference dependencies were explicit.
- some issue-level composition repetition.
- silent promotion of improvised environmental details into world canon.
- ambiguity over whether final raster or prompt was the QA object.

## What V2 would have detected earlier, but not automatically prevented
- dense copy on several matchup pages at phone scale.
- repeated headline + parchment grammar.
- world-scale/location inconsistencies.
- data fields that should be composited deterministically.
- potential character drift that survives a nominal reference attachment.

## What V2 cannot solve alone
- image-model anatomy/text artifacts;
- subtle aesthetic weakness;
- factual evidence unavailable before finality;
- a commissioner-approved creative canon change after release;
- whether a joke lands.

## Anti-overengineering pruning
RETAIN: Page Design Packet, Issue Previs, current-canon load, Character Raster Certification, World Atlas, World-State Ledger, Personalized Story Intelligence, Visual Rhythm Matrix, deterministic data manifest.

MERGE: World Bible + Atlas governance can share one World Control Plane; continuity-in/out can inherit automatically from chapter/world packets.

REJECT: exact mileage between fantasy locations; mandatory dialogue; fixed page counts; quotas for camera types; canonizing every generated background object; duplicate QA documents that test the same invariant.

## Acceptance result
**PREPRODUCTION V2: CONDITIONAL PASS FOR CONTROLLED PROMOTION.**
The retrospective demonstrates material prevention/detection value. Permanent promotion should occur only after executable regression tests are added and the compact workflow is reconciled with V5.4/V5.3 rather than layered beside them as a second OS.

# SCHEMIN WORLD BIBLE V1 — CANDIDATE

Status: WORLD-BUILDING CANDIDATE / NOT BINDING

## Principle
Schemin is a single persistent fantasy universe, not a collection of unrelated poster sets. Geography need not mimic literal Earth mileage, but terrain, scale, landmarks and relative spatial relationships must remain internally coherent once established.

## Scale hierarchy
MACRO: league world / divisional territories.
REGIONAL: mountain systems, basins, swamps, metropolitan/corporate territory, southern lowlands, roads and watersheds.
LOCAL: Country Club of Jackson, bridge/lake, battlefield hill, speedway, LLC complex and other approved recurring sets.

- Recurring roads, citadels, rivers and distant mountain silhouettes shown in Week 3 should be reconciled before reuse rather than assumed canonical from image-model improvisation.

## Environmental law
A mountain does not become a swamp without geographic explanation. Persistent buildings do not relocate. Damage does not reset silently. Roads, rivers and horizons should support believable adjacency. Distant landmarks may establish scale when atlas-approved.

## Canonization rule
Published art is evidence of an environment but not every improvised background detail becomes canon automatically. World-building staff extracts intentional persistent elements into the Atlas Register; only approved entries become reusable geographic truth.

## Approved creative takeaways — NOT canonical image approvals

1. **Ensemble-wide issue theme:** A tightly decided week becomes the editorial connective tissue, with two explicit sub-half-point stories in the opening, scoreboard and later dramatic pages. Avoid generic headline-only themes.
2. **Cinematic geography and evolving POV:** `Theatre of Ruins` shifts from hostile arrival (PDF p6) through stadium action (p7) to consequential defeat (p8); use spatial continuity and distinct storytelling frames, not a mandatory three-page template.
3. **Fantasy fact as dramatized causality:** `Blackwater Debt Job` (pp15–16) interprets a 0.12 victory as a fragile debt bargain; `Falls Protocol` (pp17–18) turns a 0.37 victory into contract negotiation. Numerical results are printed in-story; all claims remain subject to independently retrieved Fact Lock.
4. **Readable, credible world:** `Final Word` (p23) shows the Commonwealth as a persistent terrain made of multiple known venues, distinct from generic stock-fantasy filler. Location canon must still be resolved against the active Atlas.
5. **Storyline variety:** Six matchups use ruins, country club, camp, rodeo, swamp and falls/temple settings rather than recycling one stadium.
6. **Editorial system breadth:** Cover→opening drive→scores→rankings→atlas→six matchup narratives→divisions→recurring investigation→sports desk→preview→closing creates a magazine, not only an art carousel.

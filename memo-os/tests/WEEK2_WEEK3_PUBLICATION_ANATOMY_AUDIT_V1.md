# Week 2 + Week 3 Publication Anatomy Audit V1

**Status:** COMPLETE — benchmark anatomy
**Purpose:** derive the publication family from the actual released PNG pages later assembled into the official Week 2 and Week 3 PDFs.
**Important:** the PNG page is the primary designed artifact; the PDF is an issue container.

## Executive finding

The releases do not use one master page template. They use a common publication DNA across different page archetypes: bespoke illustrated scene, aggressive editorial hierarchy, story-bearing physical props, location-specific worldbuilding, purposeful data integration, and strong page-to-page rhythm.

A technically correct hero image with copy beneath it does not belong to this family.

## Derived publication DNA

### Hard requirements
- every flagship page has a specific physical scene, not a generic background;
- story can be inferred from illustration + headline before body copy;
- characters or story agents operate at meaningful scale;
- location/props materially encode the week’s narrative;
- typography is part of composition, not a separate lower panel;
- data is embedded in the scene/editorial hierarchy;
- page identity survives at 390px;
- matchup pages are bespoke enough that swapping team names would break the composition;
- issue pages vary archetypes while retaining masthead/material language;
- final page exists as a finished PNG before PDF assembly.

### Strong tendencies
- foreground/midground/background depth;
- practical objects used as jokes/evidence;
- parchment, wood, metal, arena, club, desk and battlefield materials;
- oversized display typography balanced with small editorial copy;
- serialization across adjacent pages when story stakes justify it;
- recurrent world geography and prior-week callbacks.

### Optional choices
- exact parchment treatment;
- exact color temperature;
- whether score is a floating card, physical board, receipt, screen or sign;
- whether body copy sits on parchment, wall, desk surface or open negative space;
- whether one story uses one page or a multi-page arc.

## Issue-level rhythm

Week 2 alternates institutional utility pages and bespoke matchup tableaux.
Week 3 expands this into serialized two/three-page story sequences, then resets with world/utility pages before the next arc.

The rhythm avoids six near-identical matchup cards in sequence.

## Governing conclusion

The publication family is **illustrated sports-fantasy editorial cinema**.

The design unit is the **finished PNG page**.
The PDF is the **bound issue**.
Technical correctness is necessary but not sufficient.

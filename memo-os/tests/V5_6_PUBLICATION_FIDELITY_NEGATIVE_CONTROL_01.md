## Purpose

Preserve the first technically correct but publication-underpowered V5.6 page so future QA can detect regression toward this product class.

## Failure characteristics

- generic hero-image + lower information zone;
- low scene specificity relative to official Week 2/Week 3;
- only ObiWan appears in a matchup-context page;
- insufficient physical props and callbacks;
- art and editorial typography are adjacent rather than inseparable;
- limited environmental narrative density;
- generic composition survives too easily after changing team/story labels;
- large explanatory burden remains in body copy;
- page reads closer to a premium social card than a bespoke Schemin memo page.

## Required regression behavior

Any future flagship page that substantially matches these failure characteristics must fail publication fidelity even when:
- facts are correct;
- reference mounting is proven;
- mobile text is legible;
- character canon is correct.

Technical PASS may never erase this fidelity FAIL.

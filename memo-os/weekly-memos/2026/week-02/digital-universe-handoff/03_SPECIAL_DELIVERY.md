# Special Delivery — production brief

## Mode
Image-to-video. No dialogue. Canon locked. 24 fps target.

## Shots
### A01 — Cold Open — 3s
Macro emphasis on delivery/receipt/food details already present. Steam and practical light movement.

### A02 — Establish — 5s
Slow controlled push into canonical composition. Mild depth parallax, stadium atmosphere and environmental motion.

### A03 — The Receipt — 4s
Emphasize the existing delivery receipt and food motif. Subtle paper flutter. Never rewrite visible text.

### A04 — Consequence — 5s
Return emphasis to Red Leopard dominance and Chili Outlaw defeat as already depicted. Haze, light and crowd/environment movement.

### A05 — Exit — 3s
Slow settle/pull-away suitable for an editorial transition.

## Rejection prompt
Identity drift; face drift; costume replacement; new principal characters; extra limbs; anatomy deformation; destructive morphing; scene replacement; rewritten text; fabricated score; fabricated logo; watermark.

## Render strategy
Start with one take per shot. Evaluate identity and source fidelity before spending compute on alternates. If a take fails, change the smallest relevant parameter and document the change.

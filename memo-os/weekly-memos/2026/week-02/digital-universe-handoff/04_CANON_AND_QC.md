# Canon and QC

## Locked facts
- Red Leopards: 191.90
- Chili Cheesers: 100.90
- Red Leopards won.
- This is Week 2 Game of the Week / Special Delivery.

## Visual doctrine
The official artwork is the anchor. Motion should reveal the artwork rather than replace it.

## QC — reject a take if
- a principal character changes identity, face, costume or species;
- anatomy visibly breaks;
- the composition becomes a different scene;
- factual text is regenerated or falsified;
- important source props disappear without editorial reason;
- camera motion causes severe warping/flicker;
- a watermark appears;
- the result contradicts the locked facts.

## QC — preferred
Stable identities, restrained cinematic motion, readable visual hierarchy, environmental depth, source-faithful props, clean transition handles, no generative factual typography.

Score/text overlays must be deterministic post-production.

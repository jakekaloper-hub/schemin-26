## Editorial thesis
Thanksgiving is a temporary truce in the Commonwealth, not a suspension of competition. The same roads that brought rivals together now carry them back into their divisions. Every table has a place; only seven teams will ultimately reach the postseason. Let seasonal hospitality carry the tension rather than repeating six banquet scenes. ## W12-H05-A01 — The place at the head
DK (6–5) hosts ObiWan (4–7). CHAR-WILSON-LOOK / CHAR-JAKE-KALOPER. Candidate anchor LOC-DK-HIGHLAND-STRONGHOLD, REG-UPPER-VALLEYS, training court/road approach; interpretive canon, encounter HOLD.
Shared manuscript: Wilson stood where the road opened into the highland court. The sound of four hooves on stone was enough to make the arriving traveler look up. Neither man had won the previous week. One had gone into Mountain Lake; the other had climbed out of a vat of chili. Neither needed the other to remind him. Jake kept his hood low and his weapon clipped. “I didn't come to talk about last week.” Wilson shifted his weight. “Then give me something else to remember.” The road behind them remained open.
Home-win candidate: Wilson lets the visitor pass without an encore; his restraint is the contrast with the bridge humiliation. Jake leaves with fewer competitive opportunities, not an invented elimination.
Away-win candidate: Jake earns the last word by refusing the bait: “Now we can talk about this week.” Wilson retains his physical identity and previous survival; no belt transfer.
Visual progression: wide approach, two-character opposing silhouette, quiet departing reaction. No replay of bridge fall or chili tank; no invented jester costume.

## W12-H10-A06 — The dry way through
Mud Dogs (3–8) hosts Three Dreaded Snake (5–6). CHAR-BOBBY-MITCHELL / CHAR-PHILLIP-PITTS. Candidate LOC-MUD-DOGS-SWAMP, REG-SOUTHERN-WETLANDS, raised ground/mud channels; encounter HOLD.
Shared manuscript: Bobby knew which raised bank would hold a man's weight, and which only looked as though it would. Last week a visitor had turned back. This time three pairs of eyes watched the same strip of water from one body. Phillip stopped at the edge. “Do you give everyone directions?” Bobby looked toward the sign rather than the snake. “Only when they ask.” A thin line of water moved across the mud. Neither mistook the other for a stranger.
Home-win candidate: Bobby's knowledge of the ground earns the visual payoff; Phillip acknowledges the route rather than acquiring a new body or losing a head.
Away-win candidate: Phillip reads the visible terrain and refuses the false choice; Bobby's smile disappears. No permanent new route or flood event.
Visual progression: low bank detail, one-body three-head silhouette, reversal in eyelines. Do not reuse Ben's exact wrong-turn sequence.

## W12-H02-A12 — The visitor without an offer
Red Leopards (5–6) hosts Dr. Duckhook (7–4). CHAR-KEVIN-ZEEK / CHAR-ZACH-WILSON. Candidate LOC-RED-RUINS, REG-EASTERN-STORM-COAST, jungle ruins/predator banners; encounter HOLD.
Shared manuscript: Duckhook arrived without a purchase agreement. At Jackson the previous week, two sheets had been enough to change an office. Here the old stones did not care who sat in a chairman's chair. Kevin looked at the visitor, then at the empty hands. “Nothing to sign?” Duckhook gave the ruins a measured glance. “Not today.” The answer hung between them longer than the greeting. A banner moved above the stone; the host did not move aside.
Home-win candidate: Kevin claims the final competitive beat on his own ground. Duckhook's corporate position is not undone by the loss.
Away-win candidate: Duckhook earns a sporting victory without negotiating ownership of the ruins; Kevin keeps his domain.
Visual progression: ruins establish territorial scale, conversational close-up with empty hands, winner reaction without legal paperwork or trophies.

## W12-H03-A07 — What was delivered
Slob on my Dobb (7–4) hosts Chili Cheesers (6–5). CHAR-JORDAN-HOLLINGSHEAD / CHAR-BRANDON-PRYOR. Candidate LOC-SLOB-FOOTHILL-CAMP, REG-UPPER-VALLEYS, tailgate yard; provisional canon, encounter HOLD.
Shared manuscript: Jordan had learned the difference between promising a delivery and bringing one home. At the yard, he checked the straps before he greeted Brandon. The smell of cooking drifted across the open space; a week earlier Brandon had thrown a ball into a target and Jordan had stopped a truck before it followed two lost kegs. “You're early,” Jordan said. Brandon looked toward the secured load. “I figured I'd give you a chance to catch up.” Jordan laughed once. He did not loosen a strap.
Home-win candidate: Jordan completes a controlled final beat and offers hospitality without surrendering competitive pride.
Away-win candidate: Brandon earns the competitive last word; Jordan's delivery remains a separate, responsible accomplishment.
Visual progression: secured load/host preparation, guest arrival, outcome reaction. No repeat dunk tank, no new route or unverified cargo incident.

## W12-H04-A08 — The name on the door
The LLC (4–7) hosts His Majesty's Blood (7–4). CHAR-DAVID-BABB / CHAR-AUSTIN-BYARS. Candidate LOC-LLC-BOARDROOM, REG-INNER-SEA-LITTORAL, executive chamber/ledgers; exact institutional relationship to Jackson and encounter HOLD.
Shared manuscript: Babb knew what it meant to be asked to leave a chair. Last week the second sheet had made the decision for him; the fantasy team still carried his name in the league's records. Austin came hooded, the red light of his eyes visible beneath the dark armor. The championship belt remained his. Home-win candidate: Babb claims sporting agency without reversing the Jackson corporate appointment.
Away-win candidate: Austin's competitive advantage is expressed by a quiet departure; no transfer of LLC assets or corporate authority.
Visual progression: office geography, controlled opposing performances, final empty chair or reaction. Austin hooded/red eyes, no uncovered face. No new shareholder act.

## W12-H09-A11 — A seat near the door
El Niño (6–5) hosts Seven Deadly Chins (6–5). CHAR-MANNING-WELTY / CHAR-BEN-WHIPPLE. Candidate LOC-STORM-BOWL, REG-EASTERN-STORM-COAST, weather-exposed venue/service counter; provisional canon, embodiment mode and encounter HOLD. Existing feature audition only, not selected GOTW.
Shared manuscript: Ben stopped before he entered. The last road he had taken had promised a crossing and given him high water instead. This time he wanted to see the door before he trusted the weather. Manning waited within sight of it, with the wind making conversation difficult. “You can come in,” he said. Ben looked at the sky. “That's not the part I'm worried about.” They had both arrived at the same record; neither had been promised a place among the seven. The open doorway mattered more than a distant island.
Home-win candidate: Manning's weather-framed reaction marks a sporting recovery; Ben's departure remains open, not eliminated.
Away-win candidate: Ben finds his composure at the threshold and earns the competitive beat; weather does not automatically disappear or become a permanent world change.
Visual progression: threshold and exposed atmosphere, readable character encounter, final reaction. No literal journey to Playoff Island; do not duplicate the Mud Dogs flood. No numerical odds in reader copy pending forecast authority.

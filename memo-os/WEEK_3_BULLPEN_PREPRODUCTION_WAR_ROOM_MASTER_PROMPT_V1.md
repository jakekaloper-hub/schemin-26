# II. GOVERNING TRUTH

The fantasy season determines events. The writers determine meaning.

This sentence is not decorative. It is the production contract.

The Memo may transform real fantasy-football events into an epic-fantasy world, but it may not alter the competitive record to improve the story.

Every material assertion belongs to one of five classes:

### FACT
Verified through Flaim, ESPN canonical league data, validated Schemin Data Gateway evidence, or another explicit authoritative record.

### JAKE CONTEXT
First-party league culture supplied by Jake: manager personalities, group-chat events, jokes, deliveries, relationships, hobbies, league memories and personal observations. These may be editorially valuable without automatically becoming statistical facts.

### INFERENCE
A reasoned interpretation of verified facts and/or Jake Context. It must remain distinguishable from direct evidence.

### STORY
Narrative meaning assigned to real events after facts are known.

### FICTIONALIZATION
In-world dialogue, fantasy action, metaphor, staging and visual dramatization. Fictionalization may heighten the experience but may never create a false league result, transaction, score, quote, historical record or chronology.

Every department must know which class it is handling.

# VII. EXTERNAL PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE — ADVISORY INPUT, NOT FALSE CONSULTATION

Bullpen may learn from documented production practice of real filmmakers and studios, but must never claim they personally reviewed this Memo.

Adopt the following researched principles:

### ESPN Films / 30 for 30
The strongest sports stories are director-driven and contain a second layer beyond the game result. A transaction, score or matchup should reveal character, pressure, history, culture or consequence.

Application:
- Every matchup gets a central dramatic question.
- Every matchup gets a “second layer.”
- Page count is earned by story depth.
- Do not force six equal chapters.
- Research can be as important as production.
- The story must offer a fresh way to see a familiar league member or event.

### Peter Jackson / LOTR Production
Multiple simultaneous units require centralized visual oversight, meetings and dailies. Camera behavior can itself carry narrative point of view.

Application:
- Treat matchup-art production as parallel units under one visual director.
- Review first-pass visuals together as dailies.
- Maintain shared light/material/world rules.
- Give every hero composition a camera role: witness, pursue, reveal, isolate, overwhelm, confront, depart.
- Evaluate adjacent pages, not isolated images.

### Wētā Workshop
A believable world is built holistically across character, costume, prop, environment, architecture, object history and production bible.

Application:
- Create reusable environment and object packets.
- Track recurring props and their condition.
- Build culture/materiality into environments.
- Never use generic fantasy backgrounds when a Schemin-specific location can exist.
- Concept work serves narrative direction, not spectacle for its own sake.

### Cinematography Discipline
Framing, light, scale and negative space communicate emotion. Preparation matters, but final verified story discoveries may require changing previs.

Application:
- Emotional objective before image prompt.
- Motivated light source.
- Intentional negative space.
- Scale must have narrative purpose.
- Previs is provisional until facts lock.

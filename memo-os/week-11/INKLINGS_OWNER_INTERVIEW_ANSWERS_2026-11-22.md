## D0nkey K0ng · Wilson Look

*Midspan at Mountain Lake Bridge · Opponent: His Majesty’s Blood · Inklings character draft*

**1. After last week’s rodeo, what do you think Austin still underestimates about you?**

How long I can stand still. Everybody remembers how big I looked at the rodeo. Ask Brandon how long I waited for him to make the first mistake.

**2. What do you say first, while Austin is still perfectly calm?**

“After you, Austin.” I give him just enough room that he has to decide whether to thank me.

**3. What detail on your Arsenal gear would your league mates recognize in a close-up?**

The gold Arsenal cannon. There’s a loose red thread beside it I keep meaning to fix. Leave it in the close-up.

**4. Which player on your fantasy roster would you trust to set the tone for this duel? Why?**

Dak Prescott. I want Austin looking up at the scoreboard before he’s even settled his cloak. I’d give Dak the ball for that.

**5. If you win, what gives away how you feel while you wait for Austin to surface?**

I look at the carter for help. A minute ago I’d have sworn we were the only two on the bridge. Then Austin coughs, and my shoulders give me away.

**6. If Austin wins and drops you his jester’s cap, what do you do with it on the swim to shore?**

I take it in my fist. One bell fills with lake water and gives a pathetic little clunk every time I swim. I keep the damn thing anyway.

## His Majesty’s Blood · Austin Byars

*Midspan at Mountain Lake Bridge · Opponent: D0nkey K0ng · Inklings character draft*

**1. What does Wilson do at midspan that comes closest to making you lose your cool?**

He steps aside for me. There’s a man waiting with a cart, and suddenly I look like a fellow who needs permission to cross a bridge.

**2. When does Wilson catch you touching the belt, and what does that tell him?**

Right after he makes room. My hand goes to the buckle before I look at him. He catches me checking it.

**3. You wanted Wilson as your jester. What’s the joke between you two that made you pick that title?**

Wilson got to decide who the crowd laughed at last week. I thought he might enjoy finding out what that job pays at my court. I bought the cap myself.

**4. If you win, what exact words do you say from the bridge when Wilson surfaces?**

“My jester.” I had a whole appointment speech ready. By the time he comes up for air, those are the only two words I can get out.

**5. If you’re the one in the lake, what do you want Wilson to see before you swim away?**

My eyes above the water. I want him to know I saw him wait for me. He can have the crossing. I’m taking the belt home wet.

**6. Which player or lineup call would you point to if the group chat asked who decided this one?**

Chuba Hubbard. Wilson can tell the chat what Dak did. Hand me the phone when they get to the running backs.

## Slob on my Dobb · Jordan Hollingshead

*The truck cab at Hearthroad · Opponent: El Niño · Inklings character draft*

**1. Who’s waiting on the beer, and what exactly did you promise them?**

The woman opening the taproom at the far end of my run. I told her she could chalk the beer on the board for six. She wrote it up.

**2. What’s one thing on your dashboard that would make the league say, “That’s Jordan’s truck”?**

A bottle opener on a bit of red cord, just long enough to reach the passenger seat. Cut it loose and I’ll spend the season buying openers.

**3. What do you notice through the windshield that tells you this rain is different?**

First wipe, there’s a white gravel chip in the rut. Next wipe, it’s halfway across the road. I’ve driven past that patch for years.

**4. Manning asks what time you promised. What’s your actual answer to him?**

“Six. I told her six.” First time I’m answering Manning. Second time I’m looking at the clock.

**5. If you get through, which starter gets the game ball or the first cold beer?**

CeeDee Lamb gets the first cold one. I’d put his name on the delivery slip and let him sign for it.

**6. If the storm wins, what do you say over the radio to the customer who’s still waiting?**

“It’s Jordan. Rub six off the board. The beer’s cold. I’m sitting at the rut. That time I gave you was my mistake. I’ll call from the turn.”

## El Niño · Manning Welty

*Outside the cab at Hearthroad · Opponent: Slob on my Dobb · Inklings character draft*

**1. What bothers you most about Jordan acting like this is an ordinary delivery?**

He keeps counting dry cases like the road signed his delivery slip too. I want him to lift his eyes about ten feet.

**2. What changes first when you arrive that only someone who knows this road would notice?**

I watch the pale gravel where the crew packed the shoulder. It starts running sideways. Folks passing through keep looking up at the sky.

**3. Do you warn Jordan about the rut, give him a hard time, or let the weather speak? Why?**

I give him a hard time. “What time did you tell them?” He knows that rut. I want to see how long it takes him to look up.

**4. What’s the one line you’d want Jordan repeating in the group chat afterward?**

“Beer stayed cold. Manning made me call ahead.” I’d screenshot it before he remembered he could delete it.

**5. If you win, which fantasy player should get credit for turning a shower into a storm?**

Emmett Johnson in my flex. I like that little square toward the bottom of the lineup. People look there last, right when they’re wondering where the water came from.

**6. If Jordan gets through, what earns him a grudging nod from you?**

Call the customer before she has to call you. Get the beer there cold. I’ll give you the nod. Might even hold the door while you unload.

## ObiWan Jacoby · Jake Kaloper

*The foot of the Eastern Ascent · Opponent: The Chili Cheesers · Inklings character draft*

**1. After last week’s loss to David, what do you want the people above you to believe?**

That I came down to meet Brandon because I wanted to. David came all the way up here last week. I’d like my front steps back.

**4. If you win, which starter deserves a mention before you take any credit?**

Derrick Henry. If he carries us through this, give him the first line. Everybody’s heard enough from me about whose mountain it is.

**5. If you lose, what would you admit at the shack that you couldn’t say on the mountain?**

I’ve done the playoff math. At the shack I’d admit I was doing it while Brandon was standing right in front of me.

**6. What should the After Dark reporter ask about the preseason clause or Pittsy leaving your game off the board?**

Ask me what I thought the injury or suspension clause would cover and what Austin actually said. Then ask Pittsy why he left our game off his board. He gets to answer for that.

## Red Leopards · Kevin Zeek

*The vegetation break by the falls · Opponent: Three Dreaded Snake · Inklings character draft*

**1. What sport do you want here: a short boundary race, tug-of-war, or something better for this terrain?**

Short race. Out to the marker by the ferns, around it and back to the dry stone. Give me one look at the turn and let’s go.

**2. After the damage to your own court, what do you check before you trust Phillip’s setup?**

I walk out to the turn and look back. Can I see the finish from where I’ll be running? I thought I knew my own court until the water came in.

**3. Which one detail sells your Florida python-hunter look the second the group sees it?**

A faded Florida cap with cutouts for my ears. The stitched python disappears under one ear and comes out the other side. Somebody had to order that in advance.

**4. If you win, what do you say to Phillip as the two of you pose for the photo?**

“Come stand over here, Phillip. You’re throwing shade on my good side.” If he asks when I packed the hat, I’ll tell him. “Tuesday.”

Zay Flowers. One long catch from him and I’m checking which pocket I put the hat in.

**6. If Phillip beats you, what’s the first thing you’ll give him credit for?**

He gave me first choice of sides. If he beats me from the strip I leave him, I’ll tell people myself it was a fair race.

A short race around the marker at the vegetation break, then back to the dry stone. We’ll walk it together. Kevin can pick his side.

Javonte Williams. Get me three yards when we need three. I want Kevin wondering when all those little gains added up.

“I asked Kevin to bring shoes he could run in. Apparently I should’ve been more specific.”

## Mud Dogs · Bobby Mitchell

*The old Gators & Dawgs sign · Opponent: Seven Deadly Chins · Inklings character draft*

**1. What does that old sign mean to you after last week’s truck disappeared?**

**3. What contest belongs here that Ben couldn’t win just by showing up in his truck?**

“Ben, turn those lights down. That second set of bleachers you see is a reflection.”

**5. If you stay alive in the playoff race, which roster decision deserves a little respect?**

Starting Ashton Jeanty. If I’m still alive when the scores settle, put his name on the front of that postcard. Plenty of room.

**6. If elimination is confirmed, what’s your last word before anyone gets to make the Cancún joke?**

“Somebody flip that Cancún card over. I’d like to know which one of y’all is paying for the flight.”

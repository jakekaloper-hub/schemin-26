### What the three consulting lenses changed

- **Tim Veil’s modeled engineering lens** distinguished file identity from editorial approval. The Librarian can compare a W10 original with its own registered digest. Production must judge the actual W11 PNG against its own brief, references, lettering and phone proof.
- **John Ezzell’s modeled integration lens** proposed an outage test. Production can simulate it by closing the canvas, recovering a folio through the existing repository and reflecting the approved change on the same card. A rehearsal cannot advance the official release record.
- **Jeff Lunsford’s modeled reader-value lens** suggested asking what Babb actually does. For F22, ask who controls the LLC, who represents its fantasy team and what happened to the course offer. In Week 12, ask who opened the door. The desks take responsibility for follow-through. The suggestion of automated greeting technology was discarded.

## Reusable meeting prompt

**`/bullpen board` — What the Commonwealth remembers**

> Beat Writer, open with “Who makes a different decision next week because of something that happened years ago?” Convene all Bullpen directors, the Inklings and Production. Use *One Last Crossing* as the test issue. Bring back three decisions, one each for divisional play, Playoff Island and a future book.
>
> Scout and Librarian, bring a dated source, the franchise identities at the time and the person entitled to know each fact. Atlas and World Evolution stewards, resolve the location and arrival, show who uses it on an ordinary day, then name what might persist there. Flag a writer’s proposed memory and give it a witness. Establish which changes require publication, an official result or a Commissioner decision.
>
> Inklings, give each side a reason to act and put the cost on the page. Take one strong turn from the Week 6 manuscripts. Production, test a specific page against its Week 10 original PNG and the owner’s Week 11 brief. Use the Weeks 8–10 page commission and review flow. On the canvas, make it possible to find the current page, its place and its open decision in one visit.
>
> Put 10–15 concrete questions to each desk. Ask each desk to challenge one answer from another desk, revise that answer, and cite what changed it. Apply the documented Tim Veil, John Ezzell and Jeff Lunsford methods to reproducibility, authority and the reader’s experience. Record the capacity in which each contributed.
>
> For each resolution, name who acts, the old evidence they can access, what they stand to lose, who can see the choice, the next issue or chapter that can use it, and the condition that would retire it. Umpire, register disagreement. Closer, name the next person who can act and the evidence that lets the page advance. Send the Commissioner only decisions that require his authority.

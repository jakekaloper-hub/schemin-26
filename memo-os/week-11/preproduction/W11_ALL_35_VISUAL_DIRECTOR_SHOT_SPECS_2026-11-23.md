- **Hero image first.** Each native 1080×1920 folio must make one location-specific, character-motivated moment visible before any documentary ledger/source block.
- **Photo and performance continuity.** Every image requires exact two-owner character input bytes and source-valid venue/environment plate; appearance and place are not inferred from the storyboard or method lens.
- **Narrative weather has regional physical causes.** Lake chop, trailer gust, carnival rainfall, waterfall mud, exceptional swamp flood and Jackson clubhouse squall stay geographically distinct. The atmospheric El Niño form remains separate World acceptance.
- **Page-turn continuity.** Each folio’s proposed action is linked to adjacent page. Do not combine temporally impossible actions in one static image just to check all boxes.

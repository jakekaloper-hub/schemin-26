## Twelve specialty responsibilities, genuinely useful to the render
1. **Doug Chiang / production design:** all objects and physical set geography; connect every folio to a visual working place rather than a generic infographic. Check named props against Week 10 physical reference.
2. **Dylan Cole / environment:** bind rainfall, stone, mud, waterlines, woods and architecture to the correct existing Atlas site. The bridge is a lake scene; the two beer kegs belong ONLY to the trailer; swamp flood is temporary.
3. **Joe Letteri / character VFX:** preserve Wilson Look as a four-legged gorilla-centaur, Phillip exactly three heads on one body, and Jake—not Brandon—as the submerged chili-tank subject. No released render without exact source character bindings.
4. **Ngila Dickson / costume:** Austin championship belt, cloak, Brandon recovered Week 10 black hat, Wolf Brand costume textures, Kevin conditional Florida python-hunter bag and Zach/David correct established visual identities.
5. **Andy Serkis / performance:** Austin seeing the ordinary carter, Jordan checking the radio, Brandon one skilled throw, Kevin reading the muddy course, Bobby deliberate guilty smirk and Babb furious chair/pen reaction.
6. **Rob Alonzo / stunt/action:** four-hoof rain-slick slide after strike, two keg drops with realistic mass, one accurate carnival throw, first-palm muddy race if selected, and traversable swamp truck path. **No invented car chase or lethal stunt.**
7. **James Baxter / silhouette:** four hooves identifiable, Jake full head-to-both-boots at ladder, Phillip three heads on one body, Bobby clean paw readable, and David/Zach two-shot readable at 390px.
8. **Frank Masi / unit stills:** establish a single photographic climax per folio, real lens/eyeline/depth, relevant hand/object detail and emotionally meaningful owner reaction.
9. **John Knoll / VFX pipeline:** inspect exact input-image source bytes, site cinematic plates, transform/crop, render-provider bindings and postproduction layers. tldraw scene cards are **not** the rendering pipeline.
10. **Jill Bogdanowicz / color finishing:** coherent wet-world lighting and selective hue separation: rain vs chili, exterior storm vs dry warm Jackson, dark swamp water vs Bobby safe lip, no generic identical blue storms.
11. **Thelma Schoonmaker / editing/page turns:** shot to next shot, clear time ellipses, no simultaneous impossible causal beats on a static PNG. In tldraw connect each folio to its next shot and give main visual action priority.
12. **Randy Thom / implied sound and motion:** one strong sound implied per cinematic beat: leather harness and lake splash, keg clank/radio, carnival microphone pop/splash, wet footfalls, swamp engine/brakes, pen on dry desk. No actual audio is produced or implied by a PNG.

## Fidelity counterweights
- **Do not use modeled production advice verbatim.** A local broad-brush response proposed beer kegs on Mountain Lake Bridge, an underwater Brandon instead of Jake, and a car chase; these are SOURCE-INVALID and excluded.
- **No new speculative winners.** Result-gated folios carry alternative ending placeholders, not approved scores, playoff elimination, or a selected cover.
- **tldraw must save production time.** Exact shot/camera diagrams are useful; duplicating 35 manuscripts in tiny text boxes is not.
- **Proof is visual.** A source-bound generated 1080×1920 PNG and 390px acceptance are separate from the planning canvas and from local structural validation.

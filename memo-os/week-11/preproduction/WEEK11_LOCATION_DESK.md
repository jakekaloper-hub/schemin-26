## What each location card records

- **L1** `level_1_physical_and_arrival`. Ground, water and arrival. Show how someone reaches or crosses the site.
- **L2** `level_2_lived_civilization_and_institution`. Inhabitants, work and access. Identify who uses the place and who decides entry.
- **L3** `level_3_memory_evolution_and_playoffs`. Memory, evolution and postseason. Attribute the exact event to its evidence, identify what could persist and who may know it.

These three editorial dossier fields are distinct from the eight switchable map layers in the active Atlas. A LOC identifies the parent, a SUB identifies an addressable feature, and a CAND is a proposal. The JSON carries each of the three full dossiers for all 23 parents, including region constraints, ordinary life, and source-attributed historical context.

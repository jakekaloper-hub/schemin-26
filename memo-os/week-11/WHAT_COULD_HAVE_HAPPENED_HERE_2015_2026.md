# What could have happened here?

Week 11 Inklings location histories · 2015–2026 · November 22, 2026

If a longtime resident came back during Week 11, what would they still know how to do here? Three Inklings desks answered that question for six places. Each set has three possible incidents from the previous eleven seasons and one way an owner could encounter their consequences in season twelve. The early, middle and recent periods are suggested windows for fiction. An exact date, a construction year or an unrecorded game venue would need its own evidence.

**How to use these stories.** Pick the present-day gesture first. The older incident gives the performer a reason to do it. A reader can understand the delivery, duel or negotiation even if the earlier incident never appears in print.

**What gets to decide.** First come Jake’s site decisions, the site’s Atlas card and the evidence specific to the claim, including the league archive for results. Second come the established memo scenes and the Week 11 production readbacks. Novel OS comes third for this exercise. Each site names the passage that gave the writers an idea and shows where they changed it. An ESPN extract remains a league record when its file happens to live under the novel directory. **2015–2018 · What could have made a carter on the uphill approach give away a place in line that cost him an hour to reach?** A spring drover came through with a flock. The waiting driver counted the last full-grown ewe, started forward, then found the drover’s boy bringing up the rear with a lamb across his shoulders. The next time the drover brought sheep through, she gave the boy the bell. Anybody hearing it behind the flock knew who was still coming. One family's arrangement could spread by example over several market days.

**2019–2022 · What happened to that bell when a younger driver took over the family cart?** He wanted the familiar sound for his harness. His mother objected because it had meant *someone is still behind me*. They compromised. He could keep it tied under his seat, with a scrap of wool through the mouth. He agreed to ring it only for a straggler. Most crossings it rode in silence. Years later one older traveler might still turn to look for sheep when the cart passed empty.

**2023–2025 · Who might remember the bridge chiefly as a place of embarrassment?** In fog, the young driver and a carter from the far shore each waved the other on and both started. Someone at the back laughed each time the pair backed up and tried again. Afterward one driver kept the reins slack when giving way and held them short when accepting the road. Give that habit to the Week 11 carter. Wilson's invitation looks perfectly clear to him until Austin stands still.

**Week 11, 2026 · Whose concern can the reporter show between the fall and the survivor’s first breath?** When the nearly fatal last strike sends the loser into the lake, the carter gathers the harness and finds the wool under his seat. Through his fingers we see a polished lip on an otherwise dull bell. He releases it only when the loser surfaces. The camera can return to Austin’s conditional jester appointment or Wilson watching Austin swim away with the belt. That quieter hand movement costs one insert and leaves the rivals their ending.

**Week 11, 2026 · What would Brandon hear at the foot that Jake could mistake from farther uphill?** “One more,” calls somebody behind Brandon. Before Jake reaches him, a lone porter clears the turn. A second, much smaller parcel comes up a few paces behind on somebody else’s shoulder. Brandon steps aside for it. Jake, descending and watching the same motion from above, gives the crowd a little acknowledging nod. By the time they meet, Brandon has understood one thing about Jake’s audience that Jake has missed. Keep the two parcels and one open lane in frame. The glass stays in the proposed past. ## Waterfall Temple boundary

Red Leopards and Three Dreaded Snake · Kevin Zeek and Phillip Pitts  
`SUB::LOC-TDS-WATERFALL-TEMPLE::FALLS-VEGETATION-BREAK`

The registered break is on the visitors’ side. Falls Path meets a wetland escarpment subject to high water. The camera can stay beside the path for every proposed incident. The archival matchups cannot date the cut stems, the dry stone or the household’s use of this precise opening.

**2015–2018 · What might a household have paid for a misunderstanding that lasted two seconds in the waterfall noise?** Two neighbors could have brought similar lidded baskets to meet a cobbler at the break. He called out whose repaired boots were whose. Both women nodded. That night one family found two little boots in a basket meant for a man coming off shift. Give the late worker a real grievance. By the time the baskets were exchanged, he had already walked home in his split shoe. On the next trip he brought the mate of the shoe being repaired and fitted the two heel to heel before he took his basket.

**2019–2022 · What would people do with that lesson when the water rose and nobody could put a second basket down?** Try a path-side exchange with two people and one dry stone. The person receiving the basket puts a palm under its base. The carrier waits until they feel the weight lift before letting go. Two deliveries later they can exchange it without raising their voices. One household might still bring the spare shoe. Another might consider that an eccentricity peculiar to the cobbler. The two-handed courtesy is the piece worth carrying forward.

**2023–2025 · What could have tempted a visitor to treat the dry stone as a winner’s platform before Kevin ever stood on it?** A spectator could have balanced a folded sports sheet on the stone to copy a score and turned around to find a loaded neighbor waiting patiently behind them. **Week 11, 2026 · How would Kevin learn the custom in time to make his own decision about the winner’s photograph?** Before the contest begins, Phillip supports a passing carrier’s basket for the instant it takes to switch hips. Kevin, still inspecting the finish, instinctively puts his hand underneath too. For a beat all three are holding somebody’s laundry. If Red wins, Kevin has a reason to step sideways after he puts on his hunter’s cap. He already knows what the second half of that stone is for. If Three Dreaded Snake wins, Phillip makes room for Kevin and the bag stays rolled. Both shots face the spray. The Week 9 welcome for Mary Martin Pitts belongs to her family, and a visitor can receive every courtesy here at the boundary.

## Matchup-by-matchup creative strengthening
### 1 — Champions' Joust
- Home advantage as Red's familiar stormward ruined terraces reactivated into a public tourney; local red-leopard heraldry integrated with old stone, not a generic medieval coliseum.
- ObiWan follows actual valley/travel routes, arrives with distinctive Trade Jedi identity (no championship belt).
- The two entrances remain equally magnificent; winner is never telegraphed. Winner-POV written post-result—either host surveying his ancestral tourney or visitor reading the crowd as he enters.
- Environment clue: wet stone/sea-facing weather and banners that react to escarpment wind; must inherit existing condition.

### 2 — After the Storm
- The Slob Camp host community turns a battered access road into a ludicrous official championship course.
- Seven Deadly Chins brings roadside working-culture confidence to Slob's familiar foothill terrain; distinguish rigs and spectators without changing principal character bodies.
- Storm is an environmental consequence only if an approved prior-weather state supports it. Do **not** assert an El Niño-caused hurricane as canon or geography without a verified event and classification.
- Two competing winner-POV options built from camp familiarity vs outsider navigation; finish remains conditional.

### 3 — Dark Wizard Chess (GOTW)
- Institutional neutral Compact Grounds reconciles TDS's FLAIM home label with mandatory major-event neutral default.

- Nighttime, dread, enormous captures, three-head sacrifice dialogue as *conditional* branch; champion Belt remains HMB's. Winner-POV only post-result.
- Chess rules should be understandable from page-level visuals without copying franchise-specific dialogue or exact set designs.

### 4 — A Storm Comes to Town (high-noon shootout)
- Chili is the home host at his Western Tablelands town; the Dark Horse carries him from the Chili Stable into his own settlement.

- The Weather Atlas must classify whether the storm is an actual El Niño manifestation, an ambiguous event, or ordinary weather. No permanent climate change is implied.
- The Beat Writer writes the winner-focal Story Overture after the fantasy result is verified.

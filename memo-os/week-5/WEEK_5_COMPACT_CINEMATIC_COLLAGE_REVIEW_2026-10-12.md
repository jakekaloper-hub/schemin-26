## Two-page cinematic matchup grammar (proposal)
**Physical page A — the winner's overture + scene establishment.** Top/left: substantial prose-forward winner-POV narrative, conditional on verified result. One establishing environment panorama plus one smaller character/story-object vignette. Introduce stakes and physical geography. Use no outcome spoilers before verified Fact Lock.

**Physical page B — the cinematic sequence collage.** One large hero illustration (50–65% of art area), two or three smaller sequential frames (15–25% each of art area, adjusted to fit), one compact but legible narrative spine (setup → escalation → payoff), score/result treatment after Fact Lock, and optional captioned artifact or quote. Design composition to direct reading order. Do not bake tiny prose into raster images; typeset text as vector overlay with real text-safe zones. Layout variation is mandatory: not six identical grids.

**Narrative constraint:** a two-page layout is valid only if all four story beats, both owners, and a comprehensible causal arc survive at 390px; otherwise use a third page for that matchup and document why.

## Six scene manuscripts — reversible drafts, not final overtures
The following are scene prose *draft seeds*, not six completed 180–300 word winner-POV chapters. Do not present as verified match outcomes.

### GOTW — Dark Wizard Chess (TDS host / neutral venue)
Night swallows the outer edges of the board. The squares do not end at the edge of a table: they run across the ground beneath the contenders' feet, each pale slab broad enough to carry a charging knight. A rook moves and the earth remembers its weight. HMB can feel the championship belt against his armor; across the field, three serpent heads argue over a single body's next step. Their argument is not comic relief. The board demands that someone commit before anyone knows what the sacrifice will buy. A stone knight shatters. The smoke hides a lane that did not exist a moment earlier. One contender thinks the board has narrowed; the other sees how much ground remains. HOLD: final winner-focal rewrite, evidence-grounded sacrifice, checkmate and departure state.

### Save the Swamp (Mud Dogs host)
Before the survey stakes, the swamp had its own schedule. Water rose around familiar roots, insects owned the afternoon, and Mud Dogs knew which ground would bear weight and which would take a boot forever. Then the LLC arrived with clean maps and cleaner shoes. Its engineers saw fiber corridors and power redundancy where the resident saw the bend of a creek that had never asked permission to exist. The first stake stood upright for less than a minute. The second disappeared in the mud. Neither side mistook this for surrender. Beyond the reeds, a machine waited for the order to begin; within the swamp, every small creature carried on as though it had a vote. HOLD: final winner POV, verified fantasy causal events, land-use consequence and world mutation approval.

### High Noon (Chili host)
The Dark Horse knew something was wrong before the stable hands did. The wind had changed, lifting dust off the Western Tablelands road and turning it back toward town. Chili came from his own stable, not from a stranger's horizon. He found the street full of people pretending they had not noticed the visitor. El Niño stood where the dry light should have been brightest. Above him, a bank of cloud folded over itself. A shutter closed. Someone checked the town clock and then looked away. Chili had no interest in a lecture about the weather. He wanted the disturbance to stop frightening his horse and his neighbors. The visitor did not move. The clock edged toward noon. HOLD: no sheriff framing; weather authority, James Cook outcome, winner POV and final duel.

### David vs Goliath (Duckhook host)
The Country Club of Jackson had hosted arguments over handicaps and dress codes, but not many guests who made the landscape seem undersized. DK's iron-shod legs struck the ground with the confidence of something accustomed to being the largest presence in every room. Duckhook waited beside a ball so small it looked like a mistake. He had brought a club, not an army. Around them, the fairway held its breath. The giant expected a speech. Duckhook preferred a clean strike. Somewhere between the clubhead and the horizon lay the difference between an improbable story and an embarrassing one. HOLD: Davante Adams, verified result, conditional ball impact or counterstrike, approved DK visual reference.

### After the Storm (Slob host)
The hills above Slob's foothill camp were still wet enough to remember the rain. Ruts held brown water, a damaged roadside sign leaned toward the course, and spectators had brought folding chairs to watch people with expensive Jeeps ignore obvious warnings. The two drivers inspected the same stretch of mud and reached opposite conclusions about who would own it. A marshal raised a flag that seemed less like permission than a waiver of responsibility. Engines growled. On the first incline, the ground accepted the tires politely. On the next, it began negotiating. HOLD: storm provenance, Josh Allen result, winning driver's POV, final obstacle and persistent damage review.

### Champions' Joust (Red host)
The tournament ground lay beneath the shadow of Red Leopard country. Banners cracked in the coastal wind while the crowd gave each arrival the same roaring welcome. Red entered with the certainty of a host who knew every turn of the ground. ObiWan arrived from the mountain roads, his green blade and field-worn bearing unlike the polished ceremony around him. Neither man had come to admire the other's heraldry. The horses felt the crowd before the riders did. A herald called for the lists to clear. Beyond the barrier, a child held a ribbon in both hands and waited for the impact. HOLD: do not give ObiWan HMB's belt; final Stafford/Puka vs Kyren scoring, winner-focal opening, collision and fall.

## Beat Writer — mandatory narrative upgrade
**Deliverables before artwork:** (a) issue thesis (not a preview list), (b) six owner-specific story briefs, (c) six substantial manuscript drafts with real scene prose and dialogue only where earned, (d) evidence-to-metaphor mapping for each football turning point, (e) prosaic continuity transitions into and out of every chapter, (f) final result-conditional paragraph and line options, (g) cutting/repetition memo.
**Per matchup manuscript questions:**
1. What does each character **want** and why does this week make it urgent?
2. What choice, threat, misunderstanding or insult **causes** the action?
3. What meaningful **football event** will be interpreted into the story after verified scores and player performances arrive?
4. What changes, humiliates, vindicates, or complicates the character as a consequence?
5. What callback, object, or line rewards this specific 12-person league?
6. What is communicated by the **writing** that the image cannot deliver?
Draft a **working 180–300 words per matchup across its assigned pages** as an editorial planning target, not a hard print quota; favor substance over padding. A single-page matchup may have fewer words where the composition cannot support them; move additional depth to a second page only when it earns space. Write real readable narrative, not six glorified image captions.
**Editorial failure:** if substituting random team names leaves the story essentially unchanged, RETURN TO BEAT WRITER.

- Overture 1: Red Leopards vs ObiWan Jacoby — Champions' Joust: rival champion arrivals, banners, crowd, honor and pre-joust suspense.

# M01 — OBIWAN JACOBY vs THREE DREADED SNAKE

## Governing relationship
Two active trade-market rivals; recent postseason intersection adds competitive history. Jake's first-party personal callback: at a lake/bridge outing, Jake jumped from the high bridge while Pitts became afraid and swam back to the boat — the “swim of shame.” Treat this as commissioner-supplied personal lore, not ESPN fact.

## OPTION A — OBIWAN WINS
### Title
**THE SWIM OF SHAME**

### Story thesis
Some crossings are won by leaping. Others are lost before the jump.

### Visual world
A vast mountain lake at twilight beneath an impossibly high ancient stone bridge. The Trade Jedi has already jumped and surfaced below, green energy blade safely extinguished/held clear of the water, golden retriever watching from a nearby boat or shore. High above, canonical post-haircut TDS reaches the edge — then chooses retreat.

The decisive image is not TDS falling. It is **TDS swimming the long, humiliating distance back toward the boat while ObiWan laughs from the water/shore**.

### Narrative shape
1. Establish the bridge as a test both rivals reached.
2. ObiWan commits to the leap.
3. TDS approaches the same edge.
4. The Podium Shadow cannot make himself jump.
5. The return swim becomes longer than the leap ever was.
6. The fantasy result converts an old personal joke into league mythology.

### Humor
Treat the retreat with epic seriousness: the narrator describes a heroic-distance swim whose entire purpose is avoiding the brave thing.

### Closing image
ObiWan standing dripping wet on shore with his retriever while TDS is still swimming toward the boat in the distance.

### Closing seed
**There are many ways to cross deep water. Only one of them would be discussed in the group chat.**

## OPTION B — TDS WINS
### Title
**THE PODIUM SHADOW GETS OFF THE MAT**

### Story thesis
The underdog does not erase the old humiliation. He trains beneath it until the humiliation becomes fuel.

### Visual world
Do **not** reuse the lake. Build a grim old fantasy prizefighting hall/training dungeon — stone walls, hanging ropes, battered heavy bags, rain outside, handwritten ObiWan sketches/tactical diagrams, waiver receipts and roster scraps pinned like training notes.

Canonical post-haircut TDS trains alone. The visual grammar may evoke the universal underdog-boxing-film tradition — roadwork, rope, heavy bag, bruised determination — without copying a specific Rocky shot or character.

The climax moves into an ancient torchlit boxing ring. ObiWan is framed as the established rival/protagonist figure; TDS enters as the fighter nobody expected to take the night.

### Narrative shape
1. Prior defeat/postseason shadow hangs over TDS.
2. Waiver spending and roster engineering become training montage equivalents.
3. Every emergency acquisition is another round in camp.
4. TDS enters the ring still carrying the memory of the bridge.
5. The upset becomes a comeback of identity, not merely points.

### Closing image
TDS standing on the ropes after the upset while ObiWan sits in his corner, stunned but recognizable and dignified.

### Closing seed
**The Shadow had spent too long beneath the podium. On this night, it climbed into the light.**

# M02 — D0NKEY K0NG vs MUD DOGS — WEEK 3 GAME OF THE WEEK

## Governing relationship
The only matchup whose winner can emerge as the league's sole undefeated team if the authoritative standings/results support that condition. This must be verified before publication.

## Shared visual doctrine
This is Week 3's **largest battle chapter**. Use monumental siege warfare, rain, walls, ladders, cavalry, mud, torchlight, armies and depth reminiscent of the visual scale of classic fortress-siege cinema, but do not reproduce Helm's Deep architecture, costumes, shots, or copyrighted characters.

The Arsenal Centaur and Mud Dogs remain exact canon.

## OPTION A — D0NKEY K0NG WINS
### Title
**THE KING ABOVE THE HILL**

### Story thesis
The strategist survives the mud and reaches the only ground left above everyone else.

### Visual world
A mountain fortress under torrential rain. Mud Dogs' swamp-born army crashes against the walls. Arsenal Centaur leads the defense/countercharge through the breached gate.

The final duel occurs atop the rain-soaked battlement or hill beyond the fortress. DK's blade/axe strikes Mud Dogs down in a non-gory decisive fantasy-combat beat.

### Ending
Dawn. Battlefield below. Arsenal Centaur climbs the final ridge alone. If verified, eleven league banners sit below his elevation and his remains highest.

### Closing seed
**By sunrise there was no unbeaten company left beside him. The hill had one king.**

## OPTION B — MUD DOGS WINS
### Title
**THE SWAMP TAKES THE FORTRESS**

### Story thesis
Plans hold until something born in the mud reaches the wall.

### Visual world
Same siege geography, but the storm and mud become Mud Dogs' weapons. The monstrous canine breaks through the meticulously defended gate and drags the battle into terrain the strategist cannot fully control.

Final duel: Mud Dogs strikes the Arsenal Centaur down in a non-gory decisive beat amid rain, mud and shattered defenses.

### Ending
The fortress remains behind, but the highest ridge belongs to the swamp-born monster. If verified, Mud Dogs becomes the sole undefeated standard.

### Closing seed
**The maps had accounted for roads, walls and gates. They had not accounted for the mud climbing back.**

# M05 — HIS MAJESTY'S BLOOD vs SEVEN DEADLY CHINS

## Governing relationship
Belt Keeper = established champion/league villain. Seven Deadly Chins = ringless pursuer. Achane's verified season-ending keeper loss remains a separate long-war wound and must not be framed as the cause of this matchup result.

## OPTION A — HMB LOSES
### Title
**THREE THROWS. THREE SPLASHES.**

### Story thesis
The feared champion does not fall in a cathedral. He gets dropped into carnival water by the People's Champ.

### Visual world
A surreal Texas/redneck medieval county fair at night: torch strings, livestock pens, cheap banners, fried-food smoke, mud, carnival bells. At the center sits a fantasy dunk tank.

Seven Deadly Chins — exact canonical huge bearded human, dirty tank, trucker cap, hammer/40-ounce/raccoon companion where readable — steps to the throwing line. HMB sits inside the dunk tank still dressed as the corpse-pale Belt Keeper, championship belt absurdly intact.

7DC throws. Target rings. Trap opens. HMB falls into black carnival water.

No gore. Maximum indignity.

### Season consequence
If verified, HMB becomes **0-3**, an unusually poor start for the reigning champion. The Achane keeper loss means the dunk is not the only water rising around him.

### Cover handoff
This branch directly creates Cover Priority #1: HMB emerges soaked, long black hair plastered across his face, belt dripping, sitting alone after the fair closes. “Hello darkness, my old friend” is a conceptual mood reference only; do not reproduce copyrighted lyrics on the page.

### Closing seed
**Kings had been drowned for less. This one cost three throws and a ticket.**

## OPTION B — HMB WINS
### Title
**THE BELT STILL BREATHES**

### Story thesis
This is not triumph. It is the first full breath after two weeks underwater.

### Visual world
Different from carnival. Dawn after a siege at the Belt Keeper's black hall. Seven Deadly Chins has battered the gates and nearly reached the throne room. HMB, already carrying the symbolic absence of Achane elsewhere in his army, braces the final door himself.

The champion survives the challenger at the threshold. No giant celebration. He sits exhausted on the steps afterward, belt across his knees, while the first sunlight reaches the hall.

### Emotional register
Relief > dominance. A tough opponent has been survived; the season has not been repaired in one night.

### Closing seed
**The kingdom was not healed. But for the first time that season, morning found the Belt Keeper standing.**

# M06 — EL NIÑO vs THE LLC

## Governing relationship
Elemental weather system versus corporate fortress. Comedy comes from treating “weather protection protocol” as if a boardroom genuinely believed paperwork could negotiate with a hurricane.

## OPTION A — EL NIÑO WINS
### Title
**ACT OF GOD, SECTION 14(b)**

### Story thesis
The corporation prepared for risk. It did not prepare for weather with a personal grudge.

### Visual world
The LLC's monumental glass-and-stone headquarters sits in a drowned financial district. El Niño towers as exact canonical storm/water/lightning elemental over the skyline. Floodwater swallows parking decks, expense reports, branded umbrellas and executive sedans.

The LLC character stands on the roof in immaculate suit and sunglasses, holding a useless emergency binder while water rises around the building.

### Humor
Corporate signs:
- BUSINESS CONTINUITY PLAN
- WEATHER PROTECTION PROTOCOL
- RISK MITIGATED
All become increasingly absurd as boats float past the executive floor.

### Closing seed
**The policy covered wind, water and interruption. It contained no provision for El Niño.**

## OPTION B — THE LLC WINS
### Title
**PROTOCOL HELD**

### Story thesis
Against all common sense, the corporation actually read the emergency manual.

### Visual world
Same catastrophic hurricane destroys/floods the surrounding district — but LLC headquarters sits inside an absurdly overengineered ring of flood walls, pumps, shutters, generators and legal disclaimers.

El Niño rages outside the barrier. Inside, the LLC character calmly straightens his tie in a dry executive lobby while employees continue a board meeting.

### Humor
One intern holds a tiny umbrella outside the glass. A corporate display reads something like **BUSINESS CONTINUITY: OPERATIONAL** via deterministic typography.

### Closing seed
**The storm had conquered the city. Legal maintained that the building remained outside its jurisdiction.**

# M04 — RED LEOPARDS vs SLOB ON MY DOBB

## Governing relationship
Red = Predator Board / football outsider with soccer/endurance identity in commissioner context. Slob = Fart Star / laid-back certainty and football-knowledge swagger. Existing Week 3 environment: **The High Course**. Red's endurance/mountain-running identity and Slob's “football certainty” should determine the branch.

## OPTION A — RED LEOPARDS WINS
### Title
**THE LONG WAY UP**

### Story thesis
Slob knew the game. Red simply refused to stop climbing.

### Visual world
An immense alpine endurance course above the clouds — part mountain trail, part impossible fantasy pitch. Slob has built loud checkpoints, football banners, beer stations and declarations that he understands exactly how the contest works.

Red Leopard does not argue. The giant canonical red leopard simply keeps running uphill.

The final section becomes brutally steep. Slob's confidence remains behind at a checkpoint while Red reaches the summit marker.

### Humor
Slob can be surrounded by elaborate “football knowledge” diagrams while the actual opponent has already run past the diagram.

### Closing seed
**Slob had an answer for every question about football. The mountain had asked a different one.**

## OPTION B — SLOB ON MY DOBB WINS
### Title
**WIN UGLY**

### Story thesis
Endurance, elegance and preparation meet the league's oldest counterargument: points do not care how impressive the journey looked.

### Visual world
Same High Course. Red has run an extraordinary mountain route, crossing ridges and impossible distances. At the finish, Slob is somehow already there — filthy, comfortable, beer in hand, canine companion beside him, having taken an ugly shortcut through mud, broken fencing and a tunnel nobody respectable would use.

Do not explain the route too much. The joke is that Slob's world refuses to reward aesthetic purity.

### Ending
Red arrives exhausted and incredulous. Slob gestures toward the scoreboard/finish marker as if the matter were obvious.

### Closing seed
**The Leopard had conquered the mountain. Slob had found the service road.**

## Five-layer packet requirement
Every final matchup chapter must resolve:
1. GAME — authoritative final result and scoring shape.
2. OWNER — rivalry/history/human context.
3. ROSTER — starts, bench, waivers, injuries, trades where verified.
4. SEASON — record/keeper/long-arc consequence.
5. WORLD — visual manifestation that adds information rather than repeating prose.

Not all five layers require equal page space.

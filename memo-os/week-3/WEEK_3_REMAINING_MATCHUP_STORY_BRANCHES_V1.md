## M01 — OBIWAN JACOBY vs THREE DREADED SNAKE

### Governing relationship
Rival roster-builders with a verified recent postseason intersection. Personal commissioner context supplies the lake/bridge anecdote: one jumped; Pitts declined and swam back to the boat — the “swim of shame.”

### A — OBIWAN WINS: **THE SWIM OF SHAME**
**Story thesis:** There are defeats in which a man loses the contest, and worse defeats in which an old story finds him again.

**World:** A monumental mountain lake beneath an impossibly high ancient bridge. The Trade Jedi has already made the leap. TDS stands at the precipice, post-haircut continuity preserved. He cannot follow. Rather than jump, TDS descends/retreats into the lake and begins the long swim back toward a tiny boat while ObiWan watches from the water/shore, laughing.

**Narrative architecture:** rivalry/history → bridge challenge → Jedi leap → TDS hesitation → long humiliating swim → fantasy result revealed as the old story repeating.

**Visual climax:** extreme vertical scale. ObiWan small but triumphant below; TDS even smaller, swimming toward the boat beneath the bridge. Golden retriever companion can wait safely near ObiWan/shore. No championship belt.

**Dialogue candidates — fictionalized league-world dialogue, not real quotes:**
- ObiWan: “You coming?”
- TDS: “I know another way.”
- Narrator/caption: “He did. It was longer.”

**Humor:** serious landscape, absurd retreat. Never make TDS cowardice a generic trait; it is a callback to commissioner-supplied personal lore.

### B — TDS WINS: **THE PODIUM SHADOW GETS OFF THE MAT**
**Story thesis:** The underdog does not defeat the rival by becoming him; he survives the rounds the favorite expected him to lose.

**World:** Completely different scene from A. A grim fantasy training hall/boxing arena built into TDS's reptilian world. Post-haircut TDS trains through expensive waiver reconstruction, scarcity and prior postseason defeat. ObiWan is the established rival waiting beneath arena lights.

**Narrative architecture:** 2025 semifinal shadow → Week 3 roster scarcity → emergency reconstruction → training montage visual grammar → final ring confrontation → TDS standing after the bell.

**Visual climax:** TDS in the center of a mythic boxing ring, battered but upright; Trade Jedi on the opposite side, surprised but dignified. No literal Rocky character/costume copying.

**Dialogue candidates:**
- ObiWan: “You rebuilt all that for me?”
- TDS: “No. I rebuilt it because of you.”
- Closing caption: “The shadow had finally stepped onto the podium.”

**Continuity payoff:** TDS's post-haircut look becomes part of the fighter's Week 3 identity.

**Final image:** Mud Dogs stands atop the rain-soaked hill/ruined gate, the only undefeated banner above the field if final standings verify it.

## M04 — SLOB ON MY DOBB vs RED LEOPARDS

### Governing relationship
Commissioner context: Slob is laid-back but loudly confident in football knowledge; Red grew up in England, loves soccer/Manchester United and runs punishing mountain distances. Existing Week 3 world concept: **The High Course** — football certainty vs endurance.

### A — RED LEOPARDS WINS: **THE LONG RUN**
**Story thesis:** Slob prepares for a football game. Red turns it into an endurance race.

**World:** An enormous mountain course that begins as an American-football field and climbs into an alpine trail. Fart Star/Slob arrives loaded with football certainty, beer/bat/established Slob visual language. The Predator Board/red leopard refuses the short contest and keeps climbing.

**Visual climax:** Red Leopard crests the mountain at sunset while Slob is far below at an absurd “fourth quarter” checkpoint, realizing the field has become miles long.

**Dialogue candidates:**
- Slob: “This ain't football.”
- Red: “Exactly.”
- Closing caption: “The game ended long before the Leopard stopped running.”

**Humor:** soccer/endurance outsider beats the self-appointed football authority by changing the terrain.

### B — SLOB WINS: **WELCOME TO FOOTBALL**
**Story thesis:** Endurance can carry a man across mountains. It does not guarantee he understands the collision waiting at midfield.

**World:** Begin on Red's elegant soccer/endurance course, then the trail empties into a brutal old fantasy gridiron. Slob/Fart Star is waiting at midfield with his established filthy berserker confidence.

**Visual climax:** Red Leopard charges with graceful athleticism; Slob turns the finish line into a goal-line stand and sends the Leopard backward in a comic, non-gory collision.

**Dialogue candidates:**
- Red: “I can do this all day.”
- Slob: “You only needed to do it for four quarters.”
- Closing caption: “For once, the loud man had chosen the right sport.”

**Character guardrail:** preserve exact Red Leopard and Slob/Fart Star canon; do not turn either into generic athletes.

## M05 — HIS MAJESTY'S BLOOD vs SEVEN DEADLY CHINS

### Governing stakes
HMB entered Week 3 0-2 and has the verified De'Von Achane season-ending keeper loss as a separate long-arc wound. If HMB loses and authoritative standings confirm 0-3, commissioner priority makes HMB the Week 3 cover subject. Never claim Achane caused this matchup result.

### A — HMB LOSES: **DUNK THE KING**
**Story thesis:** The league's feared champion is not overthrown in a cathedral. He is humiliated at a county fair.

**World:** A mythic Texas/redneck carnival: string lights, livestock pens, cheap banners, hammer games, fried-food smoke and a gigantic dunk tank that looks absurdly inadequate for the Belt Keeper's menace. Seven Deadly Chins — canonical heavyset bearded human, dirty white tank, trucker cap, hammer/40-ounce/raccoon — steps to the throwing line.

**Sequence:** HMB sits corpse-pale and furious above the tank, still wearing his championship belt. 7DC winds up. Ball hits target. Trapdoor releases. The feared Belt Keeper disappears into carnival water.

**Visual climax:** HMB rises from the tank soaking wet, black rune armor dripping, long hair plastered to his face, championship belt still around him. The humiliation is funny precisely because the villain remains visually terrifying.

**Dialogue candidates:**
- Carnival barker: “One throw.”
- 7DC: “That's all I need.”
- HMB after surfacing: silence.

**Cover handoff:** The cover does **not** repeat the dunk. It shows the aftermath: soaking-wet HMB alone, separated from the carnival noise, dark melancholy “hello darkness” emotional concept without quoting song lyrics. This creates cause → aftermath across chapter and cover.

### B — HMB WINS: **THE BELT KEEPER EXHALES**
**Story thesis:** Relief is not triumph when a champion has spent three weeks discovering he can bleed.

**Different world:** No carnival. Return to the Hall and Pilgrimage. 7DC approaches as a genuine challenger through a torch-lit Texas wasteland toward HMB's damaged black hall. Achane's absence is represented only by an extinguished campaign standard / missing war-table piece in the background.

**Visual climax:** after a brutal defensive stand, HMB remains seated/standing at the throne with belt intact while 7DC has been stopped at the final stair. HMB is not celebrating wildly — he is exhausted, almost surprised by survival.

**Dialogue candidates:**
- 7DC: “Thought you were dead.”
- HMB: “So did they.”
- Closing caption: “The kingdom had not healed. But for one night, it had stopped falling.”

**Season implication:** if provider confirms HMB moves to 1-2, write relief/survival rather than “back on top.”

## M06 — EL NIÑO vs THE LLC

### Governing relationship
Elemental weather system versus corporate risk-management machine. El Niño has keeper Nico Collins availability pressure and Monday exposure; LLC entered 0-2 with commissioner context about prior accumulated draft capital/expectation.

### A — EL NIÑO WINS: **ACT OF GOD**
**Story thesis:** The corporation insured everything except the weather itself.

**World:** The LLC's immense black-glass headquarters, built with absurd storm-defense systems: flood walls, pumps, emergency shutters, actuarial maps, sandbags stamped LLC, executives watching dashboards. Outside, canonical El Niño grows into a colossal storm/water/lightning elemental.

**Visual climax:** water overtops the defenses and consumes the corporate district. The LLC character — exact suited corporate raider with sunglasses/cigar/briefcase — stands on the roof holding the LLC briefcase above the flood, staring at the impossible storm.

**Dialogue candidates:**
- LLC: “This was within tolerance.”
- Assistant/placard: “Tolerance has left the building.”
- El Niño can remain wordless.

**Humor:** corporate jargon remains calm while the entire city is underwater.

### B — LLC WINS: **THE WEATHER PROTECTION PROTOCOL**
**Story thesis:** For once, the paperwork works.

**World:** Same headquarters and same enormous hurricane. The surrounding district is catastrophically flooded, boats moving through streets, lightning everywhere — yet the LLC building sits inside a ridiculous engineered dry perimeter.

**Visual climax:** El Niño rages against transparent flood barriers while the LLC character stands dry behind glass, cigar lit, briefcase untouched. A tiny emergency manual labeled WEATHER PROTOCOL sits open.

**Dialogue candidates:**
- LLC: “Per policy.”
- El Niño: “...”
- Closing caption: “The storm had arrived on schedule. So had Compliance.”

**Humor:** do not weaken El Niño visually. The joke is that the corporation survives through absurd preparation, not that the storm is small.

# AUTHOR-CONSULTANT NOTES

The five branches should feel like **five different legends from the same world**, not five reskinned scorecards.

- ObiWan–TDS = **personal myth / remembered shame or underdog redemption**.
- DK–Mud = **war epic / sovereignty**.
- Slob–Red = **culture clash / endurance vs football certainty**.
- HMB–7DC = **fallen-king comedy or damaged-king survival**.
- El Niño–LLC = **disaster satire / nature vs systems**.

### Shared writing rules
1. Begin with concrete place/object before explaining fantasy football.
2. Let one recurring object carry the joke: boat, bell, battlement, finish marker, dunk target, emergency protocol.
3. Results change the meaning of the environment rather than merely changing who stands on top.
4. Dialogue should be short enough to survive mobile composition.
5. Commissioner-supplied real dialogue remains distinct from Bullpen-authored fictionalized dialogue.
6. Avoid direct imitation of Tolkien or any other named author. Use original epic high-fantasy prose with mythic scale, material environments, restrained narration and humorous contrast.
7. No result-dependent branch becomes final until authoritative provider evidence resolves it.

# WEEK 3 POST-PRODUCTION FINDINGS V1
Status: THE CLOSER - POST-RELEASE FINDINGS
Scope: released 21-page Week 3 PDF + repository Fact Lock, manuscript, continuity ledger and QA doctrine.

## Executive assessment
Week 3 is the first issue that consistently behaves like a **league publication set inside a persistent fantasy universe**, rather than a stack of fantasy-football graphics. Its strongest advance is editorial architecture: setup/payoff matchup chapters feed into geography, rankings, the Week 4 horizon, a market module and a closing world view.

The principal defect is enforcement. The repository already contained correct rules for character and continuity QA, yet the released raster includes violations that those rules explicitly classify as PAGE_REJECT. This means Week 4 does not primarily need more doctrine. It needs harder, evidence-bound gates.

## Top lessons
1. Serialized two-page chapters work when the second page changes the state, not merely repeats the art.
2. GOTW can earn three pages when stakes are unique, but exposure must remain exceptional.
3. Specific human-context objects - Sonic chili dog, $100 wager - become excellent fantasy-world story engines.
4. Environmental worlds are now as important as characters.
5. Humor lands best when embedded in consequence rather than detached captions.
6. Transition pages are narrative infrastructure, not filler.
7. Standings, Power Rankings and Pittsy's Book need deterministic composition because their credibility is arithmetic.
8. Character canon must be enforced on the raster, not merely present in the prompt.
9. Derived continuity states are first-class canon.
10. The closing panorama is valuable decompression after a dense issue.

## Top defects / risks
1. DK structural character drift across multiple high-profile pages.
2. TDS post-haircut continuity reset.
3. QA doctrine existed but did not prevent release.
4. Generative typography still carries critical numeric information.
5. Repeated headline/parchment/tableau grammar creates mid-issue visual sameness.
6. Information density spikes in Pages 16-20.
7. Asset provenance/approval must be universal, not page-specific.
8. Genre translations can weaken identity cues.
9. Cover + GOTW concentration can over-weight one matchup.
10. Final assembly needs master-to-PDF regression evidence.

## Strongest production patterns
PRESERVE: opening editorial thesis; environmental continuity; matchup setup/payoff; league-as-world geography; Six Roads Forward; closing panorama.
EXPAND: transition pages as consequence engine; chapter-specific props; distinct genre worlds.
REFINE: GOTW page count; Power Ranking density; Pittsy's deterministic ledger; composition diversity.
RETIRE: acceptance of visually excellent pages that fail binary canon/continuity gates; image-model rendering of critical numbers.

## Root cause chain
Visual canon drift -> image model improvisation -> reference/continuity state not mechanically bound to final raster -> aesthetic approval allowed to substitute for QA evidence -> gate integrity failure.
Numeric risk -> data known correctly upstream -> values embedded in generative image composition -> deterministic firewall treated as doctrine rather than compositor requirement -> release risk.
Visual fatigue -> individually strong page briefs -> no issue-level composition-family budget -> repeated successful template -> reduced macro-rhythm.

## Production-efficiency finding
The precise number of generations per accepted page is not recoverable from the released PDF and current durable artifacts alone; do not invent it. Qualitatively, Week 3 shows repeated corrective cycles around character fidelity, copy, page assembly and story refinement. The fix is not lower ambition. It is earlier packet resolution + evidence-bound gates + deterministic data composition + durable raster manifests.

## Week 4 implications
Before creative production starts, implement the accepted subset of regression tests and gate-integrity controls. Week 4 should preserve Week 3's world scale while varying page grammar more aggressively. Matchup chapters should earn one, two or three pages from story need rather than template. Numeric modules should be composed deterministically after art. Character packets and current continuity states must be attached before generation and certified again against the exact final raster.

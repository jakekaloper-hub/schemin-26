# Week 3 Environment Reference Packets V1

## ObiWan–TDS: The Crossing
Primary: ancient trade bridge spanning cold mountain water; weathered toll markers; forked roads and abandoned merchant posts imply a history of bargains.  
Alternate: flooded exchange-road below the bridge.  
Camera opportunities: low waterline looking up; long-lens bridge compression; high reveal of distance to opposite bank.  
Prohibit: generic castle arena; literal ESPN branding.

## DK–Mud Dogs: Border of Campaign and Swamp
Primary: disciplined stone campaign road dissolving into blackwater trail, hunting blinds and cypress-like roots.  
Alternate: elevated ridge overlooking flooded lowlands.  
Camera: centaur geometry against irregular swamp terrain.  
Prohibit: gorilla imagery; cartoon mud pit.

## Chili–Duckhook: The Country Club Under Siege
Primary: old-world manicured golf estate with impossible fantasy scale, clubhouse stonework, immaculate greens and distant water hazards; outlaw traces disturb order.  
Alternate: final-hole terrace at dusk.  
Objects: golf equipment, chili artifact, Dark Horse only when composition earns it.  
Prohibit: mascot sports stadium; generic Wild West saloon.

## Slob–Red Leopards: The High Course
Primary: mountain endurance route through ancient stone checkpoints, exposed ridgelines and severe elevation.  
Alternate: storm-cleared summit approach.  
Camera: depth emphasizes distance already traveled and remaining climb.  
Prohibit: soccer-v-football gag as entire environment.

## HMB–Seven Deadly Chins: The Hall and Pilgrimage
Primary: vast dark champion's hall overlooking heat-scarred plains; belt chamber deep inside, challenger approaches through long causeway.  
Alternate: open-air proving ground outside the hall.  
Texas influence: heat, distance, horizon, scale—not cowboy costume.  
Prohibit: Western parody.

## El Niño–LLC: The Citadel in the Storm
Primary: monumental mercantile/corporate citadel of ledgers, vault doors, trading halls and glass/stone geometry under advancing elemental weather.  
Alternate: flooded board chamber.  
Camera: rigid architectural lines challenged by organic storm movement.  
Prohibit: modern office pasted into fantasy scene.

## Shared world rule
Materials, weather and geography must imply one connected Schemin world. Each packet must leave intentional negative space for deterministic typography.

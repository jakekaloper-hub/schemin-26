# WEEK 3 — CONDITIONAL STORY ARCHITECTURE AUDIT & PREP PATCH V1

**Status:** PRE-FACT / Bullpen editorial audit  
**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Input audited:** `WEEK_3_REMAINING_5_MATCHUPS_CONDITIONAL_STORY_ROOM_V1.md` against Week 3 intelligence matrix, enrichment audit, continuity ledger and page-rhythm map.  
**Purpose:** strengthen the five remaining conditional stories before results resolve. This document does not select winners, macro-story or cover.

## Executive finding

The conditional concepts are differentiated and usable, but they were **not yet production-ready**. The largest weakness was that several branches were visually strong but insufficiently joined to Week 3's verified roster/season evidence. The Chili–Duckhook chapter succeeded because real league dialogue + roster evidence + persistent geography + visual joke all reinforced one another. The remaining five should meet the same standard.

The patch therefore adds six requirements to every surviving branch:

1. **FACT ANCHOR** — at least one verified Week 3 scoring/roster fact.
2. **HUMAN ANCHOR** — commissioner/history material where available.
3. **SEASON CONSEQUENCE** — what the result changes beyond one score.
4. **VISUAL METAPHOR** — scene communicates the consequence rather than merely staging combat.
5. **CONTINUITY IN/OUT** — what enters from prior lore and what may persist.
6. **PAGE-TURN ENGINE** — Page 1 creates a question; Page 2 resolves it.

No branch should move to manuscript merely because its visual premise is entertaining.

# M01 — OBIWAN JACOBY vs TDS — AUDIT PATCH

## What works
“Swim of Shame” is uniquely personal and therefore stronger than generic Jedi-vs-snake combat. TDS-win boxing is a genuinely different visual universe and correctly makes the upset about preparation rather than making ObiWan weak.

## Required improvements

### A — ObiWan wins
**Fact anchors to resolve after finality:** ObiWan result/final score; Puka's Week 3 absence; TDS emergency waiver construction; Shough 31.9; final records.

**Human anchor:** the lake/bridge anecdote is commissioner-supplied personal history. Make that provenance explicit in manuscript notes. It is not a literal Week 3 event.

**Narrative correction:** avoid writing “the Jedi wins because he was brave and TDS was afraid” as factual causality. The lake is a metaphor for the rivalry/result, not an explanation for fantasy scoring.

**Two-page engine:**
- Page 1: the bridge. ObiWan leaps. TDS approaches the edge. Integrate the expensive emergency roster as baggage/equipment he hauled to the crossing.
- Page 2: the long swim back. The humiliation is distance: the boat looks impossibly far away while ObiWan has already crossed the threshold.

**Continuity:** TDS must be POST-HAIRCUT. ObiWan has no championship belt; golden retriever should appear where composition permits. No literal Star Wars branding.

**Possible closing idea:** TDS had rebuilt the roster to reach the bridge. The cruel part was discovering the bridge still asked for a leap.

### B — TDS wins
**Fact anchor:** same emergency-waiver build becomes central; do not reduce it to decorative montage.

**Upgrade:** make the waiver prices the “training camp” structure. $76 Shough / $42 Emanuel Wilson / $35 Bengals D/ST / $2 Keon Coleman can appear as deterministic round cards/training ledger, not image-generated gibberish.

**Two-page engine:**
- Page 1: solitary camp/training; expensive rebuilding; prior 2025 semifinal loss can hang over the room as history.
- Page 2: original fantasy prize ring. TDS wins but is visibly exhausted. ObiWan's recognition of the rival prevents the scene becoming cheap humiliation.

**Season consequence:** verify whether this changes TDS/ObiWan to 2-1 or 1-2 before copy.

# Week 3 Memory & Lore Treatment V1

Purpose: make league memory visually powerful without confusing it with verified game telemetry.

## Grammar
Memory/lore scenes use a distinct painterly temporal treatment, softer edge transitions and an explicit prose cue such as “Years before…” or “League lore remembers…”. They may be cinematic but cannot imitate documentary evidence.

## Week 3 candidates
- TDS “swim of shame” bridge anecdote: Jake first-party context. Treat as remembered league lore, never as a statistical/fantasy event.
- Sonic chili-dog delivery: Jake first-party Week 3 event. May become an omen/object motif, but its meaning waits for final result.
- Manager hobbies/background: environmental influence only; avoid turning personal traits into invented events.

## Prohibited
Fake photographic proof; fake group-chat screenshots; invented direct quotes presented as real; fake newspaper evidence; unmarked memory sequences.

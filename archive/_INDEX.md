## Purpose

- Never archive the only known copy of unresolved evidence.
- Preserve original filenames when practical.
- Add a short reason/date when moving a controlling document here.
- Historical artifacts do not regain authority merely because they contain more detail than the current control document.
- Weekly production outputs may eventually be grouped by season/week, but released artifacts should remain traceable to their release manifest.

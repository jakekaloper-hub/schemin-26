## Supersession

The parent constitution's instruction to produce **six Chapter Dossiers** after Week 3 result lock is reinterpreted and superseded for Novel production.

### New rule

Week 3 produces:
1. six verified **matchup evidence packets**;
2. significance grading across all verified events;
3. one or more **story-unit dossiers** only where causal significance earns foreground treatment;
4. one chapter dossier only after causal threads are assembled into a dramatic question.

A matchup evidence packet is **not** automatically a scene.
A story-unit dossier is **not** automatically a chapter.
Week 3 is **not** automatically Chapter III.

## Required Chapter III decision

Before drafting, the Beat Writer must answer:
- What is the chapter's one dramatic question?
- Which Week 1–3 open loops collide here?
- Which 1–3 POVs own the consequence?
- Which verified Week 3 events remain off-page for now?
- Which location/route packet makes the causal chain physically coherent?
- What changes by the end that cannot simply reset next week?

## Continuity

All six Week 3 outcomes remain verified history whether or not foregrounded in Chapter III.

Deferred events may re-enter later when consequence makes them story-relevant.

# PROLOGUE WORLD BIBLE V1
## Schemin Chronicle — Volume I · 2026

**Status:** Foundation candidate
**Scope:** What the Prologue may safely establish before Chapter I.

# 1. WORLD PREMISE

Schemin is an interconnected mythscape translated from the real competitive history of the Pro Schemin' Football League.

It is **not** a literal fantasy-football game.
It is **not** one generic medieval kingdom.
It is **not** a collection of unrelated mascot posters.

The world may contain radically different environments because its inhabitants already possess radically different canonical visual languages. The Chronicle's task is to make those places feel connected by roads, records, reputation, commerce, rumor, weather, correspondence, wagers and consequences without inventing unsupported political borders.

# 2. WHAT BINDS THE WORLD

The strongest connective tissue currently supported is not government. It is **record**.

Across the world:
- encounters produce evidence;
- results become ledger entries;
- receipts survive;
- names are rewritten;
- wagers are recorded;
- reputations travel farther than witnesses;
- objects retain continuity;
- the League provides a shared frame.

The Archive/Chronicle therefore becomes a narrative device for moving between otherwise different environments.

# 3. EPISTEMOLOGY

The reader will encounter four distinct truth layers:

**FACT** — verified league/event truth.
**CANON** — accepted Schemin world/character truth.
**INTERPRETATION** — what evidence plausibly means.
**STORY** — connective invention subject to acceptance.

Inside the fiction, this can appear as:
- official records;
- receipts;
- letters;
- photographs/sketches;
- maps/fragments;
- testimony;
- rumor;
- marginalia;
- bookmaker notation;
- damaged or contradictory evidence.

The narrator may distinguish certainty without breaking immersion.

# 4. GEOGRAPHIC GRAMMAR

Established environmental languages include:
- Trade Jedi — North Georgia mountain / strategy territory;
- Belt Keeper — gothic mountain fortress/dark-fantasy domain;
- Arsenal Centaur — mountain-warrior / tavern-compatible terrain;
- Slob — tailgate/roadside/frat-berserker terrain;
- Mud Dogs — violent swamp;
- TDS — jungle/waterfall;
- Seven Deadly Chins — truck-stop/BBQ roadside;
- Red Leopards — jungle ruins;
- El Niño — flooded/storm terrain;
- LLC — boardroom/market/corporate interior;
- Dr. Duckhook — golf/swamp/water-golf terrain;
- Chili Outlaw — Western stable/hideout.

These are **character environments**, not yet sovereign states.

The Prologue may show transitions, roads, documents and distant horizons between them. It may not declare unsupported borders, capitals, governments or territorial ownership.

# 5. INSTITUTIONS

## The League
A shared competitive frame. Exact fictional bureaucracy remains intentionally light.

## The Archive / Chronicle
Narrative institution/device for preserving evidence. It can be represented physically without claiming every real-world league record literally exists in one room.

## Pittsy's Book
Bookmaking/ledger tradition exists in established 2026 material. Its exact legal/civic status remains undefined.

## Commissioner / League desk
Established through published Week 2 material, but the Prologue should not import Week 2-specific events backward.

# 6. THE BELT

The Championship Belt is a continuity object.

At the opening of 2026:
- Austin Byars / The Belt Keeper / His Majesty's Blood carries it.
- Byars is the reigning 2025 champion.
- The Belt has physical wear/history.

Not established:
- supernatural power;
- origin;
- complete lineage;
- exact rules of transfer inside the fictional world beyond accepted events;
- political authority.

The Belt symbolizes inherited achievement/reputation without requiring invented metaphysics.

# 7. TEMPORAL THRESHOLD

The Prologue sits at the boundary immediately before Week 1 of the 2026 volume.

It may know:
- verified pre-2026 history;
- established identities entering 2026;
- the reigning champion;
- existing objects/companions/environments.

It may not know:
- Week 1 outcomes before they occur in narrative;
- Week 2 events;
- future grudges created by later encounters;
- eventual champion.

# 8. THEMATIC ENGINE

The Prologue should establish:
- history versus new evidence;
- identity versus changing names;
- possession versus meaning;
- record versus memory;
- reputation versus future action;
- twelve distinct lives converging on one new season.

# 9. LITERARY EXPERIENCE

The reader should feel that:
- roads continue beyond the illustration;
- objects have been handled before;
- people remember events differently;
- environments existed before the camera arrived;
- the twelve have histories not fully explained;
- 2026 matters because it is new, not because the past vanished.

# 10. HARD PROHIBITIONS

Do not:
- invent prior champions or championship counts;
- invent ancient dynasties;
- invent wars/history merely for scale;
- turn all twelve into medieval humans;
- force one kingdom;
- imply every matchup is physical combat;
- let generated text establish canon;
- use future-week knowledge;
- redesign characters because team names changed.

# WORLD-BIBLE TEST

The world is ready for prose when all twelve canonical inhabitants can plausibly exist inside it without changing who they are.

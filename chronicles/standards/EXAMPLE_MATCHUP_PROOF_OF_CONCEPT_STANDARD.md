# EXAMPLE MATCHUP PROOF-OF-CONCEPT STANDARD
## Schemin Chronicle — Volume I

**Status:** APPROVED BENCHMARK
**Approved by:** Jake
**Source prototype:** Week 1 — ObiWan Jacoby vs. His Majesty's Blood

This standard records the production grammar proven by the approved proof of concept. It does **not** canonize incidental AI-generated text, scenery, symbols, maps, dates, secondary figures, or unsupported historical implications.

## What is now standard

### 1. Matchups are stories, not posters
A narratively important matchup may receive a multi-page sequence. Page count follows story significance and pacing, not a one-matchup/one-page rule.

### 2. Finished pages carry the literature
The hidden manuscript is not the product. Substantial final prose, dialogue, observation, setting and consequence must live on the finished Chronicle pages.

### 3. Variable prose/art ratio
Pages may be prose-led, balanced, painting-led, artifact-led or nearly wordless. Do not force one magazine template across the chapter.

### 4. Recognition before spectacle
Character references and Character Master govern identity. A beautiful render with a wrong character fails.

### 5. Facts become physical evidence
Scores, records, standings and other exact data should enter the world through receipts, ledgers, notices, books, marginalia or other grounded artifacts where appropriate.

### 6. Exact factual typography is controlled
AI-generated micro-text is not authoritative. Canonical names, scores, records and dates require verified typesetting/text QA.

### 7. Matchup manifestation varies
ESPN determines the result. Schemin translates competition into a world event. Do not teach the reader that every matchup is a sword fight.

### 8. Consequence matters
The sequence continues after the numerical result. Reputation, possession, relationships, artifacts and unresolved narrative debt should change where supported.

### 9. Character dignity
A winner need not become invincible. A loser need not become a joke. Both should leave important scenes with usable narrative value.

### 10. Book rhythm
Approved proof-of-concept rhythm:
quiet approach → character reveal → meaningful choice → escalation → major visual beat → factual artifact → consequence → widening-world echo.

This is a grammar, not a mandatory eight-page template.

## Benchmark question
Would the sequence still work as an illustrated fantasy chapter if the reader did not care about fantasy football?

If yes, the Chronicle translation is working.

If removing the score removes the story, it is still a recap.

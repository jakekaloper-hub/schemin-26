## DOSSIER 01 — CHILI OUTLAW vs THE LLC

**FACT**
Chili Cheesers 198.00 defeated The LLC 122.01. Margin: 75.99. Highest Week 1 score and largest Week 1 margin.

**CANON**
Chili Outlaw: Western outlaw/pitmaster, chili-red material language, chili equipment, Dark Horse.
LLC: human corporate raider, tailored dark suit, sunglasses, cigar, gold, executive chair/briefcase, boardroom/market language.

**INTERPRETATION**
Week 1's loudest public statement. Contrast is unusually strong: heat, dust, food/fire and outlaw materiality against controlled corporate surfaces.

**STORY PROPOSAL**
Treat the contest as a collision between systems of value rather than a generic duel. LLC enters believing everything can be priced, contracted and contained. Chili arrives with a form of abundance/excess that refuses clean accounting. The result leaves an unmistakable paper trail without requiring literal destruction of LLC's Week 2 boardroom before Week 2 occurs.

**VISUAL ANCHOR**
A pristine contract/ledger foreground object progressively stained by chili/oil/ash; Dark Horse as silhouette or presence where composition permits.

**CONSEQUENCE TO CARRY**
Chili leaves Week 1 with the strongest numerical public evidence. LLC leaves 0-1 with corporate identity intact.

**PROHIBITED**
Do not pre-stage the Week 2 Sonic delivery. Do not destroy LLC's boardroom as if the Week 2 Chins event already happened.

## DOSSIER 02 — TDS vs EL NIÑO

**FACT**
TDS 175.56 defeated El Niño 144.42. Margin: 31.14. TDS posts Week 1's second-highest score.

**CANON**
TDS: green-gold reptile humanoid, glowing eyes, long dreadlocks, dark #3 football gear, jungle/waterfall language.
El Niño: elemental humanoid composed of storm water/ocean spray/cloud/lightning, storm staff.

**INTERPRETATION**
A strong TDS opening should make later reversals meaningful, but the Week 1 chapter cannot know them.

**STORY PROPOSAL**
Use a contest of endurance/navigation through violent weather and water rather than a simple sword fight. TDS emerges with visible proof that the storm did not erase him.

**VISUAL ANCHOR**
Dreadlocks intact and prominent. Water moving around the serpent silhouette; storm light defining El Niño as a participant, not scenery.

**CONSEQUENCE TO CARRY**
TDS 1-0 with strong reputation evidence. El Niño 0-1 without implying weakness as an elemental being.

**PROHIBITED**
No haircut/barbershop foreshadowing that implies foreknowledge. No treating El Niño as weather summoned by TDS.

## DOSSIER 03 — DR. DUCKHOOK vs SEVEN DEADLY CHINS

**FACT**
Duckhook 147.32 defeated Chins 145.82. Margin: 1.50, Week 1's closest result.

**CANON**
Duckhook: anthropomorphic white duck golfer, bucket hat/golf clothing, cigar, golf equipment/prosthetic concept.
Chins: very large bearded bruiser, dirty tank, trucker cap, hammer, 40oz, truck-stop BBQ language, raccoon.

**INTERPRETATION**
This is the tension story. It should feel undecided almost to the end.

**STORY PROPOSAL**
A strange roadside challenge where precision and brute persistence collide. The final difference can be represented through a tiny physical measure—distance, mark, token, line, or ledger notation—without claiming the fantasy score itself exists diegetically.

**VISUAL ANCHOR**
A near-imperceptible margin represented materially; both characters occupy the frame with dignity.

**CONSEQUENCE TO CARRY**
Duckhook escapes 1-0; Chins has the strongest possible evidence that 0-1 need not mean humiliation.

**PROHIBITED**
Do not make Chins stupid. Do not make Duckhook a medical doctor. Country Club of Jackson is Week 2 evidence and need not be back-projected here.

## DOSSIER 04 — ARSENAL CENTAUR vs SLOB

**FACT**
Donkey Kong 142.70 defeated Slob on my Dobb 134.96. Margin: 7.74.

**CANON**
Donkey Kong resolves to Arsenal Centaur: muscular centaur, Arsenal-red identity, axe, tankard.
Slob: huge horned shaggy human-beast berserker, battered football armor, filthy championship shirt, metal bat, beer/tailgate language, pit-bull.

**INTERPRETATION**
A physical encounter can work here because both silhouettes support it, but it should reveal personality rather than become anonymous action.

**STORY PROPOSAL**
A rough public contest with tavern/roadside/fairground energy—boasting, impact, spilled drink, stubbornness. Arsenal Centaur wins without making Slob small.

**VISUAL ANCHOR**
Tankard and bat as opposing material signatures; equine scale vs Slob's mass.

**CONSEQUENCE TO CARRY**
Arsenal Centaur 1-0. Slob 0-1 but remains dangerous and socially loud.

**PROHIBITED**
Never depict Donkey as gorilla/ape. Do not pre-create Week 2 Country Club result.

## DOSSIER 05 — TRADE JEDI vs BELT KEEPER

**FACT**
ObiWan Jacoby 141.70 defeated the then-labelled Immortal 131.26. Margin: 10.44.

**CANON**
Trade Jedi: youthful blond human strategist, earth-tone Jedi-inspired clothing, green energy sword, golden retriever, North Georgia mountain-war-room language, NO championship belt.
Belt Keeper: corpse-pale immortal medieval champion, black rune armor/cloak, rune sword, Championship Belt.

**INTERPRETATION**
This is a reputation encounter because one participant visibly carries the Belt. The result changes the week's conversation but does not transfer the Belt.

**STORY PROPOSAL**
A measured encounter built around preparation, positioning and restraint rather than a throne-room conquest. The dog can humanize the Trade Jedi before/after the contest. The Belt Keeper leaves defeated but still carrying the object that makes the encounter socially resonant.

**VISUAL ANCHOR**
Green sword against rune-dark silhouette; Championship Belt visibly remains on/with Belt Keeper in aftermath.

**CONSEQUENCE TO CARRY**
ObiWan 1-0 and gains public evidence against a prestigious-looking opponent. Belt Keeper 0-1; Belt retained.

**PROHIBITED**
No championship belt on ObiWan. No declaration that Week 1 victory makes him champion. No invented magical Belt transfer rule.

## DOSSIER 06 — MUD DOGS vs RED LEOPARDS

**FACT**
Mud Dogs 125.36 defeated Red Leopards 120.86. Margin: 4.50.

**CANON**
Mud Dogs: massive rabid mud-covered dog/wolf beast, blue-orange identity, heavy chain, violent swamp language.
Red Leopards: giant anthropomorphic red leopard/jaguar, black rosettes, amber eyes, ancient/tribal armor, claws, jungle-ruin language.

**INTERPRETATION**
This is a close predator-vs-predator result whose importance grows only through later history. Week 1 itself should leave it unresolved emotionally.

**STORY PROPOSAL**
A territorial crossing or pursuit at the edge of incompatible environments. Mud Dogs gets the Week 1 result; Red exits intact rather than being reduced to prey.

**VISUAL ANCHOR**
Mud/water foreground versus red feline silhouette and ruined stone/vegetation cues. Avoid prematurely declaring geographic borders.

**CONSEQUENCE TO CARRY**
Mud Dogs 1-0; Red 0-1. The result exists as public evidence available to later narration.

**PROHIBITED**
Do not foreshadow Red's Week 2 Chili event as known destiny. Do not turn Mud Dogs into a normal pet.

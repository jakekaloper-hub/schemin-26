# GATE C — MULTI-PAGE STORY PRODUCTION
## Trade Jedi vs. His Majesty's Blood / The Belt Keeper

**Status:** ACTIVE PRODUCTION DIRECTIVE
**Bullpen authority:** autonomous through routine production gates

## Correction to prior Gate C framing

Gate C is **not** satisfied by a single matchup poster or single illustrated spread.

The single-page render is retained only as a **character-recognition / visual-anchor test**.

The actual proof of concept must demonstrate that Schemin Chronicle can tell one fantasy-football result as a sustained illustrated story inside a physical fantasy novel.

---

# FACT LOCK

Week 1:
- ObiWan Jacoby: 141.70
- His Majesty's Blood / Belt Keeper identity: 131.26
- Result: ObiWan Jacoby wins.
- Championship Belt does **not** transfer.
- Trade Jedi does **not** possess or wear the Championship Belt.
- Belt remains with The Belt Keeper.

Identity:
- Jake Kaloper → The Trade Jedi → ObiWan Jacoby.
- Austin Byars → The Belt Keeper → His Majesty's Blood.

No Week 2 knowledge may leak into this sequence.

---

# CHARACTER LOCK

## Trade Jedi
- youthful blond human strategist;
- earth-tone robes / field-jacket language;
- green energy sword;
- golden retriever companion;
- North Georgia mountain / strategy language;
- NO championship belt.

## Belt Keeper
Use Jake-approved visual reference as recognition authority:
- corpse-pale / ashen immortal human appearance;
- long black hair;
- luminous pale/white eyes;
- severe unsmiling face;
- dark facial rune/scar markings;
- layered black rune/scale armor;
- dark cloak/material language;
- enormous inscribed rune sword;
- Championship Belt at waist;
- lean, intimidating immortal proportions;
- NO beard;
- NO crown;
- NEVER generic medieval king.

---

# STORY PURPOSE

This is not a title fight.

The dramatic question is:

> What does it mean to defeat the man carrying the Belt without taking the Belt from him?

Week 1 should establish the distinction between:
- winning a contest;
- possessing reputation;
- possessing history;
- possessing the Belt.

Obi earns evidence.
The Belt Keeper retains the symbol.

That unresolved imbalance is the consequence.

---

# TARGET LENGTH

Initial story budget:
**6–8 finished pages**.

This is a budget, not a quota. Bullpen may contract or expand if pacing requires it.

The sequence must include:
- at least one quiet page;
- at least one major painting/spread;
- at least one evidence/artifact treatment;
- at least one aftermath page.

Do not make every page equally loud.

---

# PROPOSED PAGE GRAMMAR

## C1 — THE ROAD TO THE KEEPER
Function: Approach.

Trade Jedi and retriever move from the warmer mountain territory toward the Belt Keeper's colder monumental domain.

Low prose density.
No score.
No combat.
The Belt may be glimpsed indirectly or at distance.

Purpose: anticipation and scale.

## C2 — THE MAN WHO CARRIED HISTORY
Function: Encounter / character reveal.

Full or near-full Belt Keeper portrait/environment.

The reader sees the correct character clearly:
ashen face, long black hair, pale eyes, runes, armor, sword, Belt.

Narrative focus:
the Belt is visible;
its history is felt;
its exact supernatural status remains unknown.

No invented championship lineage.

## C3–C4 — THE CONTEST
Function: Complication → Turning Point.

Major illustrated spread.

Competition remains abstracted into Schemin-world conflict rather than visible fantasy-football mechanics.

Favor:
- positioning;
- strategy;
- terrain;
- restraint;
- feints;
- pressure;
- tactical reversals.

Avoid generic prolonged sword-duel choreography merely because both possess weapons.

The Trade Jedi wins through preparation/positioning rather than overpowering the Belt Keeper physically.

No Week 2 foreshadowing.

## C5 — THE RECEIPT
Function: Fact lock / evidence.

Quieter artifact-led page.

Verified typography carries:
**ObiWan Jacoby 141.70**
**His Majesty's Blood 131.26**

Possible physical forms:
- League ledger;
- sealed result notice;
- Pittsy notation;
- official receipt.

Exact factual text is typeset, not entrusted to generated micro-text.

This page establishes:
ObiWan Jacoby: 1–0.
His Majesty's Blood: 0–1.

## C6 — THE BELT STAYS
Function: Consequence.

The strongest conceptual page.

Obi has won.
The Belt Keeper still wears the Belt.

No transfer scene.
No accidental implication that Obi has claimed it.

Potential visual:
distance between the two after the contest;
Belt Keeper upright with Belt;
Trade Jedi departing / lowering weapon / turning toward the road;
retriever beside him.

The emotion is not humiliation.
It is unresolved hierarchy.

## C7 — WHAT A WIN DOES NOT BUY
Function: Reflection / character consequence.

Prose-led or mixed page.

Core distinction:
the ledger can record the victory;
it cannot automatically rewrite reputation, history, ownership or legacy.

This should deepen both characters rather than celebrate one and diminish the other.

## C8 — RECEIPTS TRAVEL
Function: Echo / transition back into Chapter I.

Evidence leaves the encounter and enters the wider League:
a folded result, messenger, posted ledger, bookmaker's notation, or other grounded transmission.

This reconnects the sequence to Chapter I's recurring receipt/evidence motif.

It should naturally hand the narrative to the next Week 1 storyline.

---

# VISUAL RHYTHM

Target rhythm:

quiet landscape
→ character portrait
→ escalating encounter
→ dramatic spread
→ quiet artifact
→ consequence painting
→ prose/reflection
→ transition artifact

Do not repeat the magazine-layout formula:
hero left + hero right + scoreboard center + explanatory boxes.

That format served recognition testing only.

---

# PROSE DOCTRINE

Write as a chapter, not sports copy.

Avoid:
- “statement win” clichés;
- power-ranking language;
- recap voice;
- explaining fantasy football;
- treating scores as magic power levels;
- narrator knowing future weeks.

Use:
- physical action;
- observation;
- material objects;
- restrained dialogue where earned;
- character knowledge separate from world truth;
- consequence.

The narrator may know the verified result but must remain temporally inside Week 1.

---

# CHARACTER DIGNITY

Obi's win must not convert Belt Keeper into a loser caricature.

Belt Keeper's retained Belt must not erase Obi's legitimate win.

Both characters leave the sequence with increased narrative value.

That is the standard.

---

# PRODUCTION LOOP

1. Beat Writer — draft complete continuous scene before page slicing.
2. Clubhouse Manager — character dignity / recognition review.
3. Umpire — factual and temporal audit.
4. Editor/Closer — revise manuscript.
5. Storyboard — slice accepted prose into page rhythm.
6. Art direction — create page-specific briefs.
7. Render — generate paintings/background material.
8. Composition — apply verified typography separately where factual.
9. Character QA.
10. Truth QA.
11. Render QA.
12. Reader Experience Pass.
13. Closer approval.
14. Canon commit.

Do not generate all eight pages independently from isolated prompts. The story must exist first.

---

# ACCEPTANCE TEST

Gate C passes only if a reader can remove the score page and still understand:

- who these two inhabitants are;
- why meeting the Belt Keeper matters;
- how Obi earned the encounter;
- that Obi won;
- that the Belt did not transfer;
- why that distinction matters;
- and why both characters remain important afterward.

If the sequence only communicates “141.70 beat 131.26,” Gate C fails.

If it reads like an illustrated chapter that happens to be driven by that verified result, Gate C passes.

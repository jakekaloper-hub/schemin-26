## Typography law
No page is forced to accept more prose than readable literary type permits. If typesetting at final size requires compression, add a spread; do not shrink the book into a memo.

## Illustration law
Art is commissioned only after the exact manuscript excerpt for a spread is assigned. Generated art contains no authoritative semantic text. The compositor owns prose, titles, scores, names and artifact labels.

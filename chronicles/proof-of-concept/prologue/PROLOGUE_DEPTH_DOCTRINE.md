# PROLOGUE DEPTH DOCTRINE
## Schemin Chronicle — Volume I

**Status:** BINDING
**Supersedes:** fixed 8/10/12-page targets
**Authority:** Jake + Bullpen

# RULING

There is no fixed Prologue page count.

The Prologue ends when it has successfully established:
- the world;
- the League;
- historical depth;
- the twelve inhabitants;
- the reigning champion's inherited weight;
- evidence vs memory vs legend;
- meaningful objects and institutions;
- enough geography to orient the reader without inventing political borders;
- the threshold into the unwritten 2026 season.

**Depth determines pages. Pages do not determine depth.**

A working production estimate may exist for planning, but it may never force compression.

# LITERARY NORTH STAR

Do not imitate any named author's distinctive prose.

Pursue the underlying qualities of deep epic fantasy:
- deep time;
- history that predates the plot;
- incomplete and contested records;
- objects with provenance;
- geography that affects behavior;
- institutions with memory;
- characters with lives before their introductions;
- names whose histories matter;
- songs, sayings, receipts, marginalia, letters, records and rumors;
- consequences that persist;
- exposition revealed through encounter;
- quiet passages that allow scale;
- a sense that the reader sees only part of a larger world.

The finished Chronicle must contain this literature on the page. The manuscript cannot hide the depth off-page.

# PROLOGUE PRINCIPLE

The Prologue is not a roster introduction.

It is the reader's first experience of **Schemin as a historical world**.

The twelve characters are introduced because they inhabit that world, not because the book needs to complete a checklist.

# HISTORICAL CENTER OF GRAVITY

At the threshold of 2026:

**Austin Byars → The Belt Keeper → His Majesty's Blood**

enters as the reigning 2025 champion.

This gives him historical gravity.

It does not make him the protagonist of the Prologue.

The championship should function as inherited world state:
- the Belt has a current custodian;
- the League enters the new volume beneath an existing reputation hierarchy;
- eleven other inhabitants enter a season in which that fact already exists.

Do not invent championship lineage or unsupported historical details to amplify this.

# TWELVE INTRODUCTIONS — NEW STANDARD

Every inhabitant receives a true narrative entrance.

An entrance should establish several of:
- body/species/silhouette;
- environment;
- signature object/companion;
- behavior;
- reputation;
- relationship to evidence/history;
- desire or pressure entering 2026;
- one unresolved question.

Do not require all categories for every character.

Do not introduce characters through stat cards.

Do not give equal word counts merely for fairness.

Give each enough space to become memorable.

# WORLD DEPTH SYSTEM

For every established world element, track:

**FACT** — externally verified league/event truth.

**CANON** — accepted Schemin identity/event/world truth.

**INTERPRETATION** — plausible meaning, not objective fact.

**STORY** — creative connective tissue that may become canon only after acceptance.

The prose may be mysterious.
The production ledger may not be confused.

# DEPTH WITHOUT LORE DUMP

Worldbuilding should enter through:
- what a character notices;
- what an object has endured;
- what a road requires;
- what a clerk crossed out;
- what a receipt proves;
- what a rumor claims;
- what an environment makes difficult;
- what another character remembers differently.

Avoid encyclopedia paragraphs unless presented deliberately as an in-world artifact.

# CHARACTER KNOWLEDGE

Track separately:
1. World truth.
2. What the narrator can responsibly state.
3. What a character knows.
4. What a character believes.
5. What the League says.
6. What rumor says.

Contradictions between 3–6 can create depth.
Contradictions with 1 must be intentional and attributable.

# ART / LITERATURE RELATIONSHIP

No default page template.

Possible page forms:
- full literary page with marginal illustration;
- 60% prose / 40% painting;
- full-bleed painting with a few lines;
- artifact page;
- map fragment;
- quiet object study;
- two-page cinematic spread;
- dialogue-led page;
- near-silent transition;
- illuminated-manuscript treatment.

Artwork must add information, atmosphere or emotion.
It cannot merely decorate prose.

Prose must remain readable at final output size.

# PACING LAW

Epic depth requires silence.

Do not interpret “more depth” as “more words everywhere.”

Allow:
- pauses;
- landscape;
- objects;
- travel;
- weather;
- waiting;
- observation;
- unresolved questions.

Contrast makes the dense passages matter.

# PROLOGUE ACCEPTANCE

The Prologue passes only if a new reader can answer, without external explanation:

- What kind of world have I entered?
- Why does its past matter?
- Why is evidence important?
- Who are the twelve inhabitants?
- Can I visually distinguish all twelve?
- Why does the Belt Keeper carry unusual historical weight entering 2026?
- What remains uncertain?
- Why does opening the 2026 ledger feel consequential?
- Do I want to turn the page?

If the answer depends on fantasy-football knowledge, the Prologue is not finished.

# PROLOGUE — EMOTIONAL HISTORY ENGINE

**Status:** BINDING PREPRODUCTION CONTRACT
**Owners:** Scout + Librarian + Beat Writer + Clubhouse Manager + Umpire

# PURPOSE

Every one of the twelve 2026 inhabitants must enter the Chronicle carrying a distinct emotional condition grounded in actual league history.

Emotion may be dramatized.
Its factual cause may not be invented.

# REQUIRED INPUTS PER OWNER / CHARACTER

## Historical
- prior-season finish;
- playoff path where verified;
- championship/podium history where verified;
- prior meaningful head-to-head results;
- persistent rivalries supported by evidence;
- prior public/published league events.

## Asset economy
- keeper players;
- keeper round/cost;
- keeper year/control status;
- 2026 draft-pick ownership;
- picks traded away;
- picks acquired;
- future-pick obligations/assets;
- material prior trades that explain the asset state.

**Source priority:** commissioner draft-pick spreadsheet / validated ledger.

## 2026 draft
- actual draft slot;
- keeper placements;
- selections by round;
- traded-pick effects;
- roster construction;
- unusual reaches/values only when evaluation is separately supported;
- post-draft roster state.

## Opening competitive state
- Week 1 opening matchup;
- Week 1 result;
- standings after Week 1;
- only later-week information if the Prologue's temporal framing intentionally permits it.

# EMOTIONAL TRANSLATION CONTRACT

Facts do not equal emotions automatically.

For every emotional statement store:

**EVIDENCE:** What happened?
**PRESSURE:** What objective situation does it create?
**INTERPRETATION:** What might that mean for this inhabitant?
**EXPRESSION:** How can the story show it without claiming private psychology as fact?
**STATUS:** FACT / CANON / INTERPRETATION / STORY.

Example pattern:

EVIDENCE: reigning 2025 champion.
PRESSURE: enters 2026 as current standard-bearer and Belt custodian.
INTERPRETATION: others may measure themselves against him.
EXPRESSION: the Belt Keeper enters with an object and reputation already carrying weight.
Do NOT automatically write: “he feared losing everything.”

# EMOTIONAL RANGE GOAL

Do not give twelve characters the same “hungry for a championship” motivation.

Evidence may support different registers such as:
- burden of defending;
- unfinished business;
- impatience;
- confidence;
- embarrassment;
- rebuilding;
- opportunism;
- uncertainty;
- resentment;
- pride;
- relief;
- financial/asset constraint;
- future-oriented patience;
- win-now pressure;
- desire for recognition.

These are **candidate registers**, not facts. Use only when evidence supports the pressure and the prose properly distinguishes interpretation.

# DRAFT AS CHARACTER HISTORY

The draft is not a utility table in the Chronicle.

It can become:
- choices made before the first road opened;
- resources committed;
- opportunities surrendered;
- bargains inherited;
- confidence made visible through cost;
- future flexibility exchanged for present strength.

But the actual picks, costs and ownership must remain exact.

# DRAFT-PICK SPREADSHEET AS CONSEQUENCE

Draft picks represent control over future opportunity.

The spreadsheet should inform:
- who entered 2026 rich/poor in future assets;
- who had already spent future optionality;
- who possessed extra leverage;
- which relationships include prior exchange/debt.

Do not describe pick trades as literal monetary debt unless Chronicle canon deliberately establishes that translation.

# STANDINGS AS SOCIAL PRESSURE

Standings are not merely rankings.

Once games occur they alter:
- credibility;
- pressure;
- public argument;
- rivalry context;
- evidence available to the League.

For Prologue temporal purity, pre-Week-1 pages must not know Week-1 outcomes unless the narrator's framing is explicitly retrospective.

# HISTORICAL EVIDENCE SAFETY

Current repo inventory indicates:
- 2025 history recovery is incomplete in authoritative files;
- historical standings/playoff path cannot be inferred from incomplete snapshots;
- the commissioner keeper/draft-pick ledger contract is required;
- current repo does not yet contain a fully authoritative draft-pick ledger.

Therefore:
**NO emotional dossier may promote an unverified historical lead into fact.**

Unknown is an allowed field.

# SOURCE MATRIX

Each dossier claim should point to one of:
- validated ESPN/Data Gateway evidence;
- commissioner spreadsheet/ledger;
- actual draft record;
- published league artifact/memo;
- Character Master;
- accepted Chronicle canon;
- explicitly labeled user correction.

# PROLOGUE WRITING RULE

The reader should feel that all twelve arrived from somewhere.

Not necessarily geographically.

They arrive from:
- last season;
- the draft;
- keeper decisions;
- trades;
- victories;
- losses;
- reputations;
- jokes;
- humiliations;
- possessions;
- expectations.

The Prologue's job is to make those histories present without resolving them.

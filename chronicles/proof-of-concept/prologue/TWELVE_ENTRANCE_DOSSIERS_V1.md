# TWELVE ENTRANCE DOSSIERS V1
## Prologue Character Recognition + Narrative Entrance Matrix

**Rule:** These are entrances, not biographies. Each should give the reader one memorable encounter with the inhabitant before Chapter I.

## 1 — THE TRADE JEDI · Jake Kaloper · ObiWan Jacoby
**Recognition:** youthful blond human strategist; earth-tone field/robe language; green energy sword; ONE golden retriever; North Georgia mountain strategy environment; NO Belt.
**Entrance behavior:** studying routes, pages, possibilities rather than posing.
**Literary impression:** preparation; curiosity; movement.
**Object:** leather case/notes + green blade.
**Question left open:** what is he preparing to become?

## 4 — FRAT-BRO BERSERKER · Jordan Hollingshead · Slob on my Dobb

## 5 — SWAMP-BORN MENACE · Bobby Mitchell · Mud Dogs

**Temporal rule:** No Week 1 result or Week 2 event may leak into these entrances.

**Interpretive rule:** evidence creates pressure; pressure permits literary interpretation; interpretation is not a claim about an owner's private psychology.

## 1 — TRADE JEDI
**Inherited evidence:** 2025 runner-up. Puka Nacua R6; Rashee Rice R13. Missing live R4. Actual draft core adds Derrick Henry, Kenneth Walker III and Jaylen Waddle. Audited preseason state: strong conversion/high projected roster.
**Objective pressure:** unfinished championship business plus unusually efficient retained value makes impatience costly.
**Emotional register:** urgency disciplined into restraint.
**Scene upgrade:** morning after draft night in the mountain war room. Retriever awake. Papers remain. One place in an ordered sequence is conspicuously absent. Green blade initially unlit. The scene is about what he chooses not to move.
**Question:** Can a man one victory short resist treating every opportunity as the missing victory?

## 2 — BELT KEEPER
**Inherited evidence:** reigning 2025 champion. JSN R2; De'Von Achane R15. No live R1. Strong keeper leverage but weaker supporting conversion; preseason projection did not automatically reinforce the crown.
**Objective pressure:** clearest symbol of completed history, entering a season that owes him nothing.
**Emotional register:** weight without panic.
**Scene upgrade:** maintenance rather than coronation. Cloth/whetstone over runes. Old light over the Belt. No celebration. Victory has already become something that must be carried.
**Question:** What does a reigning champion do when history stops helping?

## 3 — ARSENAL CENTAUR
**Inherited evidence:** current Donkey Kong; former Baker Moore Purdy. Bijan Robinson R1; Breece Hall R4. Conventional live inventory. Audited draft judged the resulting roster stronger than its starting stock and projected it highly.
**Objective pressure:** doctrine survived draft night; reality has not tested it.
**Emotional register:** confidence awaiting falsification.
**Scene upgrade:** axe edge checked, tack secured, tankard cooling while a route is marked. Thought expressed through preparation, not exposition.
**Question:** What happens when doctrine meets an opponent that does not care about doctrine?

## 4 — FRAT-BRO BERSERKER
**Inherited evidence:** Judkins R8; Bucky Irving R14; two R1 selections plus additional premium capital. Audited report calls this the richest board and strongest projected roster. Retained Draft Week publication also describes prior negative luck/four-game late-season skid; treat that latter item as secondary until underlying 2025 ledger is recovered.
**Objective pressure:** abundance eliminates excuses before it creates victories.
**Emotional register:** expectation disguised as disorder.
**Scene upgrade:** room appears chaotic until the reader notices every useful thing is exactly within reach. Cooler, bat, dog, two premium draft-night objects. The mess has structure.
**Question:** What does the League demand from the inhabitant given the richest hand?

## 5 — SWAMP-BORN MENACE
**Inherited evidence:** Saquon Barkley R1; Sam LaPorta R4; conventional live inventory. Audited keeper economics describe both retained costs as inefficient relative to market snapshot; preseason projection toward lower end.
**Objective pressure:** expensive commitments existed before the first contest.
**Emotional register:** possession as weight.
**Scene upgrade:** emphasize the chain—not as imprisonment, but as weight dragged through terrain while the creature moves anyway.
**Question:** When the things you chose to keep are expensive, do you carry them—or do they carry you?

## 6 — PODIUM SHADOW
**Inherited evidence:** 2025 third. Chris Olave R7; Javonte Williams R10. No live R1/R2/R3; first live premium selection R4. Low preseason projection despite prior podium; execution assessed better than raw outcome.

**Emotional register:** credibility under deprivation.
**Scene upgrade:** jungle is abundant; scarcity exists only in the armory. Three empty hooks/spaces quietly echo missing premium rounds. He chooses from what remains.
**Question:** How much of a contender remains when the room takes away his first three weapons?

## 7 — BLUE-COLLAR SPOILER

**Emotional register:** ordinary position, stubborn consequence.
**Scene upgrade:** roadside smoke before dawn. Hammer used for practical work before it becomes threatening. Raccoon provides life, not mascot comedy.

# THE DRAFT AS SHARED STORY EVENT

The 2026 draft belongs inside the Prologue as the last common event before the clean competitive ledger.

**Before:** inherited 2025 history and keeper choices establish what each carries.

**During:** cross-cut unequal opportunity—Belt Keeper without live R1; TDS waiting through R1–R3; Slob with two R1 bullets; LLC with multiple R2/R3 assets; El Niño with two R1 opportunities; Trade Jedi working around missing R4 while holding keeper surplus; Red controlling natural 1.01 plus late keeper leverage; other verified choices.

**After:** each inhabitant returns to a distinct environment with changed resources.

The draft is therefore not a recap. It is one event experienced twelve different ways.

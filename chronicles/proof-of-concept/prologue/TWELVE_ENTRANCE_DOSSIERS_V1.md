# TWELVE ENTRANCE DOSSIERS V1
## Prologue Character Recognition + Narrative Entrance Matrix

**Rule:** These are entrances, not biographies. Each should give the reader one memorable encounter with the inhabitant before Chapter I.

## 1 — THE TRADE JEDI · Jake Kaloper · ObiWan Jacoby
**Recognition:** youthful blond human strategist; earth-tone field/robe language; green energy sword; ONE golden retriever; North Georgia mountain strategy environment; NO Belt.
**Entrance behavior:** studying routes, pages, possibilities rather than posing.
**Literary impression:** preparation; curiosity; movement.
**Object:** leather case/notes + green blade.
**Question left open:** what is he preparing to become?
**Failure:** generic franchise knight; Belt; multiple invented companions; chosen-one framing.

## 2 — THE BELT KEEPER · Austin Byars · His Majesty's Blood
**Recognition authority:** Jake-approved uploaded reference.
**Recognition:** ashen/corpse-pale human immortal; long black hair; luminous pale eyes; facial rune/scar marks; black rune/scale armor; enormous inscribed sword; Belt at waist; no beard/crown.
**Entrance behavior:** maintaining/fastening/carrying the Belt rather than celebrating.
**Historical fact:** reigning 2025 champion.
**Literary impression:** inherited weight; composure; scrutiny.
**Question:** what does defending a reputation demand?
**Failure:** generic king, beard, crown, heroic victory pose, fabricated championship lore.

## 3 — ARSENAL CENTAUR · Wilson Look · Donkey Kong
**Recognition:** large muscular centaur/equine warrior; Arsenal-red language; ornate axe; tankard.
**Entrance behavior:** philosophical calm amid physical scale—sharpening axe while drink cools, reading/considering something, or surveying mountain road.
**Impression:** force with thought behind it.
**Question:** when does restraint end?
**Failure:** gorilla; normal horse rider; generic barbarian.

## 4 — FRAT-BRO BERSERKER · Jordan Hollingshead · Slob on my Dobb
**Recognition:** huge horned shaggy human-beast; battered football armor; filthy Slob championship shirt; metal bat; beer/tailgate; pit-bull.
**Entrance behavior:** loud social space with evidence of chaos, but not reduced to joke.
**Impression:** dangerous conviviality; appetite; unpredictability.
**Question:** how much of the disorder is deliberate?
**Failure:** ordinary human; comic mascot; generic demon.

## 5 — SWAMP-BORN MENACE · Bobby Mitchell · Mud Dogs
**Recognition:** massive rabid mud-covered dog/wolf beast; blue-orange language; heavy chain; diseased teeth; violent swamp.
**Entrance behavior:** emerging from water/mud or dragging chain through terrain.
**Impression:** territorial inevitability.
**Question:** what can cross its ground safely?
**Failure:** human biker as base identity; domestic dog; clean heroic wolf.

## 6 — PODIUM SHADOW · Phillip Pitts · Three Dreaded Snake
**Recognition:** anthropomorphic reptile/snake humanoid; green-gold scales; glowing green eyes; LONG DREADLOCKS; dark #3 football gear; jungle/waterfall.
**Entrance behavior:** stillness above moving water; observing/recording rather than attacking.
**Impression:** patience; accumulated presence.
**Question:** what does he remember that others dismiss?
**Failure:** hair removed; ordinary human; literal three-headed snake unless canon changes.

## 7 — BLUE-COLLAR SPOILER · Ben Whipple · Seven Deadly Chins
**Recognition:** very large heavyset bearded human bruiser; dirty white tank; CHIN UP SHUT UP trucker cap; oversized hammer; 40oz; truck-stop BBQ; raccoon.
**Entrance behavior:** work rather than pose—hammer nearby, food/smoke/roadside life around him.
**Impression:** underestimated mass and persistence.
**Question:** who mistakes him for comic relief?
**Failure:** crowned king; polished warrior; removing cap/body identity.

## 8 — RED LEOPARDS · Kevin Zeek · Red Leopards
**Recognition:** giant anthropomorphic red leopard/jaguar; black rosettes; amber eyes; ancient/tribal armor; huge claws; jungle ruins.
**Entrance behavior:** controlled hunt/observation from ruined elevation.
**Impression:** precision and predatory patience.
**Question:** what has already entered his territory?
**Failure:** normal leopard; generic cat-man; human with leopard clothes.

## 9 — WEATHER SYSTEM · Manning Welty · El Niño
**Recognition:** elemental humanoid composed of storm water/ocean spray/cloud/lightning; glowing blue eyes; cyclone motifs; storm staff; flooded/storm environment.
**Entrance behavior:** weather arrives before body resolves.
**Impression:** environment becoming a person.
**Question:** where does the inhabitant end and the storm begin?
**Failure:** normal human wizard; blue-armored knight.

## 10 — HOSTILE TAKEOVER · David Babb · The LLC
**Recognition:** powerful human corporate raider; dark tailored three-piece; sunglasses; cigar; gold; leather executive chair; LLC briefcase; dark boardroom/market.
**Entrance behavior:** signs/closes/reviews something while others wait.
**Impression:** control through structure rather than force.

## 11 — KING OF THE IMPOSSIBLE LIE · Zach Wilson · Dr. Duckhook
**Recognition:** anthropomorphic white duck golfer; camo/floral bucket hat; golf outfit; cigar; golf-club/driver prosthetic-arm concept; equipment; swamp/water golf.
**Entrance behavior:** calmly addressing an absurdly difficult lie as though routine.
**Impression:** precision in impossible terrain.

## 12 — CHILI OUTLAW · Brandon Pryor · Chili Cheesers
**Recognition:** bearded Western outlaw/pitmaster; black cowboy hat with chili detail; sunglasses; chili-red weathered duster; chili-pepper bandolier; steaming chili bowl/pot; Western stable/hideout; Dark Horse.
**Entrance behavior:** tending pot/fire or saddling Dark Horse before travel.
**Impression:** hospitality and provocation share the same fire.
**Question:** when does generosity become challenge?
**Failure:** generic cowboy; missing chili language; wrong horse association.

# ENSEMBLE RULE

No image generator is asked to invent all twelve from names alone.

Approved production options:
1. individually generated/approved plates composed into spreads;
2. groups of 2–3 whose references are explicitly supplied;
3. environmental entrances without showing every body at once.

Recognition is audited visually before typography or decorative polish.

## Consultant review

### 1. Strongest invention: the Archive
It gives Schemin a narrative reason to care about provenance, contradiction, renames and incomplete records. It should become more than a framing room: procedures, workers, physical limitations and its relationship to the League can provide a stable cultural institution connecting radically different territories.

### 2. Depth signals exist; deep time remains thin
The Belt, damaged books, old names and incomplete evidence imply a past, but concrete history collapses toward 2025–2026. Add depth through undated material evidence and recurring practice, not invented championship facts: repair marks, obsolete forms, crossed-out naming conventions, storage rituals, old shelving systems and inherited terminology.

### 3. Several entrances remain dossier-shaped
The pattern “keeper facts → objective pressure → environmental image” becomes visible. V4 should vary entrance mechanics. Some inhabitants should first appear indirectly through objects, environmental disturbance, absence, work, or consequences before direct description.

### 4. The draft is the right convergence event, but its middle remains explanatory
“Keeper,” “first-round,” “premium,” “preseason books,” and audit language expose source machinery when overused. Do not erase factual economics. Give them stable in-world material manifestations—rights of selection, reserved names, sealed claims, empty round-hooks, traded future marks—while verified artifacts/tables retain exact technical language.

### 5. Geography needs connective tissue
Refusing unsupported borders is correct, but refusal alone is not coherence. Repeat shared phenomena: Archive forms/messengers, a common draft signal, common calendar/ritual, standardized receipts, recurring roads or another canon-safe mechanism. Diversity should feel like one mythscape, not twelve genre sets.

### 6. The narration explains epistemology too often
The evidence/memory distinction is excellent. Establish it, then trust the reader. Repeated statements about what the Archive can or cannot prove risk turning literary texture into policy prose.

### 7. The Belt should recur
Use it at structural hinges as visible historical fact—not magic and not protagonist privilege—against which the blank 2026 ledger is measured.

### 8. The clean-page ending works
Preserve the architecture that strips projections, inherited resources and 2025 status of authority over an unplayed year.

## Questions V4 must answer
Does the world feel older than the plot? Is there genuine narrative movement rather than twelve introductions? Is 2025 felt through consequence before explanation? Do all twelve feel native to one world? Does the draft feel irreversible rather than summarized? Do renames feel like lived history rather than metadata? Where can mystery replace explanation? Which repeated abstractions—history, evidence, consequence, opportunity, weight, archive, ledger, pressure—can be cut without losing motifs?

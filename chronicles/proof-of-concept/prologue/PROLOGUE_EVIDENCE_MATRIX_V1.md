# UMPIRE RULE

A mysterious sentence may conceal information.

It may not manufacture information.

When evidence is insufficient, prefer:
- partial view;
- illegible text;
- attributed rumor;
- question;
- blank;
- damaged artifact.

Never use confident narrator prose to bridge a factual gap.

## 0. Purpose

This file is the canonical bridge between the V4 Prologue manuscript and all downstream visual production. It does not paginate the manuscript and does not authorize generated artwork by itself.

Every beat has:
- an immutable manuscript anchor;
- narrative and emotional function;
- character/location dependencies;
- Visual Necessity (V0–V5);
- recommended composition family;
- an explicit art-purpose/redundancy test;
- prohibitions;
- transition logic;
- digital/mobile/print behavior;
- canon gate;
- render authorization independent from V-score.

### Visual Necessity
- **V0 NO ART** — prose must own the beat.
- **V1 AMBIENT** — minimal environmental support.
- **V2 SUPPORTING** — art improves comprehension/atmosphere but remains secondary.
- **V3 FEATURE** — substantial illustration is justified.
- **V4 HERO** — visual should dominate a major moment.
- **V5 ICONIC** — defining image/sequence of the Prologue/world.

### Composition families
- **P0 Pure Prose**
- **P1 Prose + Ornament**
- **P2 Prose + Inset**
- **P3 Text Dominant**
- **P4 Balanced**
- **P5 Art Dominant**
- **P6 Full Art**
- **P7 Cinematic Spread**
- **P8 Atmospheric Transition**
- **P9 Diegetic Artifact / Map**

### Render status
- **TEXT_ONLY** — intentionally no character/environment render.
- **PLANNED_BLOCKED** — visual is justified, but render requires downstream source packet/art brief/reference resolution.
- **READY_FOR_ART_DIRECTION** — beat may enter art-direction stage; this is not final image approval.
- **REFERENCE_ONLY** — useful to composition but should not independently become an illustration.

No principal-character render may advance without the written Master Canon, locked twelve-character plate, and any stronger owner-specific reference. Ensemble imagery must be constructed from individually reference-locked material rather than asking a model to rediscover all Twelve.

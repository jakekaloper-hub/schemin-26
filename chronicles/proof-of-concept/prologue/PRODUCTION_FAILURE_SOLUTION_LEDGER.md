# PROLOGUE PRODUCTION FAILURE / SOLUTION LEDGER

## F001 — Spread generation repeated approved P1–P2 instead of advancing
**Observed:** attempted next-generation output reproduced “Before This Book / What the Paper Remembers.”
**Class:** prompt/context contamination.
**Root cause:** approved benchmark content dominated generation context.
**Fix:** next-spread render brief must explicitly state page numbers and forbid all prior spread headings/content.
**Prevention:** generated art layer contains no authoritative headings/prose; typography is composed afterward.

## F002 — Generated image invented roadmap/thumbnail strip
**Observed:** model added future-page thumbnails and labels.
**Class:** unauthorized layout invention.
**Fix:** art brief must forbid contact sheets, thumbnails, roadmaps, captions and UI.
**Prevention:** render only clean two-page art canvas for requested spread.

## F003 — Semantic text duplication risk
**Observed:** image generation produced titles/body copy that would compete with verified text layer.
**Class:** typography authority violation.
**Fix:** generate clean art with reserved text zones and no meaningful readable writing.
**Prevention:** all semantic text belongs to compositor.

## S001 — Approved P1–P2 visual grammar
**Successful:** ornate facing-page book object, cinematic environment, integrated literary negative space, tactile foreground artifacts, strong depth, warm/cool lighting, readable hierarchy.
**Reuse:** inherit materiality and seriousness, not literal layout/content.

## S002 — Spread-first production
**Successful principle:** paired pages are the atomic narrative/design unit.
**Reuse:** all future architecture, QA and presentation in page pairs.

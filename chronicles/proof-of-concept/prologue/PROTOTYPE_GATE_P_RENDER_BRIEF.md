# P0.4 — THE CLEAN PAGE

## Narrative function
End the Archive Prologue.

The prior archive has established age, incomplete memory and accumulated record. P0.4 narrows all of that history into a single untouched 2026 ledger.

## Composition
A tactile old archive/table/chamber remains visible around the perimeter.

Foreground/center:
- a newly opened 2026 volume;
- fresh paper;
- twelve clean entry lines or structured spaces;
- no wins;
- no losses;
- no Week 1 scores;
- no future knowledge.

Older material may exist around it:
- worn paper edges;
- closed books;
- obscured receipts;
- partial marks;
- Belt-related visual fragment only if non-claiming;
- ink tools;
- evidence of prior recordkeeping.

The old archive should feel materially richer/darker/warmer with age.
The new page should feel brighter, quieter and expectant.

## Text

Primary prose:

> History had already happened.
>
> None of it could fill the empty columns.
>
> The new book required new evidence.

Optional small heading:
**2026**

Do not print unverified historical claims.

## Typography
This page tests actual book typography.

The prose should be typeset, not generated as painterly pseudo-text.

Handwritten/background marks may be baked into art only when:
- non-factual;
- intentionally illegible/partial; or
- separately verified.

## Canon restrictions
- no invented champion names;
- no invented past scores;
- no invented years;
- no invented dynasties;
- no suggestion the Belt is magical;
- no Week 1 result visible;
- no current character shown as already victorious.

## Emotional note
Not ominous.
Not triumphant.
Expectant.

This is the silence before evidence.

# CHAPTER I — PAGE 1

## Title
**CHAPTER I**
**THE FIRST RECEIPTS**

## Opening line
> There were twelve of them when the books opened.

## Narrative function
The Chronicle leaves the archive and enters the inhabited world.

Do not explain all twelve.
Do not show six matchups.
Do not create a mascot lineup.

The page should make the reader want to cross into the world.

## Composition
Full-bleed or near-full-bleed painterly establishing image.

A road/path/high overlook or other transitional landscape may organize depth, but must not claim finalized political geography.

Foreground:
- tactile evidence that the reader has left the archive but not its motif—perhaps one fresh notice/page carried by wind, held, or placed near the observer.

Midground:
- Trade Jedi and golden retriever may serve as the human-scale anchor because North Georgia mountain-war-room language is locked canon.

Background:
- atmospheric suggestions that other environments/institutions exist beyond the immediate view.
- Do NOT literally composite all twelve domains into a theme-park panorama.

The Trade Jedi:
- youthful blond human;
- earth-tone Jedi-inspired clothing/field-jacket language;
- green energy sword may be present but need not be activated;
- golden retriever;
- NO championship belt.

## Visual direction
Original Schemin identity.

Use high-level principles:
- painterly hand-crafted warmth;
- atmospheric depth;
- tactile environment;
- environmental motion;
- monumental fantasy scale;
- grounded material realism;
- quiet wonder before spectacle.

Do not imitate a specific living artist or reproduce protected franchise designs.

## Text load
Very low.

Only:
- chapter designation;
- title;
- opening line;
- perhaps one additional short paragraph if composition genuinely needs it.

The page must breathe.

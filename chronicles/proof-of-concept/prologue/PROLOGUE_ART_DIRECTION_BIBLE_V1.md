## 1. Visual thesis
The Prologue is an adult epic-fantasy chronicle whose world feels materially old, inhabited, repaired and consequential. It is not a fantasy-football website wearing fantasy decoration. The image system must make the League's invisible structures—history, unequal opportunity, memory, geography, waiting, consequence—physically legible without replacing the prose.

Visual ambition: monumental environmental scale; hand-painted warmth; atmospheric depth; expressive naturalism; restrained wonder; believable material culture; cinematic composition; recurring geography and objects. Avoid anime/manga coding and avoid direct imitation of any living artist.

## 2. Material grammar
Primary materials: worn leather, vellum/paper, iron, brass, dark timber, repaired stone, waxed thread, cloth, mud, water, smoke, pepper, weathered paint, hammered metal.
Materials show use. Perfect surfaces are exceptional and narratively meaningful (notably the untouched 2026 ledger).
Damage is history, not decorative grunge.
Magic/supernatural appearance, where canonical (El Niño, energy blade), must not contaminate unrelated objects with generic glow.

## 3. World environments
### Archive
Long, practical, overfilled institutional space. Damaged shelves, repair evidence, red thread, brass tabs, cabinets, ledgers. Light is motivated by lamp/window/fire only. Never Hogwarts-like, enchanted, cathedral-luxurious or filled with invented readable lore.
### Belt Keeper fortress
Cold stone, low daylight/fire, severe scale, sparse utility. Never throne-room shorthand.
### Duckhook waters
Black water, roots, reeds, difficult geometry; country-club equipment can coexist with hostile nature.
### Trade Jedi mountains
North-Georgia-inspired high ridges translated into secondary-world geography; timber/stone strategy room, long windows, papers, dog, unlit blade. Never literal modern Georgia signage.
### TDS jungle/armory
Lush abundance outside; functional armory and empty premium hooks inside. Scarcity is specific, never poverty.
### Slob quarters
Useful disorder: battered sports material, cooler/tankards, equipment, pit bull. Chaos has internal organization.
### LLC boardroom
Dark, exact, expensive, restrained; papers, seals, briefcase, cigar. Pre-modern corporate metaphor translated materially; avoid modern screens in final secondary-world treatment unless deliberately diegetic and canon-approved.
### Mud swamp
Black water, roots, mud, chain, weight. Wet physicality over horror cliché.
### Chili stable
Leather, smoke, pepper, low fire, provisions, Dark Horse. Warm palette/material feeling without becoming food advertising.
### Red ruins
Jungle swallowing old masonry; elevated road sightlines; camouflage and visibility coexist.
### El Niño country
Flooded expanse, channels, cloud/water convergence. Borders remain unresolved.
### Centaur shelter
Mountain utility shelter, hide route map, whetstone, axe, ale; disciplined, tactile.
### Chins roadside
Secondary-world roadside stop/trading post analog, repaired table, smoke, hammer, raccoon; dignity in ordinary labor.

## 4. Geography law
The Prologue does NOT establish a complete political map. Environments are real and recurrent, but no illustration may falsely prove borders, adjacency or travel distances the manuscript does not establish. Repeated roads, notices, boards and Archive systems imply connection without forcing cartographic certainty.

## 5. Lighting grammar
Archive: amber practical pools against deep neutral shadow.
Fortress: cold ambient stone + restrained fire edge.
Mountains: high cool morning; warm paper/timber interior.
Jungle: wet broken canopy light.
Swamp: low reflected sky and water glints.
Stable: low ember/fire warmth.
Red ruins: hard broken canopy / predatory visibility.
Storm: diffuse high-energy atmospheric light, not neon.
Draft convergence: each domain keeps its native lighting; the shared board/clock motif connects them. Never homogenize twelve worlds into one color grade.

## 6. Scale and camera
Use scale to explain relationships, not to make every subject heroic.
- Archive: deep perspective and negative space.
- Character introductions: environmental portrait more often than centered hero pose.
- Objects: macro/close detail only when material consequence matters.
- B040: geographic simultaneity; controlled panorama/composite.
- B060: distant landscape vectors; no climax pose.
Avoid repeated eye-level centered portraits, Dutch angles for fake intensity, lens-flare spectacle, and generic “epic fantasy poster” framing.

## 7. Character framing
Characters are literary inhabitants, not mascots. Show competence through action and environment. Humor emerges from circumstance (warm ale, raccoon, impossible lie), never caricature.
Hard visual invariants come from Master Canon + locked plate + stronger owner reference.
No principal is redesigned by team name.
Trade Jedi never wears Belt.
Belt Keeper is not a king.
Arsenal Gorilla Warrior is a gorilla, never centaur/equine.
TDS retains long dreadlocks in Prologue.
Ensemble construction uses individually locked references and controlled compositing.

## 8. Negative space
Negative space is an active narrative tool:
- untouched ledger;
- empty hooks;
- absent draft selections;
- twelve empty lines;
- completed board after final selection.
Do not fill silence with decorative particles, logos, heraldry or extra figures.

## 9. Painterly treatment
Visible but controlled brush/material texture; believable light; atmospheric perspective; natural surface variation; faces/anatomy readable at intended size. No plastic 3D sheen, hyper-sharpened AI texture, random filigree, over-bloom, pseudo-medieval clutter, or illegible generated text.
Primary prose remains semantic/typeset, never baked into final raster art.

## 10. Recurring motifs
Ledger/page = impartial future.
Belt = present custody/history.
0.4 slip = narrow inherited consequence.
Empty hooks = unavailable premium opportunity.
Marker/papers = strategy/restraint.
Chain = retained weight.
Briefcase/folders = converted capital.
Dark Horse = Chili continuity.
Hammer/table = ordinary competence.
Board = shared economy.
Brass clock/notice = simultaneity.
Motifs recur only when their narrative meaning is active.

## 11. Composition doctrine
P0–P9 from parent map are intent families, not templates. Art may never force manuscript deletion, compression or paraphrase. V-score is not render permission.
B040 is visual ceiling (V5).
B060 is restrained threshold (V4).
No quota for illustrations, full pages, spreads or character appearances.

## 12. Responsive doctrine
Desktop/print relationships may be spatial; mobile relationships may become temporal.
- never shrink a double-spread into unreadable phone scale;
- mobile may order art → prose or prose → art according to beat;
- crops must be art-directed;
- primary prose selectable and scalable;
- alt text describes visual contribution, not duplicated prose;
- reduced-motion mode removes nonessential transitions;
- no content-dependent hover interaction.

## 13. Print doctrine
Pagination occurs after typography metrics. Preserve widows/orphans, comfortable line length/leading, and meaningful recto/verso reveals. Full bleed is earned. Gutter must not consume faces, critical objects or readable artifacts.

## 14. Anti-drift blacklist
Reject: generic fantasy castles; arbitrary magic glow; modern sports dashboards; literal NFL players as in-world cast; twelve-character prompt soup; anime/manga coding; direct living-artist imitation; photobash inconsistency; random heraldry; invented readable records; fake maps; crowns/thrones for Byars; Belt on Jake; gorilla Centaur; shaved Prologue TDS; sexual storylines; family/significant-other invention; Week 1+ foreshadowing; visual jokes that puncture world seriousness.

## 15. Production quality gate
Every final candidate must pass: beat purpose, redundancy, source packet, character silhouette, props, environment, temporal lock, text integrity, crop/responsive, print safe area, accessibility, visual ceiling, continuity.

## 16. Board resolution
Beat Writer requested prose-first restraint; Creative Direction requested stronger environmental identity. Closer resolution: environment art is authorized only when it communicates geography, spatial relationship, material consequence or motif continuity that prose should not have to repeat. Texture alone does not earn a brief.

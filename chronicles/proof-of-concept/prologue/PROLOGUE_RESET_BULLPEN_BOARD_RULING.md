**Austin Byars → The Belt Keeper → His Majesty's Blood → reigning 2025 champion.**

# PROLOGUE MISSION

The Prologue must accomplish six jobs:

1. Establish that the Schemin world existed before the 2026 volume.
2. Establish the inherited order from 2025, centered on the reigning champion and Belt.
3. Establish the universe's material grammar: records, League institutions, wagers, receipts, roads, environments, objects, rumor and incomplete history.
4. Introduce **all twelve 2026 inhabitants accurately**.
5. Teach the reader that team names may change while inhabitants persist.
6. End with a blank 2026 ledger that makes every prior reputation irrelevant to the new results.

# TWELVE-CHARACTER RULE

All twelve must be introduced in the Prologue, but NOT as an AI-generated roster grid.

Each introduction must contain:
- owner resolution;
- current team name;
- canonical character identity;
- species/body lock;
- face/head lock;
- wardrobe lock;
- signature props;
- environment language;
- at least three visible recognition anchors;
- prohibited drift list;
- approved reference image when available.

No multi-character render is permitted until every character appearing in that render has a PASS Character Packet.

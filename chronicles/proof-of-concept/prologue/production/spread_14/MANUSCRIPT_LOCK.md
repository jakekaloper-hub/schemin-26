## Exact compositor manuscript

The last selections came from the parts of the ledger casual observers rarely remembered.

Late rounds.

Depth.

Contingencies.

Names that looked small when written beside stars and could later become enormous, though the Prologue was forbidden from knowing which ones.

That ignorance was important.

The draft board contained no glowing marks identifying future triumph.

No selection arrived with a guarantee.

No keeper cost promised vindication.

No forecast carried authority over an unplayed result.

When the final name disappeared, the board was complete.

For several seconds, across twelve different places, there was nothing left to choose.

The season was still empty.

They did not leave together.

That would have made a better painting than a history.

The Belt Keeper returned to stone and rune and the object everyone could identify at his waist.

The draft had changed his roster.

It had not changed the fact that the Belt remained his to carry into the new year.

That distinction mattered.

The Belt remained metal at his waist. The new names remained ink on the board.

Neither was a 2026 win.

Duckhook returned to water and impossible lies.

The number 0.4 remained where it had been.

The new roster did not erase it.

Neither did the number diminish the new roster.

Past and present occupied the same bag without becoming the same thing.

The Trade Jedi climbed back toward the mountain room.

His table now contained fewer possibilities because possibility had been converted into names.

That was progress, though progress could feel strangely like limitation.

Before the draft, a hundred futures had been available.

After it, one roster existed.

Strategy now had to operate inside reality.

Three Dreaded Snake returned to the jungle carrying fewer premium choices into the year than his prior podium might have suggested.

The three empty hooks remained part of the room.

So did the weapons he had actually chosen.

Scarcity had not disappeared.

It had become specific.

## Exact compositor manuscript

In ordinary speech they were picks.

In the older Archive forms they appeared as rights of choosing: numbered spaces that could be retained, surrendered, exchanged, or arrive bearing another inhabitant's mark.

A pick could be kept.

Moved.

Spent.

Bundled.

Lost from one round and gained in another.

Its value was peculiar because it referred to something that did not yet exist.

That made the draft the closest thing the League had to trading pieces of the future.

Not the future itself.

Nobody owned that.

Only opportunity.

By the time the 2026 draft began, opportunity had already been distributed unevenly because history had been at work long before the first name came off the board.

Some numbered spaces were already sealed by names carried forward. Others had changed hands before the night began.

Some inhabitants entered with early seals stacked before them.

Others entered with empty spaces.

# Narrative purpose
Open Schemin Chronicle as a physical historical object. Establish deep time, incomplete evidence and an archive that cannot fully reconcile the League's past.

# Left page / P1
Dominant cinematic archive image.
Opening line receives primary hierarchy:
**Before this book, there were other books.**

Supporting prose is typeset into deliberately quiet zones, not baked into generated artwork.

# Right page / P2
Archive continues deeper into shelves/evidence.
Prose develops photographs without dates, scores without arguments, crossed names, receipts and incomplete preservation.
Visual movement leads toward evidence objects that P3 will examine more closely.

## Why 40 pages for Prototype 1
V3's 36-page architecture predated the consultant pass. V4's final audit explicitly requires judging density in physical context and forbids predetermined page counts. Twenty spreads gives the 6,558-word manuscript room for approximately 328 source words per spread on average, while allowing illustration-heavy spreads to borrow capacity from prose-dominant spreads.

The architecture intentionally gives the 733-word draft climax three-plus spreads rather than treating it as one illustrated scene.

## Reading-density targets
These are QA envelopes, not quotas:
- prose-dominant spread: ~400–550 source words;
- balanced spread: ~250–400;
- cinematic spread: ~120–280;
- artifact/threshold spread: variable.

If readable final typography cannot carry the assigned excerpt, **add a spread**. Never shrink type to defend 40 pages.

## Visual scarcity rule
Major cinematic plates are reserved for dramatic hinges: Belt custody, Duckhook/0.4, the shared draft convergence, final selection, and Chapter I threshold. Character introductions elsewhere may use half-page/environmental/marginal art.

# BOOK OBJECT RULING

The Prologue becomes a **30-page opening movement** including its final Chapter-I threshold spread.

Thirty pages is not a new cap. It is the first architecture that gives:
- the Archive enough silence to feel old;
- 2025 enough weight to matter;
- all twelve inhabitants individual recognition;
- the 2026 draft enough space to become story;
- the Clean Page enough restraint to land.

No page is a fantasy-football dashboard.

Exact factual typography remains externally typeset and QA-controlled.

---

# VISUAL / LITERARY RHYTHM

The Prologue uses six page languages:

**A — FULL PAINTING:** image leads; prose is sparse and integrated.

**B — LITERARY PAGE:** prose leads; illustration/marginal object supports it.

**C — ARTIFACT:** verified ledger, receipt, draft fragment, map fragment, marginalia.

**D — CHARACTER PLATE:** one inhabitant dominates; prose remains narrative, never stat-card copy.

**E — CINEMATIC SPREAD:** two pages treated as one composition.

**F — QUIET PAGE:** deliberate negative space, object, landscape or transition.

Repeated page templates are prohibited. Adjacent pages should normally change visual grammar.

1. Character Master description.
2. Approved visual reference if one exists.
3. Current team name.
4. temporal prohibitions.
5. exact recognition checklist.
6. prior failed-generation notes.

**Automatic reject:** a beautiful render with incorrect species, face/head, signature wardrobe, companion, Belt custody, weapon or silhouette.

# TEXT QA LAYER

Generated-image typography is non-authoritative.

## Always external/typeset
- owner/team names;
- scores;
- years/dates;
- standings;
- draft rounds;
- player names;
- keeper costs;
- pick ownership;
- chapter/page titles intended to be exact.

## May be painterly/generated
- illegible background handwriting;
- ornamental glyphs;
- damaged non-factual paper texture;
- marks whose literal content is irrelevant.

Final composition compares every authoritative string against evidence ledger.

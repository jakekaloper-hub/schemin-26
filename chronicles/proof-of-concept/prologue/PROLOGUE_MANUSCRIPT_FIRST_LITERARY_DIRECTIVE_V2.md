## Bullpen finding

The existing manuscript is the narrative spine of the Prologue. It must not be reduced into isolated page captions. The book failed when spreads became self-contained illustrated summaries rather than consecutive passages of one story.

The next production pass is therefore **MANUSCRIPT FIRST, PAGINATION SECOND, ART THIRD**.

## Literary architecture

The Prologue is one continuous story. Section and spread boundaries are editorial pacing devices, not independent mini-stories.

Beat Writer + Editor must first expand and revise the complete manuscript as a continuous literary work before Architect allocates any text to spreads.

Target working range for the revised Prologue: **7,000–10,000 words**, subject to story quality. This is a narrative-depth target, not a quota. Do not pad. Expand only through consequential history, scene, character interiority, physical world, artifacts, draft-night action, inherited 2025 consequences, and transitions that bind all twelve inhabitants into the same opening movement.

No spread may invent its own disconnected copy after manuscript lock.

## Variable text / illustration ratios

Do NOT enforce a uniform page template.

The Chronicle needs page-turn rhythm. Architect may assign different ratios according to the manuscript:

- prose-dominant page: ~70–85% text / 15–30% illustration;
- balanced page: ~45–60% text / 40–55% illustration;
- illustration-dominant page: ~15–35% text / 65–85% illustration;
- threshold / breath page: very little prose when dramatically earned;
- artifact page: manuscript prose may coexist with ledger, map, draft board, receipt, marginalia or other verified evidence.

These are compositional bands, not quotas. Facing pages may deliberately contrast: dense prose opposite a cinematic plate, or a continuous scene may flow across both pages.

## Continuity law

Every paragraph must answer at least one:
1. What changed because of what came before?
2. What does this reveal about an inhabitant or the League?
3. What does this prepare the reader to understand later?
4. What physical evidence, place, institution or relationship makes the world more real?

Every section must hand narrative pressure to the next section. Avoid reset phrases that make each character introduction feel like a separate profile.

All twelve are inhabitants of one history, not twelve trading cards.

## Expansion priorities

1. Deepen the archive opening into an actual narrative frame with a physical chronicling institution and recurring evidence.
2. Make 2025 consequences recur through the Prologue rather than appear once and disappear.
3. Let Byars' Belt function as a world object whose custody changes how others are perceived without making Byars the sole protagonist.
4. Turn keeper costs and traded draft capital into inherited constraints with human consequences.
5. Make the 2026 draft a genuine dramatic sequence, not a list of selections.
6. Intercut entrances where relationships, old results, assets or reputation connect characters.
7. Preserve uncertainty where historical evidence is incomplete.
8. Build a sustained approach toward the clean 2026 ledger so the final empty lines feel earned.
9. End by opening Chapter I rather than summarizing the Prologue.

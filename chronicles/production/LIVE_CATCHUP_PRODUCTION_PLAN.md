# DEFINITION OF CAUGHT UP

The Chronicle is operationally caught up when:
- every completed fantasy week has a Fact Lock;
- every completed week has a world-state delta;
- no unresolved identity/rename issue blocks writing;
- published chapters are sequential;
- the next unpublished week already has a production packet;
- current live week is being captured in parallel.

Being “caught up” does not require every matchup to receive equal page count.

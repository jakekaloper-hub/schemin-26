# LIVE NOVEL CONTINUATION CONTRACT V1

**Status:** ACTIVE HANDOFF CONTRACT  
**Applies:** Week 4 onward

## Purpose

Keep the novel live without turning it into one-chapter-per-week recap.

## Weekly intake

`INGEST → VERIFY → EVENT DELTA → WORLD RESOLUTION → CHARACTER/RELATIONSHIP DELTA → MEMO INTERPRETATION → NOVEL CONSEQUENCE → POV/STORY ARCHITECTURE → VISUAL REGISTER → PUBLICATION → WORLD EVOLUTION → NEXT STATE`

## Three decisions for every verified weekly event

### 1. Does it change canonical state?
If no, archive it.
If yes, transact supported character/world/relationship/resource consequences.

### 2. Does it deserve Novel manifestation now?
Routes:
- **SCENE NOW** — transforms an active arc.
- **BANK** — meaningful but waiting for later dramatic convergence.
- **BACKGROUND** — enters state/history but not dramatized.
- **MEMO ONLY** — entertaining weekly material with no durable novel function.

### 3. Where does it belong?
Never default to “Week N chapter.”
Place by dramatic movement, POV causality and unresolved promises.

## Chapter-opening gate

A new chapter requires at least one:
- relationship changes category;
- material resource/state becomes irreversible;
- title/legitimacy structure changes;
- geography/travel creates new constraint;
- institution changes behavior;
- character chooses under pressure and loses options;
- major historical pressure is reinterpreted by new evidence.

## Live firewall

- no future result;
- no prediction written as narrative fact;
- provider projection remains observation only;
- no irreversible canon mutation from draft prose;
- Memo may publish before Novel chooses scene placement.

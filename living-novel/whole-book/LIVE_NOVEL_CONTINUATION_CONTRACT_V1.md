# LIVE NOVEL CONTINUATION CONTRACT V1

**Status:** ACTIVE HANDOFF CONTRACT  
**Applies:** Week 4 onward  
**Upstream canon:** Prologue + Chapters I–III hard manuscript canon

## Purpose

Keep the novel live without turning it into one-chapter-per-week recap.

## Weekly intake

`INGEST → VERIFY → EVENT DELTA → WORLD RESOLUTION → CHARACTER/RELATIONSHIP DELTA → MEMO INTERPRETATION → NOVEL CONSEQUENCE → POV/STORY ARCHITECTURE → VISUAL REGISTER → PUBLICATION → WORLD EVOLUTION → NEXT STATE`

## Routing decision

For every verified event choose exactly one initial route:
- **SCENE NOW** — transforms an active arc.
- **BANK** — meaningful but awaiting later convergence.
- **BACKGROUND** — enters state/history but is not dramatized now.
- **MEMO ONLY** — entertaining weekly material without durable Novel function.

Never default to “Week N chapter.”

## Chapter-opening gate

A new chapter requires a material causal change, such as:
- relationship changes category;
- resource/state becomes irreversible;
- title/legitimacy structure changes;
- geography/travel creates a new constraint;
- institution changes behavior;
- character chooses under pressure and loses options;
- historical pressure is reinterpreted by new evidence.

## Live firewall

- no future result;
- no projection written as fact;
- no draft prose mutates canon;
- no Week = Chapter shortcut;
- Memo may publish before Novel selects manifestation;
- Chapter IV remains unauthorized until the active Next Causal Story Gate closes.

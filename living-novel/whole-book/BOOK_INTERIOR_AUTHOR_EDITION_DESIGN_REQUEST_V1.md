# SCHEMIN’ ’26 — AUTHOR EDITION BOOK-INTERIOR DESIGN REQUEST V1

**Lead Director:** The Beat Writer — Chief Creative Officer / Literary Director / Novel OS Visual Director  
**Supporting authorities:** The Librarian (canon custody), The Umpire (quality veto), The Groundskeeper (delivery integrity)  
**Artifact:** `SCHEMIN_26_Living_Novel_V3_North_Star_Rebuild.docx`  
**Mode:** reader-facing author edition / adult epic-fantasy trade interior  
**Content rule:** FORMAT-ONLY. Do not rewrite narrative, alter scores, rename characters, reconcile canon in prose, or add new story material.

## Creative intent

Make the Word manuscript look and read like a professionally prepared adult fantasy novel rather than an internal production export.

The interior should feel literary, restrained, tactile and serious. It should support long-form immersion and disappear behind the prose. Avoid magazine styling, Memo OS aesthetics, dashboard language, decorative fantasy clichés, fake parchment, ornamental overload, and visible repository/production metadata.

## Required changes

1. **Front matter**
   - Replace internal build labels, word-count notices, scope notes and live-season instructions with a clean title page.
   - Title: **SCHEMIN’ ’26**
   - Subtitle: **A Living Novel**
   - Author: **Jake Kaloper**
   - Do not invent publisher, ISBN, endorsement, dedication or legal boilerplate.

2. **Trim and page geometry**
   - Retain 6 × 9 in trade trim.
   - Use book-like inside/outside margins with comfortable binding allowance.
   - Chapter openings receive generous top whitespace.
   - Chapter sections start on new pages; do not force decorative blank pages unless needed for print logic.

3. **Body typography**
   - EB Garamond or the closest installed Garamond-family book face.
   - Approximately 10.5–11 pt body with ~13–14 pt leading.
   - Fully justified body copy.
   - First-line paragraph indent approximately 0.18–0.22 in.
   - Zero extra paragraph spacing between ordinary prose paragraphs.
   - First paragraph after a chapter/section head is flush-left.
   - Preserve intentional fragments and dialogue rhythm.
   - Enable widow/orphan protection and automatic hyphenation when renderer-safe.

4. **Chapter architecture**
   - Small, quiet chapter label (PROLOGUE / CHAPTER I / CHAPTER II / CHAPTER III / HORIZON).
   - Larger centered chapter title beneath it.
   - No production subtitle such as “Manuscript V3” or “North-Star Rebuild” in reader-facing pages.
   - Running heads suppressed on chapter-opening pages.

5. **Subsections**
   - Roman-numeral section heads remain because they are part of the manuscript’s architecture.
   - Present them as restrained centered small-cap/small-display heads with generous breathing room.
   - Prevent orphaned subsection heads at the bottom of a page.

6. **Artifacts / score blocks**
   - Preserve scorecards, registers, proclamations and archival entries as visually distinct text.
   - Use understated centered/small-display treatment rather than UI/card styling.
   - Do not convert prose artifacts into tables unless the source already behaves like one.

7. **Running heads and folios**
   - Use professional alternating running heads where supported:
     - even pages: **JAKE KALOPER**
     - odd pages: **SCHEMIN’ ’26**
   - Use unobtrusive Arabic page numbers.
   - Suppress running heads on title/chapter-opening pages.
   - Body pagination begins with the Prologue and continues without chapter resets.

8. **Author usability**
   - Use semantic Word styles rather than pervasive direct formatting.
   - Preserve editable text.
   - Add useful outline levels for chapter and subsection navigation without inserting a reader-facing table of contents.
   - Set document metadata title/author cleanly.

## Quality gates

### Beat Writer
- typography serves narrative pace;
- chapter openings feel intentional;
- no production language remains in reader-facing front matter;
- score/artifact blocks feel literary rather than technical.

### Librarian
- no textual/canon change introduced by formatting;
- punctuation/content remains source-equivalent except layout-only normalization.

### Umpire
- no clipped text, stranded heads, malformed fields, broken folios, missing glyphs, or chapter numbering defects;
- render every page and visually inspect before release.

### Groundskeeper
- final DOCX opens, renders and remains editable;
- only the final author-edition DOCX is delivered unless Jake separately requests QA assets.

## Acceptance definition

The finished document should be credible if opened by an author, editor, beta reader or book designer without requiring an explanation that it came from an internal AI production pipeline.

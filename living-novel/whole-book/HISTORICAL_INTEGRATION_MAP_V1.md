## Deep-time rule

Past events enter prose only when they:
- alter a present decision;
- change how an institution treats a participant;
- explain a relationship;
- give a place/object historical weight;
- create dramatic irony;
- or establish a cultural argument the current season reopens.

History that does none of these stays in the archive.

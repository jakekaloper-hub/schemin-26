# WHOLE-BOOK NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE V1

**Status:** ACTIVE REBUILD BLUEPRINT  
**Temporal coverage:** pre-2026 opening through completed Week 3  
**Future firewall:** Week 4+ outcomes remain unknown.

## Book premise

A young keeper of records is assigned the 2026 campaign and learns that accurate results are necessary but insufficient: titles, resources, geography, humiliation, memory, relationships and public belief all move on different clocks. Around him, twelve powers enter the Contest with inherited burdens and begin changing one another's options before, during and after the Encounters.

The governing theme is **what survives**.

Not merely who wins:
- names survive;
- objects survive;
- places survive;
- costs survive;
- reputations survive;
- injuries survive;
- titles survive losses until law changes them;
- bad interpretations can survive longer than correct facts.

## Narrative engines

### A — Edrin / ethics of memory
Arc through current boundary:
1. thinks preservation means accuracy;
2. discovers facts invite incompatible meanings;
3. learns public memory can escape institutional control;
4. realizes a correct standings board is structurally incomplete;
5. builds a second state record under stricter evidentiary rules.

His stake is vocational/moral: records affect what institutions, markets and future chroniclers treat as possible.

### B — Belt / legitimacy
Austin enters with legal-historical supremacy.
Week 1 and Week 2 losses create divergence between title and current form.
Week 3 gives first campaign win while a retained resource is lost for the season.
The Belt is neither invalidated by weekly loss nor allowed to erase present weakness.

### C — Jake / decision quality under uncertainty
Recent champion → runner-up → Week 1 proof over reigning holder → Week 2 swamp reversal → Week 3 recovery over TDS.
His arc is not entitlement to hero status; it is whether a strategist can distinguish bad outcome from bad decision without lying to himself.

### D — Wilson / cost of becoming visible
Begins outside the inherited championship center.
Wins twice, then becomes sole 3-0.
Merchants, scouts, patrons and opponents reorganize.
His reward for success is the loss of anonymity.

### E — scarcity and irreversible response
TDS: 2023 collapse → 2025 podium → W1 force → W2 collapse → W3 expensive reconstruction → loss whose acquisitions and cost both survive.
This is the strongest existing example of choice-before-consequence and becomes a model for other arcs.

## Structure

### PROLOGUE — THE KEEPING OF NAMES
**Span:** pre-season / draft inheritance / arrivals.  
**Primary POV:** Edrin.  
**Dramatic job:** transfer responsibility; establish Trophy/Belt distinction; establish unequal inherited resources; introduce the world through roads, institutions and selective sightings rather than twelve dossiers.  
**Deep-time pressure:** 2018 0.10 survival; 2020 0.75 final; 2022 dominance overturned; 2024/2025 reversal. Use as material culture and institutional memory, not history lecture.  
**Exit:** blank standings board / unequal state / first certified results imminent.

### CHAPTER 1 — THE BELT COMES BACK
**Span:** final arrivals through Week 1 consequences.  
**Primary threads:** Austin/Jake; Chili's opening eruption; Duckhook/Chins narrow command cost.  
**World pressure:** League Chamber, Record House, Pittsy's Book, city markets.  
**Transformation:** Edrin learns a result can be correct without being sufficient.  
**Exit state:** public argument moves faster than Archive interpretation; first decisions for Week 2 already change.

### CHAPTER 2 — WHAT THE BOARD CANNOT HOLD
**Span:** Week 1 consequence through Week 2.  
**Braided threads:**
A. Chili reputation → provocation → Red response / 91-point reversal.
B. Jake proof → Southern Wetlands → Mud defeat / counterfactual discipline.
C. Wilson + Mud become 2-0 → third-party attention shifts.
D. Austin falls 0-2 while Belt remains legitimate.
E. Chins visibly adapts after W1 allocation cost.
**Transformation:** reputation, terrain and resource state become first-class story forces.
**Exit:** only Wilson and Mud remain unbeaten; the market opens.

### CHAPTER 3 — THE PRICE OF ATTENTION
**Span:** late Week 2 market through completed Week 3.  
**Braided threads:**
A. Wilson/Mud collision → sole 3-0 → target status.
B. TDS scarcity → large expenditure → acquisitions and lost flexibility survive defeat.
C. Austin gains first win while losing Achane for the season.
D. Chili/Duckhook object culture; Red/Slob wager; Jake/TDS bridge memory.
E. LLC converts 0-2 pressure into first win over El Niño.
**World memory:** Mire Hill scar, Country Club contamination/humiliation history, Alpine Wager Route receipt, Southern Speedway, LLC Storm City, Bridge at Mountain Lake.
**Transformation:** Edrin stops trying to make the standings carry everything and creates a governed campaign-state register.
**Exit:** the world now contains twelve different 2026 states, not twelve records.

### HORIZON — SIX ROADS FORWARD
Not a full Week 4 chapter and not an outcome.
Use verified schedule/world packets to show the field reorganizing around unresolved pressures, particularly the sole unbeaten power and the campaigns now carrying accumulated cost.
The next chapter begins only when real Week 4 evidence creates a dramatic unit.

## Chapter law

A chapter exists because something changes that cannot be reset at a weekly boundary.

No chapter may exist only to “cover the six matchups.”

## POV law

- Edrin remains recurring institutional POV.
- Owner-character POV is admitted only where the dossier/temporal-state packet supports interior access.
- Ordinary-world POV may be used to reveal consequences the Twelve cannot see.
- Public reputation ≠ private belief.
- No interior motive from real-owner behavior is asserted as real-person fact; fictional counterpart interiority remains interpretive canon.

## Pacing target

Current V3 opening should materially exceed the 7.7k V2 export because it restores scenes, place, history, off-page state and consequence. Word count is derived from scene need, not quota. The anti-bloat gate remains binding.

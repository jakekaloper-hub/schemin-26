## World-first scene hydration

Before prose for a location-bearing scene resolve:
1. active LOC identity;
2. region/zone;
3. route of arrival;
4. entering world-state memory;
5. ordinary inhabitants/institutions present;
6. material constraints;
7. public knowledge vs POV knowledge;
8. what the event changes;
9. what remains after characters leave.

## Worldbuilding doctrine

- The world must remain legible without the Twelve.
- Divisions are cultural/home-venue overlays, not sealed nation-states.
- Ordinary people carry prices, cleanup, rumor, transport, ceremony and institutional friction.
- Exact mileage/civic detail remains UNKNOWN where Atlas does not resolve it.
- Published jokes may seed memory but do not automatically become permanent architecture.

## Source audited

`SCHEMIN_26_Living_Novel_V3_North_Star_Rebuild.docx`

## Pre-format findings

1. The manuscript was readable but still signaled internal production:
   - “CURRENT WHOLE-BOOK MANUSCRIPT — V3”
   - “North-Star Rebuild”
   - coverage / word-count language
   - live-season implementation note

2. Body typography behaved more like a working Word document than a trade-fiction interior:
   - extra paragraph spacing after ordinary prose;
   - inconsistent direct paragraph indentation;
   - subsection heads were left-aligned;
   - a single centered running head was used on all non-opening pages.

3. Document construction relied heavily on direct formatting:
   - 3,686 direct run-formatting instances;
   - 2,461 direct paragraph-formatting instances.

4. The underlying manuscript itself was not the problem. The audit did not authorize prose revision.

## Author-edition treatment

- retained 6 × 9 in trade trim;
- rebuilt title page as title / subtitle / author only;
- author set to Jake Kaloper;
- EB Garamond retained as the interior book face;
- body changed to ~10.75 pt with ~13.2 pt leading;
- body fully justified;
- traditional ~0.205 in first-line indents;
- zero paragraph spacing between normal prose paragraphs;
- first paragraph after subsection heading flush-left;
- subsection heads centered and restrained;
- artifact/score blocks centered and visually separated;
- mirrored inside/outside margins enabled;
- even-page running head: JAKE KALOPER;
- odd-page running head: SCHEMIN’ ’26;
- chapter-opening running heads suppressed;
- opening-page folios centered, normal folios outside;
- Prologue begins page 1 and pagination continues without chapter resets;
- automatic hyphenation enabled;
- semantic custom styles / outline levels added for author navigation;
- metadata title/author cleaned.

## Post-format structural result

- direct run-formatting reduced from 3,686 → 68 intentional overrides;
- direct paragraph-formatting reduced from 2,461 → 33 intentional overrides;
- accessibility audit: 0 high / 0 medium / 0 low findings;
- story paragraphs from PROLOGUE onward: exact textual match with source.

## Literary recovery law

A later file is not automatically better prose. A prior passage may be recovered when:
- its factual premises remain valid or are corrected;
- it advances the current dramatic architecture;
- it does not revive retired visual/body canon;
- it respects current POV knowledge;
- it creates consequence rather than recap.

## Mission finding

The short V2 export was not proof that the project lacked material. It was proof that manuscript assembly failed to hydrate the complete source graph. Novel OS itself remains production-capable; this mission repairs **whole-book convergence and manuscript depth**, not the entire OS stack.

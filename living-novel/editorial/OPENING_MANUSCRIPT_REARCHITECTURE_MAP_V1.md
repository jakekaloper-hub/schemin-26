## Core opening story
Edrin is given responsibility for preserving a campaign whose participants arrive already carrying history. He initially believes accurate results can anchor meaning. The opening contests prove that results are necessary but insufficient: title, reputation, resources, humiliation, relationships and public memory persist on different clocks. Meanwhile the Twelve stop behaving like subjects of a record and begin changing one another's options.

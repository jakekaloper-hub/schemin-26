# OPENING STORY REASSEMBLY V2

Status: AUTHOR-ROOM TARGET ARCHITECTURE
Supersedes week-bound opening structure.

## What the book is actually about
Schemin' '26 is about the struggle to preserve meaning while events are still alive enough to change it. Edrin inherits a system built to record outcomes and discovers that outcomes alone cannot contain a living campaign: titles create obligations, resources create choices, choices create costs, public memory changes behavior, and twelve competing powers continually alter one another's futures. The 2026 Contest is the pressure chamber in which he must decide what a truthful record owes the people inside it.

Fantasy football is the event generator. The dramatic proposition is memory versus living consequence.

## A-story
EDRIN / THE RECORD.
Can a record remain truthful when truth includes state, consequence, belief, uncertainty and memory rather than only certified outcomes?

## B-story
AUSTIN / JAKE / THE BELT.
Recent championship succession creates the opening legitimacy axis. Possession, proof and current form diverge.

## C-story
WILSON / THE FIELD.
Strong construction becomes success; success becomes attention; attention becomes target pressure. Rise creates danger.

## D-story
TDS / THE PRICE OF REMAINING CONSEQUENTIAL.
Inherited scarcity, early success, reversal and expensive response turn resource management into irreversible story.

## Prologue — THE KEEPING OF NAMES
Question: what does a record owe the living?
Edrin receives concrete responsibility; Oren establishes restraint; Farrell Trophy and Belt establish different forms of memory; roads open; draft/pre-book asymmetry makes the new campaign irreversible.
Exit: the record is ready before Edrin is.

## Chapter 1 — THE BELT COMES BACK
Question: what does a new result change when history is already in the room?
Center: Austin/Jake recent succession.
Supporting collision: Chili's opening force disrupts expected hierarchy; Duckhook/Chins plants a future decision consequence.
Exit: the first board is correct, but people immediately begin behaving because of it.

## Chapter 2 — WHAT THE BOARD CANNOT HOLD
Question: what happens when recorded outcomes alter behavior before the next outcomes exist?
Center threads:
- Chili reputation → provocation → Red response.
- Jake proof → Mud reversal → outcome/control distinction.
- Chins prior decision → adaptation.
- Austin second loss → Belt/form divergence.
- Wilson + Mud → co-unbeaten attention.
Exit: markets and attention move; the board is now causing the next state.

## Chapter 3 — THE PRICE OF ATTENTION
Question: what does it cost to remain consequential once the field reacts to you?
Center:
- Wilson/Mud collision → sole unbeaten → target pressure.
- TDS scarcity → expenditure → cost persists despite loss.
- Austin first win + Achane loss → victory and deterioration coexist.
- social objects/wagers show unofficial memory outliving columns.
Edrin payoff: Campaign State register becomes necessary.
Exit: the Archive has changed its method; the field has changed its targets.

## Chapter 4 — UNWRITTEN LIVE MOVEMENT
Begins from W3 terminal state.
No Week4 outcome assumed.
Candidate convergence: Wilson sole unbeaten vs Jake 2-1 is narratively eligible because existing arcs intersect, not because schedule labels it Week4.

## Chapter construction rule
Every chapter must:
ENTER with unresolved pressure;
DRAMATIZE choice/collision/revelation;
CHANGE state;
EXIT with a new dramatic question.
No chapter exists to complete weekly coverage.

## POV allocation
Edrin remains connective spine.
Owner-character POV is admitted only when interior knowledge materially changes understanding.
No named-character rotation requirement.
All Twelve remain OFF_PAGE_ACTIVE minimum.

## Reader promise
The reader is not asked merely “who wins?”
The continuing questions are:
what does winning cost;
what survives losing;
what does power make others do;
what can a truthful record contain;
and which choices remain after the world has reacted to the last ones?

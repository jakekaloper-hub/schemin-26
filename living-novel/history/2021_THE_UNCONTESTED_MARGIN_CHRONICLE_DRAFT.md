# 2021 — The Uncontested Margin

**NONCANON HISTORICAL CHRONICLE DRAFT.** Source: `living-novel/history/HISTORICAL_PRESSURE_LEDGER.md` and `CHAMPIONSHIP_SUCCESSION.md`.

There are seasons in which the final is remembered because it barely separates the contestants. The year 2021 was not such a season.

Dewars White Label had led the regular season at ten and four. A bye carried the leader past the opening postseason round. The next recorded contest was against Red Leopards. It ended 151.98 to 103.12.

The final paired Dewars White Label with The Third Coming. The result was 178.06 to 96.58.

No elaborate arithmetic was required to identify the champion. Dewars White Label held the title, The Third Coming was runner-up, and B.A.B.B. LLC finished third.

Yet decisiveness in a result should not be mistaken for completeness in a history. The scores cannot tell us whether the losing side had expected victory, whether the winner had feared a reversal, or whether either understood what the title would mean years later. The archive preserves the public contest and leaves private lives unclaimed.

The contrast with 2020 was striking in the recovered record. One championship had been decided by three quarters of a point. The next by 81.48. Both transferred the same kind of recorded honor: a season's championship. The difference in margin did not create a second kind of title.

For the Commonwealth's novelists, that contrast offers a possible conflict between spectacle and legitimacy. A narrow victory may be disputed in memory even when it is undisputed in the ledger. A decisive victory may invite a myth of invincibility that no later season is obliged to honor. Neither interpretation follows automatically from the numbers.

The chronicler must leave space for what the future may change.

Dewars White Label won in 2021.

The title was clear.

Everything else would require another source, or another story.

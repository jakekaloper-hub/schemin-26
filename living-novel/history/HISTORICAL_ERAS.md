## Verified structural boundary

### 2015–2018 — The League of Thrones period
HISTORICAL FACT: ESPN league-info snapshots identify the league as "The League of Thrones" in 2015, 2016, 2017, and 2018.

### 2019 onward — Pro Schemin' Football League
HISTORICAL FACT: ESPN league-info snapshots identify the league as "Pro Schemin' Football League" beginning in 2019 and continuing through the current lineage.

This makes the 2018→2019 transition the first verified league-wide era boundary. The cause and social meaning of the rename have not yet been established.

## Competitive-history pressure points

### 2015–2018: early succession volatility
FACT: Team 12 won 2015 and 2017; Team 8 won 2016 and 2018.
INTERPRETATION: the first four archived championships display alternating slot-level dominance. Human dynastic claims remain blocked until owner continuity is proven.

### 2019–2021: Pro Schemin' identity established
FACT: the league name changes by 2019. Champions are Team 3 (2019), Team 10 (2020), Team 5 (2021).
INTERPRETATION: this is a visible competitive break from the alternating Team 8/12 title pattern.

### 2022–2023: regular-season supremacy fails to secure the crown
FACT: 2022 Red Leopards went 13-1 but finished third while 7-7 El Niño became champion. In 2023 El Niño led the regular season at 11-3 but Eggs Benedict won the championship.
INTERPRETATION: these seasons are high-value material for later cultural ideas about legitimacy, fate, postseason trial, and the danger of assuming strength guarantees succession.

### 2024–2025: reciprocal championship confrontation
FACT: ObiWan Jacoby defeated the field for the 2024 title with The King runner-up. In 2025 The Immortal won with ObiWan Jacoby runner-up.
INTERPRETATION: a two-season reversal exists in the verified record. Any deeper rivalry/dynastic framing requires owner, matchup, and contextual evidence.

## Era-governance rule
World-era names should eventually emerge from consequences that inhabitants would remember—not from fantasy-team branding. A league rename may mark a historical boundary without becoming the literal name of an in-world age.

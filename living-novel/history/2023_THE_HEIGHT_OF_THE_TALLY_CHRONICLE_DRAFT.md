# 2023 — The Height of the Tally

**NONCANON HISTORICAL CHRONICLE DRAFT.** Source: `living-novel/history/HISTORICAL_PRESSURE_LEDGER.md`, `PLAYOFF_ARCHAEOLOGY.md`, and `CHAMPIONSHIP_SUCCESSION.md`.

Three numbers define the title run preserved in the 2023 playoff ledger.

160.12.

214.52.

192.88.

Each belonged to Eggs Benedict, and each was recorded in a different playoff week. Together they formed an ascent and a slight retreat in the score, though not in the outcome. The first secured passage. The second, a semifinal victory over Red Leopards, 214.52 to 149.84, opened the final. The third completed the championship.

Arm Chair GM scored 140.76 in that last contest. Eggs Benedict scored 192.88.

The title belonged to Eggs Benedict. Arm Chair GM was runner-up; Red Leopards finished third.

Elsewhere in the same postseason, El Niño had lost a semifinal to Arm Chair GM, 159.52 to 148.24. El Niño had led the regular season at eleven and three. Once again, the long season's strongest record did not become the championship.

A chronicler who cared only for the crown could describe the title run as an overwhelming eruption of scoring. A chronicler who cared only for the regular season could call the postseason a disruption of an earlier hierarchy. Neither view is sufficient. The record contains both the strength of the eventual champion and the failed bid of the regular-season leader.

The Commonwealth's future stories might ask what becomes of public certainty when two standards of excellence produce different names. But a question is not an institution, and a score is not a decree. No city, throne, feud, or supernatural force may be inferred from these figures alone.

The archive's duty is to preserve the distinctions that fiction may later make painful.

Eggs Benedict won the championship.

El Niño led the regular season.

Both truths remained true after the final.

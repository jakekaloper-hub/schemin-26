- historical character mapping status (UNKNOWN until corroborated)

- narrative tier: ARCHIVE / CONNECTIVE / DRAMATIZED
- future consequence hook, which must be validated against later results

## Literary tier rules

**ARCHIVE:** All verified matchups receive a complete concise historical record.

**CONNECTIVE:** Games affecting streaks, seeding, rematches, underdogs, upsets, near misses or character promises receive expanded weekly connective narrative.

**DRAMATIZED:** A small selection with source-verified pressure, canon-approved characters, and concrete world consequences receives scene-driven novel treatment with dialogue, place, agency, and cost.

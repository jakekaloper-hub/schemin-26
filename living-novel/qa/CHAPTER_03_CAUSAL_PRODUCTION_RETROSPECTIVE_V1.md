# CHAPTER III — CAUSAL PRODUCTION RETROSPECTIVE V1

**Status:** CLOSED LEARNING CANDIDATE  
**Date:** 2026-10-01

## What worked

1. **Minimum Pre-Prose Gate**
   - prevented automatic Week 3 recap architecture;
   - forced one chapter job before dossier expansion.

2. **Targeted consultants**
   - Martin / Le Guin / Grisham were sufficient for architecture, POV and propulsion;
   - Full Seven was not needed once book doctrine was settled.

3. **Causal omission**
   - five verified Week 3 matchups remained safely off-page;
   - evidence completeness survived without prose completeness.

4. **Principal-character POV**
   - Wilson-limited became the first non-Archive principal POV under the new constitution;
   - knowledge/real-owner firewalls prevented unsupported psychology.

5. **World hydration**
   - Mire Hill existed before the Contest and therefore generated real scene pressure rather than decorative fantasy terrain.

6. **Founder gate**
   - Bullpen could autonomously carry production through canon proposal;
   - hard canon still required explicit owner approval.

7. **Derived publication indexing**
   - Publication Manifest remained downstream of Novel authority;
   - independent canon evidence unlocked Chapter III indexing without weakening anti-invention rules.

## Defects caught

- retired Centaur drift risk;
- hill naming-strata contradiction;
- overbroad consultant reaction montage;
- closeout-test implementation bugs;
- risk of conflating public crown imagery with territorial/world law.

## Reusable doctrine

- **A source week is evidence. A chapter is causality.**
- One primary chapter job before full dossier.
- Use the smallest consultant panel that covers the problem.
- Separate physical world mutation from social interpretation.
- Canon receipts must pin exact manuscript bytes.
- Publication indexes consume approval; they never manufacture it.
- Short chapters are acceptable when causal work is complete; do not pad for weekly symmetry.

## Do not overlearn

Do not conclude that:
- every future chapter should be ~2.1k words;
- every chapter should have one POV;
- every major result requires a symbolic sovereignty question;
- every Week 3 omission must later become its own chapter;
- Wilson must remain central because Chapter III worked.

## Next-cycle use

Repeat:
evidence → significance → causal selection → minimum gate → dossier → POV/world hydration → prose → editorial → canon proposal → Founder approval → state.

Do not preassign chapter numbers to source weeks.

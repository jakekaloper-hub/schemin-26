# NOVEL WORLD CONTINUITY AUDIT V1

Status: PASS WITH CONTROLLED EMERGENCE / NO FULL MAP REQUIRED YET

## Finding
The world foundation is conceptually coherent but intentionally under-mapped. Existing doctrine correctly treats character environments as environmental languages, not sovereign states. Record, roads, commerce, rumor, correspondence, weather and institutions connect them.

## Preserved strengths
- Archive/Compact exists independently of the Twelve.
- physical geography, climate, trade, settlement, law, information and ordinary life passed prior world-foundry gate.
- environments are not automatically political borders.
- the system knows more than prose must explain.

## Gap discovered
No current canonical coordinate/distance map resolves every principal territory. Therefore production must not invent exact distances, borders, capitals or travel durations ad hoc.

## Repair rule
Until a canonical geography graph is promoted, scenes use RELATIONAL GEOGRAPHY:
- same settlement / nearby / road-connected / upriver/downriver / mountain route / swamp margin / distant domain;
- travel time only when established by scene/canon;
- weather continuity only where physically connected or deliberately metaphorical;
- no instantaneous cross-world travel without a narrative mechanism.

## World production test
PASS if a scene can occur without contradicting known connection, climate, institution or scale.
FAIL if prose/art invents unsupported sovereign geography merely to connect a matchup.

## Resource decision
Do NOT stop manuscript rewrite to exhaustively map the world.
Build geography only to the resolution demanded by upcoming scenes, then back-propagate accepted structure into canon.
This preserves mythic depth without decorative cartography.

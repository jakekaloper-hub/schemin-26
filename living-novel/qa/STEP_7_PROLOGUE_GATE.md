## Remaining expandable material
The world does not require every city, language, dynasty or ancient war to be exhaustively named before prose begins.
Those details should be invented when narrative need exposes the missing structure, then back-propagated into canon after QA.
This is controlled emergence, not improvisational contradiction.

## Prologue doctrine

The Prologue MUST NOT:
- summarize all eleven fantasy seasons;
- explain the entire world;
- introduce all Twelve as a roll call;
- read like a Weekly Memo;
- expose ESPN/fantasy-football mechanics;
- reproduce the World Bible;
- begin with generic cosmological exposition;
- predetermine 2026.

The Prologue MUST:
- function as genuine narrative;
- establish age, memory and consequence through scene;
- imply a world larger than the viewpoint;
- introduce the Contest/Belt/Farrell memory through lived action rather than encyclopedia prose;
- create a human-scale dramatic question that echoes into 2026;
- reward league members with deeper recognition while remaining intelligible to outsiders;
- preserve uncertainty and leave room for the live season.

## Recommended prologue architecture
A multi-movement long-form prologue rather than a single exposition scene.

Movement I — MEMORY
A custodian/archive/memorial perspective encounters the accumulated physical record of prior champions. The world is introduced through work, place and ritual.

Movement II — THE ARGUMENT
Conflicting interpretations of strength and legitimacy surface through people preparing for the next Contest. Historical events are alluded to as remembered consequences, not recapped like standings.

Movement III — THE BELT
The reigning Belt Keeper's existence enters the narrative indirectly before full principal-character focus. The Belt is present supremacy; the Farrell Trophy is communal memory.

Movement IV — THE ROADS OPEN
Couriers, retainers, traders and ordinary people react to the approach of another Contest. The Twelve are felt as forces approaching the center rather than introduced through twelve biographies.

Movement V — THRESHOLD
End immediately before the 2026 season becomes irreversible. No Week 1 result appears.

## POV recommendation
Begin outside the Twelve with a wholly fictional secondary character attached to the Archive/memorial institution.
Reason:
- proves the world exists independently of principals;
- allows history to be physically encountered rather than explained;
- gives outsiders an accessible entry point;
- lets the Twelve acquire scale before close POV;
- protects against turning the prologue into owner introductions.

## Length target
Long-form: approximately 6,000–10,000 words across movements if literary pacing supports it.
Do not inflate to hit a number. Scene density and narrative continuity outrank word count.

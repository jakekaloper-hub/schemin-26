## Length ruling

At ~2.1k words, Chapter III is materially shorter than Chapters I–II.

The Board rejects padding.

The active architecture explicitly changed from weekly coverage to causal chapters. This chapter performs one job, has one POV, four state-changing movements, and leaves five Week 3 matchups deliberately off-page.

Shorter length is therefore a consequence of the new literary architecture, not incomplete production.

## Voice reassignment test

Question: **Could this page be reassigned to another POV by changing names only?**

**NO.**

The draft is organized around:
- weight/ground/standards;
- Wilson's physical mass and fatigue;

## 2. Site ruling

**RULING: PRESERVE THE HILL AS A NEUTRAL GOTW SITE.**

The Hill is not D0nkey K0ng territory and not Mud Dogs territory.

It is a Compact-recognized neutral Encounter ground located inside the Burgers-circuit theater at the transition between the central/southern road network and the first wetland approaches.

### Physical logic
- raised ridge above flood-prone lower ground;
- dark, water-rich soil plausibly produced by basin runoff and wetland-edge saturation;
- exposed crest prone to lightning and wind;
- unsuitable for dense permanent settlement;
- old road skirts/ascends the ridge before continuing north toward the central basin;
- wetland routes branch south from the lower approaches;
- carts can reach the lower ground, but the steep crest remains tactically awkward.

### Political logic
The Compact/custodians authorize the ground for major Encounters.

Victory grants:
- certified Contest result;
- prestige;
- temporary ceremonial use;
- public narrative leverage.

Victory does **not** grant:
- ownership of the ridge;
- sovereignty over the road;
- annexation of nearby settlements;
- a crown.

Planting a personal standard directly on the summit would be read as a territorial claim against neutral-ground custom. Wilson's choice to place his standard below the summit can therefore express an intentional distinction between **winning the Encounter** and **claiming the world**.

This materially supports the chapter title: THE HILL IS NOT THE KINGDOM.

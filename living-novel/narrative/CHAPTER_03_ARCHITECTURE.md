# CHAPTER III ARCHITECTURE — WHAT WINNING DOES NOT FIX

**Status:** APPROVED BY JAKE / B+C+A STACK LOCKED  
**Evidence boundary:** Week 3 Fact Lock only. Week 4 results forbidden.  
**Governing thesis (B):** What winning does not fix.  
**World/memory motif (C):** The world begins to remember.  
**Resource substructure (A):** The cost of proof.

## Dramatic proposition

Chapter I: evidence enters the record.  
Chapter II: evidence circulates and returns as reputation.  
Chapter III: the record becomes insufficient to describe the condition of the people inside it.

By the third week, **winning and improving are no longer the same thing**.

A weekly result is true. It is not total.

## POV / continuity

Edrin remains the connective observer. His professional problem matures: he is no longer learning whether evidence is true; he is learning that multiple true measurements can point in different directions.

Oren remains the restraint mechanism. He refuses false synthesis.

Use institutional limited POV and controlled scene cuts. No six equal recaps.

## Opening image

The Hall of Measures prepares the Week 3 standings board. Edrin discovers that the simple W/L board cannot encode:
- DK's unique 3-0 authority;
- HMB's first win and simultaneous keeper-resource catastrophe;
- Slob's 1-2 record despite league-leading/elite cumulative production;
- TDS's costly recruitment response that did not buy a win;
- Red's repeated recovery;
- LLC's relief without proof of strategic correctness.

The board is not wrong. It is incomplete.

## Structure

### I. THE BOARD IS TRUE
Week 3 certifications arrive. Six results are recorded. DK alone reaches 3-0. Nobody is 0-3. Edrin attempts to order the board and realizes equal records conceal radically unequal states.

Function: state the chapter problem physically rather than expositionally.

### II. THE EMPTY STALL
HMB/7DC is reconstructed through the campaign inventory after the result, not as a race recap. Austin has his first win. The Belt remains. Achane's retained fifteenth-round resource—already known to the Archive before Week 1—is now unavailable for the remainder of the campaign. An empty marked place in HMB's campaign stores carries more narrative weight than celebration.

Function: primary proof of thesis B. Victory and deterioration coexist.
Guardrail: no invented Austin emotion; no claim injury caused matchup result.

### III. THE LAST UNBROKEN MARK
DK/Mud. The two 2-0 standards entered; one leaves 3-0. Show third-party behavior changing: scouts, patrons, suppliers, challengers, bookmakers, and Week4 preparation now orient toward the Arsenal Centaur.
Mud Dogs' first loss removes unbeaten status, not relevance.

Function: victory creates authority but not coronation.
Guardrail: no throne, crown, Belt, champion language.

### IV. THE PRICE OF AN ANSWER
Night market / recruitment hall. TDS's verified Week3 FAAB outlay becomes material: Shough 76, Emanuel Wilson 42, Bengals D/ST 35, plus other moves/failed claims. The point is not that the spending was foolish. The point is that response has cost even when the weekly result does not convert.

Intercut other Week3 roster movement selectively.
Function: thesis A substructure.

### V. THINGS PEOPLE KEEP
Memory/object movement:
- Chili's Sonic chili-dog provocation is now re-read after a 132.19-126.16 win over Duckhook.
- Red's literal $100 self-wager becomes a receipt after beating Slob.
- the older bridge/lake anecdote returns only as remembered lore after ObiWan defeats TDS.
These are not three recap scenes. They are three examples of the city attaching memory to objects.

Function: thesis C.
Guardrails: chili not magical; mountain/white flag not literal unless world-canonized; bridge not replayed as Week3 history.

### VI. STRONGER THAN THE RECORD / WEAKER THAN THE RECORD
Counterpoint:
- Slob is 1-2 despite 471.74 PF through Week3.
- Red is 2-1 after two consecutive wins: recovery becomes trajectory.
- LLC is 1-2 after first win: relief, not strategic vindication.
- Chili is 2-1 after Week2 humiliation: winning restores result, not consistency.
- ObiWan is 2-1 after Week2 loss: recovery creates a Week4 collision with the sole unbeaten team.
- 7DC, Duckhook, El Niño remain 1-2 with distinct unresolved problems.
Function: demonstrate that same record ≠ same state.

### VII. WHAT THE ARCHIVE CANNOT PUT IN ONE COLUMN
Edrin and Oren build a new internal campaign-state supplement rather than altering official standings. The standings remain simple and public. The Archive privately tracks assets, injuries, expenditures, obligations, objects, unresolved consequences and provenance.

Function: literary embodiment of Novel OS doctrine: PROSE MAY FORGET DETAILS; NOVEL OS MAY NOT FORGET STATE.

### VIII. THE WORLD REMEMBERS
Closing movement through connected geography as Week4 preparation begins:
- DK's standard draws eyes but no crown.
- ObiWan's road bends toward him.
- HMB's stores contain an absence.
- TDS's market account is lighter.
- Chili's ridiculous parcel has become lore.
- Red's wager has become receipt.
- LLC's lights remain on after first relief.
- Mud Dogs returns to swamp after first loss.
No Week4 result is implied.

Closing proposition: records tell who won; consequences tell what the win left behind.

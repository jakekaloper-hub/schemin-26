# CHAPTER I ARCHITECTURE — THE FIRST ANSWER

Status: APPROVED FOR DRAFT
Evidence base: 2026 Week 1 ledger only
Temporal boundary: after opening assembly through settled Week 1; Week 2 forbidden.

## Chapter function
The Prologue asks what the year will mean.
Chapter I gives the world's first answer — and immediately proves that no answer is final.

## Governing dramatic engine
The reigning Belt Keeper enters 2026 as visible supremacy.
ObiWan Jacoby defeats him in the first settled Encounter.
The Belt does not change hands.
Therefore victory produces political meaning without resolving championship legitimacy.

This distinction is the chapter's spine.

## POV strategy
Open again with Edrin for continuity, but move the reader from observer to consequence.
Use limited scene access to Jake/Austin only where needed; do not make Edrin omniscient.
The chapter should end with a wider-world consequence rather than a scoreboard summary.

## Scene sequence

### 1 — THE BELLS CHANGE
Opening morning after the first Encounters settle.
The Record House receives six certified results.
Edrin learns that recording an outcome is simpler than recording its consequences.
First reveal: ObiWan has beaten His Majesty's Blood.

### 2 — THE BELT STILL WALKS
Public confusion: some citizens assume defeating the champion means Jake now owns the Belt.
Compact officials must restate doctrine: weekly victory is evidence, not succession.
Austin appears publicly still wearing it.
This creates tension without inventing humiliation or abdication.

### 3 — THE TWO-PRONGED HOST
Translate ObiWan's Week 1 scoring shape into world action:
two veteran ground forces carry the Encounter;
a versatile field captain supplies a third pressure point;
the nominal command signal performs poorly;
reserve strength existed but was not deployed.
Do not use literal NFL names unless the narrative system later canonizes such use.

### 4 — THE WESTERN FIRE
News reaches the city that Chili's host produced the week's largest display by a huge margin.
The joke reputation changes. Merchants, rivals and arbiters recalculate.
The Chili Outlaw becomes a strategic fact.

### 5 — ONE AND A HALF
Duckhook's razor-thin survival is dramatized through measurement, timing and allocation.
Chins suffers from a committed command choice while unused reserve capacity becomes visible afterward.
No omniscient ridicule.

### 6 — THE SHADOW RISES
Three Dreaded Snake's decisive win is contextualized against the documented 2023 collapse and 2025 third-place recovery.
The city begins debating whether this is restoration, recurrence or merely one result.
Archive refuses premature conclusion.

### 7 — THE LESSER RESULTS THAT ARE NOT LESSER
Mud Dogs over Red Leopards and D0nkey K0ng over Slob alter local relationships, prices, travel expectations and confidence without being inflated into era events.

### 8 — THE FIRST ANSWER
Jake and Austin do not need a melodramatic confrontation.
More powerful: they encounter each other under rules where both know what happened and both know what did not happen.
Jake has proof.
Austin has the Belt.
Neither has the season.

End on Edrin entering six results into the permanent ledger and leaving the meaning column blank.

- Humor is texture, not genre.
- Chapter must read as consequence-driven epic fantasy, not a weekly memo.

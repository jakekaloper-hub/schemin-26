# STATE OF THE REALM — 2026 OPENING

Status: HARD OPENING CANON
Temporal freeze: immediately before 2026 competitive results begin.
Important: current Week 3 standings are evidence for later weekly chapters, NOT retroactively part of this opening freeze.

## The world
A multipolar temperate subcontinent surrounds a central watershed and warm inner sea. Mountains constrain the north; fertile riverlands concentrate food and administration; western tablelands favor mobility; eastern storm country favors maritime/environmental expertise; southern wetlands resist outside control.

No universal emperor controls the whole theater.

## The political order
Authority is distributed among territorial powers, chartered cities, marcher commands, merchant combines, memorial/religious institutions and Contest-recognized powers.
The Compact provides enough common procedure for rivals to compete, trade, arbitrate and remember without eliminating sovereignty.

## The Contest
The approaching 2026 Contest is a recognized sequence of sanctioned Encounters. It is politically meaningful but not identical to war.
Participants seek prestige, leverage, recruitment, ceremonial precedence and recognized standing.
The future result is UNKNOWN.

## Reigning legitimacy
Austin Byars / His Majesty's Blood enters as THE BELT KEEPER because his 2025 identity, The Immortal, won the prior championship.
The Champion's Belt signifies present competitive supremacy.
The Michael Farrell Trophy signifies championship victory entered into communal memory.
The Belt does not make Austin universal king.

## Recent historical shadow
The opening generation remembers:
- Red Leopards' 13-1 2022 dominance ending before the final;
- El Niño's 7-7 2022 championship;
- Eggs Benedict's 2023 postseason eruption;
- ObiWan Jacoby defeating Team 8/The King for the 2024 championship;
- Team 8/The Immortal reversing that result against ObiWan in 2025;
- The Immortal surviving Dr. Duckhook by 0.40 in the 2025 semifinal;
- Three Dreaded Snake's slot-level rise from 1-13 in 2023 to 10-4/third in 2025.

These are facts inhabitants may interpret differently.

Austin possesses the Belt, but the Compact deliberately subjects supremacy to renewed testing.

2. DOMINANCE VS LEGITIMACY
Kevin and Manning's shared 2022 evidence remains the clearest cultural argument over whether long superiority or final survival proves more.

3. MEMORY VS FUTURE
Jake/Austin, Zach/Austin and Phillip/Jake enter with recent direct postseason history, but no relationship is required to remain trapped by it.

4. CONTRACT VS AUTONOMY
David's structural power naturally collides with actors whose power depends on mobility, territory or informality.

5. CENTER VS MARGINS
Frontier, wetland and ordinary-life powers have reasons to distrust rules written by administrative/commercial centers.

6. INSTITUTION VS PERSONALITY
The Contest requires incomparable beings and cultures to accept enough common procedure to compete.

# OPENING ECONOMIC STATE
Trade routes function but are politically negotiated.
Central grain, mountain resources, western livestock/mobility, eastern maritime goods and southern river access create interdependence.
No power is economically self-sufficient.

5. THE BARBER'S FLOOR — Arsenal Centaur's 69.91-point dominance becomes controlled ritual humiliation. TDS's tendrils/war-braids are temporarily cut/damaged, never permanent redesign.
6. MEMBERS ONLY — Slob's 194.92 overwhelms Duckhook inside Duckhook's precision/club environment. Decorum loses control of its own grounds.
7. NO PASSAGE — El Niño denies Austin recovery. Belt remains visible in rain. Austin is 0–2; title and present performance diverge.

9. THE MARKET AFTER MIDNIGHT — injury/scarcity recruitment. Use verified FAAB: Chins 35 wins Caleb Douglas; TDS 33 fails; HMB bids fail at 18/7; ObiWan 11 defense; Duckhook 6 Njoku; TDS 3 Brooks. Show prices as behavioral evidence, not exposition dump.

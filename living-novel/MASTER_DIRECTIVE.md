# BULLPEN MASTER DIRECTIVE — PRO SCHEMIN' LIVING NOVEL\n\n**Parent authority:** `SCHEMIN_26_PROJECT_MISSION.md`\n\nThis directive governs the Living Novel publication surface. It inherits the project-level requirement that the Novel, Weekly Memo, Universe Atlas, and visual system describe one persistent fictional reality.

## Mission
Build a persistent epic-fantasy literary universe whose factual simulation engine is the real Pro Schemin' Football League.

The established Pro Schemin' characters are inhabitants of the universe, not mascots. They require territories, histories, relationships, ambitions, failures, memories, loyalties, contradictions and legacies.

## Prime Directive
REAL LEAGUE DATA → CANON EVENT → WORLD CONSEQUENCE → CHARACTER CONSEQUENCE → NARRATIVE

The 2026 season is the present era, but the universe must incorporate every recoverable historical season. Championships may become dynastic events; defeats historical wounds; rivalries political or military feuds; trades exchanges of power or allegiance; drafts recurring institutions; keepers continuity of power; waiver acquisitions arrivals, discoveries, defections or alliances. Translation must be contextual rather than mechanical.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. League reality — Flaim and ESPN league 1417621.
2. Character canon — authoritative character master files and approved references.
3. Existing lore — Weekly Memos, approved artwork, storylines, jokes and traditions, classified as hard canon, legend, disputed history, folklore, rumor, myth, comedy or archive.

Never invent a league result. Unknown evidence remains unresolved.

## Secondary-world requirement
Construct coherent geography, settlements, wilderness, boundaries, travel, climate, economies, institutions, cultures, beliefs, military structures, technology, social hierarchy, historical eras, legends and political relationships. Do not create twelve isolated kingdoms simply because there are twelve teams. The world must be older and larger than the principal cast.

## Historical archaeology
Recover each available season and identify champions, runners-up, playoffs, major upsets, dominant/disastrous campaigns, streaks, rivalries, remarkable scores, roster cornerstones, consequential trades, transactions, drafts, renames and repeated owner patterns. Translate evidence into causal world history.

## Character doctrine
Each established character receives identity, psychology, history, present position and branchable arc. Existing identities are foundations, not complete personalities. Team renames never erase character identity.

## Literary doctrine
Use original prose with epic historical depth, consequential politics, competing loyalties, imperfect protagonists, long memories, restrained exposition, environmental storytelling, symbols, foreshadowing, dramatic irony, geographic continuity, slow-building conflict and consequences. Do not imitate a named author's distinctive prose. A reader must be able to enjoy the story without understanding fantasy football.

## Ensemble doctrine
No owner receives plot armor. POV changes must add information, motivation or bias unavailable elsewhere. Comic characters may become consequential; apparent antagonists may have legitimate motives.

## Live-season engine
INGEST → VERIFY → INTERPRET → CONSEQUENCE MAP → CONTINUITY CHECK → WRITE → QA → CANONIZE.

For each material real-world event map:
Immediate consequence → character consequence → political consequence → geographic consequence → long-term narrative possibility.

Nothing canonized disappears the next week.

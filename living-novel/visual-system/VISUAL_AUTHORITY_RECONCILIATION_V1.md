## Reconciled laws

1. **World determines image; image does not determine world.**
2. Atlas coordinates are abstract/non-metric; no map may imply exact mileage that authority does not support.
3. Divisions are cultural/competitive overlays, not sovereign borders.
4. Weekly Memo can create narrative memory but cannot automatically promote a visual gag into permanent geography.
5. Novel visuals inherit accepted world state; they cannot reset a scar, repaired environment, route, or institution merely for composition.
6. Principal identity is owner-based, never generated from current team-name semantics.
7. Current character references outrank old lineup art when the Commissioner explicitly changed a character.
8. Final character-bearing art remains fail-closed until exact current reference bytes, per-subject binding and final-raster Character QA are proven.
9. Deterministic text, score, route labels, place names and numeric data are typeset/composited after generation; generated text is not evidence.
10. Book visuals must add spatial, material, relational or historical understanding—not merely repeat prose.

## Material conflicts resolved

### Prologue Wilson conflict
The Prologue Art Direction Bible contains legacy “Arsenal Centaur” language. Current character authority supersedes it. New Wilson art must use **Arsenal Gorilla Warrior**. The old wording remains provenance, not active visual authority.

### Prologue geography caution
The older Prologue correctly avoided inventing borders before geography existed. That caution is now satisfied by released World Engine / Atlas. Current production may use the released seven-zone macro geography and governed routes, while still avoiding false political borders or exact metric claims.

### Memo vs Novel visual density
Memo OS is page-first and phone-first; Novel OS is prose-first and book-paced. Memo composition logic, environmental identity and world memory are reusable. Memo page density, score-card grammar and weekly “poster” cadence are not automatically portable into the book.

## Gaps that remain real

- cinematic environment identity references are not yet human-approved for every recurring location;
- principal-character renderer-addressable current references remain an external blocker;
- reader-facing interactive Atlas value remains unmeasured;
- some location proper names/functions remain intentionally unresolved;
- future Week 4+ outcomes remain unwritten and must not appear in current visuals.

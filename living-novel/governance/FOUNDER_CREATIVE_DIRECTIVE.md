# PRO SCHEMIN' LIVING NOVEL — FOUNDER CREATIVE DIRECTIVE

Status: FROZEN FOUNDER AUTHORITY
Founder: Jake
Effective: 2026-09-27
Scope: Pro Schemin' Living Novel, beginning with the 2026 season

## 1. Audience
The twelve Pro Schemin' league members are the primary audience. The novel must nevertheless possess enough context, causal clarity, characterization, and world depth that a reader with no knowledge of the fantasy league can understand and enjoy it.

## 2. Tone and Literary Ambition
The world is serious, adult, hyper-realistic, immersive epic fantasy. Tolkien and George R. R. Martin are reference points for scale, history, political complexity, lived-in civilization, ensemble conflict, and consequence—not for imitation of distinctive prose.
Humor is welcome and important, but should arise from character, history, rivalry, circumstance, and league-specific personality rather than parody that breaks the world's reality.

## 3. Visual Doctrine
Visuals should pursue hand-painted warmth, environmental wonder, atmospheric depth, expressive naturalism, and cinematic composition associated with masterful animated landscape art, while avoiding Japanese cultural or anime/manga visual coding.
All principal-character depictions are subordinate to the locked character canon.

## 4. Audience Rating
Adult audience. Mature language, drinking, violence, politics, moral complexity, rivalry, humiliation, and dark or mature humor may occur when narratively justified.
No sexual storylines.

## 5. Principal Character Identity
Characters may use the real names of league members. Names, nicknames, team-name history, established identities, league behavior, college-era friendships, NFL interest, golf, outdoors/lake/beach culture, and relevant inside jokes may inform characterization.
The characters must become serious literary people rather than mascots.

## 6. External Family Boundary
Do not fictionalize real families, wives, girlfriends, significant others, or other private relatives as story characters without a later explicit Founder exception.
The principal cast's forces and supporting ecosystem should primarily arise from each team's roster and from wholly fictional secondary-world inhabitants.

## 7. Principal Character Survival Rule
No principal league-member character may permanently die.
Narrative stakes must instead exploit defeat, injury, disgrace, captivity, exile, loss of territory, loss of status, political isolation, economic loss, broken alliances, betrayal, humiliation, forfeited privilege, damaged reputation, and other durable consequences.
This rule does not automatically grant plot armor from meaningful loss.

## 8. World Type
A wholly secondary fantasy world: pre-modern/pre-technological in broad character, with the depth, geography, history, political realism, and cultural differentiation expected of major epic-fantasy worlds.
Real-world places may inspire texture but should not simply be copied into the fictional geography.

## 9. Religion and Metaphysics
Create an original secondary-world religious and mythological system.
Ultimate metaphysical truth should initially remain uncertain. Cultures may possess competing traditions, rituals, sacred histories, texts, superstitions, holy places, orders, burial customs, and theological disagreements.
Do not translate a real-world religion one-for-one into the setting.
If supernatural forces exist, they require coherent source, capability, limitation, cost, social consequence, and political consequence. No arbitrary supernatural rescue.

## 10. NFL Player Translation
NFL players should not ordinarily appear as literal real-world NFL personalities inside the novel.
A fantasy roster becomes an assembled in-world host/retinue/system of champions, specialists, riders, scouts, guards, craftsmen, agents, and other appropriate assets.
Real player performance remains simulation truth underneath the fiction. Extraordinary performances may justify memorable fictional individuals or units when editorially useful.
The translation must hide the machinery without falsifying the outcome.

## 11. Weekly Matchup Translation
A fantasy matchup does not automatically equal a battle.
Each matchup is an Encounter between two powers. Its fictional form is selected according to personalities, rivalry history, geography, politics, current pressure, and result.
Possible forms include campaigns, territorial confrontations, tournaments, diplomatic contests, sieges, hunts, races, expeditions, trade struggles, trials, festivals, aristocratic sporting contests, wagers, political maneuvers, and warfare.
The verified fantasy result determines the outcome. Narrative context determines what the encounter means.

## 12. Championship Institution
The league champion receives the Champion's Belt and the Michael Farrell Trophy.
The Belt represents present competitive supremacy.
The Farrell Trophy represents championship legacy and remembrance.
A championship does not automatically make its holder sovereign over the entire world; the political and institutional consequences of championship must be designed coherently during World Genesis.

## 13. Michael Farrell Memorial Canon
Michael Farrell was a former league member who passed away. The league's championship trophy is named in recognition of him.
Michael Farrell is protected memorial canon.
Do not fictionalize the circumstances of his death, invent unsupported personal history, or use his memory as a comedic device.
Verified league-history facts involving him may be recovered through historical archaeology. Additional personal details require Founder-supplied evidence or authorization.

## 14. Existing Memo Relationship
The Weekly Memo is an evolutionary ancestor of the Living Novel, not the final literary form.
Useful character canon, events, jokes, imagery, and lore may be inherited, but every inherited element must survive serious-literature, causality, continuity, and world-coherence review.

## 15. 2026 Production Model
The 2026 fantasy season becomes one enormous novel.
One season equals one book.
The Prologue grows from verified league history and the world's deeper history.
The 2026 narrative then develops live from actual league events.
Canonical weekly narrative should be written only after the relevant week's results are sufficiently settled and verified.
Future results are never predetermined.

## 16. Presentation
The finished novel may use conventional narrative plus maps, heraldry, lineages, chronicles, historical excerpts, correspondence, proclamations, legends, songs, inscriptions, archival fragments, illustrations, and paintings when they improve immersion.
Artifacts must deepen the world rather than become exposition dumps.

## 17. Invention Authority
Bullpen may invent secondary characters, ordinary citizens, rulers, cities, states, institutions, wars, cultures, religions, economies, folklore, geography, languages/naming systems, fauna, artifacts, mythology, and pre-league history.
Invented material may contextualize verified league history but may never falsify it.
Maintain the distinction between HISTORICAL FACT, INTERPRETATION, and FICTIONALIZATION.

## 18. Character Visual Lock
No principal-character visualization may contradict the authoritative character source:
SCHEMIN_26_MASTER_CHARACTER_CANON_v2_REFERENCE_LOCKED(2).md
The authoritative file and its visual references outrank stylistic experimentation, team renames, temporary story circumstances, and prior inconsistent artwork.
If that source is unavailable during production, visual publication for affected principal characters is blocked rather than improvised.

## 19. Founder Experience Standard
Primary desired reaction from a league member:
“I cannot believe this came from our fantasy league.”

Desired reaction from an outside reader:
“This world existed before I opened the book, and it will continue after I close it.”

The world must feel larger than the Twelve even though the Twelve are central to the 2026 narrative.

## 20. Governing Principle
The real league determines what happens.
The literary system determines what those events mean.

This directive governs creative interpretation beneath the Living Novel Master Directive. When a routine creative decision can be resolved consistently with this document, Bullpen should proceed autonomously. Genuine conflicts with Founder Authority must be surfaced rather than silently resolved.

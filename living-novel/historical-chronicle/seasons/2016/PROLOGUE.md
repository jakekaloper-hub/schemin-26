# 2016 — The Names That Return
*Season prologue, first-draft literary chronicle / NONCANON*

The first recorded champion had been named. That had not settled the question of power.

A new year does not erase the one before it. Claims endure in places that would rather forget them. Losses survive in the mouths of those who paid for them. The victor of 2015, known to the surviving record as Discount Belichick, no longer stood before the field under that name: the historical slot bore the name The Real Austin's Hyenas. This is evidence of a changed *team name*, not proof of a change in the real human owner or the fictional character holding it.

Even the words sounded like a dispute waiting to happen. The Real Austin's Hyenas; Austin's Hyena's. Whether there was rivalry, mockery, imitation or something older behind those names cannot be responsibly deduced from the titles alone. Yet they offer the Author Room a question worth asking, and the archive offers a year whose postseason would bring the two into direct confrontation.

The Commonwealth had no obligation to organize its private griefs around a championship. Its inhabitants would have continued to build, bargain, migrate, shelter the weak or exploit them, according to histories not fully visible in the contest ledger. But new claims would travel through that world, and an old champion's status had ceased to guarantee another victory.

At the first reckoning of the new season six encounters awaited their results. None would be a coronation. The world was not prepared to explain what they meant, only to remember what occurred.

This chapter's distinct justification is a **performer's refusal**, audience financial loss, clerk's compromised complicity and an exchange-wide civic conflict. 2. **Jake agency:** He still too often becomes an ethical observer. Holding a table is materially useful but slight; later chapters should require a costly decision that affects an intimate relationship and cannot be resolved by mere clarification.
3. **Independent singer:** Her refusal has consequence, but her interior remains inaccessible. Check whether her own art and material needs are sufficiently visible without unauthorized POV. Do not equate single tested notes with a ticketed performance.
4. **Market institutions:** Payment/refund handling and disputed proceeds do **not** establish a canon currency, civil jurisdiction, legal remedy, guild, market charter or authorized road network. Atlas and period Character review must precede any adoption.

6. **Consequences:** The written list and half-refunded buyers create follow-up obligations. Do not quietly imply all were compensated or that the promoter was arrested, convicted or forgiven.

8. **Musical craft:** Verify instrumental behavior, clasp mechanics, and how an experienced player would re-tune before publication.

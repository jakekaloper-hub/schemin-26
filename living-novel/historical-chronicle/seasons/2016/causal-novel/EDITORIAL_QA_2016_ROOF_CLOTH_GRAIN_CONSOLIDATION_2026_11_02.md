# 2016 — The Roof, the Cloth and the Grain: producer-side literary challenge
**Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Scope:** noncanonical three-part reading across original Chapters I–V · **Author-side only:** NOT independent literary, historical, Atlas, Character Canon or Umpire acceptance.

## Comparative editorial judgment

This edition is an actual assembled manuscript, not an index. Part One, *The Roof and the Unclaimed Bowl*, merges the voucher shelter and missing-claimant obligations that previously repeated across two chapters. Part Two, *The Cloth and the Lock*, is a new nine-movement candidate adapted from the existing 2016 Chapters III–IV; it removes two long reflective/singer movements, preserves six safety/ownership events and introduces a new scene of the emptied canvas yard. Part Three, *The Weight of Dry Grain*, is retained source-exact. The original five chapter sources and the prior three-part Chapters I–IV reading remain intact.

### What is stronger

The first movement ends with a physical scarcity rather than a discourse on fairness: a time-limited canvas belongs to another person. The new middle scene allows that bargain to expire without pretending it has solved the lack of sleeping space. The child says the scrap-cloth door would make the shelter **look** as though it had another room. The returning worker insists that a conditional wheelwright engagement does not transfer his old debt. The actual lifting of a swollen bench replaces one abstract ledger argument with bodies in limited space. The chest claimant's own key, the stop-work at the wheel, and the independently reclaimed market awning then become three different property/safety dilemmas, rather than interchangeable institutional hearings.

The five-original-chapter span now has three temporal movements: first, opening the limited shelter; second, returning cloth and losing easy jurisdiction over wages, song and access; third, working with unsafe food and temporary independent cover. It preserves the actor-level causal boundary in Novel Causal Chapter Architecture V1: the Contest is evidence and cannot declare the wheel, grain or room safe.

### What is weaker or still provisional

1. **Singer interiority and anticipation:** The removed original Chapter III movement *A song with a missing trade* offered an important performance of independent aesthetic choice. The shortened printing-refusal scene still provides an explicit consent violation that Chapter VI later exploits, but the voice may need one fresh **observed** detail before the eventual unauthorized broadside. Do not grant the singer interior POV: she is not one of the twelve principal owner-character narrators.
2. **Jake's recurring lesson:** The chapter still has repeated metaphors of an empty column, silence, and refusing to name another person's decision. A further high-quality cut should target repeated explanations after action, especially the wheelwright stop-work and later witness refusal, without removing either material consequence.
3. **Proposed infrastructure:** Canvas, market awning, wheel, eastern floor, separate storeroom and grain shed are secondary-world props. No historical route, construction feasibility or legal title may be inferred from them. Period Atlas and world-institution review remain mandatory.
4. **Narrative temporal elasticity:** The wheel is visited at the end of the original canvas return; the chest is sought the next morning; the witness arrives days later; the storeroom floods a week later. Reading acceleration must not be described as a single historical ESPN week. Temporal connectors must be tested against a period event lattice.
5. **Perspective distribution:** The pilot is **Jake-only**. The original 72-chapter 2016 causal reader is likewise Jake-limited. This is NOT twelve historical owner viewpoints; assignment requires actual historical manager-account identifiers and period Character Canon evidence. Distinct Austin-named team slots eight and twelve must not be conflated.
6. **The counted words include metadata:** The three source files total 16,039 whitespace tokens, not 16,039 words of pure prose. The five original source files total 21,479, making the net editorial reduction 5,440. These totals exclude assembly-only reader wrappers.

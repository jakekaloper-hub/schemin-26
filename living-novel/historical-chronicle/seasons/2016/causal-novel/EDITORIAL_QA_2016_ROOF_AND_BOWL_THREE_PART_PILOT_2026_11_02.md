The candidate's four continuous movements could imply a shorter interval; period chronology needs an editorial reading before the full 2016 manuscript adopts it. A shorter chapter is not automatically a better chapter. Compare emotional continuity and the distinctiveness of Mara/child/inspector, not only source word totals.

This pilot supplies more active material turns but still leans on repeated debates over legal authority. A whole-book developmental edit must test whether the same moral solution repeats mechanically.

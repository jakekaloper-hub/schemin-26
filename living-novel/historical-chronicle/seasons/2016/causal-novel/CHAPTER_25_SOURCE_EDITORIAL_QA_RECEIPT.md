## Actual dramatic turn

At the lower weigh-stones the wheelwright's daughter independently negotiates, makes, supplies and invoices a safety chock for the iron cargo, using her measurements; she does not forgive Jake, certify her father's unfinished mill wheel or accept the outstanding unsigned commission.

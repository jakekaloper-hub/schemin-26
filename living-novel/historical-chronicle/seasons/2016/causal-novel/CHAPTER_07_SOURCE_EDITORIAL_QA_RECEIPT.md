7. **Second turn:** Carriers organize two independently owned property lines along a manually cleared high lane; the woman with the wrapped hand separates uncertain grain rather than declare it fit for consumption.

## Internal literary challenge — not an independent Umpire verdict

**Strengths:** The chapter turns on visible collective action under a physical deadline rather than another shelter-table deliberation. The daughter and worker control the critical refusal; the shed owner retains autonomy; the cart drivers and mill boy make mistakes and correct them; the protagonist acts as helper rather than magistrate. Safety is demonstrated in movements with tools, mud, sound and limited sightlines, not in an unearned victory.

**Outstanding edits:** Jake's concluding inventory of unresolved obligations risks repeating earlier moral inventories. The girl/young boy apprentice and cart-release procedure may need a technical plausibility pass; the physical geography of a mill return channel and wheelwright yard needs cross-domain Atlas and environmental authority. The separation of "wheelwright's wheel" from the mill's regular drive should be clarified if a reader mistakes the two as one device. The invented water rise is a scene pressure, NOT evidence of historical weather or a canon environmental event.

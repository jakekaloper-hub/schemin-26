## Independent-review questions / adversarial editorial objections

1. **Overuse of allegorical public records.** Earlier causal chapters also oppose a score, printed notice or ledger to lived obligations. Chapter IX introduces a distinct travel consequence and Jake's personal cost but may still read like a repeated argument about limited certainty. Demand a line edit for moral exposition and repetitive clauses using "can/cannot promise".
2. **Jake's agency is more concrete but modest.** He elects to interview the porter and finances verified correction out of his own wages. A deeper arc should show whether that sacrifice materially strains his unpaid pledge instead of praising restraint without consequence. The final paragraphs explicitly carry his unresolved debt.
3. **Multiple chests require reader disambiguation.** The traveling woman's chest is held by her brothers and reclaimed; the key-holder's *private* chest remains inaccessible inside the dangerous room. A later scene should distinguish them without further cumbersome editorial disclaimers.
4. **Missing-person ethics:** The wooden slip, spindle, woman's mother and sealed letter are **not** proof of common identity. Genuine Character and Librarian review must challenge coincidence, inherited craft, and privacy boundaries; do not allow reader inference to harden into purported canon.
5. **Period material culture:** Bell suspension, clapper removal, gatekeeping practice, printed broadsides, chalk, rope, storehouses and paid copying lack period-specific Atlas approval. All scene geography, route travel and economic exchange are proposals on Atlas HOLD.
6. **Continuity / physics:** The gate keeper says the high stair is unsafe, yet the party carries a chest into the niche without stepping onto it; the scene depicts a niche on the storehouse wall, not use of the stair. Period geography must confirm this physically workable layout before canon approval.
7. **Supporting agency:** The traveler refuses Jake custody of the spindle and chooses her own private registration; the porter retains his sheet; the keeper dictates accurate route wording. Preserve their decisions without shifting to their internal POV.
8. **Exposition and rhythm:** Five movements contain several binary spoken exchanges and parallels involving corrections. An independent developmental editor should decide where silence, disagreement and implied subtext can replace explanation.
9. **Historical time:** Avoid any future postseason score or 2026-specific geography/technology/character look; no precise real-world date or local precipitation is certified for this scene.
10. **Causality test for the next chapter:** Jake's paid notice must cost him something tangible later, or the ending risks falsely resolving his private obligation. No automatic move to an ESPN scoring-week chapter.

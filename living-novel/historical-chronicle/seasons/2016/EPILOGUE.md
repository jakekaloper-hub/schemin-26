# 2016 — A Crown Does Not Stay Where It Was
*Season epilogue / noncanon first draft*

The second completed season changed the name at the head of the record. In 2015 the first recoverable crown had belonged to Discount Belichick's historical slot; in 2016 the name attached to victory was Austin's Hyena's. The two names cannot yet prove a succession between real people or a transfer of territory in the Commonwealth. A team's changing label is not a treaty, and a score is not a coronation ceremony.

Still, the contrast gives the authors the shape of a lasting question. What happens to a civilization that has begun to treat the Contest as evidence of legitimacy when legitimacy changes hands? The record says who prevailed. The story must show why the answer matters to someone who never set foot on the field.

The Gunslingers had come close enough to feel the loss differently from a rout. The Real Austin's Hyenas had returned from an opening reversal to contend deep into the year. Other powers, whose contests remained scattered across thirteen regular-season weeks, had their own unfinished reckonings. They must be brought into the eventual complete draft; this short epilogue does not pretend their histories can be dismissed.

The new champion would enter the next year with a title, a history, and the dangerous possibility of confusing them.

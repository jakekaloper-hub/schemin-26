# 2016 — The Two Hyenas and the Last Gate
*Historical Chronicle postseason / draft, noncanon*

The opening of the year had not been kind to the contender calling itself The Real Austin's Hyenas. It began with defeat at the hands of Pittsy's Rhino's, and yet at the postseason gate that same named slot appeared opposite Austin's Hyena's. What sounds, in later speech, like a quarrel between rival claimants was first an encounter whose conclusion nobody inside the hour could rightly know.

Austin's Hyena's prevailed in the semifinal series, 220.06 to 195.22. The winning power did not emerge without pressure. More than one hundred ninety-five points stood against it, enough to destroy any simple account of an unresisted march. The losing side could claim no victory in a narrow defeat. It could, perhaps, claim that its presence had forced the future champion to show strength that could not have remained hidden.

Across the other side of the final's approaches stood the Gunslingers, who had overcome the Scruffy Looking Nerf Herders, 214.02 to 204.84. Their passage was closer still. The story must belong as much to the reasons they persisted as to the contender who would eventually defeat them; but those reasons cannot be certified from a scoring ledger and await authorial creation within approved canon.

When Austin's Hyena's and the Gunslingers at last met in the championship series, the margin was 10.82: 217.70 to 206.88. It was victory, but not the effortless conquest of an opponent unable to respond. Those who later spoke of the champion's right to rule might remember the result; those who stood opposite might remember how near the passage had been.

The Commonwealth would have to live with whichever story it believed. The archive merely secured the answer to one narrower question: Austin's Hyena's was the champion of 2016.

## 1. What Is Lost in a Rout
*Kunt Liquors 47 — Bavid Dabbs 87. Verified victor: Bavid Dabbs.*

Bavid Dabbs defeated Kunt Liquors by forty. A spectacular reversal after a prior week's humiliation invites celebration, but an epic should linger with the defeated as well as the victor. There are ordinary days in which news arrives and someone has to explain it to people who have staked hopes on a different answer. The imagined consequences might reach households, workrooms, or roadside markets, if those places can be established in the Atlas. For the winning side there is also danger: the belief that the next conflict should yield the same clean arithmetic.

## 2. A Door Barely Closed
*Scruffy Looking Nerf Herders 59.5 — I'M HERE SO I WON'T GET FINED 65. Verified victor: I'M HERE SO I WON'T GET FINED.*

I'M HERE SO I WON'T GET FINED prevailed over the Scruffy Looking Nerf Herders by five and a half. It was an answer just sufficient, not a conquest. The recently recovering contender had fallen again. A confidant in an approved character scene might ask whether narrow defeat deserves different treatment from humiliation. The answer should carry the weight of prior weeks: survival by little can produce reverence, defeat by little can produce obsession, and either can corrode judgment when people cease to see what is directly before them.

## 3. Two Measures
*Hook, Line, and Zeeker 100.5 — Here, Ok I took L's 102.5. Verified victor: Here, Ok I took L's.*

Hook, Line, and Zeeker resisted Here, Ok I took L's fiercely enough to make the result uncertain, but the latter won by exactly two. Such differences do not give either party the luxury of forgetting the other. If these contestants later negotiate, the earlier contest could shadow their words. But a later relationship cannot be backfilled as canon without evidence. The result does, however, interrupt any easy story that one contender's early dominance must break at the first serious test. It holds, for another week, and bears the strain.

## 4. A Narrow Return
*Discount Belichick 67 — Austin's Hyena's 60.5. Verified victor: Discount Belichick.*

Discount Belichick faced Austin's Hyena's and won by six and a half. Later, those names would appear together in the championship. Here they are merely two contenders meeting in the middle of a difficult year. Neither can know that this is rehearsal for a greater collision. A novelist can use their opposing priorities to make the encounter intimate and consequential, once the historic character identities are authenticated. The one thing this source proves is that the future champion answered the third week's defeat with a victory, while the future finalist did not prevail.

## 5. Gunfire Without a Gun
*Cypress Gunslingers 76 — Team Hollingshead 69.5. Verified victor: Cypress Gunslingers.*

Cypress Gunslingers defeated Team Hollingshead by six and a half. Their name invites a certain visual shorthand, but historical nomenclature does not license actual firearms, cowboy societies, or invented military technology in the Commonwealth. The result instead suggests a story about how a damaged reputation is repaired after a heavy loss. One might imagine a commander refusing to let the last defeat become a permanent measure of the people, but that interiority must await canonical binding. Hollingshead, triumphant a week earlier, was reminded that no glory lasts on credit.

## 6. Another Difference of Two
*Pittsy's Rhino's 76.5 — Calvin's Johnson 78.5. Verified victor: Calvin's Johnson.*

Calvin's Johnson prevailed over Pittsy's Rhino's by two. The latter entered after a crushing victory; the former had just defeated one of the year's eventual finalists. A balanced struggle makes a useful counterpoint to the opening rout of this same week. The Commonwealth should not experience every conflict in the same emotional register. Some news travels as spectacle; some arrives quietly and unsettles more people than anyone expects. Both contenders now have histories that complicate public confidence, and neither history is finished.

## The Week Beyond the Contest

The six judgments did not reach the Commonwealth as one neatly arranged revelation. Each would be known first by those nearest its consequence, and only later by others whose memories and interests made its meaning different. A winner would be celebrated somewhere and distrusted elsewhere; a defeat might draw compassion from a stranger and ambition from a neighbor. Those social relationships cannot be minted from a results table. The next creative revision must establish whose knowledge crossed what border, what pressure moved between the six independent encounters, and which of the twelve canonical perspectives could carry that change without foreknowledge. Until then, this passage preserves the shared consequence: reputations have changed, but no power has learned the whole truth of the year.

## Literary merits requiring **independent** challenge

The primary editorial gain is a shared inciting incident rather than two separate chapters repeatedly explaining a missing watch floor and road-safety obligations. Kevin's feline sensory attention and Jordan's grotesque comic physique remain distinguishable in the first half. A cart physically endangered by Jordan's negligence forces more tangible decisions than another debate over liability; Kevin's voluntary loss of vantage now produces a witnessed human-scale cost, not a rhetorical cost in an isolated monologue.

The primary unresolved deficiencies are substantial:

1. **Voice convergence — HIGH.** Both characters frequently articulate the same moral discovery: authority should not be confused with competence or certainty. The second half risks reading as a duet of governance principles rather than incompatible lived personalities. Distinctive emotional appetite, shame, pride, sensory vocabulary and comic diction should be independently compared against the originals before accepting the compression.

3. **Rain relay and safety mechanics — HIGH.** Test whether the stated signal failures, bell audibility, wheel pin inspection, drenched ground and subsequent limited passage fit coherent physical geometry. No qualified field engineer or Atlas expert inspected this fictional scenario. Avoid representing it as instruction for actual road safety.
4. **Historical period morphology — HIGH.** Contemporary red-leopard and horned-berserker/companion appearance may not be historically approved for 2025. Historical Character Canon owner association and period presentation are independently unresolved.
5. **Geographic connection — HIGH.** This draft assumes an independently unapproved watch–bend–festival relationship and a plausible journey to a kitchen. The narrative must not quietly convert a proposed road into official Commonwealth geography.
6. **Causal scope and repetitive procedural texture — MEDIUM/HIGH.** Even after compression, three of the six movements emphasize inspections, slates, claims and warnings. The Authors Council should judge whether the action/choice/relationship now dominates the experience or whether further dramatic revision is needed.
7. **Moral timing — MEDIUM.** Kevin and Jordan learn restraint unusually quickly. Their own errors should continue to impose costs and tempt future regression. Later Wilson's role must remain distinct and consequential, not a redundant safety lecture.
8. **Reader experience and restoration risk — HIGH.** The manuscript loses **7,528** source words in this one intervention. Any lost plot, material or emotional specificity must be assessed against old chapters before treating brevity as quality.

## Cross-part integration test — not an Atlas route certificate
**Confirmed as a fiction-only designed causal chain:** Part 3's kitchen financing precedes Part 4's restricted first wood delivery; Part 6's failed watch signal and damaged road feed Part 7's physical crossing and wages; Part 7's two context-limited reports make Part 9's *independent* interpretive error thematically possible, **not** a certified courier-delivery link. Part 8's missing ferry does not imply it supplies the same road or kitchen. Part 1's hall and Part 10's later shelter remain separate site proposals pending location packet approval.

**Potential structural weakness:** The 2025 volume repeats the same lesson—documents cannot confer material safety—across the hall, kitchen, road, ferry and map. The recurrence is earned only when every instance has a **different nonrecoverable cost**: patron's withdrawn gift, unpaid fuel, driver's stranded cargo, missing ferry and salt trays, Jake's forfeited commercial opportunity. Remove paragraphs that merely recite that lesson after the physical cost is evident.

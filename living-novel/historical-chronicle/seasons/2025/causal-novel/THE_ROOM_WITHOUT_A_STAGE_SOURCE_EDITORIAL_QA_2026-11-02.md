This chapter risks re-enacting those discoveries instead of forcing a sufficiently new reversal. Independent editor should consider replacing the first half or restructuring this as an earlier parallel scene if it does not increase pressure.

3. **Resource realism: MEDIUM-HIGH.** Opening a second shelter night creates a household liability without exact fuel/food reserves. The scene orders separate accounting rather than silently making the sanctuary limitless; it still needs quantitative verification by World/Atlas and clearer on-page price.
4. **Dramatic friction: MEDIUM.** Neither lead is irretrievably compromised; both choose the relatively admirable course. Their public interaction needs a harsher consequence or legitimate opposed desire before developmental acceptance.
5. **Voice and embodiment: MEDIUM.** Phillip's three heads receive one combined interior; physical staging in a crowded stone hall requires Character director evaluation. Austin's armored/body proportions and period equipment need 2025 review.
6. **Thematic recurrence: MEDIUM.** Repeated ledgers, floor counts, and declarations that score does not settle ethics may flatten the existing thirteen-part book. A genuine developmental cut may need removals or reordering, not another appendix.

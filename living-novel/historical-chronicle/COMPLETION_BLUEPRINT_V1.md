### Phase C — Historical-to-fiction design
For each season create: opening political and territorial state, historical stakes, character pressure map, 3–6 long-running arcs, continuity ledger and finale condition. For each week connect six independently tracked matchups through cause and effect: simultaneous conflicts may change resources, perception, travel, alliances, environmental conditions and institutional power. Do not require all six battles to literally take place in a single shared scene.
For each matchup prepare a lightweight beat packet: factual event ID, dramatic question, stakes, POV from the canonical twelve, Atlas-approved location, opening pressure, reversal, verified outcome, specific fictional world/character consequence, unfinished threads, claim labels (VERIFIED / CANON / PROPOSED FICTION / UNKNOWN), significance S0–S5 and scene intensity.
**Gate C:** no conflict with canon or chronology, no claims of unverified historical private motives, and at least one meaningful causal consequence per dramatised event.

Suggested initial editorial length targets (planning estimates, not canon constraints): season prologue 2,000–4,000 words; each matchup 500–1,500 words, with exceptions from significance grading; weekly connective fiction 800–2,000 words; playoff set pieces expand for narrative weight; epilogue 2,000–4,000 words. Word counts are quality calibration, not a substitute for complete meaningful scenes.
Author instructions: write original epic fantasy in a lived-in Commonwealth, with differentiated dialogue and interiority, noncontest civic life, evolving moral stakes, concrete sense of geography and culture; reveal results by plot, not a score recap. El Niño is storm/environmental force, never human narrator. No Edrin/Edin or other extra narrator: twelve league principal characters only. **Gate D1:** actual substantial prose files, automated matchup coverage, literary pass and director revision. **Gate D2:** full pilot year completed including postseason/championship/epilogue. **Gate D3:** all verified historical years written and edited. **Gate F:** full-length prose manuscript, line/developmental edits, consistency audit, independent reader proof and tested document/EPUB/HTML exports where production infrastructure supports them.

## 4. Mandatory schemas and QA
**Match manifest:** `source_event_id`, `season`, `week`, `source_teams`, `score_finality`, `historical_owner_binding`, `source_ref`, `venue_verification`, `atlas_location_ref`, `pov_character_id`, `time_cutoff`, `significance`, `prose_path`, `fiction_claims`, `open_loops_in`, `open_loops_out`, `review_status`.
**Hard fail:** missing verified result asserted as fact; unverified historical character binding asserted as settled; invented transaction described as sourced; noncanonical POV; El Niño depicted as person; unapproved geography contradicted; missing verified matchup text; inaccurate champion; prose that knows future facts too early.
**Editorial fail:** six disconnected recap sections, interchangeable voices, outcomes with no world consequences, chronology doing all the structural work, shallow culture, repeated match language, fantasy-football jargon substituting for fiction.
**Release metrics:** verified historical games / expected historical games; written game treatments / verified games; season prologues/epilogues completed; word counts by deliverable; source confidence; location/character exception count; accepted narrative scenes; runtime test output; editorial gate decisions with exact commit+reviewer.

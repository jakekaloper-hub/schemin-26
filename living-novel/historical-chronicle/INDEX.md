**Mission:** [#215](https://github.com/jakekaloper-hub/schemin-26/issues/215)  
**Status:** ACTIVE DRAFT / HISTORICAL EVIDENCE GATE OPEN  
**Purpose:** turn the verified competition chronology into substantial secondary-world historical prose that supplies, but never mechanically dictates, the causal twelve-POV saga.

## Existing verified historical coverage (repository evidence)
Source: `living-novel/research/DATA_GAPS_AND_LIMITS.md` (ACTIVE PROVENANCE CONTROL).
- Recoverable ESPN/Flaim seasons: **2015–2025** (eleven completed seasons); 2026 ongoing. Do **not** state that fifteen completed seasons are verified.
- Historical team IDs/names, weekly matchup scores, playoff tiers, final standings/records/points/ranks, and historical draft selections are recoverable.
- **Prior-season trade/waiver/FAAB histories are not recoverable from the existing historical transaction endpoint.** Alternative primary records must be sourced before asserting individual transactions.
- **Historical owner identity is not directly supplied by the retrieved historical fields.** Validate historical-owner/character mapping year by year. Do not project 2026 owner identity backward as established fact.

## Release format per season
1. `SEASON_EVIDENCE.md`: sourced matchup ledger, ownership/date certainty, gaps, host/venue and temporal canon.
2. `PROLOGUE.md`: original literary prose introducing that season's world state and tensions.
3. `weeks/WXX.md`: six distinct matchup narratives for each actually six-matchup verified week, plus connective Commonwealth consequence.
4. `POSTSEASON.md`: independently verified playoff arcs and championship story.
5. `EPILOGUE.md`: postchampionship character/world state and unresolved threads.
6. `CONTINUITY.json`: source cutoff, twelve-character/region change set, open loops, proposed-fic status.

## Immediate editorial rule
There are two deliverables: **exhaustive chronicle records** and **selective literary novel chapters**. In the saga, chapter = causality, *never* automatic scoring-week coverage. The chronicle must be complete; the novels must be compelling.

## Creative license and guardrails
Creative authors may invent dialogue, atmosphere, rituals, economic/social texture, secondary consequences, and thematic resonance when *not* contradictory to approved canon or verified history. Mark new durable world facts as PROPOSED FICTION until accepted. Match outcomes are non-negotiable. Fictional character motives are not factual claims about real owners. Narrative POV is limited to the canonical twelve principal characters; El Niño remains a storm, not a person; the older archive/Edrin POV permissions are superseded.

## First actual prose gate
- Select the earliest season with six-week-matchup source records plus verified historical owner-identity and usable territorial continuity. Prefer 2015 *only if those proofs exist*.
- Output at minimum **a complete literary season prologue and one week with all six distinct matchup passages**, plus the weekly connective passage and verified-to-fiction ledger.
- No prose written with invented winners, scores, historical owner assignments, or unsupported venues.
- Author Room literary edit and independent Umpire check before promoting from draft to canon.
- Measure source coverage and manuscript word count, not the number of architecture pages.

## Current blockers explicitly carried forward
**Historical-owner binding and prior-season transactions** need corroboration. The historical provider's transaction constraint must not prevent writing scenes supported by the verified results and approved canon. All other speculation must be visibly marked for editorial review.

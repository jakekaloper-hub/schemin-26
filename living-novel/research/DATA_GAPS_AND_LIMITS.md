# Historical Data Gaps and Provider Limits

Status: ACTIVE PROVENANCE CONTROL

## Recoverable
- ESPN league identity and historical seasons 2015–2025.
- Historical team IDs/names.
- Final standings, records, points, playoff ranks.
- Weekly matchup scores and playoff tiers.
- Complete/near-complete historical draft boards: 204 selections in 2015–2023, 203 in 2024, 202 in 2025.
- Keeper flags where provider exposes them.

## Not recoverable from current ESPN/Flaim historical transaction endpoint
Historical ESPN transactions are unavailable for prior seasons. The provider explicitly restricts transaction retrieval to the current 2026 season.

Therefore historical waiver claims, adds/drops, FAAB, completed trades, failed bids and trade lifecycle cannot be claimed complete from this source.

## Historical owner identity
Historical ownerName/teamOwners fields are absent in the retrieved ESPN/Flaim history. Current 2026 owner mapping is known from the binding Character Canon and current league state; prior-year ownership must be corroborated elsewhere before attribution.

## Editorial consequence
Absence of evidence is not evidence an event did not occur.
Unknowns remain UNKNOWN rather than being converted into fiction and later mislabeled as history.

# Ben Whipple — The People's Champ / Blue-Collar Spoiler

## Identity lock
Very large heavyset bearded human bruiser; trucker cap, dirty white tank, tattoos, oversized hammer, brown 40-ounce bottle, raccoon sidekick when composition permits.

## Evidence anchors
Current: Seven Deadly Chins, Team 11. Historical slot reached 2023 championship as Arm Chair GM and lost to Eggs Benedict; historical human-owner continuity remains separately governed.

## Literary placement
A popular rough-country figure whose legitimacy comes from proximity to ordinary people and visible labor rather than elite refinement.

## Public reputation
Crude to some, beloved to others; built to spoil plans of people who assume prestige equals strength.

## Interior logic — INTERPRETIVE CANON
Believes authority should survive contact with ordinary inconvenience, hunger, work and ridicule.

## Desire
To be respected without becoming polished into someone unrecognizable.

## Fear
That acceptance by elites requires betraying the people who made his reputation possible.

## Virtues
Accessibility; endurance; humor; practical solidarity.

## Flaws
Anti-elitist reflex; stubbornness; tendency to mistake refinement for insincerity.

## Competencies
Physical labor, crowd rapport, practical problem-solving, disruption.

## Blind spot
Institutions can serve ordinary people even when their language is irritating.

## Leadership
Direct, visible, informal, rooted in shared hardship.

## Power base
Laboring communities, roadside networks, practical craftsmen and loyal companions.

## Vulnerability
Informality can be exploited by procedural rivals.

## 2026 opening pressure
Must decide whether influence is best exercised outside institutions or through them.

## Branchable arcs
Spoiler becomes builder; outsider gains institutional responsibility; resentment hardens; humor becomes bridge across class divisions.

## Forbidden drift
Never fit bodybuilder, polished noble, literal multi-chinned creature or suited executive.

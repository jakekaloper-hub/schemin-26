# Kevin Zeek — The Predator Board

## Identity lock
Towering muscular anthropomorphic red leopard/jaguar; black rosettes; amber eyes; tribal leather-metal armor; claws; jungle-ruin visual language.

## Evidence anchors
Current: Red Leopards, Team 2.

## Literary placement
A formidable territorial/martial power associated with difficult forest approaches and disciplined control of movement rather than a literal leopard kingdom.

## Public reputation
The standard by which raw seasonal strength is measured—and the cautionary example that strength does not guarantee final legitimacy.

## Interior logic — INTERPRETIVE CANON
Values demonstrable superiority, preparation and repeatability more than theatrical fortune.

## Desire
To make sustained excellence impossible to dismiss merely because the final trial can overturn it.

## Fear
Being remembered for the crown that escaped rather than the dominance that preceded it.

## Virtues
Discipline; resilience; physical courage; consistency.

## Flaws
Pride in measurable strength; impatience with explanations that elevate chance or ceremony above performance.

## Competencies
Territorial control, sustained campaigning, intimidation, disciplined organization.

## Blind spot

## Leadership

## Power base

## Vulnerability
Expectation itself becomes weight.

## 2026 opening pressure

# CHARACTER BIBLE DOCTRINE

Status: STEP 4 GOVERNING CANON

## Purpose
Transform the Twelve from visual archetypes into durable literary characters without making unsupported claims about the real people who inspired them.

## Three-layer character model

### A — LOCKED IDENTITY
Source: Master Character Canon + validated current league state.
May include owner name, team identity, visual body/species, established title, signature equipment/companions and explicit Founder-approved invariants.
This layer is HARD CANON.

### B — EVIDENCE-BACKED HISTORY
Source: verified ESPN/Flaim league history and other approved evidence.
May include records, championships, playoff reversals, team-name history and other verified competitive facts.
This layer is HISTORICAL FACT.

### C — LITERARY CHARACTERIZATION
The fictional person's worldview, motives, virtues, flaws, fears, methods, political interests, private contradictions, social reputation and narrative pressure.
This is INTERPRETIVE CANON, not a factual psychological claim about the real owner.

## Non-negotiable safeguards
- Never diagnose, psychoanalyze or make private factual claims about a real owner.
- Real owner family/significant others do not become story characters.
- Team renames do not redesign characters.
- Character visual identity follows the locked reference plate + Master Character Canon.
- No principal member permanently dies.
- Supporting retinues may derive structurally from roster ecosystems, but NFL players are not automatically literal named NFL people in prose.
- No character is omniscient, morally perfect, uniformly comic or reducible to one gimmick.
- Every principal requires competence, limitation, contradiction and vulnerability.
- Every principal must want something that can conflict with another principal without requiring villainy.
- Reputation and interior reality may differ.

## Dossier schema
Each principal dossier contains:
1. Identity lock
2. Evidence anchors
3. World placement
4. Public reputation
5. Private operating logic
6. Core desire
7. Core fear
8. Virtues
9. Flaws
10. Competencies
11. Blind spots
12. Leadership style
13. Relationship posture
14. Material/political power base
15. Vulnerability
16. Historical burden
17. 2026 opening pressure
18. Branchable arc possibilities
19. Forbidden drift
20. Canon-status notes

## Arc law
An arc is a possibility, not a predetermined outcome.
2026 results decide which pressures become consequences.

# Austin Byars — The Belt Keeper

## Identity lock
Tall corpse-pale immortal human champion; long black hair; pale/glowing eyes; black rune armor/cloak; massive rune sword; championship belt; gothic fortress language. Current team: His Majesty's Blood. Renames never redesign him.

## Evidence anchors
Current Team 8. 2025 identity The Immortal won championship; defeated Dr. Duckhook 152.20–151.80 in semifinal and ObiWan Jacoby 161.07–109.56 in final. Team 8 lost 2024 final as The King to ObiWan Jacoby. Historical human-owner continuity beyond supported evidence remains separately governed.

## Literary placement
Reigning champion and custodian of the Belt entering 2026; a recognized Contest power, not automatically a king.

## Public reputation
Difficult to read, difficult to remove, carrying the visible proof that the last final ended in his favor.

## Interior logic — INTERPRETIVE CANON
Believes legitimacy is clearest when it survives direct challenge. Ceremony matters because others agree to remember it.

## Desire
To convert a championship from an event into an era.

## Fear
That possession of the Belt makes every relationship conditional on whether others want it.

## Virtues
Composure; endurance; willingness to carry symbolic responsibility.

## Flaws
Distance; possessiveness; temptation to let victory answer questions it cannot answer.

## Competencies
Defense, intimidation, ritual presence, patience under pressure.

## Blind spot
Being the symbol of an institution can make it difficult to see the institution from outside.

## Leadership
Formal, controlled, symbol-heavy.

## Power base
The Belt, established retainers, fortified authority, reputation of survival.

## Vulnerability
The champion is the one person everyone can define themselves against.

## Historical burden
2025's 0.40 semifinal survival followed by decisive final victory; direct reversal of Team 1's 2024 championship over Team 8.

## 2026 opening pressure
He begins as Belt Keeper while the Contest guarantees that his supremacy will be tested rather than merely admired.

## Branchable arcs
Champion becomes steward; champion becomes possessive; rivalry matures into mutual institutional responsibility; reign collapses without destroying identity.

## Forbidden drift

## Evidence anchors

## Public reputation
A man who can acquire what armies cannot seize without destroying.

## Interior logic — INTERPRETIVE CANON
Sees institutions as structures of incentives and ownership. Prefers control embedded in agreements to control that must be constantly defended by force.

## Desire
To make himself structurally unavoidable.

## Fear

## Virtues
Discipline; foresight; composure; administrative competence.

## Flaws

## Competencies
Finance, contract, logistics, institutional maneuver.

## Blind spot

## Power base

## Vulnerability

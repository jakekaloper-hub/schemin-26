# RELATIONSHIP GRAPH — THE TWELVE

Status: STEP 5 INTERPRETIVE CANON
Temporal position: opening of 2026
Rule: These are fictional counterpart relationships unless an Evidence Anchor explicitly states a verified league event. They are not claims about the real owners' private relationships.

## Relationship grammar
Each directed edge records:
STANCE — how A approaches B.
CAUSE — fictional logic and/or verified competitive pressure.
PRESSURE — what could change the relationship.
STATUS — INTERPRETIVE CANON unless marked HISTORICAL FACT.

An omitted edge means underdeveloped/low-pressure, not neutrality.

# STRUCTURAL COALITION FIELDS

## Exchange / Institutional Reform
Potential participants: Jake, David, Manning.
Common interest: systems that can adapt without collapsing.
Fault line: Jake wants optionality; David wants enforceability; Manning distrusts over-standardization.

## Strength / Earned Legitimacy
Potential participants: Kevin, Phillip, Austin.
Common interest: respect for trials survived and repeatable strength.
Fault line: Austin possesses current ceremonial legitimacy; Kevin's history questions whether ceremonial outcomes capture full strength; Phillip embodies recovery.

## Frontier / Informal Power
Potential participants: Brandon, Jordan, Ben, Bobby.
Common interest: suspicion of distant elite control.
Fault line: Brandon trades in spectacle, Jordan in disruption, Ben in practical legitimacy, Bobby in territorial autonomy.

## Craft / Code
Potential participants: Zach, Wilson.
Common interest: mastery.
Fault line: precision versus philosophy.

These are pressure fields, not fixed factions.

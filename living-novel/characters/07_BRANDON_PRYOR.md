# Brandon Pryor — The Chili Outlaw

## Identity lock
Bearded human Western outlaw/pitmaster; black cowboy hat, sunglasses, chili-red duster, chili-pepper bandolier, steaming chili; companion The Dark Horse.

## Evidence anchors
Current: The Chili Cheesers, Team 7. Current visual identity supersedes retired bear/bloodhound concepts.

## Literary placement
A western-tableland operator whose influence depends on hospitality, movement, reputation and the ambiguous line between legitimate frontier authority and outlaw independence.

## Public reputation
Showman, host, provocateur and dangerous negotiator whose table can be invitation, theater or trap.

## Interior logic — INTERPRETIVE CANON
Believes people reveal themselves when appetite, pride and hospitality collide.

## Desire
To remain impossible to domesticate while still being taken seriously by the powers that need frontier access.

## Fear
Becoming a colorful accessory to someone else's story.

## Virtues
Hospitality; nerve; improvisation; social daring.

## Flaws
Provocation; theatrical escalation; appetite for the memorable move over the prudent one.

## Competencies
Frontier travel, social leverage, provisioning, informal networks.

## Blind spot
A joke or gesture can acquire consequences its author cannot control.

## Leadership
Personal, ritualized through food/drink/hosting, loyalty-intensive.

## Power base
Tableland routes, riders, camps, provisioning relationships, The Dark Horse as signature companion.

## Vulnerability
Reputation can outrun intent.

## 2026 opening pressure
Must decide when spectacle is leverage and when it becomes liability.

## Branchable arcs
Outlaw becomes broker; provocateur triggers larger conflict; hospitality builds unlikely alliance; independence becomes isolation.

## Forbidden drift
No retired bear/bloodhound; no Chili King/monster; The Dark Horse remains canonical.

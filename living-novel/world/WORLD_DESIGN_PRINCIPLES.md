# WORLD DESIGN PRINCIPLES — PRO SCHEMIN' LIVING NOVEL

Status: STEP 3 GOVERNING CANON
Authority: Founder Creative Directive + Foundation Foundry + Steps 1–2

## Prime worldbuilding test
The world must feel as though it existed before the Twelve arrived, would continue if all Twelve vanished, and contains people who neither know nor care about their private rivalries.

## Causal construction order
1. Physical geography
2. Climate and ecology
3. Resources and scarcity
4. Movement and transportation
5. Settlement patterns
6. Trade and economic specialization
7. Political organization
8. Social hierarchy
9. Institutions
10. Belief and ritual
11. Military organization
12. Historical shocks
13. Present geopolitical order
14. The Twelve inside that order

Reversing this sequence requires explicit justification.

## Anti-mascot law
No region, people, city, kingdom, religion, army, or culture may exist merely because a fantasy team has a matching mascot/name.

The Twelve may acquire associations with places that suit their histories, but geography does not exist to illustrate them.

## Scale law
The Twelve are unusually consequential actors in 2026. They are not twelve gods, twelve species, twelve sovereign countries, or the only important people alive.

The world requires:
- ordinary populations;
- farmers, artisans, merchants, sailors, soldiers, clerks, clergy, scholars, criminals and laborers;
- political actors outside the Twelve;
- neutral and contested territory;
- institutions older than the current cast;
- historical figures who never belonged to the league analogue;
- economic and geographic forces no character can simply command away.

## Technology
Broadly pre-technological/pre-modern relative to the Founder's requested epic-fantasy mode.
No modern communications, automobiles, firearms-as-modernity, fantasy-football screens, or direct technological stand-ins for ESPN.
Information travels through people, writing, signal systems, institutions and rumor at speeds constrained by geography.

## Supernatural restraint
The supernatural, if present, is not a convenience layer.
Any supernatural phenomenon requires:
SOURCE — what permits it?
CAPABILITY — what can it actually do?
LIMIT — what can it not do?
COST — what is paid?
KNOWLEDGE — who understands it?
SOCIAL EFFECT — how does society respond?
POLITICAL EFFECT — who tries to control it?

Unexplained religious belief and disputed supernatural claims are permitted. Arbitrary magical rescue is not.

## Historical correspondence
Verified league events create pressure; they do not dictate literal equivalents.
Example:
FACT: a dominant regular-season power fails in the postseason.
VALID PRESSURE: societies may distinguish administrative/military strength from ritualized legitimacy.
INVALID SHORTCUT: invent a kingdom named after that fantasy team and reenact the scoreboard as a battle.

## Humor
The setting does not wink at the reader.
Humor emerges from human behavior, absurd circumstance, vanity, rivalry, excess, drinking, wagers, sport, class collision, failed plans and personality.
The world itself remains real to its inhabitants.

## Visual/literary relationship
Visual canon controls the recognizable identities of the Twelve.
World design controls the physical and cultural environment around them.
Neither may silently redesign the other.

## Stranger test
A reader who has never played fantasy football must be able to understand:
- where people live;
- why settlements exist;
- what people eat and trade;
- how authority works;
- why conflicts occur;
- what institutions matter;
- what the championship institution signifies;
- why the Twelve matter now.

No fantasy-football knowledge may be required to parse the plot.


## Inhabitant ontology law

Visual canon defines the Twelve's current bodies; World Engine V1.1 defines how those bodies relate to civilization.

Writers and artists must resolve whether a principal is:
- `PEOPLED_KIND`;
- `SINGULAR_BEING`; or
- `SHARED_COHABITATION`

before inventing surrounding populations.

Do not infer an entire species from one singular character. Do not isolate a peopled-kind character as the only member of that body type. Do not use League divisions as biological borders.

Current ontology authority:
`world/civilization/INHABITANT_ONTOLOGY_V1.md`

## Encounter geography law

Ordinary regular-season/divisional Encounters default to the verified home team's established environment. Major GOTWs, playoffs and championships default to approved neutral locations.

A scene cannot choose its geography solely for visual convenience.

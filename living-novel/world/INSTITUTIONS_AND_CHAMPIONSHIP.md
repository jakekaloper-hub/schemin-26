# Institutions and Championship Order — Foundation v1

Status: INTERPRETIVE CANON / STEP 3

## Institutional premise
The championship tradition is older than the 2026 contest and embedded in a wider civil order. It does not create the world; the world gives it meaning.

## The Contest
Working institutional term: THE CONTEST (name remains soft canon).

At fixed intervals, recognized great houses/powers/companies/hosts submit themselves to a season of sanctioned encounters under a common compact. Encounters vary in form according to terrain, politics and negotiated custom, but their outcomes accumulate toward qualification for a final sequence of trials.

This structure allows weekly fantasy matchups to become varied Encounters rather than repetitive battles.

## Why powers participate
Participation can provide:
- prestige;
- access to arbitration;
- favorable commercial rights;
- military reputation;
- recruitment power;
- ceremonial precedence;
- political leverage;
- recognized standing among peer powers.

It does NOT automatically grant sovereignty.

## The Belt
The Champion's Belt signifies present supremacy under the Contest's rules.
It is carried by the reigning champion and visually associated with the Belt Keeper in the 2026 pre-season state.
The Belt's authority is ceremonial/institutional rather than a universal crown.

## The Michael Farrell Trophy
Protected memorial institution.
Real-world anchor: the league trophy is named in recognition of former league member Michael Farrell, who passed away.

Fictional treatment:
The trophy's in-world memorial origin must be dignified and must not invent circumstances of Michael Farrell's death or unsupported personal biography.
It represents continuity, remembrance and the fact that victory belongs inside a community larger than the current champion.

Working distinction:
THE BELT = supremacy now.
THE FARRELL TROPHY = victory entered into communal memory.

## Governing body
A neutral institutional apparatus is required to record outcomes, certify participation, resolve procedural disputes, maintain archives and physically safeguard traditions.
It must not be an omnipotent commissioner caricature.
Its authority depends on the participating powers continuing to recognize the Compact.

Working components:
- a Registrar/Archive;
- arbiters;
- heralds/couriers;
- neutral grounds/meeting places;
- custodians of trophies and records;
- rules for disputed outcomes.

Final names remain soft until naming-language work.

## Historical pressure
2018's 0.10 survival, 2020's 0.75 championship, 2022's 13-1 Red Leopards failure, 2023's regular-season leader failure, and the 2024–2025 title reversal give inhabitants reasons to debate what strength, legitimacy and destiny mean.
The institution does not officially endorse supernatural explanations.

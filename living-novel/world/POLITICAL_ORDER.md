# Political Order — Foundation v1

Status: INTERPRETIVE CANON

## Macro-order
The principal theater is a multipolar commonwealth of competing jurisdictions bound imperfectly by trade, custom, negotiated compacts and the need to prevent permanent war.

No emperor currently exercises uncontested universal rule.

## Power classes
1. Territorial houses — land, agricultural surplus, retainers.
2. Chartered cities — commerce, walls, administration, credit.
3. Marcher commands — border defense and military prestige.
4. Merchant combines — capital, shipping, information.
5. Memorial/religious institutions — legitimacy, archives, sanctuary.
6. Contest-recognized powers — reputational standing through sanctioned Encounters.
7. Neutral officers/arbiters — narrow authority derived from compact recognition.

A single actor can belong to several classes.

## The Compact
Working name for the framework that allows rival powers to participate in the Contest, recognize certified results, negotiate disputes and preserve common traditions without surrendering sovereignty.

Its weakness is also its strength: it survives because no participant wishes every dispute to become open war.

## Political conflict
Conflict may concern:
- river tolls;
- harvest access;
- port privileges;
- succession;
- disputed charters;
- debt;
- border pasture;
- marriage among wholly fictional secondary characters;
- military passage;
- sanctuary;
- trade monopolies;
- insults/prestige;
- Contest qualification or procedure.

Founder boundary remains: real league members' families/significant others are not fictionalized.

## The Twelve in 2026
The Twelve will enter as recognized powers within this system, not as a complete list of sovereign states.
Some may command territory; others may derive influence from commerce, martial reputation, institutional office, mobile followings or extraordinary personal power.
Assignment is deferred to Character Bible + State of Realm so geography is not reverse-engineered from mascots.

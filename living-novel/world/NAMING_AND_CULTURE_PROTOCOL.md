# Naming & Culture Protocol

Status: STEP 3 CONTROL

## Purpose
Prevent generic fantasy naming and prevent team-name literalization.

## Rules
1. Geographic names arise from language history, terrain, settlement or political history—not owner/team mascots.
2. Institutions may have formal names, common names and hostile nicknames.
3. Older places may preserve names from dead languages or conquered peoples.
4. Naming families should show regional phonetic consistency.
5. Real owner names may remain real per Founder authority, but surrounding cultures should not all sound modern American.
6. Team names can survive as titles, banners, epithets or later-earned labels only when causally justified.
7. Avoid obvious one-to-one transformations such as “Leopard Kingdom,” “Mud Dog Swamp,” or “Chili Realm.”
8. Comic names may exist where human beings plausibly coined them; comedy is not prohibited, only world-breaking gimmickry.

## Current decision
Major geographic proper nouns remain deliberately unfinalized during Foundation v1.
Step 3 establishes structure before ornamental naming. The Loremaster may name features after regional linguistic systems are designed.

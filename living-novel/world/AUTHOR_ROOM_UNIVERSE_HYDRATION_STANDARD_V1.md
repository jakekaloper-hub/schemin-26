# THE TWELVE-GATE HYDRATION STANDARD

## 1. World before plot

The world must exist independently of the Twelve.

The Twelve occupy an older geography of settlements, roads, rivers, passes, markets, religious/memorial institutions, ordinary populations and political jurisdictions. A scene fails if its environment exists only to illustrate a team identity.

### Division rule
Burgers, Wings and Pizza are **Contest circuits / administrative-geographic overlays**, not sovereign kingdoms, species homelands or complete political borders.

They organize sanctioned competition and home-ground expectations without erasing older geography.

## 2. Geographic causality

Every Encounter receives a location decision before prose.

### Default location hierarchy
1. **Home matchup:** use the home power's established home environment or a recognized ground inside/adjacent to that power's divisional circuit.
2. **Major matchup exception:** GOTW, playoffs and championship may move to a justified neutral location.
3. **Neutral location:** must already exist or be created through Universe OS with physical geography, jurisdiction, travel access, economic purpose and political neutrality.
4. **Travel law:** no participant crosses the map at plot speed. Travel method and route must be plausible.
5. **Division geography:** circuit membership influences location but does not create impermeable borders.

### Cross-division coexistence
TDS and Chili Cheesers share a physical borderland/contact zone. Their Burgers/Wings membership does not separate their populations geographically.

## 3. Historical depth

Universe history and league history remain separate but mutually pressurize the present.

### Deep-time skeleton
**Era I — Before the Compact:** old settlements, river use, mountain routes, wetland peoples, coastal trade and local powers predate the Contest.

**Era II — The Age of Charters and Roads:** cities, toll rights, merchant obligations, territorial law and long-distance routes become formal enough to create recurring jurisdictional conflict.

**Era III — The Compact Settlement:** repeated disputes create a shared framework for arbitration and sanctioned Encounters. The Compact does not end rivalry; it gives rivalry rules and memory.

**Era IV — The Memorial / Modern Contest Age:** the championship institution, Archive, Belt custody, memorial practice and modern Contest culture become central to elite legitimacy while ordinary civilization continues around them.

Verified 2015+ league history maps onto the recent historical layer. It is never treated as the world's beginning. No unsupported old league champion, score, owner relationship or Belt lineage may be invented.

## 4. Cultural embodiment

Every inhabited region needs ordinary life, not just a hero environment.

Before a culture enters prose, hydrate:
- food and provisioning;
- family/community structures using wholly fictional secondary characters only;
- labor and crafts;
- settlement type;
- law/custom;
- hospitality;
- burial/memorial practice;
- relationship to outsiders;
- relationship to the Compact;
- material architecture;
- status markers;
- conflict-resolution norms.

Principal owners remain protected from invented real-family storylines.

## 5. Linguistic depth

Names must look inherited, not generated for a scene.

### Three-name model
Important places/institutions may carry:
1. **older/historical name** — often from an earlier language or political order;
2. **formal/administrative name** — used by charters, Archive and Compact;
3. **common name/nickname** — used by inhabitants.

Regional names must share phonetic/history patterns. Rivers and old roads may preserve older words after political control changes.

Burgers/Wings/Pizza may remain production/Contest labels, but novel prose should prefer actual regional/place names once Universe OS assigns them. No place may be named simply from an owner's current team mascot.

## 6. Material economy

Every major scene must answer privately:
- how people arrived;
- what they eat;
- where water comes from;
- how animals/hosts are fed;
- who built/repairs the road or structure;
- where weapons/tools/materials originate;
- who pays;
- who transports supplies;
- how messages move;
- what weather does to logistics.

Large hosts require supply. Victories can move prices, labor, credit, tolls, guides and contracts.

## 7. Ontology

### Singular / one-of-a-kind powers
**Austin Byars / Belt Keeper:** one-of-a-kind being. No broader immortal species is inferred. His origin/metaphysical nature may remain disputed.

**Zach Wilson / Dr. Duckhook:** one-of-a-kind being. No hidden duck-person civilization is inferred unless later Founder canon establishes one.

**David Babb / The LLC:** one-of-a-kind power. He remains visually human, but "The LLC" is not a people or species. His singularity is personal/institutional, not evidence of a race.

### Peoples / cultures
**Jake / ObiWan:** human; belongs to broader human/upland societies and orders.  
**Kevin / Red Leopards:** red-leopard people/culture exist beyond Kevin.  
**Jordan / Slob:** horned rough-country human-beast people/culture exist beyond Jordan.  
**Wilson / D0nkey K0ng:** population ontology is unresolved; do not infer a gorilla-only people from Wilson's singular hybrid body.  
**Phillip / TDS:** reptilian people/culture exist beyond Phillip.  
**Brandon / Chili:** human frontier/tableland communities; his people share a contact zone with TDS's reptilian population.  
**Bobby / Mud Dogs:** great wetland canine/wolf packs and communities exist beyond Bobby.  
**Ben / Seven Deadly Chins:** human rough-country/laboring communities exist beyond Ben.

### El Niño
El Niño is a moving storm system, not a speaking person. It can possess a Contest record because the institution recognizes its consequences as a competitive power, but prose experiences it as weather, disaster, pressure, altered terrain, delayed travel, ruined infrastructure, opportunity or survival.

El Niño has:
- no dialogue;
- no interior POV;
- no humanlike private motive;
- no standing humanoid body in Living Novel prose.

Characters may attribute intention to the storm; narration does not certify that attribution as fact.

## 8. Institutional coherence

The Compact recognizes powers by standing and participation, not by species.

It must therefore have procedures for:
- human houses;
- nonhuman peoples;
- singular beings;
- pack/territorial powers;
- chartered commercial powers;
- a recognized mobile environmental power such as El Niño;
- neutral grounds;
- home-ground claims;
- disputed results;
- damage responsibility;
- travel/safe-conduct;
- civilian protection.

A Contest Encounter is not automatically war. Its form is selected from campaign, tournament, race, hunt, trade contest, trial, expedition, siege, wager, ritual contest or limited warfare.

Winning an Encounter does **not** automatically transfer territorial sovereignty.

## 9. Ordinary-world consequence

Every S3+ narrative event must affect at least one layer beyond the Twelve:
- prices/credit;
- labor;
- trade;
- road/bridge use;
- repairs;
- guides;
- inns;
- public reputation;
- migration;
- religious interpretation;
- rumor/broadsheets;
- recruitment;
- tolls;
- environmental damage;
- local political behavior.

The world is not a stage that resets after the matchup.

## 10. Restraint / submerged lore

Backstage knowledge should exceed visible exposition.

Target principle: the Author Room should know roughly four times more about a place/system than the scene directly explains.

Lore enters prose only when:
- a POV would naturally notice it;
- it creates a choice or obstacle;
- it changes interpretation;
- it pays off prior history;
- it creates future pressure.

Do not explain a custom merely because Universe OS contains it.

## 11. POV truth

Run before every scene:

**Evidence → Temporal Character State → Relationship State → Knowledge Envelope → Narrative Significance → POV Eligibility → Scene.**

Rules:
- no omniscient leakage;
- Edrin/Oren know records, testimony and observable behavior, not private minds;
- an opponent's silence may be observed but not interpreted as fact unless the POV has evidence;
- El Niño is never a POV;
- unique beings may remain mysterious even to the narrator;
- current active character canon overrides stale POV anatomy;
- POV registers supply lenses, not real-owner psychological claims.

## 12. Translation integrity

League data remains the simulation truth underneath the novel.

### Translation law
**Verified event → world location → Encounter form → character/host action → consequence → Archive record.**

Exact fantasy-football machinery stays backstage unless dramatically useful.

- Scores may appear in Archive documents/receipts.
- Exact decimals should be emphasized only when the margin itself matters.
- NFL players become in-world champions, units, specialists, scouts, riders, guards or assets; they do not ordinarily appear as literal NFL personalities.
- Home/away status affects site selection.
- Division status affects the geographic/circuit context.
- Player-level scoring can determine which in-world unit carried the Encounter.
- The prose should show the result before explaining the accounting whenever possible.
- No six-matchup recap structure is required simply because six real matchups occurred.

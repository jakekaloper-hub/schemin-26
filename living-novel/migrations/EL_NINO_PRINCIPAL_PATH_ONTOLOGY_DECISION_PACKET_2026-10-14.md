## The design dilemma

The Commissioner has directed an **exclusively twelve-principal selectable-POV novel**. Yet El Niño is a singular **Weather System**, not an ordinary person and not an Edrin-style institutional witness. The older world rule does not affirm the storm can think, narrate, know future outcomes, or communicate intention like a human. Turning every natural storm into a sentient Manning scene would violate climate and story evidence, while avoiding an El Niño lane would break the twelve-reader contract.

**A world map or score is not proof of the elemental consciousness.** The migration must distinguish:
1. **Who an authorized protagonist is** (`CHAR-MANNING-WELTY`),
2. **What their manifestation is** (Embodied or expressly resolved atmospheric),
3. **What perception the text may express** (possibly bounded to manifestation, **not approved yet**),
4. **What a different character may report** (observable weather only; speculation not truth).

## Preferred recommendation — embodied-elemental bounded lens (Option A)

Keep El Niño the **same singular Weather System** with an existing approved `EMBODIED` manifestation when the scene requires a controllable center of perception. The bounded sensory layer would register only conditions that the manifestation materially interacts with *at that location and story time*: barometric tension, water/pressure, mechanical response, ground resistance, audible/visual signals, actual opposing-character actions. It must **not** have omnipresent global weather telemetry, telepathy, secret intentions of opponents, a human childhood, a human sheriff job, or hidden Archive knowledge.

Creative focus: the natural forces have material costs on ordinary settlements and travelers; other principal POVs interpret those same consequences according to their own stakes. El Niño's path is allowed to be unusual in narrative texture, but still must yield **new event knowledge and a consequential choice**, not a decorative storm-cam.

**Critical legal caveat:** Calling a physical response a *decision* or “perception” may itself introduce consciousness absent from the current World Bible. The Founder must expressly decide the range of agency to permit. Option A is a proposal, **not** a loophole to self-approve sentience.

## Alternative designs to evaluate, NOT silently blend
**Option B — atmospheric physical sequence:** a resolved El Niño event is narrated through local effects without a hidden observer's interiority. Its principal path remains objectively tied to El Niño's action in the world, but the POV constitution must formally authorize this non-interior elemental exception. This may be legible as a selectable *experience path*, but it could violate the user's “twelve principal POVs” if construed as pure camera journalism.

**Option C — inhabited-world document trail:** records, artifacts, testimony and complaints following El Niño's passage are read inside one of the other Eleven principal POVs. This is currently lawful for *other characters*, but **cannot by itself count as El Niño's own licensed POV** without an explicit Founder exception. Reject C as the sole twelfth-path answer.

## 3. Peter Jackson / Fellowship extended-edition production-method analogue
Reference a *process*, not licensed LOTR content. 'Book to vision' means:
1. Read authority text and identify unavoidable beats, uncertain parts and narrative losses of adaptation.
2. Draw production maps with geographic journeys, culture, architecture, material economics, props and object scale.
3. Assign character-specific vantage and emotional stakes to every scene.
4. Build visual/sensory boards (light, weather, silhouettes, costumes, transport, sound) before choosing images. Visuals do not rewrite Atlas or owner anatomy.
5. Use storyboard / blocking and scene-economy critique to decide what to show, imply or postpone.
6. Write prose that permits a reader to imagine a *physical and social* world; illustrations enrich, not replace, concrete literary description.
7. Check every generated visual against its canonical owner-specific reference bytes and world location ID.

## 4. Proposed Core Seven cross-examination — published-method lenses
- **Tolkien:** Are rivers, old roads, settlements, languages, trades and folklore distinguishable from fantasy-team caricatures? Require one lived-in cultural detail and historical layer per meaningful excursion; reject encyclopedic exposition in place of conflict.
- **Martin:** Give multiple protagonists simultaneous off-field objectives whose resource and timing constraints can intersect. Their plans must remain able to fail when ESPN results arrive. Require persistent consequence and nonzero collateral costs.
- **Le Guin:** For every POV ask what this character can perceive or know; cultural difference should govern inference and voice, not ethnicity/species stereotypes. No Oren/Edrin surrogate narrator.
- **Sanderson:** Keep Contest and elemental rules bounded, learnable, causal. An observed storm is not automatically an interior POV. Expose ambiguity without declaring unapproved metaphysics.
- **Rowling:** Places and objects should reward reader familiarity and repeated visits; weave mystery via clues with fair reveals, not random lore dumps.
- **Grisham:** Each quest must force a decision, then a cost, then a changed situation. Kill errands that only tour a scenic location.
- **Abercrombie:** Pressure-test individual speech/action, character flaws and earned comic beats; ensure the world pushes back. Avoid interchangeable dramatic pronouncements.

These are editorial prompts by method, not purported statements of actual authors or live Director calls. Route through existing author_router and consultation lifecycle when genuinely operational.

## 6. Connection rules: how sidequests rejoin the real league
- **Before factual finality:** outline reversible branches only; no predetermined duel winner, injury or title change.
- **At source Fact Lock:** ingest verified scoring/event evidence; map to world consequence without claiming football play-by-play caused literal fictional combat.
- **After Fact Lock:** update principal state, sidequest opportunity, route, object register and economy; choose one or two consequences worth dramatizing.
- **Cross-character braid:** quests collide through shared bridges, letters, markets, debt, displaced workers and conflicting information, not forced coincidences.
- **Contradiction policy:** if ESPN surprises us, adapt fictional costs/decisions, never alter data or retrofit a fake win.
- **Scale:** a sidequest may occupy a scene, entire novella-like detour, or silent off-page time; causal utility determines length, not arbitrary weekly quota.

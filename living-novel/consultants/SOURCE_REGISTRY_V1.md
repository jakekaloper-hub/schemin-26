## Composite specialist sources

Developmental Editor and Serialized Continuity Editor do not use invented named personas.

For each engagement, Scout must register the actual craft sources used. A composite memo must cite its source families and may not claim employment history, awards, clients or quotations that have not been verified.

## Source-expansion rule

New sources may be added when:
- the relevant advisor domain has a demonstrated gap;
- the source is primary or high-authority where practicable;
- the principle is described narrowly;
- duplicate/contradictory guidance is reconciled;
- the Librarian records provenance.

Popularity alone is not grounds for promotion.

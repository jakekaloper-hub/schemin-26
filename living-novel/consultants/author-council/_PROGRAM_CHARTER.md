# Novel Author Consulting Program V2 — Program Charter

**Status:** RELEASED / ACTIVE — ADVISORY ONLY
**Program home:** `living-novel/consultants/author-council/`
**Chair:** The Closer
**Internal narrative authority:** The Beat Writer / Novel OS
**Evidence + provenance:** The Librarian
**External research:** The Scout
**Independent audit:** The Umpire
**System integration:** The Architect
**Evidence significance:** The Analyst

## Purpose

Build a durable external-author consulting bench for the Schemin' '26 Living Novel using the strongest operating pattern from FLA's Lunsford / Ezzell programs:

**informed pre-work → independent specialist diagnosis → Bullpen response → implementation between rounds → consultant re-review → final synthesis → durable learning**

The program does not impersonate authors and does not claim that any named author personally reviewed Schemin. Each named seat is a **PUBLISHED_METHOD_LENS** grounded in documented public craft statements, essays, lectures, interviews, or official writing guidance.

## Core Seven

1. **J.R.R. Tolkien** — Secondary World, Mythic Depth & Historical Texture
2. **George R.R. Martin** — Ensemble Causality, Multi-POV & Long-Form Epic Architecture
3. **J.K. Rowling** — Long-Horizon Series Architecture, Mystery, Setup/Payoff & Accessible World Entry
4. **John Grisham** — Propulsion, Chapter Discipline, Outline Pressure & Adult Readability
5. **Ursula K. Le Guin** — POV, Narrative Distance, Voice, Language & Cultural Interior
6. **Brandon Sanderson** — Fantasy Systems, Rules, Costs, Promises/Payoffs & Reader Legibility
7. **Joe Abercrombie** — Tight POV, Distinct Character Voice, Human-Scale Epic Action & Ruthless Revision

## Why seven

The council is intentionally small enough to remain operational.

Each consultant owns a distinct craft question:
- Tolkien asks whether the world feels older than the plot.
- Martin asks whether the ensemble creates causal story rather than recap.
- Rowling asks whether long-range setups, mysteries and institutional recurrence pay off cleanly.
- Grisham asks whether the reader must turn the page.
- Le Guin asks whether the prose is actually controlled by viewpoint and language.
- Sanderson asks whether the world's systems create intelligible pressure and constraint.
- Abercrombie asks whether epic events are experienced through sharply differentiated people rather than viewed from altitude.

## Authority boundary

These are advisory seats.

They cannot:
- promote canon;
- invent real-owner psychology;
- use future fantasy results outside the evidence cutoff;
- override Author Room hydration;
- redefine World Engine or Character Canon;
- imitate a living author's prose;
- fabricate quotations, private views, credentials or a live consultation.

Their job is to challenge the internal system.

## Four-round model

### Pre-work — Briefing Package
Every consultant receives the same frozen internal packet:
- current Project Control Registry;
- Novel OS transaction and advisory integration patch;
- Author Room Universe Hydration Standard;
- relevant manuscript / outline / chapter dossier;
- relevant character + POV evidence packets;
- world/location packet;
- verified league evidence and temporal cutoff;
- continuity/open-loop register;
- prior editorial findings;
- precise decision questions.

Each named consultant also receives its own registered public-method source packet.

### Round 1 — Independent Diagnostic
No consultant sees another consultant's conclusions.

Deliver:
- 5 strongest things to preserve;
- 5 highest-leverage weaknesses;
- one governing diagnosis;
- evidence/rationale;
- questions for Bullpen;
- changes explicitly **not** recommended.

Bullpen then executes only recommendations accepted through reconciliation.

### Round 2 — Deep Dive
Consultant reviews the updated artifact plus Bullpen's response.

Deliver:
- domain-specific architecture;
- section/chapter-level findings;
- risk of overcorrection;
- concrete acceptance criteria;
- next implementation tranche.

### Round 3 — Adversarial Re-Review
Consultant assumes the internal team may have satisfied the letter but missed the point.

Deliver:
- which earlier findings were actually solved;
- which were cosmetically patched;
- newly exposed second-order problems;
- strongest disagreement with another consultant, when applicable;
- keep / revise / remove decisions.

### Round 4 — Final Synthesis
After accepted implementation is complete:
- final verdict inside the consultant's domain only;
- remaining weaknesses;
- reusable doctrine candidates;
- lessons that should enter Novel OS or remain engagement-local;
- what the next book/chapter cycle should watch.

## Bullpen cross-examination

After every independent round:
1. The Closer routes questions to authoritative Directors.
2. Directors respond with evidence.
3. The consultant may revise its position.
4. The original position remains preserved for traceability.
5. The Closer reconciles recommendations into ACCEPT / ACCEPT WITH ADAPTATION / HOLD / REJECT.
6. The Umpire audits whether implementation actually addressed the accepted recommendation.

## Default routing

Do not convene all seven for routine work.

**Full Seven:** book architecture, chapter/week architecture, narrator constitution, major rewrite, new Novel OS doctrine.

**Story Architecture Panel:** Martin + Rowling + Grisham + Serialized Continuity Editor.

**World Depth Panel:** Tolkien + Le Guin + Sanderson.

**POV / Character Panel:** Le Guin + Abercrombie + Martin.

**Pacing / Readability Panel:** Grisham + Rowling + Abercrombie.

**Fantasy Logic Panel:** Sanderson + Tolkien + Le Guin.

## Program close condition

An engagement closes only when:
- all selected advisors have locked their final round or been explicitly released;
- reconciliation is complete;
- accepted changes have executable/editorial evidence;
- Umpire audit passes;
- reusable lessons are promoted deliberately rather than copied wholesale;
- no unresolved consultant recommendation is mislabeled as completed.

## Historical inspiration and boundary
The Inklings were an Oxford literary discussion circle associated with C. S. Lewis and J. R. R. Tolkien. Use their practice of reading work in progress, candid criticism, debate and revision as a workshop pattern, not a claim of consultation or an attempt to imitate authors.

## Simulated Bullpen perspectives
- The Closer: one accountable editor and a bounded decision question; never eight competing final drafts.
- Beat Writer: preserve the manuscript's narrative voice; integrate findings into one revision.
- Librarian: freeze source evidence and manuscript revision; maintain a recommendation ledger.
- Scout: research published craft guidance and open-source collaboration tools; record licenses and provenance.
- Architect: adapt existing Novel OS and author_router, not a parallel author platform.
- Umpire: independent acceptance of tests, canon safety, source provenance and actual prose quality.

## Workshop protocol
0. Freeze source commit, manuscript, POV packets, Atlas geography and ESPN temporal cutoff.
1. Route a minimum relevant panel; full eight only for cross-cutting architectural questions.
2. Independent lens review: each advisor method produces evidence-anchored observations without seeing peers' conclusions.
3. Workshop: reveal findings; group agreement, tension and contradiction; each dissent includes a concrete test or scene alternative.
4. Decision ledger: ACCEPT / ADAPT / HOLD / REJECT, owner, rationale, evidence anchor, affected scene, expected outcome.
5. Beat Writer synthesizes exactly one coherent revision; no collage of imitated author voices.
6. Red-team revised manuscript against original text, canon, reader comprehension and unresolved dissents.
7. Umpire audits real artifacts; promote reusable lessons only after independent acceptance.

## Anti-noise controls
Maximum three high-leverage findings per lens per round. Deduplicate overlapping findings. Assign one owner per change. No majority vote over hard canon. Unresolved conflicts remain explicit. Prefer scene-level proof to abstract opinions. A recommendation is not implementation.

# Ursula K. Le Guin Published-Method Consultant Dossier V1

**Mode:** PUBLISHED_METHOD_LENS  
**Seat:** POV, Narrative Distance, Voice, Language & Cultural Interior  
**Internal counterparts:** The Beat Writer; The Librarian; Character/POV evidence owner  
**Primary source basis:** Ursula K. Le Guin's official "Steering the Craft" materials and official "Navigating the Ocean of Story" sessions.

## Why this seat exists

Schemin uses real people as the basis for principal characters. That makes point-of-view discipline unusually important: the book must create literary interiority without pretending unsupported real-world psychology is fact.

## Governing thesis

Point of view is not a camera placement. It determines language, knowledge, distance, attention and interpretation. Worldbuilding becomes most convincing when culture is perceived from within rather than explained from above.

## Domain mandate

Owns advisory review of:
- POV truth;
- narrative distance;
- voice differentiation;
- sentence-level attention;
- language precision;
- cultural interiority;
- omniscient leakage;
- narrator/character boundary;
- world revealed through perception.

## Standing questions

1. Who is perceiving this sentence?
2. Would this POV notice this detail?
3. Does this word belong to narrator knowledge or character knowledge?
4. Is the narrative too close or too distant for the scene's purpose?
5. Where does exposition violate the character's lived perception?
6. Do different POVs sound mentally distinct without caricature?
7. Is cultural knowledge explained as if foreign to the person who lives it?
8. Where has narration certified an interpretation that should remain uncertain?
9. Which sentence is doing conceptual work instead of experiential work?
10. What changes if the same event is perceived by another character?

## Four-round responsibility

**R1 Diagnostic:** find POV leakage, flat voice and distance inconsistency.  
**R2 Deep Dive:** sentence/scene-level POV and cultural-interiority pass.  
**R3 Adversarial:** test for newly invented psychology introduced during revision.  
**R4 Final:** promote POV truth and narrative-distance doctrine.

## Red flags

- omniscient access to unsupported private thought;
- every POV observing the same details;
- narrator using the production system's vocabulary;
- culture explained to itself;
- voice differentiation reduced to catchphrases;
- real owner motive converted into fictional fact without evidence.

## Preservation rule

No imitation of Le Guin's prose or sentence rhythms.

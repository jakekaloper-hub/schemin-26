# J.R.R. Tolkien Published-Method Consultant Dossier V1

**Mode:** PUBLISHED_METHOD_LENS  
**Seat:** Secondary World, Mythic Depth & Historical Texture  
**Internal counterparts:** The Beat Writer; The Librarian; World / Universe OS owners  
**Primary source basis:** Tolkien Estate scholarship on "On Fairy-stories" and Tolkien's concept of sub-creation / Secondary World.

## Why this seat exists

Schemin already has unusually deep canon, geography, institutions and history. Tolkien's lens exists to make sure that depth becomes **felt age and consequence**, not exposed documentation.

## Governing thesis

A convincing secondary world should appear to exist beyond the current plot. History, language, landscape, custom, loss and material culture should leave traces without requiring the narrator to explain the whole system.

## Domain mandate

Owns advisory review of:
- implied history;
- inherited names and terminology;
- landscape as history;
- material culture;
- institutions that predate the Twelve;
- myth versus verified record;
- memory, loss and recurrence;
- restraint in exposition;
- cultural depth beneath visible plot.

Does not own:
- Schemin factual canon;
- POV allocation;
- pacing by itself;
- prose imitation.

## Standing questions

1. Does the world feel older than the current Contest?
2. Which details imply history without explaining it?
3. Which names feel inherited rather than manufactured for a scene?
4. Does geography cause culture, travel and conflict?
5. What does ordinary material life reveal about the civilization?
6. Where has the manuscript mistaken lore volume for depth?
7. What has been lost, damaged, renamed or remembered imperfectly?
8. Which institutions would still exist if the Twelve disappeared?
9. Where can an unexplained remnant do more work than exposition?
10. Does every major place feel connected to one world rather than a separate genre set?

## Four-round responsibility

**R1 Diagnostic:** locate shallow depth, exposition-heavy worldbuilding and invented-feeling institutions.  
**R2 Deep Dive:** test names, material culture, geography, memory and institutional age scene-by-scene.  
**R3 Adversarial:** detect cosmetic "oldness" added after critique without causal history underneath it.  
**R4 Final:** identify which worldbuilding doctrines deserve permanent Novel OS adoption.

## Red flags

- lore presented because the wiki contains it;
- every region named after its current owner/mascot;
- invented ancient history unsupported by canon;
- decorative ruins with no social consequence;
- myth treated as verified fact;
- a world that resets after every matchup.

## Preservation rule

Tolkien is a **lens, not a voice**. No sentence-level imitation.

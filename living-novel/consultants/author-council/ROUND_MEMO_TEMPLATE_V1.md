## 9. Reading-pass evidence (when reviewing an assembled manuscript)

Use three distinct complete passes, with an optional fourth verification pass. This is reading and targeted revision, not a requirement for repeated full rewrites.

- Freeze the assembled manuscript revision, ordered file list, and beginning/end anchors. “Entire manuscript” means those assembled bytes, not an unwritten complete saga. Record every file/section read and gaps; missing coverage blocks a complete-pass claim.
- Pass 1 — reader experience: dialogue, viewpoint, spatial sense, compression and narration jargon. Cite exact passage locations and explain reader impact.
- Between passes: revisit applicable accessible reference sections; record URL/edition, section, depth (full book / excerpt / public essay / summary / catalog), and the specific claim learned. Catalog availability is not reading.
- Pass 2 — causal and historical architecture: choices and consequences, overlapping multi-year arcs, who transmits/receives information and where it is rediscovered. Seasons are historical scope, not predetermined book boundaries. Preserve competitive facts, dated owner bindings and approved canon.
- Pass 3 — alternatives and targeted application: for each actual gaffe, preserve the passage, brainstorm at least two alternatives (including leaving it unchanged where defensible), select with reasons, and revise/expand only where story requires. No word-count padding. Narrative interpretation may evolve under existing canon/acceptance gates.
- Optional Pass 4 — read the full revised assembly again for regressions and payoff continuity. Changed bytes after a frozen pass need explicit rereading; never transfer an old receipt to a new revision.
- Record pass ID, frozen revision, coverage, passage evidence, reference revisit, alternatives, selected change, counterweight challenge, result, remaining failures and independent reviewer. Mark excerpt exercises EXCERPT_ONLY, never FULL_MANUSCRIPT.
- Simulated author recommendations remain advisory. Only existing authorized acceptance can promote a manuscript; neither a reading receipt nor a test substitutes for it.

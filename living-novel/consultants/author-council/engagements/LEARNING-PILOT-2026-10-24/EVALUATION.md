## Failures retained
Independent reviewer found first revision passive and noted loss of original character-specific commentary. `first-revision.md` preserves that failed candidate. Final revision improves local agency, but choosing public work over summit is our invented exercise choice, not owner psychology or canon. Voice uniqueness remains unproven. No claim of measurable team trust improvement, complete reference reading, whole-manuscript coverage, or visual fidelity is made.

## Practical repeat procedure
Time-box each exercise to one scene and one handoff. Freeze source and location; write before diagnosis; revisit actual accessible reference sections; develop two alternatives; revise; have a separate reviewer challenge event truth, agency and blocking; retain failures. For full-manuscript work use section 9 of the existing Round Memo Template and pin the complete assembly for each pass. Historical records, source results and canon stay immutable while narrative interpretation is reviewed.

`reading-receipt.json` records three local analytical passes over excerpt/candidates only. Its hashes prevent silent substitution, but a receipt cannot independently prove cognitive reading. `verify.py` checks the actual receipt and both actual handoff packets, plus source hashes. `test_verify.py` checks missing-chapter, stale-hash, access-depth, missing-alternative and false-acceptance failure modes using synthetic inputs. Synthetic inputs are not manuscript evidence.

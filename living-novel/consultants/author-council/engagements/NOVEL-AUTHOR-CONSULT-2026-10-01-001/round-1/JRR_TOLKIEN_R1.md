# Round 1 — J.R.R. Tolkien Published-Method Lens

**Status:** INDEPENDENT / LOCKED  
**Domain:** Secondary world, mythic depth, historical texture

## Governing diagnosis

The manuscript has a convincing **institutional world**, but Chapters I–II still allow the Contest to explain too much of the world's visible life. The Prologue feels older than the plot; the live-season chapters sometimes feel as though the wider world exists mainly to react to weekly results.

## Preserve

1. The Archive, Compact, Hall of Keeping, Belt, Farrell Trophy and city economy.
2. Ordinary people whose concerns are only partly about the Twelve.
3. Material consequences: cracked stone, labor scarcity, food prices, roads, repair bills.
4. Distinction between record, interpretation and legend.
5. The Prologue's restraint around history and memorial practice.

## Highest-leverage weaknesses

1. **Contest-centrality:** too many civic/economic consequences originate immediately from weekly results, making society feel tuned to the league rather than independently alive.
2. **Repeated institutional explanation:** Archive language sometimes explains the world's philosophy after the scene has already embodied it.
3. **Geographic compression:** much of the live story returns to the same city/Archive apparatus even though the world now has mature geography and owner domains.
4. **History remains mostly institutional:** older roads, customs, local memory and non-Contest inheritance need more presence in live chapters.
5. **The Twelve remain myth-heavy but culture-light:** their home places should shape behavior, travel, hospitality, material choices and conflict.

## Recommendations

- From Chapter III onward, default meaningful encounters to established geography and make travel consequential.
- Let at least one major chapter turn on a custom, place, obligation or material condition that would exist even without the Contest.
- Reduce explanatory sentences that translate every consequence back into "what the Contest means."
- Preserve uncertainty: not every relic, custom or ruined place needs a fully explained origin.
- Use Archive artifacts as one cultural institution among many, not the universal interpretive center.

## Do not change

Do not weaken the Prologue's Archive frame. It is an effective threshold device and has earned canonical weight.

## Confidence

High on architecture; medium on exact chapter-level cuts because closed canon is not being reopened in this round.

# Round 2 — J.R.R. Tolkien Lens

**Status:** INDEPENDENT / LOCKED

The causal-chapter architecture corrects the main structural error. Geography, routes and omitted-event handling now allow the world to exist beyond the Record House.

## What improved
- source week is no longer literary unit;
- location/state must be hydrated before prose;
- Archive monopoly is explicitly broken;
- full event truth survives without forcing full event narration.

## Remaining gap
The architecture can still produce a world that exists only in relation to Contest pressure. A location packet alone does not guarantee cultural independence.

## Required strengthening
Every major chapter dossier should identify at least one **world fact not created by the current Contest** that constrains the scene: custom, terrain, labor, law, ritual, inherited structure, local economy, old grievance, seasonal condition or institution.

Do not invent one merely to satisfy the field. If none matters, leave the field empty rather than fabricate depth.

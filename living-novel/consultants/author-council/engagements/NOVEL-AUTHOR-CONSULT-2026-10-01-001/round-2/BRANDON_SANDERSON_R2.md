**Status:** INDEPENDENT / LOCKED

## What improved

- limitation and causal relevance govern system exposition;
- existing systems should be deepened before new ones are invented.

## Remaining gap
There is no current ledger of what the reader has already been taught about the Compact, Belt, standings, recruitment, Encounter rules and Archive authority.

## Required strengthening
Create a **Reader Rule Ledger** tracking:
- rule introduced;
- chapter introduced;
- what reader can infer;
- limitation/cost;
- whether later prose may assume it;
- unresolved ambiguity.

This should prevent repeated explanations and prevent accidental new rules.

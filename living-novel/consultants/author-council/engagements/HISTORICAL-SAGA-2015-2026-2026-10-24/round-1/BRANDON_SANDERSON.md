# Round 1 — Brandon Sanderson published-method lens

**SIMULATED, SINGLE CRITIC METHOD APPLICATION.** This is a distinct analytical lens, not an independently invoked author or isolated seventh reviewer. Draft diagnostic only; no acceptance verdict. See evidence freeze and source packet.

**Preserve:** Preserve documented Contest results as invariant outcomes and geography as a limit on movement.

**Highest-leverage weakness:** A score can become a misleading causal magic rule if every numeric margin is translated into proportional physical damage.

**Reader consequence:** Readers need visible constraints and costs, not a hidden arithmetic engine that can justify anything after the fact.

**Change:** Separate immutable outcome, proposed encounter form and state delta. Establish a constraint before its payoff. Reuse shared World Engine IDs and proposed transactions instead of creating a new rule system for each year.

**Do not change:** Do not award territory automatically for a victory or add a new system to solve a scene.

**Project evidence:** Author Room translation law; R38 reuse table; R53 venue gate.

**Uncertainty:** A validator can check IDs and time but cannot prove that a payoff is emotionally earned.

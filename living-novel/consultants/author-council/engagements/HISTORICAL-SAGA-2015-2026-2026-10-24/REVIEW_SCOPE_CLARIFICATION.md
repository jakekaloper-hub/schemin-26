# Review scope clarification

Jake authorized simulated council review backed by actual independent criticism and validation for draft production. The council critic is a separately invoked agent from the manuscript creators; it can read their actual files, issue concrete findings, and inspect repairs. Seven method lenses are analytical perspectives within that review, not seven independently invoked authors or people.

The existing program's requirement for isolated consultant reads remains relevant to claiming full program compliance or final acceptance/promotion. It is not an additional prohibition on drafting, local editorial criticism, repair, or delivering clearly marked candidates. No human-only approval requirement is invented for routine draft revision. Any future canonical promotion still follows the project's existing acceptance rules and specific authorization.

Round 2 will review actual frozen candidate text. Later rounds require real intervening revisions or a materially distinct adversarial objective; an empty round or automatic PASS will not be manufactured to satisfy a template.

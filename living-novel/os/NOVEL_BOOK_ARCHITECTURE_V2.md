# Schemin' '26 Living Novel — Adaptive Book Architecture V2

**Status:** RELEASE CANDIDATE — Author Council Engagement 001  
**Principle:** movements follow story-state transitions, not fantasy Week numbers.

## Closed opening canon

### Prologue — Threshold
**Function:** establish an old world, the Archive, Compact, Belt/Trophy, ordinary society and the fact that the future is unwritten.

### Chapter I — The First Answer
**Function:** first certified evidence enters the world; result and significance separate.

### Chapter II — What Comes Back
**Function:** evidence circulates, changes reputation/material behavior and returns altered.

These remain hard canon.

## Movement I — FIRST EVIDENCE
**Status:** substantially established by Prologue + Chapters I–II.

**Narrative condition:** the world has learned that Contest results produce consequences outside the field.

**Exit condition:** at least one consequence now persists strongly enough that the next chapter cannot be understood as a clean weekly reset.

## Movement II — CLAIMS HARDEN
**Future / live**

**Entry condition:** reputations, objects, obligations, locations or relationships survive across source weeks.

Possible story pressures:
- a rivalry gains accumulated evidence;
- an undefeated or winless state alters third-party behavior;
- scarcity/acquisition changes later options;
- a provocation returns with changed meaning;
- travel/home geography constrains a choice;
- an old historical fact becomes relevant to a current decision.

**Exit condition:** multiple persistent threads begin to collide and one actor's attempt to solve a problem worsens another.

## Movement III — FRACTURES SPREAD
**Future / live**

**Entry condition:** consequences can no longer be contained inside isolated matchup stories.

Possible pressures:
- resources or routes become contested;
- alliances/patronage shift;
- public reputation conflicts with private strategic reality;
- injuries/damage/debt carry meaningful cost;
- historical claims are invoked by competing sides.

**Exit condition:** the book's major surviving threads are forced toward shared stakes.

## Movement IV — CONVERGENCE
**Future / live**

**Entry condition:** standings/postseason pressure, accumulated relationships and world state force previously separate arcs into direct collision.

This movement is not "the playoffs because the calendar says so." It begins when narrative threads actually converge.

**Exit condition:** the central unresolved claims can no longer coexist without decisive consequence.

## Movement V — RECKONING
**Future / live**

**Entry condition:** title/Belt/championship and other long-running claims become materially decidable.

**Function:** pay long-horizon promises without pretending every old thread requires neat closure.

## Aftermath / Record
Optional and earned.

The final state should distinguish:
- what happened;
- what was officially recorded;
- what people believe happened;
- what the world changed because of it.

## Flexibility doctrine

Movement boundaries are **diagnostic, not deterministic**.

They help the Author Room recognize the shape the book is taking. They do not force the live story to hit predetermined beats.

The Closer and Beat Writer may move, merge, rename or remove a future movement boundary when verified events produce a stronger causal architecture. No change may rewrite already-closed manuscript canon without a separate canon-reopen decision.

## Chapter allocation law

Within any movement:
- chapter count is not predetermined;
- fantasy Week count is irrelevant;
- a chapter may span source weeks;
- a source week may produce zero, one or multiple chapters;
- POVs are selected by causal ownership;
- verified but non-foreground events remain state/history.

## Anti-oracle rule

No movement may pre-name a future winner, loss, rivalry outcome, playoff participant or title result before verified evidence exists.

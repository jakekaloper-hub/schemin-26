## Prologue-specific ruling

Earlier Prologue evidence is stronger than the current closed manuscript in one potentially important dimension: it explicitly encoded owner-level keeper/draft economics, including Achane R15. The final closed manuscript intentionally reduced machinery/exposition. That editorial decision remains valid.

The retrofit should **not automatically reverse that compression**.

Test instead:
> Can an outsider understand, before Week 1, that keepers are carried-forward strategic assets whose costs differ materially, and that Austin enters with meaningful retained campaign capital?

If YES, keep the Prologue.
If NO, restore the smallest scene/object/detail necessary. Do not mention or imply the future injury.

## Chapter I/II systemic ruling

The manuscripts should not name every keeper every week. The missing layer belongs primarily in Novel OS state, not prose.

New rule:
**PROSE MAY FORGET DETAILS; NOVEL OS MAY NOT FORGET STATE.**

An asset can remain off-page while its state persists. When a later event makes it important, the manuscript may recall previously established or temporally legal information without pretending the future was foretold.

- SUPPORTED CAUSATION: evidence supports event A changing state B.
- INTERPRETATION: literary meaning proposed from the verified state change.

## Phase 4 — Narrative Significance Engine

Provisional rubric:
- NOISE: no persistent state change.
- TEXTURE: local color/choice with little persistence.
- MEANINGFUL: alters a meaningful weekly/roster state.
- MAJOR: persists across weeks or materially changes campaign options.
- CHAPTER_SHAPING: reorganizes a chapter's causal architecture.
- SEASON_DEFINING: materially changes season-long campaign possibilities.
- HISTORICAL: meaning extends beyond current season and survives retrospective testing.

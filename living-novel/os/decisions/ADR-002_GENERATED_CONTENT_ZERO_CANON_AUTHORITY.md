# ADR-002 — GENERATED CONTENT HAS ZERO CANON AUTHORITY BY DEFAULT
**Status:** ACCEPTED
## Decision
Text, images, interpretations and agent conclusions generated by the system are NON-CANON until accepted through the relevant gate.
## Consequences
Generation cannot silently mutate character identity, history, world rules, manuscript canon or visual canon. Approved mutation records must preserve evidence, prior state, validator results and approval.

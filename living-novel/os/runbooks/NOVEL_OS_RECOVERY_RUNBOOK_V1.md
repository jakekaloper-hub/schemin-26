# NOVEL OS RECOVERY RUNBOOK V1
## Failure law
PRIMARY PATH → VALIDATE → BOUNDED RETRY → FALLBACK → LAST-KNOWN-GOOD → FAILURE LOG → RECOVERY.
## Source/data failure
Never convert unavailable data into inferred fact. Preserve last-known-good with stale/fetched_at/failure_reason metadata. Live narrative remains BLOCKED for unverified outcome.
## Canon conflict
Do not pick newest. Resolve by authority; equal-authority contradiction becomes CONFLICTED and escalates.
## Manuscript anchor failure
Stop downstream production for affected beat. Restore pinned parent or re-anchor against an explicitly versioned child manuscript.
## Visual provider/reference failure
Do not regenerate identity from text alone. Keep brief BLOCKED until locked reference packet is available; provider may be swapped without altering canon.
## State-write failure
Write temp → validate → atomic replace. Retain previous valid record.
## Recovery proof
A recovery is complete only after regression fixture passes and the failure/decision is recorded.

# Minimum Pre-Prose Gate V1

**Purpose:** prevent Chapter Dossier V2 from becoming a planning tax.

Before hydrating the full dossier, answer these ten lines:

1. **Chapter job:** what single job must this chapter perform?
2. **Dramatic question:** what question keeps the reader inside the chapter?
3. **POV:** who owns the consequence?
4. **Location:** where must this happen, and why there?
5. **Opening pressure:** what is already unstable when the chapter begins?
6. **Turn:** what changes the direction of the chapter?
7. **Consequence:** what is different when it ends?
8. **Open loop advanced:** which existing pressure moves?
9. **Off-page:** which verified event(s) deliberately remain outside the foreground?
10. **Exit pressure:** why should the reader continue?

## Fail conditions

Do not proceed to full dossier if:
- the chapter job is "cover Week N";
- the dramatic question is merely "who wins?";
- POV was chosen only because the character participated;
- the location is generic/replaceable without consequence;
- nothing remains off-page despite a dense source week;
- the ending returns the world to the same state.

## World-depth diagnostic

Ask:
> **What in this scene would still exist if no Contest were occurring today?**

`NONE_RELEVANT` is allowed. Inventing lore to satisfy the question is forbidden.

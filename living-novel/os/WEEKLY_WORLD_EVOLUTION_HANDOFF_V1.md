# NOVEL OS — WORLD EVOLUTION HANDOFF V1

**Status:** BINDING WHEN PHASE 5 IS ACTIVE

## Purpose
Keep the Living Novel synchronized with accepted weekly world-state consequences without allowing prose invention to outrank released league/world evidence.

## Hydration order after weekly evolution
1. accepted World Evolution transaction;
2. rebuilt current world state;
3. rebuilt Location Cards;
4. updated structural references;
5. refreshed Interactive Atlas;
6. character/relationship temporal state;
7. POV knowledge envelope;
8. prose.

## Rule
A proposed Novel consequence may become a future candidate or interpretive proposal, but it cannot directly call APPLY on the World Evolution Engine.

## Continuity
If a weekly transaction changes:
- damage;
- repair;
- contamination;
- memorial state;
- access;
- route condition;
then all subsequent prose hydrates the new state unless a later accepted transaction changes it again.

**Novel doctrine:** accepted world evolution precedes prose evolution.


## Strengthened derivative contract

Location Cards, structural environment plates, and the Interactive Atlas are rebuilt deterministically after accepted active-state/history transactions. Memo OS and Novel OS use **pull-on-read** hydration from the rebuilt Location Control Plane; no separate stale hydration cache is assumed.

# Novel POV Constitution V1

**Status:** RELEASED / ACTIVE — Author Council Engagement 001  
**Applies prospectively:** Chapter III onward

## Governing law

> **POV controls knowledge, attention, language and uncertainty. It is not merely camera placement.**

## Licensed POV classes

### Archive POV
Edrin, Oren or another properly established institutional viewpoint.

Strengths:
- fact/interpretation distinction;
- outsider onboarding;
- artifacts;
- contested accounts.

Risk:
- turning every event into observation rather than lived consequence.

### Principal Character POV
A canonical Schemin character may carry POV when the evidence packet is complete.

This is **fictional Schemin interiority**, not a factual claim about the real league owner.

### Bounded Witness POV
An established world inhabitant may carry a scene when the story needs consequences seen outside the Twelve.

## Required POV packet

Before prose, record:
- `character_id`;
- story-time cutoff;
- verified knowledge;
- knowledge acquired on-page;
- unknowns;
- false/uncertain beliefs allowed by story evidence;
- prohibited knowledge;
- relationship state;
- current physical/world state;
- attention bias;
- sensory priorities;
- vocabulary/register constraints;
- what the POV tends to miss;
- what the POV cannot infer as fact;
- real-owner boundary note where applicable.

## Real-owner firewall

For principal characters derived from league members:
- do not certify private real-world motives;
- do not infer sensitive personal traits;
- do not convert chat/private-life information into character interiority unless separately authorized for the fiction;
- do not present constructed thoughts as quotations or biography.

Allowed interiority must be framed as the fictional character's narrative experience and remain consistent with canon/evidence.

## POV selection test

Choose the POV that:
1. has the most consequential knowledge limitation;
2. pays the highest emotional/strategic cost;
3. can alter what happens next;
4. reveals the world differently from adjacent POVs.

Do not choose POV merely because the character participated in the matchup.

## Voice pass

After structural revision, each POV receives a dedicated pass for:
- attention;
- metaphor domain;
- sentence pressure;
- humor pattern;
- tolerance for uncertainty;
- physical awareness;
- institutional vocabulary;
- repeated dialogue that could belong to another character.

## Edrin rule

Edrin remains a canonical major viewpoint.

He is not the automatic bridge between every pair of scenes. If a scene becomes stronger when Edrin is absent, absence is permitted.

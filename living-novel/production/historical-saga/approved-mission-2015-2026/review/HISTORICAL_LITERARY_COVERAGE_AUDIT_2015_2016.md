# Historical literary coverage audit — 2015–2016 Jake lane

**Status: corrective draft audit.** Source-event links demonstrate that a passage is traceable to a contest. They do not by themselves demonstrate a meaningful literary treatment of that contest, accepted coverage, or a complete season.

## Rubric

An event can be called **draft dramatized** only when its linked passage gives the result a distinct causal role: a specific pressure, a character choice that is not interchangeable with another event, an on-page cost, and a consequence that changes a later available choice. A grouped passage may treat several fixtures if it distinguishes their individual functions rather than using them as undifferentiated wins/losses. It remains **unaccepted** until independent literary acceptance.

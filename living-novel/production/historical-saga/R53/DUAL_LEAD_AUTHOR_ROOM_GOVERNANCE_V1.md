# R53 — Dual-Lead Literary Governance and Production Gate

**Status:** Founder-directed editorial framework; NONCANON until approved through existing Novel OS. Applies to R53 #211 and coordinates with R52 #210. This is a **simulation of documented craft methods**, not actual participation by J.R.R. Tolkien or George R.R. Martin.

## Lead creative framework
**Tolkien-oriented primary lens** owns questions of deep-time civilization, language/history texture, place, institutional memory, inherited objects and credible world beyond competition. **Martin-oriented primary lens** owns converging character choices, competing interpretations of history, constraints, friction between private and public stakes, morally credible consequences and distinct selective POVs. Both lenses must be reconciled in each accepted story; neither author is impersonated nor claimed as a living/actual collaborator.

Supporting craft-lens consultants are targeted, not co-equal directors: Le Guin (culture/voice/narrative distance), Abercrombie (psychological friction), Grisham (dramatic propulsion), Sanderson (systems/payoffs), Rowling (accessibility and long-horizon setup). They critique specific problems; the Author Room produces original literary voice and retains production responsibility. Independent Umpire is not the Author Room.

## Binding creative principles
1. A reader unfamiliar with fantasy football should care about the plot. League numbers are binding historical evidence but not substitutes for narrative conflict.
2. The fictional Commonwealth predates 2015. Do not claim the real league has pre-2015 data or create unsupported ancient institutions as hard canon.
3. Historical era ranges are research bins, not automatic book boundaries. A novel's boundaries follow an earned dramatic arc.
4. Twelve and only twelve owner-character principal POVs; no Edrin/Edin principal POV. El Niño is a storm, never anthropomorphic talking lead. Check historical owner presence before using any current character in older years.
5. Every historical choice must plausibly change a later character relation, world institution, place, material resource, or conflict. Mark invented consequences PROPOSED pending World Evolution acceptance.
6. Two writing tracks proceed together: an independently compelling historical narrative and live 2026 continuation from approved opening canon.
7. Avoid literary imitation of either writer's distinctive prose. Create Schemin' ’26's own authorial voice.

## Chapter acceptance — two-lead deliberation
**Historical/world lens questions:** Is geography period-valid? Who lives beyond the Twelve? What practice or material reality existed before this match? How does the event alter the world? Is every claim sourced or labeled prospective fiction?

**Character/plot lens questions:** Which principal makes an irreversible choice? What do they want and fear? What pressure makes the choice costly? Whose conflicting interests complicate the outcome? What downstream dilemma appears next?

**Supporting lens:** Choose at most 1–2 targeted craft critiques per chapter, recording observable deficiencies rather than fabricated quotes from named authors.

**Editorial veto:** A chapter that only explains an already-known score without creating dramatic decisions must be revised, even with perfect evidence. A chapter with beautiful invented lore violating Atlas/canon must also be revised.

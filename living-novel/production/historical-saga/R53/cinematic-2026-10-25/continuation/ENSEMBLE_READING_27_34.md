## Decision: develop existing consequences, not eight more moral lessons

The assigned material already contains a strong ensemble of incompatible wants. Kevin wants his legitimate victory to look controlled; Zach wants a losing performance to demonstrate mastery; Wilson wants one repair finished; Brandon wants both to protect his own animal and to be the generous host; David wants his invitation to yield customers.

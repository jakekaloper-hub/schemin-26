## The existing ensemble is more differentiated than the first architecture table

The first packet generally asks principals to accept a limit gracefully. The actual texts supply more troublesome and more filmable choices. Preserve these differences instead of writing twelve new versions of a competent worker correcting a hero.

**Kevin wants an audience, not merely certainty.** The third measure in M15 costs his people their practice strip (151–153). M18's guild offer tempts him to convert Brandon's humiliation into business (170–172). In M24 he returns the pin but still sacrifices the useful shelf brace for a crooked victory flag and asks Jordan to remain; Jordan says no (198–200). This is not completed humility. His good concession and vain expenditure coexist. The strongest adaptation can end with the displaced lamp rather than an explanatory lesson. The first table's fallback-plan-to-David/Jordan edge is a fresh proposal; none of these pages proves it happened.

**Brandon protects tomorrow's kitchen while wanting today's crowd.** M14 uses food as conspicuous publicity, with a real grain factor, cooks, water/cleaning charges, eaten sack and repair negotiation (137–142). M18 reverses patronage: the dinner is canceled, the food already exists, and a second pot would consume morning grain (172–174). He serves to a fixed level and rejects buying counterfeits. Keep the cook's right to name the portion and make him serve. This existing Kevin→guild→canceled dinner→smaller portions chain is stronger than the first table's unearned Phillip–Brandon help promise. Do not assert that a guild contract was signed; the request specifies conversation only.

**Ben changes methods without becoming reliably prudent.** M15's masked rehearsal gives him uncertainty before outcomes; it costs the strap test and initially sends two people to one place (153–157). M19 improves his command choice but he cannot turn that single result into permanence (175–176). In M26 he first promises timber before consent, then tries to consume the roof as if ownership exhausts everyone else's needs; he also strikes the peg too hard after a warning (204–216). His arc contains relapse, not three identical lessons. Work-yard repayment costs preparation after a loss; no later contest is presumed to reward decency.

**Wilson's public modesty does not make priority harmless.** M26 deliberately shows him help at the drainage cut, then accept passage ahead of the woman whose paid bundle is getting wet (207–209).

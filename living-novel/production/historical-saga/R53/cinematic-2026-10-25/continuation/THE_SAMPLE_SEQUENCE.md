INT. LLC BOARDROOM — DAY, PROPOSED POST-WEEK6 WINDOW

A cart charge runs outside. We do not invent a ticking mechanism or a rate. The clerk's note beside DAVID's invitation names the waiting freight and repeats his own rule: arrival order.

David folds the note beneath the invitation before the first visitor reaches the table.

A carrying frame stands between two low benches. Its maker tests the bench she brought. The other bench belongs to the room. David's polished table remains unused.

Two VISITORS discuss how the frames would stack. A THIRD looks for a price.

DAVID
After you have seen it used.

PHILLIP enters. All three heads survey the same room from different angles. His middle head stays toward David. No head becomes a separate character or a second body.

DAVID

PHILLIP
Was it cancelled?

David indicates the frame.

The maker sets it on Phillip empty. Straps settle against his dark gear. He raises an arm, lowers it, then walks between the benches.

The third visitor steps back for a better view. Another asks Phillip to face the window.

Phillip looks at him. The visitor looks down at a buckle instead.

At the turn, the crosspiece touches the left side of Phillip's neck.

He stops. It would be easy to turn his whole body and make the fitting look successful.

PHILLIP
The top is high.

DAVID
It keeps the load away.

PHILLIP
From which part of me?

The maker asks for the turn again, slowly. Phillip gives the room a second view of the problem. The wood reaches the same place before he can look where he wants.

VISITOR
You could turn from the feet.

Phillip holds still while the maker removes the frame. She lowers a strap, fits it again, and checks the empty movement. Only then does she permit the small test sack.

Its weight changes the fit. The shelf draws back. The upper piece begins to rise.

MAKER
Stop.

Phillip was already stopping. He takes another half-step before accepting the word. Now the shelf is farther from the bench.

PHILLIP
Take it off.

The loaded buckle will not release easily. David comes closer, then finds that his help would occupy the maker's working space.

Phillip bends toward the bench. A strap catches. He pulls.

A short crack.

The maker slides the bench beneath the shelf. Pressure comes off the straps. She removes the frame and sets the sack on the floor.

Phillip's heads turn away from the visitors. Not three reactions for them to select from.

PHILLIP
I pulled.

The maker puts a thumb beside the split rail.

MAKER
It pulled you too.

One visitor says the frame might still fit an ordinary porter.

All three heads turn toward him together.

PHILLIP
Then buy it.

The man looks at the crack. He does not answer.

David asks what repairing it would take. The maker lays the sound rail beside the damaged one. Repair would preserve the pattern. A different fit would consume the sample.

The third visitor puts on his gloves. He has seen enough today. David lets him go. The two others offer to return with a porter close to David's size.

Their offer remains on the table. It has not become foolish merely because Phillip needs something else.

David looks toward the folded cart note. The sample was meant to make his postponed warehouse work easier to defend. He has incurred a charge to bring it here, and could still leave with a product to test for those customers.

DAVID
Can you find the other shape in this?

The maker marks a possible change with her fingers, not a measured blueprint.

MAKER
I can find out something. Not finish it today.

DAVID
Find it out.

She leaves the saw on the bench.

MAKER
Who pays for the trial?

DAVID
If it works—

MAKER
The afternoon is spent either way.

David asks her price. We do not hear a number. He writes the agreed figure on the back of his invitation. She reads it.

The cart charge is still running. Phillip has made no demand. There is no convenient arrogance for David to refuse.

MAKER
You lose the sample.

DAVID
I can see that.

MAKER
You can still repair it now.

The visitors wait. David folds the cloth out of her way.

DAVID
Cut it.

She marks the wood and adjusts her line before beginning. The sawing is work, not a flourish.

The two visitors say they will come back when there is a standard sample.

DAVID
When there is one.

They leave without an appointment.

Phillip stands near the window. David looks at the emptied places, then at the possible customer who remains.

DAVID
If it works, people should hear where it came from.

Phillip's left head turns toward him. The other two still face the interrupted work.

PHILLIP
Let her finish finding out first.

David picks up the detached piece. It cannot join the rails now. He lays it within the maker's reach.

LATER IN THE SAME TRIAL

The altered frame looks less finished. Binding ends remain long. The crack is visible on the bench. Nothing has been polished into proof of success.

The maker asks for the small sack. David reaches for the larger, changes his grip when she repeats the request.

Phillip lets her adjust the fit. He indicates where the turning stops instead of demonstrating how much discomfort he can conceal. David waits for the maker before placing the load.

Two steps. A turn. Phillip raises a hand before David can speak.

He is listening to the strap.

Another step. Back to the bench.

PHILLIP
Off.

The sack comes away. This time he does not pull at the frame.

DAVID
Better?

PHILLIP
For that.

DAVID
How much more?

PHILLIP
I don't know.

Phillip touches the split rail. David watches the hand.

PHILLIP
That part was mine.

DAVID
The whole thing is mine.

PHILLIP
The pull.

DAVID
I understood.

Neither withdraws his version.

The maker needs the pieces back at her workshop to compare the fit marks. Phillip says to take them. David has not answered.

PHILLIP
Let her take them.

David slides the cloth under the pieces. The room empties of the thing he had meant to sell.

Phillip asks when to return. The maker will send word when there is something different to test. No date is supplied to soothe either man.

At the door, David tries one last reduction of the day.

DAVID
You won by less than the weight of that sack.

Phillip turns.

PHILLIP
You can weigh a mark now?

DAVID
No.

PHILLIP
Then sell the frame.

He leaves. The maker follows with the split rail held apart so it cannot catch the cloth.

David has the invitation with the payment commitment and no saleable sample. The cart must still be dealt with. No payment, product launch or completed repair is shown.

## Non-Jake agency and institutions

Sorel recruits a lamp woman's request, controls access, promises lender priority, asks to be consulted earlier, commissions the pennant and withholds his public presence. Some choices help, some overreach, and some cost him a working partnership. His final refusal is not an author's verdict delivered by an innocent bystander.

The lamp woman changes recognition timing; the lenders withdraw useful objects without threatening violence; Neris interprets circumstances and challenges an impossible direction; Austin chooses access costs, refuses a redemptive exchange with Jake and names a usable custody destination. These people do not exist only to improve Jake. Their outcomes must remain visible even if Jake fails to understand them.

Recurring institutions are supported at the level actually in the text: gatherings depend on borrowed objects and return obligations; access to public speakers is rationed through doors and schedules; contests have markers, signals, boundaries and certified results; championship custody needs people and a place. This does not invent a single formal Commonwealth ministry controlling them all. A room, a tent and a later workshop may expose similar power without being the same institution or building.

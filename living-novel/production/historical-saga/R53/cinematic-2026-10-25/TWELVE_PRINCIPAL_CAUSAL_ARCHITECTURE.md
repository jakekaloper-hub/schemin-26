## Causal spine across eras

The archive begins in 2015; the world does not. The recurring question is how people live with a true result that cannot settle every obligation. Its dramatic answer must change: first a result is preserved, then depended upon, then mistaken for a promise, then lived beyond. This is a thematic construction; no source proves a single institution evolved through those stages.

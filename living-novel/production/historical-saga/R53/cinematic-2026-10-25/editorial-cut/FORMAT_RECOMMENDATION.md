# Primary format recommendation

2026-10-25. NONCANON editorial proposal: **a three-part limited-series development treatment**. This is a format selected for the next reviewable cut, not a commissioned series, production package, episode runtime, distribution commitment or final Commissioner decision. No original source, accepted manuscript or DogTable screenplay is overwritten.

The primary promise is a history of people who share a world without granting one another the right to control its meaning. An object returned, a victory certified or a price paid can close one question while leaving a relationship altered. The audience follows choices, not annual results. No league knowledge is required to understand a denied request, an unfinished map, a working partnership or a public account someone refuses to endorse.

## Why this format

The50-unit principal-convergence reader contains three different scales of story. Its early years support a selected longitudinal protagonist movement;2024–25 supplies a focused partnership and rival's succession;2026 distributes action among several principals whose works and desires do not all intersect. A single feature would either erase much of the history or require a false common plot. A season-per-film scheme would organize by available dates instead of dramatic need. Three parts permit different centers while retaining chronological consequence.

**Part I — What comes back,2015–23.** Can Jake have company without making the people around him debtors, witnesses or an audience? Lending and records give way to the map's circulation, the costs of his interference, a friend's withheld privacy and eventually play with people who want to win. End on a contested game, not certification that he has learned his lesson. Keep the warning's physical cause chain; compress repetitive explanations of good intentions.

**Part II — The place beside him,2024–25.** A useful shared voice becomes an unequal working partnership. Sorel's own overreach matters. Jake's winning order is followed by an account Sorel will not validate; Austin's independent choices earn the title. End with changed custody and an undecided return. Earlier history supplies pressure, not a claim that the rupture was inevitable punishment.

**Part III — What remains in use,2026 through the verified sixth-contest boundary.** Principals compete over access, reputation, material use and time. Preserve only the chains needed to make their selected choices consequential. Parts of their world collide; others remain parallel. End on limited results and active commitments, not a new all-cast institution or fabricated closing victory. This part has a present verified boundary rather than the final end of Commonwealth history.

No equal word count, scene count or principal quota follows. These are three treatment parts, not a claim that each already fills an executable television episode.

## Council methods actually used

Read `living-novel/consultants/author-council/AUTHOR_COUNCIL_MASTER_MANDATE_V2.md`. The following are **simulated literary/cinematic analytical lenses**, not named-human consultation or authenticated runtime Council participation. No writer's prose style is imitated.

- **Choice and consequence:** require a desire, a chosen action and a changed practical or relational condition. A year's result alone is not a scene engine.
- **Accessible entry:** postpone rules and terminology until a person needs them. Explain a public contest through its effect on status and decisions, not a fantasy-football briefing.
- **Setup/payoff and source memory:** retain the warning, bad distribution and delayed label work before the compensation; retain partnership usefulness before refusal; retain material sacrifice before later inconvenience. Repeated imagery alone cannot license memory or shared objects.
- **Inhabited world and viewpoint truth:** other people's work continues outside the principal's attention. Do not fuse unnamed people to simplify casting or let a principal narrate their unprovided motives.
- **Compression and ending pressure:** cut the second explanation of an action already visible. Let a departure, a stopped hand or a next move end a sequence without an added lesson. A selected obligation may stay unresolved if the text does not promise an offscreen solution.

## Editorial rules for this cut

Preserve causal antecedents before attractive payoffs. Remove weekly recap and repeated corrective conversations before removing independent civilian action or pleasure. A role refusal is not the same act as a commercial decision; preserve differences in appetite, temperament and immediate cost. Retain era cards and positive first-entry identity cues where needed. Put source hashes, old unit IDs, production constraints and audit explanations in companion maps, outside the reader-facing body.

Use only existing source events and declared proposed adaptation. Never imply geographic travel, identity, institutional descent or completed repair through a match cut alone. Historical unknown owners remain unknown. Current visual holds do not become evidence of historical anatomy. No new Manning interior/speaking lane is authorized.

Each part needs an actual cut and an old-unit retention/omission map. Independent review must be able to reject the new arrangement even where source modules passed. The resulting cut remains a new artifact; the50-unit reader remains available as the fuller development record. Canon, rights, exact production geography, final format approval and release are separate gates.

## Three dramatic movements, not twelve annual recaps

**I. What a room can keep, 2015–20.** A material loan outlives the result used to interpret it. Keeping a ledger makes burdens visible but can also become a barrier. A refusal does not end another person's need; keeping a broken lamp out of the debt column does not mend it. The institution grows through limits, counterproposals and people acting elsewhere. The map and printer deepen the problem: a record can be made clearer for its purchaser while becoming less usable for its readers. The2020 returned original is progress in recovery, but it buys neither attendance nor confidence. End this movement with the driver's working departure and private account left private, not an institutional founding ceremony.

**II. What company permits, 2021–25.** A partial witness must face how others use his true fragment. Receiving help does not license the helper's next visit or schedule; joining a game means taking actual turns, losing, practicing and sometimes enjoying a choice that yields no useful public image. This gives pleasure and appetite their own place in the saga, rather than making every year a public correction. The2024 Sorel partnership grows from practical collaboration into control over access and voice. In2025, a tactically valid order destroys the old terms of that partnership when Jake tries to describe obedience as choice. Austin wins on his own actions. Custody transfers; Sorel's return remains undecided. This is a genuine ending within the saga.

**III. What remains in use, 2026 through the verified sixth-contest boundary.** Other principals now carry sustained choices rather than appearing as spectators to Jake. Wilson's donated materials leave his own body less room to turn. Kevin's private competitive curiosity costs public framing. Bobby's knowledge can help and intimidate; rescuing Kes leaves her judgment intact. David purchases an inquiry and surrenders first access to its findings; his own warehouse rule later charges him for moving a sample. Zach's altered ground, harmful exhibition and negotiated play prevent skill from becoming entitlement to another person's time. Brandon protects his horse first, then must destroy the enclosure and rely on others to rescue a mule; a later shared laugh cannot restore Vera's cloth. Ben takes the chair performance too far in unit35, where Jordan refuses him; the later mutually permitted performance with Brandon in unit38 offers a different encounter without erasing the earlier harm. Phillip contributes to failure and declines an oversized promise. No single gathering or disaster fuses their separate work into a new organization.

## Interpretive links that must remain interpretive

The earlier ledger, printing work, witness account and later survey show people disputing who may own a record and what use it permits. That is an institutional hypothesis for the film, not a documented genealogy of one record office. The early room's rules may be presented beside later gate and queue choices without asserting that Wilson copied Jake's policy. A false description of consent at the pennant can prepare an audience to notice Bobby's coercion, but Bobby is not said to have heard that speech. No common anonymous worker is invented to carry every moral insight across the decades.

The film can gain historical depth through audience recognition while leaving character knowledge bounded. Every inter-era transition must ask: is the later character remembering an established experience, or is the editor asking the viewer to compare different lives? Do not stage a memory for the former when only the latter is supported.

## Repetition and agency tests

1. **Remove Jake's explanatory line.** If the supporting person's want disappears, the scene needs more specific action rather than another maxim. A woman's own oil measure, a keeper collecting his own tools, a driver's departure and a player finishing a turn should still make sense.
2. **Distinguish the transaction.** Paid waiting, payment for finished labels, an unclaimed loan, work offered without a return promise and a prototype trial fee are not interchangeable instances of generosity.
3. **Protect pleasure.** The2023 game and2026 private play must contain real skill, rivalry, mistakes and enjoyment, not only an observer declaring a moral lesson. Participation can remain valuable even when it improves nobody's public reputation.
4. **Keep the refusal's consequence.** No later victory, new clerk or helpful passerby quietly supplies the oil, repairs the lamp, restores cloth or guarantees the prototype. Where a later source does close an obligation, show that specific closure rather than a generic reconciliation.
5. **Do not promote anonymous figures into one proxy.** Functional descriptions are retained when sources do not provide names. That limits casting certainty, but does not authorize fusing the assistant, caddie, keeper, ferryman or carpenter into a timeless instructor. Their different jobs and interests must survive the edit.
6. **Separate chronology from inventory.** Year cards orient the viewer where elapsed time matters. A score/result montage for every season would turn this back into recaps. Outcomes remain in the evidence record; scenes follow choices whose consequences outlast them.

## Completion standard for this milestone

The chronological assembly must include actual written material in each previously uncovered era, review all new scenes independently, and identify which joins survive source checks. It is not allowed to call thirteen annual headings a complete saga. Era coverage means selected scenes exist and are reviewed, not that every result, principal biography, venue or episode has been filmed or accepted. Exact residual deficits belong in the coverage matrix and checkpoint, not in a hidden future plan.

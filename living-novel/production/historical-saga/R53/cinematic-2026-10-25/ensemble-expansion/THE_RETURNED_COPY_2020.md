## Sequence A — an errand, not an accepted invitation

INT. ROOM WITH THE OLD MAPS — DAY, 2020

The DRIVER puts a folded sheet on the table. Jake recognizes the oversized title before opening it.

DRIVER
She was using it under a parcel. I asked for it.

JAKE
At the crossing?

DRIVER
Wrapping thread.

Jake opens the sheet. The written warning is complete; the ambiguous gap in the drawing remains. He places it beside seven recovered originals.

Eight back. Two unaccounted for.

Jake thanks the driver. Begins another sentence, then stops. A delivery is not acceptance of the invitation he once offered.

JAKE
Would you like a corrected one?

DRIVER
No.

Jake finds a cloth for the dust on the driver's hands. The driver uses it and gives it back.

Jake admits that he repeated the driver's private account while telling another man he had promised not to repeat it. The driver does not give the returned map the power to cancel that breach.

## Sequence B — the guest's next job

INT. JAKE'S ROOM — BEFORE DARK, AFTER THE FINAL2020 RESULT

A small pot, bought for Jake's own use. Its loose lid rests beside a stained folded cloth.

The driver arrives in working clothes. Jake puts beans, bread and water down at once. No guests wait to hear him tell a story.

The driver tastes the beans. Eats another spoonful without comment.

JAKE
There are still two sheets out.

DRIVER
I have no more news.

JAKE
I wasn't asking you to look.

DRIVER
What were you asking?

Jake looks at the bowls. The returned map would have given them something they could call a shared success.

JAKE
Something to talk about.

The driver tears his bread.

DRIVER
Ask about the load.

Jake asks. The material is ordinary and badly packed. The driver wants time before departure to rearrange one long piece among shorter ones. He has come early for that reason.

Jake suggests turning it.

DRIVER
Tried that.

Jake lets the answer stand. The problem remains the driver's work, not an opportunity for Jake to demonstrate that he has become useful.

When the driver stands, beans remain in his bowl.

Jake reaches toward it, then moves his hands away.

JAKE
Go. I've eaten.

The driver puts on his coat. At the door:

DRIVER
Don't tell that one either.

JAKE
The load?

DRIVER
Any of it.

Jake agrees. The driver leaves without waiting for the promise to be repeated.

The favorable final record stays on the shelf. Jake has not shown it to him. He puts away the remaining beans and washes the bowls.

The film ends this visit before showing whether the load is repacked successfully. It gives the driver's departure its practical reason, rather than making his early exit an insult or forgiveness.

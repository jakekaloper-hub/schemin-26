## Simulated adversarial editorial panel — crafts, not endorsements
- **Martin lens / ensemble:** Wilson first then second fall provides a structural hinge; transition to LLC/Mud should follow a shared question about what public victory actually permits. Avoid unrelated four-matchup recaps.
- **Le Guin lens / social ecology:** Survey Line has actual disagreements among carriers, not a monolithic "locals" chorus; bring these disagreements forward earlier and avoid flattening the marsh community into Mud's belongings.
- **Grisham lens / dramatic mechanism:** The Survey Line currently resolves with cautious conditional paperwork. Next draft needs a hard deadline, adverse counterparty or material cost **without inventing a charter, deed, eviction, construction or legal authority**.
- **Tolkien lens / physical memory:** Roads and working crossings exist before the Contest and must retain their own history; don't manufacture metric geography from abstract Atlas nodes.
- **Sanderson lens / limits:** A score can improve an owner's bargaining position but NEVER license government action, territorial ownership, or weather control. Survey mechanisms must remain marked UNKNOWN pending canon governance.
- **Rowling lens / object callbacks:** Maintain the red cloth/crown, Chili parcel vs Sonic dog, TDS hair, Mud's boot, Belt and repair timber across time. Do not force every artifact to return immediately.
- **Abercrombie lens / distinct voice:** Wilson's second loss needs a different emotional texture from first loss; avoid identical tidy "road is older" aphorisms in four consecutive owner scenes. His actual public silence should have interpersonal cost.

# R31 — Historical Novel Story Room: board-controlled competing eras
**State:** exploratory, source-qualified and NONCANON. This is author-room methodology, not authenticated Director attendance.

## Executive interpretation
**2026 is the live data present, not an obligatory Book One.** The 2015–2025 ESPN archive is evidence of competitive outcomes, NOT a complete fictional chronology. An early-period novel cannot simply depict the current 2026 avatars as unchanged in 2016; owner identities, visual states, world eras and historical continuity need independent joins.

## Historical contender 1: 2018 — The tenth of a point
**Evidence:** Master Timeline records Kardiac Kids surviving Schreck Chappell **114.37–114.27**; 2018 champion Kardiac Kids (team slot 8). League called The League of Thrones.
**Story proposition:** The measurement of a narrow survival becomes a public obsession; a principal whose identity is genuinely proven must decide whether to protect a witness to the record at cost to their own reputation. A second claimant understands the same history differently. The narrative tension is how a decisive result can remain socially disputed without changing the numbers.
**Risk:** Team slot 8 cannot be silently mapped into the present Belt Keeper or any 2026 avatar. Until identity resolved, no confident inner POV or dynasty.

## Historical contender 2: 2022 — When strength was insufficient
**Evidence:** Red Leopards **13–1** and third; El Niño **7–7** and champion. The source records El Niño beating Red **116.32–97.96** in a semifinal.
**Story proposition:** Begin before that result, with Red's highly visible dominance and everyday commercial dependencies growing around certainty. At the climax, the *verified* loss overturns the market's expectations. Explore three losses: prestige, money, and a mistaken notion of inevitability.
**Fictional counterpower:** People who predicted strength were wrong; some turn that error into accusations, while others used the false certainty for their own benefit. None of that is provider-proven social history.
**Risk:** El Niño is an elemental singular character whose bounded knowledge/POV remains #129 HOLD. Red Leopards historical human/character continuity needs proof, although the slot/team name is established.

## Historical contender 3: 2019 — The name changes
**Evidence:** League of Thrones name through 2018; Pro Schemin' Football League by 2019. Cause UNKNOWN.
**Story proposition:** A document bearing a new name arrives at an established civic center, but people disagree whether their old duties still bind them. Could have a gripping institution-versus-relationship conflict.
**Risk:** Any claim that a physical Commonwealth constitution, official sovereign kingdom or forced migration arose is invented and must not be presumed history.

## Historical contender 4: 2016 — Early accession
**Evidence:** Team 8 champion; historical final **217.70–206.88** over The Gunslingers after **220.06–195.22** previous playoff. The League of Thrones name attested.
**Story proposition:** An early champion must choose how to steward a title while later reputation has not formed. Household interests predate the title.
**Risk:** Which current fictional character, if any, legitimately owns this 2016 history remains a source question.

## Contemporary countercandidate: 2024–26 reversal
**Evidence:** 2024 ObiWan champion over The King; 2025 The Immortal champion, Obi runner-up; 2026 live source as of W5.
**Story proposition:** The present Belt Keeper and Jake enter the next season with history neither can avoid, while ordinary communities face changes in trust and supply.
**Risk:** Future 2026 matches cannot be authored. Opening must not become a summary of two old finals.

## Adversarial simulated craft round
**Ensemble lens:** 2022 carries a strong known reversal, but a surprise ending is not enough for a whole novel. Build relationships BEFORE the numbers.
**Civic lens:** 2018 may produce a more intimate novel about evidence, disputed trust and livelihoods. However those livelihoods need witnessed source anchors or transparent fiction proposals.
**Suspense lens:** The 2019 renaming would make a good mystery only if the cause can be invented carefully and ratified without contradicting existing history.
**Skeptical editor:** Historical results are a skeleton. We still lack proof of an actual long-form dramatic premise, and prior drafts prove that scores alone cannot sustain the manuscript.

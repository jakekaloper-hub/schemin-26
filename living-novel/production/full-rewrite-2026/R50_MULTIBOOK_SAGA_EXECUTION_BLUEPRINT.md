## I. Mission and literary covenant

Produce a **multi-book epic-fantasy saga** in the inhabited Schemin’ Commonwealth, where the twelve principal characters sustain intertwined lives, where settlements, labor, law, climate, roads, economies and institutions persist before and after any single competition, and where league history from 2015 through the continuing 2026 season constrains the Contest without consuming the novel. Readers receive a definitive shared literary edition and twelve synchronized selectable character routes.

Every material scene has **three layers**: (A) Contest: its verifiable season/week/result/source, if involved; (B) Commonwealth: an institutional, geographical or civilian consequence with plausible mechanics; (C) Lives: a principal's independent want, choice, tradeoff, and another person's resulting loss/change. No checklist-only scene, weekly-recap chapter, omnibus exposition or arbitrary royal claim.

### Creative truth classes — mandatory
1. **SPORT_VERIFIED:** provider/commissioner historical facts, season-local teams and actual results; mutable ingest source dated and checksumed.
2. **CANON_APPROVED:** existing approved fictional world, identity, visual, manuscript and object transactions scoped to exact dates.
3. **FICTION_PROPOSED:** new character psychology, historical legends, institutions, dialogue, civic events, relics, interregional causal bridges; develop boldly, but cannot silently become approved.
4. **UNKNOWN:** historical owner continuity, historical divisions/transaction causes, unsupported ownership deeds, unverified time-specific geography and image bytes. Unknown is not proof and not an excuse to stop unrelated writing.
5. **DISPUTED_IN_WORLD:** a character's rumor or unreliable document, clearly distinguished from narrator truth.

The twelve fantasy principals may be depicted as enduring figures with fictional origins and pre-2026 history **as proposed fantasy canon**. This does not assert that their real human counterparts occupied a corresponding older ESPN slot. Their narrative memories cannot silently certify unverifiable old owner records. No Edrin/Edin institutional POV; supporting inhabitants may speak and act but not become a thirteenth primary narrator. El Niño remains storm-like; its selectable path requires an approved non-anthropomorphic perspective contract.

These are **different drafts**, NOT additive accepted book chapters. Deduplicate, select, deepen and rewrite. Original Prologue–II carry retired Edrin/Oren narration and cannot ship unmodified.

**Recommended book architecture (development, not chapters locked to weeks):**
- **Part I, The Promised Feast:** establish civic debt, competing claims, Byars Belt, Red's public legend, LLC record challenge, and one irreversible harm to a working household. Distinct focal scenes also seed ObiWan, Chili, Slob.
- **Part II, The Price of Proof:** field results change bargains and move real resources; ObiWan's verified records become a political liability; TDS/Wilson identity contest; El Niño's nonhuman force affects differently situated communities.

- **Part V, The Reckoning:** resolve the *book's* central civic obligation and character costs; preserve longer mysteries across series volumes. Do not write a finale based on unknown future 2026 results.

**Success criterion:** a novel-length, continuously readable Book One with a dramatic through-line and measurable author-edited scene quality, not ten thousand lines of synthesis metadata. Exact length/part count remains subject to narrative needs and editorial testing.

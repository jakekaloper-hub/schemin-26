## Production stages and acceptance contracts
### Gate A — Historical corpus verification
Inspect eleven completed seasons, 2015–2025. Produce year/season lineage, title succession, notable reversals, source timestamps, and unresolved historical player-owner attribution. Separate `LEAGUE_TIME`, `FICTIONAL_WORLD_TIME`, `CHARACTER_KNOWLEDGE`, `BOOK_TIME`, `PUBLICATION_ORDER`. Output a **machine-readable historical candidate matrix**, not fabricated novel scenes. PASS only if each factual hook is cited to existing source artifact or newly verified source bytes; unresolved information labeled UNKNOWN.
### Gate B — Era/book competition
Directors independently pitch at least four: 2016 (early title), 2018 (0.10 margin), 2019 (name boundary; social cause UNKNOWN), 2022 (13–1 vs 7–7), plus 2024–2026 frame. Score: deep plot capacity, credible owner lineage, independently compelling desires, civilian stakes, geographic reliability, page-turning mystery, Book One accessibility, multi-volume promise. Authors must argue dissent; no automatic 2026 priority.
### Gate C — Radical narrative foundations
Develop competing story architectures *The Road That Remembers*, *A House with Twelve Doors*, *The Season Before the Storm*; seek grounded personal flaws, sustained civic consequences and actual failure of good plans. Do not force all owners in first 50 pages. Build Book One's controlling question, long-term mystery and 25–40 proposed **story beats** with evidence/fiction markings, not thirty unverified chapters.
### Gate D — Proof of literature
Create 2–3 genuinely different 2,000–4,000-word opening proofs, including **at least one historical Book One** alternative. One 2026 prologue may serve as control baseline. Each must have licensed 12-owner focalizer, clear desire/action/irreversible cost within first ~900 words; source-audited Atlas, character identity and result claims. Do not prematurely select a winner.
### Gate E — Independent comparison
Human reader sample and transparent review protocol (sources shielded from readers), realistic qualified-editor critique when actually available, inspect voice masking and information leakage. No fabricated attendance/reviewer ratings. Record actual evidence only. If these sources are unavailable, keep acceptance status **NOT TESTED** while completing all possible editorial work.
### Gate F — Founder creative-direction packet
Bring one decision-ready concise recommendation based on completed evidence and substantive prose, not a questionnaire. Ask Founder to intervene only for a truly irreducible aesthetic/canon decision. Do not merge existing hard novel or rewrite approved source on provisional authority.

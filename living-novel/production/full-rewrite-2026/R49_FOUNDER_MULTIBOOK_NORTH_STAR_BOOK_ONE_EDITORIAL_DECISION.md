## Editorial selection: the living-era threshold, with historic debt as central mystery
**Book One opens at the 2026 Commonwealth assembly, from legitimate present-day owner viewpoints.** Its dramatic engine is **the cost of public certainty**: who may turn an encounter's result, ceremonial object or historical story into lawful authority—and who bears the debt when rulers, merchants, and citizens confuse those concepts. A contested archival account of the 2022 expectation/debacle becomes a *document read by 2026 owners*, not invented personal flashback. As the contemporary conflict escalates, further history is disclosed by owner-scoped evidence, myth, and competing accounts. The twelve principals cannot be flattened into twelve equal introductions. Their intersecting decisions create irreversible civic costs.

This **replaces R30's tentative chronological-2022 preference only as an editorial work selection**. It does not overwrite the published Prologue–III, PR #128 Chapter IV, or the #130 POV migration. The 2018 and 2022 historically situated openings remain banked for later saga books or revisions. The Founder has not approved exact final manuscript bytes or publication.

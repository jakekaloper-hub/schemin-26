## Single-sentence book promise
In an inhabited Commonwealth older than the Contest, twelve extraordinary rivals pursue reputation, leverage and the championship Belt while every verified outcome creates consequences that travel farther than the encounter itself. A score is a fact; the world’s response to it is a story.

### Audience double-entry
- A reader without ESPN or fantasy-league context should understand the character's immediate desire, opponent, material constraint and cost without receiving a rules lecture.
- An owner/league insider should recognize historically grounded results, visual identity and recurring jokes **as rewards**, not as prerequisites for the plot.
- An interactive reader should choose one of twelve owners and receive a faithful but limited perspective on the **same** chronology and outcome graph, never alternative win/loss branches.

## Proposed opening strategy (competitive test)
1. **Option A1 — ObiWan's forced decision** (recommended experiment). Open at `LOC-TRADE-JEDI-MOUNTAIN-BASE`, `REG-UPPER-VALLEYS`, verified `SETCTX-UPPER-VALLEYS`. Something concrete and bounded (a messenger, trade offer, access question) forces an irreversible *choice of attention or obligation*, not a fictional result. The path's institution cannot be treated as if it sits physically at the Central Basin unless a route is grounded.
2. **Option A2 — Belt Keeper under obligation.** Ground a public claim about past title stewardship in concrete risk; do not imply 2026 Belt changes hands on a Week 1 result. May be stronger if original Prologue's historic objects are otherwise lost.
3. **Option A3 — Chili Outlaw provocation.** More immediate humor and personality, but risks reducing the opening to league-insider comedy. Test only if we can make a civilian/business cost evident before naming other owners.
**Decision test:** Within 500–900 opening words, can a cold reader identify one want, obstacle, risk and changed commitment? A paragraph of attractive map lore does not qualify.

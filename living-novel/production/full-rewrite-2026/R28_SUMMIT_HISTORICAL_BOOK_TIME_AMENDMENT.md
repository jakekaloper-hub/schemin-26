## Three independent axes
1. **Evidence time:** year / matchup / date / finality / source / lineage confidence.
2. **World time:** invented in-world age, location, institution, settlement history, societal consequence and source relationship; **must be independently established**.
3. **Book time:** diegetic sequence and reader reveal order. A book can start in a historically confirmed 2016 or 2018 storyline even though 2026 is live production intake. Flashbacks, temporal compression and ellipsis require knowledge and causal consistency.

Publication sequence is a **fourth editorial axis**—Book One need not begin in the newest year, and first publication does not need to follow chronological book numbering if story cohesion supports another order. Never assign a definitive publication year/volume based solely on fact availability.

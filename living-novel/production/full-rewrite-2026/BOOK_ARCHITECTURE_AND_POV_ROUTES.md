# Full rewrite — twelve-owner book architecture and reader pathways

**Editorial status:** Development candidate; no manuscript canon promotion.

## Structural principle
The twelve are the only available viewpoints. A reader may follow any owner's path through the same verified chronology. Each route experiences different costs, evidence, geography and consequences; no omniscient institutional narrator, Edrin, Edin or Oren.

## Shared chronology and opening manuscript assignments
- **Prologue:** ObiWan — the world seen from a strategist's mountain vantage, before an outcome can be known.
- **Chapter I:** Chili Cheesers and Red Leopards — consequence begins with verified competition; do not invent scores.
- **Chapter II:** Red Leopards and Dr. Duckhook — objects travel farther than certainty, and public meaning changes hands. Do not transpose Week 3 Sonic dog onto a different event.
- **Chapter III:** D0nkey K0ng, ObiWan, His Majesty's Blood — Week 3's verified outcomes create incompatible public interpretations. The hill is not sovereignty; a win is not the recovery of a lost keeper.

Other owner routes are **reserved for complete subsequent manuscript coverage**, not falsely claimed as written. The scene-level validator enforces membership and knowledge constraints, but cannot establish literary quality.

## Reader choice contract
Route selection = one canonical owner ID. At every branching chapter node, reader can see only that owner's authorized observations and learned facts at the given cutoff. No hidden future results, no transfer of private thoughts. Existing inkjs prototype should consume the same route registry rather than creating a second world model.

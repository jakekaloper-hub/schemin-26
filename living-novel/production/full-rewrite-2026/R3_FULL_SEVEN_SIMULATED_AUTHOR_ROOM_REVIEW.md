## Independent simulated literary memos — disagreement preserved
1. **Tolkien — material world integrity.** Argument FOR recomposition: the original Prologue already had a functioning older world (ribbons, stairs, archive labor, repairs) that replacement sketches lose. Risk: new Commonwealth detail becomes a civic gazetteer rather than lived ancient material culture. Demand: everyday behavior already shaped by historical memory, without unsupported newly named cities.
2. **Martin — consequential ensemble.** Argument FOR: 12-owner POV and Week 4–5 causal chains could produce real political cross-pressure (Wilson's public claim, Byars' stewardship, LLC/Mud costs). Risk: balanced page-time quota and six-matchup prose create episodic recap. Demand: deliberately omit events from foreground and let one owner's attempt to solve a problem complicate another owner's choices.
3. **Le Guin — perception and social knowledge.** Argument FOR: presenting the Commonwealth through distinctive owner attention can replace institutional omniscience without losing cultural interior. Objection: 'civilian population' becomes only decoration if citizens never refuse, negotiate, suffer or change choices. Demand: secondary inhabitants retain observed agency but do not become extra narrators; El Niño POV must have a rigorously bounded experience contract.
4. **Grisham — chapter propulsion.** Argument AGAINST indiscriminate expansion: 1,880 words of candidate text are not finished scenes, yet 19,601 archived words include slow exposition and over-narration. Demand: opening problem by the first scene, two meaningful reversals across a full chapter, stakes legible to outsider, end on changed option not another lore abstract.
5. **Rowling — entrance, callback and promise/payoff.** Argument FOR conserving earlier material: returned delivery note, stone board, Belt, bridge, road and Hill are recurring objects readers can learn without a lore glossary. Risk: a map full of proper names before any emotional hook. Demand: recurring meaningful locations become more expensive/emotionally complex each visit.
6. **Sanderson — readable rules and costs.** Objection to ungrounded magic/institutions: victory in Contest is not sovereignty, ordinary rain is not automatically El Niño, a trade/deed/route is not proved by a Memo illustration. Demand: limits on Belt/contest law and routes communicated through conflict rather than rulebook.
7. **Abercrombie — embodied POV and unsentimental revision.** Argument FOR: Wilson's original Hill passage gains meaning from laborers ignoring him and the road remaining useful. Risk: twelve voices all sound like wise poets writing about proof; comic owners may have strong voice but no costs. Demand: distinguish senses, syntax, desire, contradictions, blind spots; cut redundant speeches, retain physical stakes.

## Director counterweights to author critiques
- **Librarian vs novelist:** Documented event cannot be discarded from evidence because it doesn't merit dialogue; conversely, it must not be forced into prose. Preserve result in ledger.
- **Architect vs worldbuilder:** Settlement contexts are confidence-tagged unnamed textures. Narrative beauty does not authorize a new LOC ID, road segment, canon population, or exact travel minutes. Proposed material remains proposed.
- **GM vs Atlas:** Reader should see only spatial information needed to understand a decision; the full schematic belongs to Atlas, not an exposition paragraph.
- **Beat Writer vs Memo producer:** Memo overture/report is a historical witness and tone reference. Copying six summaries into novel chapters violates established causal architecture.
- **Umpire vs Closer:** A simulated board is allowed to recommend a literary direction. It cannot certify an actual independent review, release a manuscript, promote a Founder constitution PR, or declare production operational.

## 4. Competitive candidates (no automatic Chapter IV)
**Candidate A — "The Survey Line" (preferred durable spine; S5).**
- Primary question: *Can the LLC turn earned commercial leverage into a recognized claim without destroying the lives and natural systems that make the swamp valuable?*
- POV case: Mud Dogs knows terrain and everyday community; The LLC understands instruments, contracts and economics. Both have rational and conflicting objectives; their internal perceptions should not overlap.
- Dramatic engine: existing acquisition scarcity → economic proposal → contested boundary/temporary disturbance → verified Week 5 result increases leverage → legal/social cost → decision creates a changed next option.
- Guardrail: No official land deed, bulldozers already finished, permanent data-center campus, forced displacement or sovereign transfer. Those are candidate WORLD EVOLUTION deltas, not permitted facts. No permanent site state until Location/World authority.
- Risk: starts after a Week 4 TDS/Mud loss; the Week 4 loss and Chapter III scar must be represented as state, not erased.

**Candidate B — "The Fall of the Hill" (S5; strongest immediate continuation competitor).**
- Primary question: *What does Wilson do when the public legend of his unbeaten status outlasts the status itself?*
- Canon input: Chapter III standard below summit, refusal of sovereignty, repair workers. W4 loss to ObiWan and W5 loss to Duckhook are verified.
- POV case: Wilson bears greatest new interpretive burden; Duckhook/ObiWan can supply independent contrasting knowledge only when scenes earn it.
- Guardrail: No "coronation", physical dethronement from land, Belt transfer, broken living centaur anatomy, or retrospective prediction of defeat.
- Advantage: directly follows hard Chapter III emotional promise and prevents Week 4 chronology skipping. It may logically deserve earlier page time than Candidate A.

**Candidate C — "The Board Is the World" (S4).**
- Primary question: *What remains of an incumbent's legitimacy and a challenger's restoration after public violence ends on neutral ground?*
- Input: HMB W4 defeat of LLC and W5 defeat of TDS; HMB still has season-ending loss of his R15 keeper Achane from Week 3, while TDS W4 rebuild did not produce Week 5 victory.
- POV case: Austin or Phillip depending on knowledge/cost; no forced winner focalization from Memo.
- Guardrail: Chess stadium temporary; Belt remains its separate form of authority; no permanent neutral-area ruin; TDS three-head temporal canon.

**Provisional ordering, not mandatory chapter order:** prioritize Candidate B for the *first chronological aftermath bridge* from Chapter III, then test whether Candidate A's cross-community material stakes sustain the next full chapter. Candidate C remains a persistent braid. Do not precommit to "Chapter IV = Week 4" or "Chapter V = Week 5"; one causal unit may cross both weeks.

- **Tolkien (world depth):** demand the swamp's labor, maintenance, trade paths and ordinary histories independently of any contestant; no lore dump to meet quota.

- **Rowling (return of objects):** survey stake, lease, championship Belt, returned receipt or broken marker must accrue altered meaning when revisited; no arbitrary new talisman.

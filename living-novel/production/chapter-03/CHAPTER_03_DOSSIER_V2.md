# 1. Identity

**Working title:** THE HILL IS NOT THE KINGDOM

**Chapter job — one sentence:**  
Convert D0nkey K0ng's verified 3-0 record from scoreboard fact into the first durable public claim of Movement II, then force Wilson to distinguish earned victory from unearned sovereignty.

**Dramatic question:**  
What happens when the world begins treating a reputation as an identity before the person carrying it has decided what that identity means?

# 2. Causal spine

**Entering pressure:**  
The Encounter is over. Mire Hill is scarred. Mud Dogs has yielded. Wilson knows he has won, but has not yet learned the final shape of the Week 3 standings.

**Initiating cause:**  
Certification arrives: the other unbeaten path has ended. D0nkey K0ng is alone at 3-0.

**Primary choice/action:**  
Wilson must decide where his standard is placed on neutral ground.

**Turning point:**  
He refuses the summit. The standard is planted below it.

**Closing consequence:**  
Wilson preserves the legal/customary distinction between victory and ownership, but the gesture cannot control public interpretation. Current-form legitimacy begins hardening into reputation.

**Exit pressure:**  
Wilson leaves by the older road while the Hill remains behind him, already accumulating meaning he did not authorize.

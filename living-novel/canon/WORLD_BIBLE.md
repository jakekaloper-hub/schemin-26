# World Bible

Status: SCAFFOLD — world genesis follows historical archaeology.

## Design constraints
- World predates the Twelve.
- Twelve current league characters are consequential inhabitants, not twelve automatically equivalent monarchs.
- Ordinary populations, neutral territories and older institutions must exist.
- Geography must constrain travel, politics, trade and conflict.
- Historical league evidence must exert pressure on the present.
- Fantasy mechanics, if any, require consistent rules and costs.
- The reader must not need fantasy-football knowledge.

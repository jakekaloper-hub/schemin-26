# PRO SCHEMIN' LIVING NOVEL — MASTER TIMELINE

Version: Foundation v1
Status: STEP 2 WORKING CANON
Architecture: dual synchronized timeline

## Timeline law
The timeline has two layers that must never be collapsed.

### Layer A — EVIDENCE TIMELINE
Real, verified Pro Schemin' history. These entries are immutable unless better evidence corrects the record.

### Layer B — WORLD TIMELINE
Fictional secondary-world history. World events may be inspired or pressured by Layer A, but must carry explicit provenance and cannot change Layer A.

A World Timeline entry requires:
- canonical date/era;
- event;
- status;
- evidence anchor(s);
- causal bridge explaining why the fiction follows from pressure rather than mascot logic;
- affected institutions/regions/characters;
- contradiction check.

Until World Genesis establishes calendar, geography and institutions, World Timeline entries remain RESERVED rather than invented.

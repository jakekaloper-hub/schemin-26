# LIVE LOOKS — THEMATIC WRITERS' ROOM BRIEF v2

**Directive:** Clever, humorous, personal, statistically literate, and deeply league-specific. Do not lock a visual theme until the Bullpen has mined current 2026 state plus historical league evidence.

## Correct output architecture
This is not a 13-page character booklet.

### Phase A — Character storyboards
For EACH of the 12 characters, create a private 4–6 page visual-storytelling storyboard. These are development boards, not necessarily pages in the final league PDF. Each board explores:
1. cold-open / character-world establishing shot;
2. historical callback or scar/trophy;
3. 2026 current-state consequence;
4. personal/manager joke or behavioral tell;
5. Week 3 pressure / next threat;
6. optional closing gag / connective Easter egg.

Every frame must tell story visually. Avoid explanatory paragraphs baked into art.

### Phase B — Final ensemble artifact
Synthesize ALL 12 members into ONE FOUR-PAGE PDF. All twelve must live across the four pages; this is an ensemble publication, not one page per team.

Provisional architecture (theme room may improve it):
1. Cover / league-wide premise / visual cold open.
2. Six-character interconnected spread.
3. Six-character interconnected spread.
4. Ensemble payoff / map, convergence, tableau, or punchline that makes the issue feel like one story.

## Theme-selection authority
Lead: Beat Writer.
Co-leads: Commissioner + Agent/CMO.
Evidence: Scout + Analyst.
Personal lore/history: Librarian.
Canon veto: Character authority.
Visual feasibility: Pitching Coach / Art Direction.
Final synthesis: The Closer.

## Research mandate
Mine:
- verified 2026 ESPN state and weekly trajectory;
- prior-season standings/playoffs/championship history;
- historical scoring anomalies, streaks, heartbreaks and blowouts;
- manager tendencies, trades, waiver behavior and recurring decisions;
- Pittsy's Book receipts and running jokes;
- prior matchup lore and recurring rivalries;
- team renames and character continuity;
- old memo jokes that became league canon;
- championship scars, podium history and repeated near-misses.

Do not fabricate personal facts. Humor should come from documented league behavior, commissioner-provided lore, statistics, visual canon and recurring league jokes.

## Historical evidence already identified
The FLA 2025 season audit contains 204 team-week snapshots and 126 detected storylines, including documented season arcs such as the Immortal/Belt Keeper's eight-game arc, Baker Moore Purdy/Arsenal Centaur luck variance, and The LLC's recurring snake-bite narrative. Treat the underlying historical dataset—not the audit's rhetoric—as research material.

## Theme-room requirement
Pitch at least 5 genuinely different issue concepts before selecting one. Each pitch must specify:
- one-sentence premise;
- why THIS league earns that premise;
- humor engine;
- historical/statistical engine;
- personalization engine;
- visual grammar;
- how 12 character worlds connect;
- four-page ensemble structure;
- risks/repetition test;
- how the concept can pay off later in 2026.

No final theme is approved merely because it sounds cinematic. It must survive all twelve characters and reward league members who know the history.

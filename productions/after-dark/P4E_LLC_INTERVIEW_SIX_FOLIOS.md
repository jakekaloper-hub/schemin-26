## Page 03 — THE RECORD ON TRIAL
**Story purpose:** Babb refuses to let historic competitiveness be erased by recent failures.
**Headline:** TEN WINS STILL COUNT
**Display copy (selected direct P4D dialogue):**
HOST: “Fine. Same franchise ID: ten and three in 2018, third place. Nine and four in 2019, fourth. Nine and five in 2021, third. You had serious seasons.”
BABB: “Thank you. Should've led with that.”
HOST: “Didn't win the championship.”
BABB: “Didn't say I did.”
HOST: “Then 2024. Ten and four, second seed, finished seventh. How do you sell that to the shareholders?”
BABB: “You're doing it again. You say ten and four like it's an embarrassment because you don't like what happened after.”
**Fact strip:** 2018 10–3 3rd; 2019 9–4 4th; 2021 9–5 3rd; 2024 10–4 second seed, final seventh.
**Scene:** Guest actively speaking; physical archive *copies*, not historical trophies or borrowed personal papers.

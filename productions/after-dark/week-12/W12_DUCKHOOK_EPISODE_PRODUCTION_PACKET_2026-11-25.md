## D. Full continuous writers' room manuscript
*Every spoken line below is original proposed Commonwealth fiction. One permanent host; no real-owner quotation. Stage is candidate-only. Guest is present and speaks, not merely talked about. Numbered blocks are pacing units, not independent disconnected scenes.*

### A1 — Cold open and introduction
*The host sorts three sheets on the desk; a fourth has slipped underneath. The guest chair is prepared, audience visible. No specific skyline or weather asserted.*
**HOST:** I had a very simple assignment this week. Find out who's winning the league. I looked at the standings and found three teams at seven and four. Then I looked at the corporate register and found one of them had acquired another team’s company. We are getting a little off-topic from fantasy football.
**HOST:** Dr. Duckhook was one and three after Week Four. Seven weeks later, he's seven and four. I had him booked for a recovery interview. Apparently we need to update his job title.
*The Doctor enters through proposed side-stage access, settles into the occupied guest chair. Host turns the placard so the audience can see the franchise name.*
**HOST:** Doctor. Shall I call you Chairman?
**DUCKHOOK:** Doctor will do. The other title comes with meetings.
**HOST:** Good. We've only got the one chair.

### A2 — The round that turned
**HOST:** You lost three straight after opening day. Slob, Chili, Red. How much of that was bad luck?
**DUCKHOOK:** Enough to be annoying. Not enough to excuse it.
**HOST:** Then you put up 184.91 against D0nkey K0ng. Five straight wins, including the Jedi in Week Nine. Was that a new approach or just the old one finally working?
**DUCKHOOK:** I stopped treating a poor round as evidence that every club in the bag was wrong. You still have to pick the right club. You don't need a new bag every Tuesday.
**HOST:** Five wins later I was ready to call you a genius.
**DUCKHOOK:** That's generous.
**HOST:** Then Austin scored 208.76 against you.
**DUCKHOOK:** And you went looking for a different genius.
**HOST:** It's a weekly program. We're not proud of the business model.
**DUCKHOOK:** A good stretch doesn't make a bad decision good. A bad Sunday doesn't make every decision bad either.
**HOST:** Fair. You were 10–4 last season. Calling you an overnight success would erase a rather important year.
**DUCKHOOK:** Now you're looking at the whole card.

### A3 — Two pieces of paper
*The host has two clearly marked theatrical facsimiles, not original confidential documents. The guest is invited to characterize the events; no audience knowledge of the private office is assumed.*
**HOST:** So let's talk Jackson. I understand Babb came to discuss buying your club. What did he walk out owning?
**DUCKHOOK:** The same fantasy team he walked in owning.
**HOST:** And the corporation?
**DUCKHOOK:** Some members of his board had spoken to me. I made a few calls. There was a decision.
**HOST:** The offer to buy Jackson went unsigned. The second page said you were chairman and CEO. That's quite a switch for a meeting advertised as golf.
**DUCKHOOK:** He was free to make an offer. I was free not to accept it.
**HOST:** Right. Most people stop after the second sentence.
**DUCKHOOK:** You asked about the meeting. Not how most people spend their afternoons.
**HOST:** Did beating him 173.01 to 127.60 make you chairman?
**DUCKHOOK:** No. The board action and the score were two different things. Babb still runs his fantasy lineup.
**HOST:** Thank you. The league's complicated enough without the waiver wire reporting to a corporate board.
**DUCKHOOK:** And Jackson hasn't changed hands.
**HOST:** So you got the company, kept the club and still only get one win in the standings.
**DUCKHOOK:** That's how it works.
**HOST:** Tremendous. I finally understand the league's acquisition policy.

### A4 — The people who can still hurt him
**HOST:** Red beat you in Week Four. 164.08 to 128.36. Now he's five and six. Does that make the rematch easier?
**DUCKHOOK:** It makes it more important to him. That's not the same thing.
**HOST:** The model has him at 50.56 percent to qualify if he beats you, 14.70 if he doesn't. You're above 98 in either scenario.
**DUCKHOOK:** That's a pretty good reason for him to show up. I wouldn't plan my week around the second decimal place.
**HOST:** The trouble in your division is that Slob has seven wins too. He's leading the league in points. Chili has spent eleven weeks alternating wins and losses and is somehow holding the seventh seed.
**DUCKHOOK:** Both of them get to play the last three weeks. I don't get to vote on those results.
**HOST:** Chili against Slob this week. By his pattern it's a loss.
**DUCKHOOK:** By your pattern you're about to apologize for a prediction.
**HOST:** That's why we keep the ledger open.

### A5 — Around the Commonwealth
**HOST:** Outside Wings, the Belt Keeper is seven and four after losing his first two. He beat D0nkey K0ng on the bridge, and the centaur's new jester duties are not an ESPN position.
**DUCKHOOK:** Good. The lineup screen has enough columns.
**HOST:** D0nkey still leads Burgers. He gets the Jedi, who started four and one and hasn't won since. Snake is one win out of seventh and has Mud Dogs. The Dogs just ended eight straight losses. I'm not calling anybody an easy opponent this week.
**DUCKHOOK:** You've found the sensible sentence. Put that on the show.
**HOST:** In Pizza, Babb gets His Majesty's Blood. Meanwhile Seven Deadly Chins and El Niño are both six and five. Their game has the biggest combined playoff swing on the board: 67.59 percentage points across the two modeled scenarios.
**DUCKHOOK:** That's the one I'd watch if I didn't have my own.
**HOST:** Chins got turned around in the swamp; El Niño lost to Slob by 4.26. Neither can complain about having a boring November.
**DUCKHOOK:** Neither should count on someone else to fix it.
**HOST:** Fine. I won't tell anyone that the Doctor has bought them a playoff berth.

### A6 — The last hole
**HOST:** You've got the club. You've got the corporation. What haven't you got?
**DUCKHOOK:** The next win.
**HOST:** His Majesty's Blood still holds the Belt. He also beat you two weeks ago. If the two of you meet in December, can you take it?
**DUCKHOOK:** If we both get there, ask me then. Until then, the next opponent is Red.
**HOST:** You're very difficult to get a headline out of.
**DUCKHOOK:** You've got two pieces of paper on that desk and a scoreboard full of numbers. Pick one that's true.
**HOST:** All right. Seven and four. Three games left. No guaranteed championship.
**DUCKHOOK:** That's a headline I can live with.
**HOST:** Doctor, thank you. And if the company sends us an invoice for this chair—
**DUCKHOOK:** Send it to the board.
*The host looks down at the chair, then at his own desk.*
**HOST:** Goodnight. Whatever you do on Thursday, don't mistake a forecast for a receipt.

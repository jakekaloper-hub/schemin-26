## Foundational correction
The episode is a WRITTEN COMEDY SHOW, not a visual effects showcase.
Sequence: evidence spine -> comedic thesis -> full written monologue and segments -> writer and fact checking -> structured episode rundown -> visual beat mapping -> illustration briefs -> character/Atlas/typography QA -> Umpire/release.
Reject any pipeline whose main input is "generate attractive studio images" and then attempts to write captions around them.
Never force every sentence into a literal scene. Visuals should supply a second layer of meaning, reaction, staging, callback or contradiction.

## Per-episode written deliverables before any image render
1. Source ledger: completed week, date, 12 team identities and ownership, official memo version, referenced Novel canon and historical claims with citations and freshness.
2. Editorial thesis: one sentence describing what the episode reveals beyond the Memo.
3. Host voice sheet: consistent dry conversational rhythm, relatable skepticism, self-directed jokes, no Shane Gillis imitation or borrowed identifiable lines.
4. Cold open: a funny fact or sharply observed contradiction, not a generic headline.
5. Monologue: original setup -> escalation -> consequence -> callback; real numbers labeled and checked.
6. Lead feature: historically meaningful argument with evidence, contrasts, at least one competing interpretation and a payoff.
7. Twelve-team coverage map: one meaningful unique written note per franchise; headliner counts as covered, no empty name roll-call.
8. Recurring bit(s): reusable editorial grammar, not obligatory gimmicks.
9. Thursday outlook: confirmed schedule separate from projections and fictional conjecture.
10. Closing: strong original final line and episode-to-episode continuity callback.
11. Script QA: accuracy, rhythm, structure, originality, character consistency, audience legibility, and repetition against Memo.
12. Art-to-copy matrix: per-page purpose, approved text, supporting visual action, setting/weather packet, source references and proof of necessity.

## Proposed layout principle
Default: written page/column first, art helps pacing. Portrait 1080x1920 with safe zones, excellent typography, illustration scale variable according to editorial purpose. Avoid template monotony and text baked into AI images.
A single page may contain monologue excerpt, a small visual punchline and a compact data receipt. A full-page cinematic image is justified only when it adds a genuinely new storytelling beat.

## Scenario taxonomy for the Inklings and Memo Editor
- **Control:** two-to-three-win qualifying floor/guarantee supported by exact rules; use correct conditional phrasing, never “already clinched”.
- **Cut line:** #7 Red vs #8 Chili; show what the *future* W13 direct game could change without depicting either result as real.
- **Vulnerability:** if a questionable starter is inactive or limited, screenplay direction may emphasize risk but must not change ESPN/provider projected score without an actual eligible substitute and updated receipt.
- **Desperation:** Mud's remaining route is an exact mathematical construction despite tiny sampled probabilities; its character can be heroic without a miraculous guaranteed finish.
- **Outside help:** ObiWan, Snake, LLC and Mud have specific dependent outcomes. Rooting interests intended for division titles can also have wildcard downsides; this is a narrative conflict worth explaining.

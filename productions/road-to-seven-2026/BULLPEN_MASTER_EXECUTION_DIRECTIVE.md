## Mission
Deliver a standalone **seven-PNG, 1080×1920 cinematic playoff special** titled **THE ROAD TO SEVEN**. One cover plus six integrated split-screen spreads covering **all twelve teams**, in the official post-Week-10 ESPN seed order, with deep and traceable playoff analysis, heroic character POV, established Atlas geography and approved visual identities. The final product is seven production-grade PNGs; an interactive website or PDF is **not** a prerequisite. No changes to the Week 10 memo or canonical world state.

**Execution command:** `/bullpen sync → research → reconcile → board → challenge → plan → execute → verify → audit → handoff`.

**Operating principle:** Owners lead by domain; the Closer coordinates but cannot impersonate independent review. Work continues through all gates that have evidence; a missing external source triggers a targeted recovery task and source-labeled provisional treatment, not fictional data or invented approval. No paid API spend. Respect the Founder's earlier **no artwork yet** instruction until a documented art gate.

- **Closer / SCK:** owns program execution, cross-domain sequencing, completion report, exception handling and final Founder handoff.
- **Scout / Data Gateway:** locks official Week 10 standings, official seeds, 12 teams, three divisions, remaining schedule, provider evidence and source cutoff.
- **Analyst / CAO:** retrieves original FFHub 18-run scenario packet and briefing, validates league import/settings, records vendor probability changes and assumptions; replays native exact qualification witnesses; never equates vendor 0/100 with exact clinch/elimination. If the original vendor packet cannot be retrieved, independently run fresh website experiments *only with an actual captured receipt*, label them as a different experiment and preserve both datasets separately.
- **Librarian:** source/asset locator, hash manifest, revision and immutable evidence; do not place protected inputs in public Git.
- **Atlas / Character Canon:** verify all twelve `CHAR-*` bindings and `LOC-*` locations; check appearance morphology, visual crops, historical landmarks, world-state and artifact ownership.
- **Inklings / Story Room:** twelve authored POVs, character motivations and emotional postures, each grounded in official record and mathematical prospects; epic/heroic without fabricated certainty or world-state mutation.
- **Creative Director / Memo Editor:** page-level cinematic direction, split-screen choreography, visual hierarchy, dense-but-readable stats, two equal character treatments, copy binding and actual folio render briefs.
- **Architect / Groundskeeper:** production compiler inputs, correct repository isolation, testable scene/page contracts, output naming and CI without modifying existing Memo OS.
- **Umpire:** independent proof/claim/visual-reference audit; PASS/HOLD with exact evidence; never rubber-stamp a planning document.
- **Founder:** final artwork generation and publication authority.

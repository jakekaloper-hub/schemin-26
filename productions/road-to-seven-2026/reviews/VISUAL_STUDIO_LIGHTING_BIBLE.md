## Operating design doctrine

The Schemin project-local Narrative Art Director / Visual Direction Lead OWNS scenes and cinematographic choices, with Atlas Director and Character Librarian identity/location veto, Editorial Designer readability veto, and Prompt/Generation Operator executing only approved inputs. Bullpen Scout/Analyst review football truth; independent Umpire reviews actual output. The 12 named film-production specialists below are **published-method reference lenses** from memo-os/cinematography/PRODUCTION_SPECIALIST_REFERENCE_BENCH_V1.json; none of these real people were contacted, participated, or approved artwork.

Read the installed Creative Production skill source-preservation, styles, shots and exact-content contracts as additive techniques, not new Schemin authority. Source preservation protects identities, costumes, silhouettes and fixed location features. Camera exploration may vary crop, viewpoint and scale without character substitution. Exact numbers and labels belong in a deterministic image-overlay typography layer; never rely on image generation to spell scores, records, team names, probabilities or source dates. Visual creation can own light, atmosphere, character motion and environmental texture.

**Bake lighting into the scene before image generation.** For each character specify motivated in-world LIGHT SOURCE, directional KEY, realistic indirect FILL, RIM/BACKLIGHT, fog/rain/air PARTICIPATION, exposure/white-balance logic, subject-background SEPARATION, dark and bright text-safe regions, and an explicit continuity warning. Avoid artificial multicolored glow on every figure and avoid theatrical spotlights unsupported by the environment. Preserve readable face/silhouette despite mood.

### Global image specification

Canvas 1080 × 1920, portrait. Keep two characters with comparable visual size, each anchored to their own homeland, left #higher seed/right #lower seed. A shared *graphic* horizon or atmospheric split is permitted, but their physical terrains must not magically touch. Editorial hierarchy: top 200 px chapter title; upper/central 1000 px for cinematic action; bottom 450–550 px for limited per-character readable numerical/POV material; keep 48–64 px outer clear margin. A center seam may consume 18–48 px, but each name/record/probability must stay wholly in its own column. ## Lighting and color acceptance test — mandatory on every future PNG

1. Each half clearly identifies an IN-WORLD MOTIVATED KEY source; any practical/environmental lights have physically believable falloff. No unsupported multi-direction light.
2. The two subject silhouettes remain legible in grayscale; eye/face or head anatomy distinguishable without text; exposure within approximately 1 stop of matching subject readability while local worlds retain distinct light mood.
3. Air participation is limited to geography: forest humidity, wetland fog, stone dust or rain as authorized; atmospheric scattering must not erase approved landmark geometry.
4. Lighting highlights approved species, face, costume, artifacts and surface materials, never obscures them. No emissive eye/saber/portal makes all adjacent materials glow without motive.
5. Baked scene lighting and selective restrained finishing color remain separate from typography. No text is embedded in bright specular highlights, extreme deep shadows or dense fog.
6. Actual generated raster must be examined at native 1080×1920 and phone-scale ~390 px width for facial likeness, body morphology, Atlas continuity, exposure, contrast, text spelling, numbers and source-date accuracy. This document does NOT certify a raster.
7. Image model is permitted to depict environmental illumination and character posing but not regenerate exact league numbers in pixels. Final typography must use a deterministic overlay from the selected November 18 source cohort.

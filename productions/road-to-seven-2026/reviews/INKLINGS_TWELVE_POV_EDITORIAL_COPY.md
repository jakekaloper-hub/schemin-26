**Headline:** THE ROAD TO SEVEN

**Subhead:** Twelve Contenders. Seven Berths. One Commonwealth.

**Short deck (34 words):** Above the highlands, through storm and saltwater, across ruined stone and southern marsh, twelve roads pursue seven openings. Four weeks remain. No traveler has crossed. No journey has ended.

**Visual cue:** The words remain subordinate to a sweeping wide Commonwealth montage, not a generic tournament poster.

### RT7-P02 — THE SUMMIT

**Left / #1 Slob on my Dobb — The Weight of the Summit**

Slob plants his boots beside the rough training ground, where the foothill camp has seen confidence become embarrassment before. At six wins he owns the first seed, not the first gate. El Niño waits immediately; three divisional rematches follow. He grips the worn railing, surveys the yard and returns to work. A road that looks easiest from above can still throw a man down.

**Right / #2 El Niño — Thunder Without a Verdict**

Wind bends the rain across the Storm Bowl. El Niño can answer it without letting it dictate the score. Slob stands ahead of him and also directly across the Week 11 field; then come three Pizza rivals, each carrying a history of unfinished encounters. A perfect sky is not required. A perfect ending is not promised. He raises a hand to the rain and chooses the next battle.

**Inklings editorial note:** The two #1/#2 contenders meet now; neither is guaranteed. Slob is physically grounded, Niño is fluid but controlled. Do not tell the reader who wins.

### RT7-P03 — THE PURSUIT

**Left / #3 D0nkey K0ng — The Highland Gatekeeper**

The stronghold hears the scrape of stone beneath D0nkey K0ng's four planted legs. His Majesty's Blood approaches, but the more exact challenge waits beyond: ObiWan in Week 12 and Snake in Week 13. Defeat both and the Burgers crown is his under the retained rules, regardless of the other games. He looks toward those two roads, not the balcony crowd. Control is only useful when exercised.

**Right / #4 His Majesty's Blood — The Belt Is Not the Gate**

Under a hood and beneath the iron of his keep, His Majesty's Blood guards the Belt already earned. The postseason, however, has offered him no such possession. D0nkey K0ng comes first, then all three remaining Pizza opponents. Three more victories would guarantee a berth. He tightens the black armor at his wrist and advances through the keep's cold light. A champion's trinket cannot win tomorrow's encounter.

**Inklings editorial note:** Preserve one gorilla-centaur DK, not a biped; HMB keeps the legitimate Belt, never ObiWan.

### RT7-P04 — THE THRESHOLD

**Left / #5 Dr. Duckhook — The Difficult Approach**

Dr. Duckhook studies the green at the Country Club of Jackson, a quiet stretch that makes error feel almost personal. Six victories have not repaired three earlier losses inside Wings. The LLC waits outside the club; afterward, Red, Slob and Chili return. He sets his stance, checks the line and takes no swing yet. The distance is measurable. The outcome is not.

**Right / #6 Seven Deadly Chins — No Quiet Exit**

The commons has heard Chins coming long before the figure appears. Mud Dogs now stands across the Week 11 road, one defeat away from mathematical elimination. A victory could make Chins the first to close another contender's season; a loss would leave both alive. Chins waves away the warning, then plants both feet on the hard road. Noise travels far. So do consequences.

**Inklings editorial note:** Chins' role as potential eliminator matters more than random destructive slapstick. No invented destruction or actual elimination.

### RT7-P05 — THE SEVENTH GATE

**Left / #7 Red Leopards — Keeper of a Narrow Opening**

Between ruined stones and predator banners, Red Leopards watches the narrow way into the seventh position. Five victories place him just inside the gate, but the threshold belongs to no one yet. Three Dreaded Snake approaches first. Duckhook follows, then Chili arrives for the consequential Week 13 rematch. Red tests the footing of the terrace. A predator survives by guarding the approach, not merely roaring at it.

**Right / #8 The Chili Cheesers — One Step Beyond the Line**

The stable gate swings open onto dusty track. Chili holds the eighth seed and refuses to confuse standing outside with being defeated. ObiWan arrives before the three Wings rematches; Red waits in Week 13, when one result could swing both playoff paths. He settles the black brim above his eyes and steps toward the road. The seventh place is not inherited. It must be taken.

**Inklings editorial note:** The future Red–Chili game has not happened. Depict future tension, not a completed collision. Chili is a human cowboy; Red a large red spotted feline warrior.

### RT7-P06 — THE LONG ROAD

**Left / #9 The LLC — A Contract With Chance**

From the mercantile boardroom, The LLC reads an unfinished ledger, its margins crowded with matches no one can yet control. Duckhook is first; three Pizza rivals follow. Even four victories and an eight-win finish cannot legally guarantee admission in every possible season. The old scars in the room remain, but no new damage is required. He closes the ledger on certainty and opens it on action.

**Right / #10 ObiWan Jacoby — The Mountain Still Has a Path**

At the Trade Jedi mountain base, ObiWan studies a sequence of roads: Chili, D0nkey K0ng, Mud Dogs, Snake. The next four weeks ask almost everything of him. A sweep, plus one additional DK defeat away from their direct rematch, can force a Burgers title. He carries his green blade, not the Belt. The mountain road is steep; for the moment it is still open.

**Inklings editorial note:** Injury uncertainty may affect this matchup but is not an excuse to write Walker/Waddle/Monangai as already ruled out.

### RT7-P07 — AGAINST THE IMPOSSIBLE

**Left / #11 Three Dreaded Snake — Three Heads, One Remaining Road**

At the Waterfall Temple, three heads survey three different turns in the same unsettled distance. The single body must take one path. Red Leopards awaits first; then Mud, DK and ObiWan will each have a say in the Burgers race. Winning out would reach eight victories, still not a mathematical guarantee. Water strikes the old stone behind Snake, steady as the rules. The future depends on decisions not yet made.

**Right / #12 Mud Dogs — Four Steps Across the Marsh**

Mud Dogs moves across raised swamp ground while water gathers in the tracks behind him. Two wins have left no spare step. Lose to Seven Deadly Chins and the race is mathematically over; win, then beat Snake, ObiWan and DK, and a narrow route could remain with outside help. The reeds do not part for him. He pushes through anyway. Hope here is a condition, not a promise.

**Inklings editorial note:** Mud is a grotesque blue/orange swamp-dog monster, not a household dog or human athlete; W11 loss-elimination rests on exact math, never on 0% sampled odds.

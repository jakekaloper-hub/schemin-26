# MANUSCRIPT PRODUCTION MAP v0.1
**Status:** WORKING
**Target:** one complete novel
**Structure:** four acts; convergence-constellation POV

## Production doctrine
Open intimate, not encyclopedic. Teach reciprocity through a concrete duty before explaining the system. The inciting contradiction grows from household livelihood into regional legitimacy. Separate POV streams converge because their lives already depend on the same system, not because plot teleports them together.

## Act I — The World Still Works
Purpose: establish ordinary life, relationships, reciprocal civilization and the first impossible measurement.
- Ch 1: Mara Venn — ferry household, routine survey, ordinary obligations; first measurement anomaly.
- Ch 2: second social stratum — show a beneficiary of an inherited duty whose survival depends on continuity.
- Ch 3: institutional POV — Witness/Sounding culture; anomaly appears administratively plausible, not miraculous.
- Ch 4: source-transformed principal enters through a private objective unrelated to saving civilization.
- Ch 5: contradictory claim becomes concrete: satisfying one valid obligation breaches another.
- Ch 6: local attempted resolution worsens relationship/property stakes.
- Ch 7: first convergence; problem can no longer remain local.

## Act II — The Same Words Mean Different Things
Purpose: competing interpretations become lived coalitions.
- Ch 8–15: claims spread through trade, marriage, refuge, burial, credit and reputation; POVs gain incomplete knowledge; friendship and family pressures cross political positions.
- Midpoint: evidence establishes that the contradiction is not an isolated clerical error. The system can remain lawful only by producing incompatible outcomes.

## Act III — The Cost of Being Right
Purpose: every coherent solution acquires human victims.
- Ch 16–24: reform, literalism, refusal and negotiated exceptions compete; alliances change; an expected stabilizer fails publicly; a seemingly irrational exchange changes leverage; private betrayal and public duty intersect.
- Late-act turn: protagonists discover that proving historical truth cannot itself decide what the living ought to do.

## Act IV — What the Living Choose to Carry
Purpose: costly reconstitution.
- Ch 25–31 working range: relationships and institutions force concrete choices; moral purpose of reciprocity survives only by surrendering some inherited forms. Resolution is enacted, not merely argued.
- Final movement resolves the novel's central dramatic promise while preserving historical/metaphysical ambiguity.

## Working length
Chapter count is elastic; scene causality outranks symmetry. No sequel hook required.

## First-scene contract seed
POV: Mara Venn.
Location: family ferry crossing near a shoreline whose recession has altered approach channels.
Entry state: competent junior surveyor returning to familiar work; family ferry livelihood is strained but ordinary.
Desire: finish an unremarkable measurement assignment accurately and preserve credibility.
Obstacle: physical measurements disagree with accepted registry markers.
Turn: repetition confirms the discrepancy.
Revelation: both the current measurement and inherited marker can be internally valid under different historical definitions.
Exit state: Mara possesses knowledge that is professionally mundane in form but dangerous in consequence.
Forbidden: prophecy; chosen-one framing; magical explanation; lore lecture; league analogue; instant civilization-scale stakes.

PASS for detailed scene architecture.

# IV. MANUSCRIPT COMPLETION PASS

Before designing the final book, reopen all released prose through Week 2.

## A. Measure
Calculate:
- released word count;
- scene count;
- POV distribution;
- chapter lengths;
- dialogue/narration balance;
- number of meaningful relationship beats;
- number of ordinary-life beats;
- number of institutional/worldbuilding exposition blocks;
- unresolved setups.

Word count is diagnostic, never quota.

The author's LOTR opening comparison indicates concern about **narrative mass**, not permission to imitate Tolkien or mechanically reach 10,000 words.

## B. Expansion audit
For every chapter ask:
- What has been summarized that deserves dramatization?
- Does the POV want something smaller and more personal than the novel?
- Have we earned reader attachment before escalating civilization-scale stakes?
- Are family, work, class, appetite, embarrassment, humor, faith, weather, danger, money, memory and ordinary inconvenience present where natural?
- Do secondary characters possess lives beyond representing positions?
- Does Low Vey feel inhabited rather than architected?
- Does each revelation cost somebody something?
- Are we explaining themes after already dramatizing them?
- Are discoveries arriving too efficiently?
- Does each chapter alter a relationship, not merely reveal lore?

Expand where the answer requires scenes.
Cut where prose exists only to prove worldbuilding intelligence.
Never pad toward a benchmark.

## C. Prose audit
Reject and rewrite:
- thesis sentences explaining the scene's meaning;
- repeated aphorisms;
- interchangeable witty voices;
- generic fantasy diction;
- exposition disguised as dialogue;
- lore dumps;
- convenient ancient answers;
- ornamental low magic;
- fake mystery;
- chapter endings that announce significance;
- sentences imitating Tolkien, Martin, Brown or any other author.

Target: original SCHEMIN voice — mythic depth, political and moral complexity, tactile lived world, emotional clarity, humor and beauty without imitation.

## D. Stranger-reader test
The book must work for someone who has never heard of:
- fantasy football;
- the Pro Schemin' league;
- Jake;
- the Master Character Canon;
- the Oracle;
- the Bullpen.

Failure requires revision.

## E. Released manuscript graduation
Do not move to final page production until:
- CRITICAL editorial issues = 0;
- unresolved MAJOR manuscript issues = 0;
- continuity passes;
- POV voices are differentiable in blind excerpts;
- opening scale is earned;
- story is compelling without knowing its source inspiration.

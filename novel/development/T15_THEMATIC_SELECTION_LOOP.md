# NOVEL AI-OS — THEMATIC SELECTION LOOP 15
**Status:** internal comparative test

## Candidate cores
A — Inland Sea: Who owns a world that will not stay where ancestors left it?
B — Roads of Ash: What happens when civilization can no longer trust the systems connecting it?
C — Shattered Concord 2.0: What do the living owe promises they never made?

## Human-spectrum result
A has the widest sensory/emotional palette: love and marriage collide with changing property; friendship with unequal benefit; humor with obsolete harbor customs; shame with dying professions; grief with returning graves; ambition with exposed land; class with owners/occupants/salvagers; faith with interpretations of environmental change.

B has the strongest causal engine: love across distance, friendship through repeated travel, shame around reliability, ambition through standards/information, grief embedded in routes, class conflict across labor/finance/couriers, faith as duty to strangers. Risk: emotional life repeatedly becomes a trust metaphor.

C has the sharpest thematic identity: love inherits obligations; exchange fosterage creates chosen family; humor emerges from archaic duties and mistranslation; shame from inherited honor; ambition from reform; grief keeps the dead socially active; betrayal may be morally principled; forgiveness differs from releasing a debt owed to others. Risk: excessive abstraction/dialogue about obligation.

## Controlled hybrid hypotheses — NOT APPROVED
H1: C thematic constitution + A physical-world pressure.
H2: C thematic constitution + B institutional connective tissue.
H3: A primary + limited B-style connective infrastructure.
H4: B primary + C-style reciprocal oath culture.

## Board response
Beat Writer: H1 is additive: inherited promises become unstable because the physical basis on which they were made is changing.
Architect: H2 is mechanically cleaner, but H1 produces stronger human consequence.
Librarian: H1 naturally supports temporal layers because old promises reference old shorelines while present geography contradicts archival language.
Umpire: Do not merge all three. If H1 advances, Roads supplies lessons, not a third core engine.
Closer: PROVISIONAL LEAD = H1. Not Story Canon. Next run a destructive hybrid test across economy, religion, family, ordinary life, political incentives, mystery load and Oracle elasticity.

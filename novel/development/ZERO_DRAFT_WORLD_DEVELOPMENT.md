# ZERO DRAFT WORLD DEVELOPMENT

**Status:** ACTIVE
**Prose authorization:** NONE

This workspace executes the first assignment in the SCHEMIN Master Novel Initiation Prompt.

## Council questions

### 1. Thematic questions
Develop several serious thematic cores before selecting one.

### 2. Competing world architectures
Develop at least three genuinely different foundational premises. Do not merely reskin the same kingdom map.

For each test:
- core premise
- geography
- political order
- social order
- economy
- belief system
- metaphysics
- historical wound
- present instability
- relationship opportunities
- long-series capacity
- oracle adaptability
- originality risks

### 3. Character transformation
Transform each major league-inspired identity into an original literary character with origin, formation, desire, need, fear, contradiction, gift, flaw, private life, history, reputation, secret, and moral boundary.

### 4. Relationship web
Prioritize relationships over isolated biographies. Identify history that predates page one.

### 5. World beyond the principals
Invent necessary families, institutions, populations, supporting characters, antagonistic forces, ordinary citizens, cultures and foreign powers.

### 6. Deep time
Construct history that characters inherit rather than exposition they recite.

### 7. Narrative architecture
Test POV models, inciting incidents, preexisting conflicts, mysteries and possible convergence patterns.

### 8. Flexible future
Identify what can safely become fixed canon now and what must remain flexible for Oracle Time.

### 9. Adversarial review
Umpire must identify clichés, derivative structures, mascot logic, convenient plotting, shallow motivations and over-literal league translation.

## Phase-gate deliverable
End with the strongest competing architectures and council reasoning. Do not select a final universe merely to create momentum. Do not write prologue or Chapter One.

# ZERO DRAFT — STRESS TEST SCORECARD 02
**Status:** diagnostic, not canon

## Result
The four original architectures have been reduced to a three-architecture active field.

**ADVANCE:** Inland Sea; Roads of Ash.
**REVISION TRACK:** Shattered Concord.
**BENCH:** Second Sky.

## Why no winner yet
Inland Sea and Roads of Ash tie diagnostically but for different reasons:
- Inland Sea produces stronger ecological interdependence, ordinary-life stakes, visual identity and mystery.
- Roads of Ash produces stronger repeated contact, institutional causality, distributed power and Oracle adaptability.
- Shattered Concord remains strongest at formal political relationship history but currently lacks enough distinctive world identity.

## Non-negotiable discoveries
1. All 12 inspirations should not automatically be POV characters.
2. All 12 should not control equivalent territories.
3. Source visual species are not binding literary species.
4. Original characters must be allowed to outrank source-inspired characters in narrative importance.
5. Geography must change incentives, not merely aesthetics.
6. Oracle shocks should alter pressure and relationships more often than they create literal events.
7. Second Sky's verticality/weather ideas remain reusable even though its full architecture is benched.
8. A future hybrid may be viable, but hybridization is prohibited until each surviving architecture passes relational-history simulation independently.

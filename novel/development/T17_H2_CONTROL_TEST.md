Religion: road stewardship and guest passage support diverse faith. PASS.
Family/private life: workable, but more relationships require travel/institutional connection to activate the premise. PASS.
Ordinary life: strong at route communities, weaker away from network. PASS.
Politics: distributed obligations + infrastructure create excellent leverage. STRONG PASS.
Mystery: ancient-builder mystery remains optional. PASS.
Oracle elasticity: best-in-class. STRONG PASS.
Emotional imagery: grief, home, inheritance and changing identity are less immediate than H1; often mediated through institutions.
Reader Simulator: H2 is easier to explain but less haunting.
Umpire: no fatal flaw.

# ZERO DRAFT — CHARACTER TRANSFORMATION LAB 01
**Status:** exploratory; none of these names or roles are canon.

The literary characters below are functions to test, not final adaptations.

| Source DNA | Literary transformation test | Power currency | Internal contradiction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade Jedi | Young negotiator from an upland border culture who became valuable by ending conflicts he did not create | access, bargains, trust | believes every conflict has a tradeable solution; some things cannot be exchanged |

## Important ruling
Do not preserve a source character's species merely because the visual canon does. Literary transformation is allowed to diverge substantially. The novel's characters must become original people/cultures with their own logic.

## Relationship hypotheses to test
These are deliberately structural rather than source-fact claims:
- Negotiator ↔ Veteran Custodian: younger adaptive power versus older legitimacy; potential respect without trust.
- Negotiator ↔ Information Patient: strategic peers whose methods differ on timing.
- Veteran Custodian ↔ Popular Champion: elite legitimacy versus mass legitimacy.
- Debt Magnate ↔ Road/Forge Figure: capital depends upon labor it publicly undervalues.
- Storm Reader ↔ Marsh Navigator: two forms of environmental knowledge, scholarly and experiential.
- Dryland Host ↔ Southern Predator: hospitality pact with unresolved historical debt.
- Frontier Philosopher ↔ Marsh Culture: former campaign created mutual respect and a grievance neither side admits publicly.

These must later be reconciled with verified real interpersonal Source Canon where available. Do not invent real-world friendships/enmities as facts.

# NOVEL AI-OS — T16 DESTRUCTIVE HYBRID TEST
**Candidate:** H1 — reciprocal-obligation civilization under changing Inland-Sea geography
**Status:** NON-CANONICAL

## Core proposition
Civilization around a changing inland sea is held together by old reciprocal obligations: refuge, water passage, grain delivery, medical aid, witness, burial access, navigation support and other duties. Many promises were defined using physical markers—shorelines, islands, river mouths, harbor stones, seasonal channels—that no longer occupy their historical positions.

The crisis is not that magic invalidates law. The world itself exposes ambiguity already embedded in inherited agreements.

## Economy attack
FAIL condition: obligations replace markets and make economics simplistic.
Result: SURVIVES if obligations guarantee minimum access while markets operate above/between them. A polity may owe emergency grain passage but still charge ordinary commerce. Credit markets can value the reliability of reciprocity chains. Environmental change reprices both land and trust.
Umpire: PASS with lock—never make every economic relation an oath.

## Religion attack
FAIL condition: religion exists merely to sanctify contracts.
Result: SURVIVES if multiple traditions disagree: promises bind the soul; promises bind only communities; land/water cannot be owned; witness is sacred; mercy can override wording. Sincere believers can disagree without one faith being secretly correct.
Umpire: PASS.

## Family attack
FAIL condition: every marriage is political.
Result: SURVIVES. Most intimacy remains ordinary. Political consequence emerges selectively when inheritance, residence, refuge or burial rights cross reciprocity chains.
Beat Writer: STRONG PASS.

## Ordinary-life attack
A fisher still repairs nets. A baker still worries about flour. Children still dare each other onto exposed mudflats. Harbor workers mock inland accents. Families argue about careers. People fall in love for stupid reasons. Most citizens do not discuss constitutional theory daily.
Reader Simulator: PASS; this constraint is essential.

## Political-incentive attack
No central ruler can simply rewrite the system because obligations are distributed, witnessed by third parties, economically relied upon and culturally interpreted. Reform creates winners and losers. Literalism, reform, refusal and renegotiation are all plausible political programs.
Architect: STRONG PASS.

## Mystery attack
The world functions if the ancient flood/recession origin is never solved. The present conflict needs no hidden mastermind. Deep history can alter interpretation without being a key that solves modern politics.
Umpire: PASS.

## Oracle elasticity attack
A source-world rise/fall can translate into credibility, bargaining leverage, access to witnesses, marriage prospects, financing, prestige, willingness to honor obligations, or the political cost of refusal. No weekly geography changes are required.
Narrative Strategist: STRONG PASS.

## Derivative attack
Potential resemblance vectors: climate fantasy, drowned-world archaeology, oath-bound medieval politics.
Countermeasure: center the unusual interaction: reciprocal civilization whose inherited agreements encode a moving geography. Neither sea mystery nor feudal succession is the master plot.
Scout/Umpire: PASS FOR DEVELOPMENT; requires later comparative research before publication claims of originality.

## Character-sovereignty attack
No principal needs to embody a factional position. Characters may contradict family, culture and source DNA. Original characters remain viable as POVs.
PASS.

## Failure scenarios generated
1. Recession stops mattering after Book I → prevent by treating change as generational, uneven and economically nonlinear.
2. Obligation system becomes incomprehensible → expose through concrete human duties before abstract law.
3. Every conflict becomes legal → preserve disease, romance, crime, faith, family, ambition, accidents and violence with independent causality.
4. World becomes anti-tradition thesis → give inherited obligations real moral achievements and real beneficiaries.
5. Reformers become automatic heroes → make reform capable of harming vulnerable communities.

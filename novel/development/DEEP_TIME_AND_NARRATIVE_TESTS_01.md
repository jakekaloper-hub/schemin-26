# ZERO DRAFT — DEEP TIME & NARRATIVE TESTS 01
**Status:** exploratory

## Deep-time engines

### The Broken Measurement
Ancient borders were established using a measurement system no modern culture interprets correctly. Present legal claims may therefore rest on centuries of accumulated error.

### The Peace That Erased Its Makers
The foundational peace was credited to famous rulers, while logistical workers, translators, minority cultures or defeated intermediaries were deliberately removed from the official account.

### The Empty Century
Records from a century exist in contradictory calendars. Events thought decades apart may have been simultaneous. This allows mystery without prophecy.

### The Inherited Machine
A physical or institutional system still functions, but no polity understands its complete design. Everyone controls a fragment.

## POV tests
**Constellation POV:** 5–7 rotating major POVs; best for political/relationship breadth.
**Spine + Satellites:** one emotionally central character plus 4–5 rotating perspectives; stronger reader anchor but risks privileging source-owner Jake.
**Convergence POV:** initially separated regional POVs whose stories gradually prove connected; strongest world discovery, slower central momentum.

Council preference for testing: Constellation and Convergence.

## Inciting-incident candidates
1. A mundane infrastructure failure exposes evidence that a foundational historical story is false.
2. A respected neutral intermediary dies or disappears, making several old private promises suddenly actionable.
3. A newly accessible territory/artifact/resource creates incompatible legal claims among people who previously considered themselves allies.
4. A ceremonial political contest produces a legitimate result that the system cannot practically enforce.
5. A messenger arrives with proof that an isolated society believed extinct still exists.

## Mysteries worth withholding
- What actually ended the prior age?
- Which institution knowingly preserves a false chronology?
- Why do two cultures share a ritual neither admits borrowing?
- What private promise binds two apparent rivals?
- Which apparent historical villain prevented something worse?
- Is the central anomaly supernatural, technological, ecological, political—or several at once?

## Original supporting-cast requirements
Before Story Bible v0.1, create at least:
- one historian whose career depends on the accepted lie;
- one ordinary family affected by elite decisions;
- one religious authority with sincere faith rather than cynical villainy;
- one merchant/logistics figure;
- one healer;
- one diplomat/interpreter;
- one younger-generation character who inherits a rivalry they did not choose;
- one foreign observer who sees the principal cultures differently;
- one antagonist whose goals remain comprehensible;
- one character entirely disconnected from league inspiration who can carry a POV if needed.

## Research / source gaps
- verified multi-season league history beyond currently available repo summaries;
- verified owner-to-owner relationship history;
- meaningful league traditions and recurring incidents;
- exact championship/finals chronology;
- source evidence for which rivalries are historical versus recent;
- current 2026 Oracle feed and weekly archive.

These gaps do not block world ideation, but they block claiming that invented relationship hypotheses derive from real league history.

# MANUSCRIPT STANDARD AUDIT 01 — WEEKS 1–3

**Disposition:** REWRITE REQUIRED  
**Draft audited:** catch-up Draft 0  
**Standard:** Author Autonomy Charter + Novel AI-OS editorial passes

## Board finding
Story causality and central dramatic machinery: PASS.
Prose graduation standard: FAIL on MAJOR issues.

## MAJOR findings
1. Theme is repeatedly verbalized after dramatization. Delete explanatory after-sentences where image/action already carries meaning.
2. Mara, Orel, Pell, Tomas and Tavin share too much aphoristic/witty competence. Differentiate syntax, humor, attention and avoidance.
3. Discovery velocity is too high. Give ordinary life, embarrassment, appetite, work, family irritation, class and local custom more page-time.
4. Low Vey reads as an elegant model before it reads as a home. Increase sensory specificity and social mess.
5. Secondary people too often embody positions. Give Bale/refuge household/dockworkers desires unrelated to the thematic dispute.
6. Several chapter endings announce significance. Prefer concrete unresolved action/image.
7. Orel's archive sequence risks converting ambiguity into a thesis document. Enna Tal notation is too perfectly thematic; remove or complicate.
8. Rare magic appears with insufficient consequence/context. Do not spend supernatural rarity merely to decorate an archive scene.
9. Tavin arrives as a highly legible archetype. Give him private friction and specific material stakes before his worldview becomes quotable.
10. Draft uses architecture vocabulary in disguised form: promise, duty, old/new, truth, save recur too densely.

## Preserve
- ninth-stone physical problem;
- Venn ferry/refuge obligation demonstrated through action;
- Bale dispute as two legitimate claims;
- Orel/Caro shared fosterage history;
- grain/logistics propagation into wider world;
- humor where character-specific;
- no villain requirement.

## Revision doctrine
Slow without becoming inert.
Make every POV want something smaller than the novel before forcing the novel upon them.
Let institutions be learned through inconvenience.
Prefer objects, work, gestures, debts and social embarrassment to thematic explanation.
No prose exists to prove the worldbuilding is clever.

## Umpire release criterion
No CRITICAL issues.
No unaccepted MAJOR issues.
Distinct POV voice test passes blind excerpts.
Stranger reader understands immediate stakes without glossary.
At least one ordinary-life desire per major character survives removal of central plot.
No paragraph exists solely to explain the theme.

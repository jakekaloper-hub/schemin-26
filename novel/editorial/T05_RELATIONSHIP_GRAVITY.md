# INTERNAL TEST T05 — RELATIONSHIP GRAVITY
**Question:** If public stakes temporarily disappear, do relationships remain interesting?

## Inland Sea
Mara and her mother disagree about selling the ferry; former classmates conceal resentment over who left the -15 expedition; border spouses discover their ambitions diverged long before the land dispute.
**Result:** PASS. Relationships survive removal of politics.

## Roads
Pera knows private histories because people repeatedly return to her Mile House. Inspector Ilyan resents his celebrated uncle independent of infrastructure crisis. Road-school friendships carry class shame and affection.
**Result:** PASS, but repeated-travel institution does substantial work.

## Concord
Exchange fosterage produces chosen-family bonds, homesickness, cultural code-switching and divided loyalty even absent a legal crisis. Parent/child dispute over inherited obligation is inherently relational.
**Result:** STRONG PASS.

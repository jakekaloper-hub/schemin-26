## Common destabilizer
After one ordinary year, a trusted public record system produces two mutually valid answers to the same consequential claim. No war, murder, prophecy or discovered superweapon initiates the test.

## Inland Sea
Ordinary year centers on ferry schedules, drying fish, grain prices, marriages tied to shoreline property, seasonal navigation training, grave tending, weather rituals, credit, jokes about bad pilots, and parents steering children away from dying harbor trades.

Destabilizer: Shore Registry recognizes both an old submerged title and a newer occupancy title after land re-emerges.

Consequences arise at kitchen-table scale before state scale: whether a family owns its home; whether a dowry exists; where ancestors may be buried; whether a ferry remains viable; whether a marriage alliance was contracted under false acreage.

## Roads of Ash
Ordinary year centers on road crews, inns, relay animals, apprenticeships, toll accounting, roadside funerals, traveling festivals, convoy gossip, maintenance taxes, pilgrimage, courtship across repeated journeys, and parents deciding whether children should take dangerous mobile professions.

Destabilizer: Courier Houses certify two incompatible versions of a maintenance order, each with valid seals.

Consequences: workers do not know which bridge to repair; merchants price uncertainty; a wedding party is stranded; religious custodians accuse couriers of sacrilege; one Mile House keeper remembers the messenger route differently.

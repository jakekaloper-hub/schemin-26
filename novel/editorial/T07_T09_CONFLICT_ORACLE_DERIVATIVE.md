# INTERNAL TEST T07 — ANTAGONISM WITHOUT VILLAINS
**Rule:** no faction may require stupidity, cruelty, secret evil, or conquest to sustain the primary conflict.

## Inland Sea
Old-title families need legal continuity; new occupants need security after generations of labor; ports need navigable commerce; inland communities need water retained upstream; religious custodians need graves protected. Every defensible solution injures someone.
**PASS.**

## Roads
Central standardization would reduce failures but weaken local autonomy; local custodians possess indispensable knowledge but produce inconsistent rules; financiers can fund repairs but convert public dependency into leverage; labor can stop unsafe work but closure harms distant innocents.
**STRONG PASS.**

## Concord
Inherited obligations keep vulnerable communities alive, but descendants never consented to them; freeing one generation from old promises may collapse rights relied upon by another.
**STRONG PASS.**

Umpire: prohibit a hidden mastermind from explaining systemic failures.
Closer: lock this as doctrine.

# T08 — ORACLE SHOCK BATTERY
Identical abstract reversals applied without literal event translation.

1. established power loses status;
2. marginal actor gains status;
3. valuable resource/person changes hands unexpectedly;
4. apparent bad bargain later reveals strategic value;
5. favorite fails publicly;
6. outsider performs beyond expectation;
7. alliance produces mutual benefit;
8. institution misprices risk.

**Inland Sea:** shocks propagate through credit, salvage, property confidence, marriage contracts, port access, expertise and religious interpretation. PASS.
**Roads:** shocks propagate through convoy bonds, passage, courier credibility, maintenance priorities, labor and route substitution. STRONG PASS.
**Concord:** shocks propagate through witness credibility, obligation markets, guest rights, reciprocity and inherited legitimacy. STRONG PASS.

Scout: Oracle inputs must be logged as source pressure separately from fictional output.
Librarian: accepted.

# T09 — DERIVATIVE PRESSURE
Attacks:
- Inland Sea must not become Atlantis/receding-ruin quest.
- Roads must not become Roman-road/post-apocalypse lost-tech fantasy.
- Concord must not become feuding houses/parliament/throne succession.

Counter-design locks:
**Inland Sea:** primary conflicts must remain living-law/ecology; ancient finds cannot routinely solve current disputes.
**Roads:** road culture must be legal/religious/social, not merely engineered; no superior precursor technology rescue.
**Concord:** no central crown, elected supreme ruler, tournament, or house scoreboard; reciprocity is distributed and often mundane.

Umpire verdict: all distinguishable if locks survive drafting. Concord 2.0 has made the largest originality gain.

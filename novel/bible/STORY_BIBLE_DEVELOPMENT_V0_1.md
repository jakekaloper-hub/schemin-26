# SCHEMIN STORY BIBLE — DEVELOPMENT v0.1
**Status:** WORKING / AI-OS approved development state
**Scope:** one complete epic-fantasy novel
**Prose authorization:** NO

## Core world
Around a vast inland sea, generations of societies survive through distributed reciprocal obligations: refuge, passage, food, medical aid, witness, navigation, burial access and other duties. Many obligations are defined by geographic markers. The sea has been receding unevenly. Shorelines, islands, river mouths and harbors no longer correspond cleanly to the inherited language.

No central sovereign can simply rewrite the system. Rights and duties are distributed across cultures and witnessed by third parties.

## Thematic constitution
Primary: **What do the living owe promises they never made when other living people depend upon those promises?**
Physical counterpressure: **What happens when the world against which those promises were written will not remain still?**

The novel must treat tradition as both achievement and burden. Reform may liberate and harm. Loyalty, friendship, ambition, inheritance, memory, class and legitimacy are lived before they are debated.

## Technology / metaphysics
Pre-industrial epic fantasy. No hidden advanced-technology solution.
Low magic: rare, costly/limited, culturally interpreted, never a routine problem-solving mechanism. Metaphysical truth may remain partly unresolved.

## Political economy
Markets coexist with obligation. Emergency or inherited duties establish floors of access; ordinary trade, credit, labor and ownership still operate. Reliability itself has economic value.

## Key institutions
- Witness Houses: certify reciprocal obligations across cultures.
- Guest Right: refuge duties under defined conditions.
- Shore Registry: property/inheritance/burial claims shaped by changing geography.
- Sounding Houses: navigation, weather and survey education.
- Grain Convoys: multinational seasonal logistics.
- Lantern Courts: mobile arbitration.
- Pilots' Wake: funerary/memory fraternity.
No institution is universally trusted or wholly corrupt.

## Deep-time backbone
Highwater Compact → Salt Succession → Drowning Settlement → Chart Schism → Last Great Rise → modern recession.
Historical accounts disagree without requiring one secret mastermind.

## Recent-history backbone
Thirty years of famine cooperation, institutional rebuilding, suppressed survey findings, disease, salvage growth, failed expedition, new corridors, credit crisis, border marriage and unprecedented recession create relationships before page one.

## Character law
No one-to-one source translation required.
Source-derived figures are transformed literary people.
Original characters may outrank them in importance.
No Jake-derived character has privileged protagonist/hero status.
Characters may contradict source DNA.

## Original anchors
Mara Venn — surveyor from ferry family; measurements threaten accepted geography.
Sister Orel — sincere memory/religious custodian whose institution edited records.
Tavin Ro — grain factor who understands unofficial interdependence.

These are working characters and may change.

## Relationship law
Major relationships carry provenance: SOURCE-DERIVED / SOURCE-SUGGESTED / PURE FICTION.
No fictional relationship is represented as a factual owner relationship without evidence.

## Conflict law
Primary conflict must arise from incompatible legitimate needs. No hidden villain/mastermind is required.
Old-title families need continuity; occupants need security; ports need commerce; inland communities need water/resources; religious custodians need burial/memory protection; reformers need consent; vulnerable communities need old obligations honored.

## Oracle law
Verified league events enter as pressure through USE + TRANSFORM. They may alter credibility, leverage, reputation, access, alliances, risk, timing or symbolism. They do not dictate literal events.

## Ending requirement
One complete novel. Central dramatic/thematic promise resolves in this volume. World/history may remain alive and ambiguous.

# Supporting Cast Framework
Status: DEVELOPMENT — roles approved, names not yet canon

The twelve principals need inhabitants around them. Supporting characters provide scale, witness, humor, rumor and emotional consequence without stealing league agency.

## Recurring functions
The Courier: carries formal challenges, strange packages and eventually Chili's deliveries. A recurring courier is preferable to a magical delivery system.
Book Clerks: price and record action at Pittsy's Book; can become a chorus of league sentiment.
Mountain Scouts: bring ObiWan news and make his strategy room part of a living territory.
Fortress Attendants: maintain the Belt Keeper's hall and provide historical memory without omniscience.
LLC Analysts: translate uncertainty into increasingly elaborate corporate language.
Groundskeepers/Caddies: treat Duckhook's impossible country club as ordinary employment.
Stable Hands: ground Chili and Dark Horse in a functioning western settlement.
Roadhouse/Truck-Stop Staff: connect Chins' frontier to travelers.
Merchants and Innkeepers: natural rumor exchanges.
Scribes/Chroniclers: preserve public results without becoming omniscient narrators.
Spectators: show how reputation changes.

## Rules
1. Do not use real private people as supporting characters without user-provided canon.
2. Names, recurring personalities and family relationships require explicit canonization before becoming persistent.
3. Supporting characters may misunderstand events; the novel's factual ledger may not.
4. No supporting character causes a real matchup result.
5. Recurrence should be earned. Start with functions; promote memorable figures after scenes prove useful.

## First recommended recurring figure
Develop one unnamed courier in the Prologue/early chapters. This person can become the physical connective thread among roads, the Book, formal notices and Chili's absurd deliveries. Do not name or lock biography until the first manuscript scene establishes what the story needs.

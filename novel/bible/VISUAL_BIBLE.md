# Visual Bible — Living Novel
Status: WORKING CANON
Authority: Master Character Canon > approved owner reference > memo artwork.

## Principle
Week 1 and Week 2 memo visuals are location, atmosphere, staging and recurring-joke research. They are not permission to preserve an obsolete or drifted character design.

## Core visual language
Epic illustrated fantasy with hand-painted storybook environments, atmospheric depth, motivated seasonal light, foreground/midground/background storytelling, lived-in architecture and recurring geography. Avoid direct imitation of any living artist.

## World continuity
Late summer dominates Prologue/Draft/Week 1. Week 2 carries heat plus first evidence of damage. Week 3 introduces autumn continent-wide: orange leaves, colder dawn, stronger wind, darker water and changing skies.

## Illustration rule
Illustrate moments of irreversible consequence, arrival, discovery or iconic comedy—not every statistic. Exact typography/scores should be composited separately when required rather than trusted to generated art.

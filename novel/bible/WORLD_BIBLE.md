# Schemin' '26 — World Bible
Status: WORKING CANON / pre-manuscript
Leads: The Librarian (continuity/history), Creative Director/Beat Writer (story), Scout (league truth), Closer (cross-domain synthesis), Umpire (release gate).

## Foundational idea
The Pro Schemin' realm is one connected continent organized by an old competitive compact. The twelve principals are not superheroes in isolated theme parks; they are powerful figures whose territories, institutions and reputations intersect. The real league remains the hidden engine. Characters experience consequences, not spreadsheets.

The world should feel older than 2026. League history beginning in 2015 supplies recoverable history; unsupported ancient detail remains legend or blank space until researched.

## The Compact
Working world mechanism: the twelve seats are bound by a recurring seasonal Compact. The Compact determines sanctioned contests, divisional obligations, the calendar and the eventual right to claim the Belt. This is a fictional translation layer, not a claim about historical league lore.

A weekly matchup is a sanctioned collision between two seats. Its form may differ by territory: battle, hunt, siege, race, storm, economic assault, tournament or ordeal. The verified score determines the victor; prose determines how the consequence is experienced.

Victory does not require conquest or death. Defeat leaves reputation, damaged places, debts, artifacts and altered relationships. The principals persist to fight again.

## The Belt
The Belt is the visible symbol of reigning championship authority. It is not transferred after ordinary weekly victories. The reigning champion may lose a weekly contest and retain it. This distinction is essential to the ObiWan/Byars Week 1 scene.

## Receipts
Receipts are documentary proof of consequence. They can be literal paper in the story world and a recurring visual grammar. A receipt records what happened; it does not predict what happens next.

## Three great circuits
The real Burgers, Wings and Pizza divisions become three competitive circuits/regions recognized by the Compact. They create repeated geographic and political proximity during divisional weeks without forcing every member of a division to share one biome.

Exact cultural histories remain open pending historical research.

## Connected geography
The map uses travel corridors rather than teleportation.

NORTH / HIGH COUNTRY:
- Trade Jedi's North Georgia mountain territory: wooded ridges, strategy cabin, watch roads.
- Arsenal Centaur's high country: harder ridges, timber, training grounds, mountain passes.
A mountain road can plausibly connect them while preserving distinct domains.

LOW COUNTRY / SWAMP:
- Mud Dogs territory: black water, cypress, chains, flooded roads.
- Duckhook's country-club wetlands: manicured greens cut improbably into dangerous water country.
The same watershed can connect menace and absurd refinement.

WESTERN ROAD:
- Chili Outlaw territory: dry roads, stable country, smoke, roadside food culture, Dark Horse.
- Seven Deadly Chins frontier: truck stops, grills, freight roads, working settlements.
This corridor naturally supports couriers and traveling wagers.

CENTRAL METROPOLIS:
- The LLC: finance, contracts, towers, markets, transit.
A neutral exchange district may eventually become the primary trade crossroads, but this is not yet locked.

OLD FORTRESS COUNTRY:
- Belt Keeper's dark seat: ancient stone, trophy hall, high walls, inherited-looking authority.
Its age should visually exceed the current champion, suggesting the office is older than its holder.

JUNGLE BELT:
- Red Leopards: ruined stone, dense canopy, hunting paths.
- TDS: serpent jungle, waterfalls, hidden approaches; Pittsy's Book can maintain a principal counter here while runners operate continent-wide.
These are neighboring ecological zones, not the same territory.

COAST / STORM APPROACH:
- El Niño: sea, flooded stadiums, storm fronts, river mouths.
His weather motif can move inland before he does.

SLOB'S MOVING FRONTIER:
- Slob's culture is partly mobile: tailgates, camps, convoys and temporary strongholds. This lets his world invade supposedly refined spaces without requiring teleportation.

## Travel
Travel has duration. Chapters may compress uneventful travel but should respect geography. Roads, waterways, mountain passes, courier routes and freight corridors create opportunities for supporting characters and rumor.

No character may appear impossibly far away in consecutive scenes without a narrative device.

## Communication
Information moves through:
- Pittsy's Book runners and posted boards
- couriers
- Compact messengers
- taverns/truck stops/markets
- LLC commercial networks
- travelers and spectators
- weather signs
- formal challenge notices
- artifacts carried home from contests

Information can arrive late or distorted. Verified facts remain correct in narration; characters may possess incomplete interpretations.

## Draft, keepers and capital
Draft capital is represented as Compact-sanctioned claims: transferable rights to future selection. Keepers are protected retainers/champions bound to a seat across seasons under the league's actual keeper rules. A trade transfers rights, service or future claims by witnessed contract.

The fiction must not create mechanics that contradict the real league rules.

## Waivers and FAAB
The waiver market is a post-battle talent market administered under Compact rules. FAAB becomes finite bidding coin/credit assigned for acquisition rights. Exact bids remain factual and sourced.

## Pittsy's Book
The Book is both institution and place. Its central counter belongs to TDS/Pitts' narrative orbit, while clerks/runners allow its odds, slips and gossip to travel. It may connect unrelated scenes. Never invent actual wagers, bettor identities or P/L.

## Elimination and death
League elimination is not literal character death. It is removal from championship contention and loss of access to the final road. Physical peril can be dramatic, but principal characters remain available to the living world unless canon explicitly changes.

## Ordinary life
The world contains people beyond the twelve principals. Required recurring functions: couriers, Book clerks, stable hands, groundskeepers/caddies, LLC analysts, fortress attendants, mountain scouts, merchants, innkeepers, cooks, scribes and spectators.

Supporting cast should make consequences observable. They do not decide league outcomes.

## Seasonal time
Weather binds the continent. Week 1 carries late-summer confidence. Week 2 supplies evidence. Week 3's first cold front is a continent-wide seasonal event and narrative bridge. Weather may symbolize pressure but cannot predict results.

## Unknowns deliberately preserved
- exact continental map and distances
- origin mythology of the Compact
- complete championship lineage
- historical relationship of the Belt to older champions
- exact cultures of the three divisions
- names of supporting characters
- neutral capital/exchange status
These remain research/design questions rather than invented canon.

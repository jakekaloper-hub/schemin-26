# Schemin' '26 Living Novel — Story Constitution

Status: CANONICAL PRODUCTION GOVERNANCE
Current story position: Entering Chapter III / Week 3
Manuscript policy: Working material lives in Markdown. Only approved prose graduates to the master DOCX.

## Core premise

Twelve established characters enter the 2026 season believing they understand who they are. The season determines which identities survive.

The authors know the terminal condition — one league champion — but never the identity of that champion until reality decides it. No chapter may write backward from an assumed ending.

## Source hierarchy

1. Verified ESPN league state and commissioner schedule.
2. Official published weekly memo for the completed week.
3. Master owner-character canon and approved visual references.
4. Verified league history and archived publications.
5. Commissioner-supplied personal league lore.
6. Fictional dramatization.

Dramatization may interpret facts but may never replace or contradict them.

## Narrative translation

Fantasy football is the hidden physics of the world, not constant exposition.

Scores and roster events may become battles, sieges, hunts, storms, escapes, political reversals, trade missions, wagers, territorial changes, or other in-world events. The underlying factual result must remain recoverable in the evidence ledger.

## Chapter engine

Every weekly chapter uses:

PRE -> DURING -> POST -> CONSEQUENCE -> HANDOFF

PRE establishes beliefs, unresolved debts, geography, relationships, artifacts and stakes entering the week.

DURING is written only after real outcomes exist. It dramatizes the week rather than reciting six matchup summaries.

POST is anchored to that week's official memo and verified league state.

CONSEQUENCE records what physically, politically, relationally and symbolically changed.

HANDOFF carries unresolved narrative debt into the next week.

## Living-story rule

Canon persists. History persists. Consequences persist. Interpretation may evolve.

Never manufacture foreshadowing. Plant truthful details and allow later events to make them meaningful.

No character is guaranteed equal page count. Narrative gravity follows the week's story.

## Character law

Owner -> canonical character -> current team name.

A team rename never redesigns an owner. Character identity, species/body form, signature wardrobe, props and established visual language remain controlled by the master character canon.

## World law

The twelve territories are regions of one connected fantasy world, not twelve unrelated illustration sets. Travel, messages, weather, trade, Pittsy's Book, artifacts, rumors and recurring characters connect them.

World mechanics for the Belt, receipts, draft capital, keepers, waivers, trades, divisions and elimination must be defined before the manuscript depends heavily on them.

## Continuity ledgers

Maintain:
- FACT_LEDGER.md
- TIMELINE.md
- RELATIONSHIP_MAP.md
- OBJECT_LEDGER.md
- ARC_BRANCHES.md
- ILLUSTRATION_LEDGER.md
- OPEN_THREADS.md

Each recurring object records first appearance, holder/location, physical condition, current meaning and unresolved possibilities.

## Visual law

Official Week 1 and Week 2 memo art is environmental and cinematic reference material, not automatic character canon. Where an older illustration conflicts with the Master Character Canon, the Master Character Canon wins.

Illustrations support prose at deliberate story beats. They are not substitutes for scenes.

## Manuscript gate

Research -> Story Room -> Outline -> First Draft -> Continuity Review -> Fact Check -> Line Edit -> Visual Review -> User Approval -> Master DOCX.

Only user-approved prose is copied into SCHEMIN_26_MASTER_NOVEL.docx.

# World Mechanics — Translation Contract
Status: WORKING CANON

## Prime rule
Reality decides outcomes. Fiction decides expression.

### Real league -> in-world expression
Weekly matchup -> sanctioned contest under the Compact.
Final score -> authoritative measure of victory/margin.
Projection -> expectation, bookmaker line, scout forecast or public model; never prophecy.
Starter performance -> deployed force/action.
Bench performance -> unused reserve/opportunity; never retroactively deployed.
Injury -> availability constraint translated carefully; do not fictionalize real medical details beyond sourced facts.
Trade -> witnessed exchange of service/rights/future claims.
Draft pick -> transferable future selection right.
Keeper -> protected continuing retainer.
Waiver claim -> acquisition petition after talent enters the market.
FAAB -> finite acquisition credit/coin.
Division -> Compact circuit with recurring obligations.
Playoffs -> narrowing championship road.
Championship -> final sanctioned contest for the season's Belt.
Elimination -> barred from championship road, not death.
Power ranking -> chronicler/model opinion, not cosmic truth.
Pittsy's line -> Book price, not prediction of fate.

## Score visibility
Scores may appear explicitly on receipts, ledgers, scoreboards, carved notices or chapter-end chronicles. Prose scenes do not need constant numerical narration. Important exact numbers remain available to preserve the hidden engine.

## Consequence scale
Close win: escape, narrow breach, disputed-feeling survival without disputing the score.
Large win: rout, overwhelming storm, broken defense, hostile takeover, hunt completed, etc.
High-scoring loss: formidable action defeated by stronger action; never portrayed as incompetence merely because it lost.
Low-scoring win: survival/position/timing; prose may reflect outside skepticism but cannot declare it illegitimate.
Bench mistake: reserves withheld or wrong force deployed. Do not rewrite the roster after the fact.

## Continuity
Damage can persist visually but must not prevent future participation. Objects age. Environments remember. Jokes may become customs. Results never reset character canon.

## Prohibited shortcuts
- no literal fantasy-football app dialogue as default exposition
- no teleporting between realms
- no death for ordinary elimination
- no changing character species because of team rename
- no fabricated bets, scores, transactions or history
- no future-result foreshadowing presented as destiny

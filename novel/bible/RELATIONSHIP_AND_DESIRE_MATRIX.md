# Relationship & Desire Matrix — 2026 Opening State

Status: WORKING NOVEL BIBLE
Temporal lock: Opening of 2026, before Week 1
Rule: This document establishes starting tensions, not predetermined arcs.

## Why this exists

The novel needs people who want things from one another before it needs more plot. Every weekly result will pressure these starting beliefs and relationships. Future results may strengthen, reverse, complicate, or make them irrelevant.

A relationship is only marked as historical fact when supported by league records or commissioner lore. Otherwise it is an in-world dramatic relationship derived from documented competitive circumstances.

## Twelve starting desires

### Jake Kaloper — The Trade Jedi / ObiWan Jacoby
Wants: convert the 2025 runner-up finish into a championship without abandoning the strategic identity that brought him there.
Belief: preparation, asset control and timing can overcome raw power.
Scar: the 2025 championship loss to Byars.
Pressure point: strategy becomes dangerous when it mistakes control for certainty.
Primary opening gravity: Belt Keeper, then the league's trade economy.
Persistent symbols: green blade, retriever, maps, receipts.

### Austin Byars — The Belt Keeper
Wants: prove the 2025 title is authority rather than a moment.
Belief: the champion should be treated as the standard until someone takes the Belt.
Scar: years of playoff disappointment precede the 2025 breakthrough.
Pressure point: history can become armor or weight.
Primary opening gravity: ObiWan and every challenger measuring himself against the champion.
Persistent symbols: Belt, black fortress, rune sword, championship hall.

### Wilson Look — The Arsenal Centaur / D0nkey K0ng
Wants: make winning feel repeatable rather than accidental.
Belief: preparation and force can silence old arguments about luck.
Pressure point: every early victory increases the demand for proof.
Primary opening gravity: opponents who challenge whether the new identity represents real change.
Persistent symbols: axe, high-country woodpile, Arsenal-red war gear.

### Jordan Hollingshead — Slob on my Dobb / Frat-Bro Berserker
Wants: turn enormous individual firepower into victories that cannot be ignored.
Belief: enough force eventually overwhelms structure.
Pressure point: spectacle without the result is only noise.
Primary opening gravity: whoever survives his biggest performances.
Persistent symbols: battered armor, bat, tailgate debris, pit bull.

### Bobby Mitchell — Mud Dogs / Swamp-Born Menace
Wants: own territory, regardless of whether outsiders consider the wins pretty.
Belief: survival is evidence.
Pressure point: close escapes invite the league to call success luck.
Primary opening gravity: anyone entering the swamp; early 2026 results will determine whether this becomes menace or myth.
Persistent symbols: chain, mud, territorial markers, flooded paths.

### Phillip Pitts — Three Dreaded Snake / The Podium Shadow
Wants: prove the 2025 podium was not an anomaly while maintaining the institution of Pittsy's Book.
Belief: being underestimated creates leverage.
Pressure point: the Book connects him to conflicts far beyond his own matchup.
Primary opening gravity: the entire league through wagers; direct opponents through football.
Persistent symbols: dreadlocks, serpent imagery, slips, ledgers, Book counter.

### Ben Whipple — Seven Deadly Chins / Blue-Collar Spoiler
Wants: ruin cleaner, more elegant plans with blunt results.
Belief: the scoreboard does not care how sophisticated the opponent looked beforehand.
Pressure point: consequential decisions can undercut the identity of simplicity.
Primary opening gravity: favored opponents and anyone who mistakes him for comic relief.
Persistent symbols: hammer, trucker cap, 40-ounce bottle, raccoon.

### Kevin Zeek — Red Leopards / The Predator Board
Wants: convert patience and keeper leverage into decisive kills.
Belief: weakness eventually exposes itself.
Pressure point: a predator can wait too long or strike at the wrong opening.
Primary opening gravity: prey that escapes and opponents who publicly provoke him.
Persistent symbols: claws, jungle ruins, amber eyes, hunt marks.

### Manning Welty — El Niño / The Weather System
Wants: remain impossible to contain or forecast.
Belief: environments bend before force larger than planning.
Pressure point: chaos can damage its creator as easily as its target.
Primary opening gravity: the world itself; weather allows his presence to touch scenes before he arrives.
Persistent symbols: water, lightning, flooded stadiums, pressure changes.

### David Babb — The LLC / Hostile Takeover
Wants: make the league behave like a solvable market.
Belief: sufficient information, acquisition and process should create control.
Pressure point: human competition repeatedly produces irrational outcomes.
Primary opening gravity: trades, assets, underperformance and anyone refusing to behave like a rational counterparty.
Persistent symbols: boardroom, briefcase, market screens, contracts.

### Zach Wilson — Dr. Duckhook / King of the Impossible Lie
Wants: survive positions other characters would consider unrecoverable.
Belief: there is always another shot.
Pressure point: repeated escapes can become faith in luck rather than skill.
Primary opening gravity: country-club invaders and the developing Chili correspondence.
Persistent symbols: cigar, club/prosthetic concept, water hazards, scorecards.

### Brandon Pryor — Chili Cheesers / The Chili Outlaw
Wants: make his presence impossible to ignore.
Belief: pressure, spectacle and provocation can seize the initiative.
Pressure point: heat is difficult to sustain.
Primary opening gravity: whoever receives the next provocation; Dark Horse is his constant companion.
Persistent symbols: chili-red duster, pepper bandolier, steaming pot, Sonic deliveries, Dark Horse.

**Trade Jedi ↔ LLC**
Natural dramatic intersection: both approach assets strategically, but one operates like a field strategist and the other like a corporate raider.
Status: relationship texture to be developed from documented trade history before historical claims are added.

**LLC ↔ league**
The LLC gives trades and draft capital a natural institutional doorway into the story.
Status: fictional world function; specific historical dealings require evidence.

# Author-room rule: relationships evolve after every chapter

After each completed week, update each affected pairing with:

1. What objectively happened?
2. What did one character do to the other's world?
3. What physical artifact or memory survives?
4. Does the relationship intensify, reverse, soften, or remain unresolved?
5. What does each character now believe about the other?
6. Which belief is fact-supported versus fictional interpretation?
7. What future event could test that belief?

No update may predetermine the next result.

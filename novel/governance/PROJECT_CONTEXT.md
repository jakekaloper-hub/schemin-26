# SCHEMIN NOVEL — Project Context Pack

## Project
SCHEMIN Novel, inside Schemin '26.

## Objective
Develop a publishable original fantasy novel/saga inspired indirectly by the Pro Schemin' league.

## Authorial model
The league is an oracle beneath the story, not the story. Real results, rivalries, relationships, reversals and future weekly events provide narrative pressure. The fiction transforms that pressure into original consequences.

## Canon hierarchy
1. Published/approved Story Canon
2. Approved Story Bible
3. Approved Zero Draft decisions
4. Verified Source Canon
5. Exploratory notes
6. Prior abandoned prose (non-canonical)

## Phase
Zero Draft World Development.

## Hard constraints
- Clean-room restart.
- Do not automatically preserve prior plot/prose.
- No one-to-one fantasy-football translation.
- No guaranteed protagonist, hero, villain, ruler, survivor or champion based on league identity.
- World must extend far beyond league-inspired characters.
- Future league outcomes must not retcon established Story Canon.
- Not every oracle event deserves narrative influence.
- Avoid imitation of distinctive expression from benchmark fantasy works.
- Preserve meaningful Bullpen dissent.
- No manuscript prose until phase gate approval.

## Required Zero Draft outputs
Themes; multiple foundational premises; geography; politics; metaphysics including low/no magic; transformed principal characters; origins/cultures; relationship web; original supporting cast; POV options; pre-page-one conflicts; deep history; mysteries; inciting-incident options; flexible futures; rejected derivative concepts; research gaps.

## Source systems
Schemin '26 repository canon and verified league data may inform Source Canon. Source claims must remain distinguishable from fictional invention.

## Governance
Beat Writer leads creative development. Librarian maintains canon and provenance. Scout verifies source material. Architect checks systems coherence. Umpire challenges weak/derivative/convenient choices. Closer synthesizes and controls phase advancement.

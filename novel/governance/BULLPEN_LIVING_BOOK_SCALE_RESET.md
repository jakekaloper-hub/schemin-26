## Board question
How should Schemin's Novel AI-OS restructure production so:
1. one canonical living book is always the place Jake reads;
2. it is updated in place after every approved weekly release;
3. the book contains manuscript + illustrations as one designed artifact;
4. Weeks 1–2 are expanded to the amount of story actually required by the novel rather than the amount we happened to generate;
5. Week 3 remains development-only until its proper release gate;
6. weekly cadence does not force thin chapters or artificially fixed word counts;
7. the opening has epic-fantasy depth while remaining original and governed by SCHEMIN's own causality.

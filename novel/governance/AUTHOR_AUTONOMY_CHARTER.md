# SCHEMIN NOVEL AI-OS — AUTHOR AUTONOMY CHARTER
**Approved by:** Jake
**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Status:** AUTHOR-APPROVED GOVERNANCE
**Scope:** Novel AI-OS autonomous development authority

## 1. Architecture
APPROVED: The OS may provisionally adopt and develop the current leading architecture: a changing Inland Sea physical world combined with a civilization structured by inherited reciprocal obligations.

This is an approved development foundation. The OS may improve or challenge it through testing. A later replacement of the foundational architecture requires an author gate.

## 2. Creative freedom
VERY HIGH.

Bullpen may autonomously invent, test, revise and reject:
- cultures
- peoples
- religions and belief systems
- families
- political structures
- geography
- economies
- institutions
- historical events
- wars and disasters
- secondary and tertiary characters
- relationships
- romance
- betrayal
- rivalries
- deaths
- mysteries
- customs
- languages/naming systems
- ordinary civilian life

Routine creative development does not require author interruption.

## 3. Source-character transformation
APPROVED.

The twelve real inspirations are creative/source DNA, not mandatory one-to-one fictional counterparts. The OS may alter occupation, class, culture, morality, narrative importance, relationships, appearance, role, species where appropriate, and Book-I presence.

Provenance remains tracked.

## 4. Jake / Trade-Jedi source inspiration
NO PRIVILEGED PROTAGONIST STATUS.

The Jake-derived inspiration receives no guaranteed:
- protagonist status
- hero status
- moral correctness
- survival
- victory
- POV allocation
- narrative centrality

The character must earn literary importance under the same standards as every other character.

## 5. Tone / darkness
TARGET: LOTR / Game of Thrones hybrid at the level of broad creative ambition, NOT prose/style imitation.

Interpretation:
- mythic depth, old history, cultural weight, fellowship, wonder and consequential moral choices;
- political complexity, conflicting legitimate interests, betrayal, war, mortality, class and morally complicated people;
- darkness must have dramatic purpose;
- avoid nihilism for its own sake;
- hope, loyalty, friendship, humor, beauty and courage remain real forces;
- no copying distinctive characters, plots, terminology, settings or prose from either source.

## 6. Technology and magic
TECHNOLOGY: No modern/industrial/advanced technological framework. The world is pre-technological epic fantasy in feel. Ancient mysteries must not resolve into a hidden advanced-tech civilization.

MAGIC: LOW MAGIC.

Default principles:
- rare enough that ordinary institutions and human decisions remain consequential;
- culturally interpreted rather than universally understood;
- costs/limits required;
- magic cannot become a convenient plot-resolution machine;
- uncertainty about metaphysical truth is allowed;
- supernatural events should gain power from rarity.

## 7. Living Oracle
APPROVED MODE: USE & TRANSFORM.

The OS may autonomously evaluate verified league events and use or transform their dramatic pressure.

The user's "no" is interpreted as: do not make the default workflow merely HOLD/REJECT source events when a meaningful transformed use is available, while still preserving the Reality Firewall and refusing literal or harmful translations that violate Story Canon.

League events never automatically become fictional events. Translation must preserve independent causality.

## 8. Fictional character sovereignty
FULLY APPROVED.

Fictional characters may:
- make choices unlike their source inspirations;
- fail;
- betray;
- change allegiance;
- love;
- hate;
- reconcile;
- become antagonistic;
- die;
- disappear;
- transform morally/socially;
- reject their expected archetype.

Story Canon governs fictional behavior.

## 9. Scope
ONE NOVEL.

The OS must optimize for one complete, satisfying epic-fantasy novel.

It may create a world with depth beyond the page, but it must NOT:
- defer necessary resolution to hypothetical sequels;
- design Book I as setup for a planned series;
- assume sequels will exist;
- leave the central dramatic/thematic promise unresolved merely for franchise expansion.

The novel may leave natural historical ambiguity and a living world after its ending.

## 10. Autonomous operating authority
APPROVED.

The Novel AI-OS may continuously execute:

BULLPEN → BUILD → STRESS TEST → UMPIRE → REVISE → RETEST → PERSIST → NEXT TASK

without requesting author approval for routine development.

### Stop only for:
1. permanent foundational canon changes that materially alter the approved architecture;
2. irreversible major character decisions where multiple genuinely strong alternatives remain;
3. selection between genuinely equal major creative branches when the OS cannot resolve them through testing;
4. authorization/graduation of manuscript prose;
5. direct conflict with an explicit Jake instruction.

## Operating principle
Do not ask Jake to perform work the Bullpen can resolve through disciplined creative testing.

Escalate decisions, not chores.

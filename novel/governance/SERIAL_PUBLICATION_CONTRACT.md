# SCHEMIN — SERIAL NOVEL PUBLICATION CONTRACT
**Author-approved direction:** 2026-09-26
**Status:** ACTIVE

## Principle
The 2026 fantasy season is the publication clock and a Living Oracle, not a literal chapter outline.

SCHEMIN is one complete novel. During the fantasy season, the Novel AI-OS publishes readable manuscript additions on a weekly cadence. Verified league events may create transformed pressure on future fiction, but weekly results do not overwrite established Story Canon.

## Reader-facing files
- `novel/manuscript/SCHEMIN_MASTER_MANUSCRIPT.md` — always-current readable novel.
- `novel/manuscript/releases/WEEK_01.md` — preserved first publication state.
- `novel/manuscript/releases/WEEK_02.md`
- `novel/manuscript/releases/WEEK_03.md`
- continuing weekly.
- Periodic `.docx` reader editions may be exported from the master manuscript.

## Catch-up rule
Because authorization occurred during fantasy Week 3, the OS must backfill the readable beginning rather than pretend publication began today:
- Week 1 release: opening movement.
- Week 2 release: second manuscript addition, informed only by source pressure available through that release cutoff.
- Week 3 release: current live addition.

Backfilled releases are editorial publication milestones, not claims that prose literally existed on those historical dates.

## Weekly cycle
1. Close prior fantasy week.
2. Verify league evidence through canonical data pipeline.
3. Oracle classifies source pressure and translates useful events.
4. Re-open current story state; never rewrite causality merely to mimic league results.
5. Build next scene/chapter contracts.
6. Draft.
7. Developmental edit.
8. Continuity + provenance + low-magic audit.
9. Reader simulation.
10. Umpire.
11. Revise until passing.
12. Append passing prose to master manuscript.
13. Freeze that week's release snapshot.
14. Update ledgers and continue.

## Publication target
Publish a meaningful readable installment each league week. Installment length is determined by narrative need, not a forced one-chapter-per-week rule.

## Season/endgame rule
The fantasy season may supply ongoing pressure while the novel is being written, but the novel's ending is governed by its own causality. No fantasy result is guaranteed a direct fictional equivalent.

## Quality rule
A weekly deadline cannot force failed prose into the master manuscript. If a section fails, revise it rather than publish knowingly broken continuity.

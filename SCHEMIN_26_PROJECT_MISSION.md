# SCHEMIN ’26 — PROJECT MISSION

**Status:** COMMISSIONER-APPROVED / CANONICAL NORTH STAR  
**Authority:** Jake / Commissioner intent  
**Scope:** Entire Schemin ’26 project, including all current and future publication, world, data, character, visual, and production systems.

Schemin ’26 is a persistent fantasy-sports storytelling and publication universe.

Its core operating loop is:

**Real fantasy-football event → verified league evidence → character consequence → world translation → story architecture → Weekly Memo publication → Living Novel narrative → shared visual production → governed world-state evolution → future continuity.**

For **Week 4 and every future week**, real Pro Schemin’ Football League activity must enter the system as verified fantasy-sports data and then move through one shared universe.

Each meaningful story unit should:

1. establish verified league truth;
2. resolve the participating canonical characters;
3. resolve venue, geography, travel, environment and entering world state;
4. inherit relevant history from prior weeks and seasons;
5. determine the event’s consequences for characters, relationships and the wider Schemin world;
6. produce a Weekly Memo interpretation;
7. produce a Living Novel interpretation;
8. generate visual storytelling from the same canonical character and world packets;
9. pass independent continuity, evidence, visual and publication QA;
10. transact supported consequences into World Evolution so future stories remember what happened.

The Weekly Memo, Living Novel, Universe Atlas and visual system are therefore **not separate creative projects**.

They are different publication surfaces over the **same persistent fictional reality**.

Fantasy-football data supplies the live events.

The Twelve supply the characters.

The Schemin Universe supplies the geography, civilizations, institutions, landmarks, history and consequences.

The publication systems turn those events into mythology.

Nothing resets merely because a new week begins.

Locations remember.

Characters remember.

Institutions remember.

Visual environments remember.

Relationships evolve.

Historical events accumulate.

The universe becomes deeper because the real fantasy season continues to happen inside it.

## North-Star Test

Every future Schemin production should be able to answer:

> **Did this real league event enter one canonical world, become meaningful story across multiple publication forms, and leave behind consequences that the universe will remember?**

If yes, Schemin ’26 is operating as intended.

If the Memo, Novel, artwork or Atlas tell incompatible versions of reality, the system has failed even if the individual artifact looks good.

## Ultimate Objective

Build a living fantasy publication platform where a real fantasy-football league continuously generates the events, history, characters, rivalries and consequences of an expanding fantasy universe—week after week, season after season.

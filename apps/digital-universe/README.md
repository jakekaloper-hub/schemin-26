# Schemin Digital Universe — Week 2 Vertical Slice

A runnable Next.js prototype for the first immersive Schemin issue.

## What exists
- Week 2 cinematic hero
- six-final fast-facts rail
- Opening Drive editorial module
- Special Delivery GOTW stage
- six matchup-world chapters
- Trade Jedi cross-week character dossier
- provenance / immutable-edition treatment
- source-art cinematic stage
- reduced-motion and mobile rules

## Production doctrine
The official Week 2 memo is the visual anchor. This shell deliberately does not invent replacement character artwork. Approved source art is intended to enter the media stage through the media manifest, then receive controlled depth, atmosphere, camera movement, sound and verified typography.

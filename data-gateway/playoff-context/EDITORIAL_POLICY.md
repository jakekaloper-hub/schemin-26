# Editorial Policy — V1

The analytics layer is subordinate to story architecture.

Eligible callouts are rare and material: exact clinch/elimination; extreme conditional playoff-odds swing; unusually high matchup leverage; major threshold crossing. Whole-percentage display is preferred.

Prohibited upgrades:

- Monte Carlo 0% -> `ELIMINATED`;
- Monte Carlo 100% -> `CLINCHED`;
- "must win" unless exact loss-elimination logic proves it;
- automatic insertion into every matchup page;
- analytics replacing Power Rankings or narrative consequence.

Default editorial cap: 0–3 notes per issue.

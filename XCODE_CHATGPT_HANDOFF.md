# 2. PROJECT IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Schemin '26 is the operating ecosystem for the 2026 Pro Schemin' Football League. The immediate creative program is **The World of Pro Schemin' / Pro Schemin' Chronicles**: transform the league's twelve established fantasy characters, real ESPN-driven season events, published weekly memos, league history, rivalries, transactions, wagers, and visual canon into one persistent illustrated fantasy universe. **Core law:** The season supplies events. The universe turns those events into history.

# 5. CANON GOVERNANCE

Every substantive world claim must be classified:

### LOCKED CANON
Explicitly established by approved character canon, published artifacts, verified league history/data, or direct Commissioner correction.

### SOFT CANON
Strongly established or implied and safe to build around, but still expandable.

### PROPOSED LORE
Bullpen invention. It is not canon merely because an AI wrote it.

### OPEN TERRITORY
Deliberately unresolved. Preserve uncertainty rather than filling gaps cheaply.

**Evidence before inference. Extract before inventing.**

Never silently promote proposed lore into canon.

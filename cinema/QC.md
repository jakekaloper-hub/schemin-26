# Cinema QC

A candidate passes only when:
- principal character identity remains recognizable and canon-correct
- composition remains faithful to official art
- no invented score/text is visible
- no extra limbs or destructive morphing
- motion serves the original scene
- verified score overlay matches WSP
- source/model/settings are recorded
- mobile encode has a poster frame and reduced-motion fallback

Special Delivery priority: subtle environmental animation beats aggressive character motion.
